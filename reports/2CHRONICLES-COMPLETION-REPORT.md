## FLAGS CARRIED FOR ROBERT

1. **The deliberative-question item, as it touches this book — PENDING ROBERT, undecided; this book's places on the item are 18:5 (twice), 18:14 (twice), and 25:9, and its confirmed non-members are named so the item is not read more widely than the audits placed it.** The item is campaign-wide and undecided: three positions stand on record for deliberative “shall” questions — keep “shall,” render “will,” or render “should” — and Robert's one ruling will apply back uniformly. At 18:5 Ahab asks the assembled prophets, “Will we go to Ramoth Gilead to battle, or will I forbear?” and at 18:14 the same question is put to Micaiah in the same words; Audit Crew AS-1 placed all four occurrences on the item, with reasoning: the kings are weighing their own act, and the alternative (go, or forbear) is stated in the question itself (compare the parallel at 1 Kings 22:15). The rendering “will” stands as the crews' best reading at all four places. At 25:9 Amaziah asks the man of God, “But what will we do for the hundred talents which I have given to the army of Israel?” Audit Crew AU-1 placed the verse on the item: Amaziah is weighing his own outlay and asks before he acts. Expressly OFF the item, with protective placements, are these. At 18:15 the king's “How many times will I adjure you that you speak to me nothing but the truth in Yahweh's name?” is excluded by AS-1, a reasoned dissent from the drafter's flag: the question weighs no act of the king's and states no alternatives, so it belongs to the fact family; Robert may revisit the exclusion when he rules the item. The third occurrence at 18:14, Micaiah's “They will be delivered into your hand,” is a declared outcome in the same verse, fact family, and is not swept onto the item with the two question places. At 2:6 Solomon's questions (“But who is able to build him a house…? Who am I then, that I should build him a house…?”) are excluded by AQ-1: they are capacity questions, and the build decision was already made and announced before they were asked. At 19:2 Jehu's question is a rebuke of a past act, excluded by AT-1. The book's remaining counsel and prayer questions carry no “shall” and join nothing: 1:7 and 1:10; 6:18; 10:6 and 10:9; 20:6, 20:7, and 20:12; 24:6 and 24:20; 28:10; and chapter 32's questions.
