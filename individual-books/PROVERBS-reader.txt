Clear English Bible — Proverbs (Chapters 1–31): A Clear English Translation

This completes the Masoretic book of Proverbs in this edition. The remaining books of the Old Testament stand in this edition as a clear-English modernization of the World English Bible (WEB) Classic text named in the front matter, except the books the front matter names as fresh translations made directly from the Hebrew; the New Testament stands on the Greek text named there. The book as printed here stands as DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED.

Insertion record — 24 November 2026. The Masoretic book of Proverbs (31 chapters, 915 verses) was inserted as a fresh translation made directly from the Masoretic Hebrew under Robert Tilford's Fresh Tanakh commission ruling of 23 November 2026 and its authorization-forward amendment, confirmed the same day, replacing the earlier WEB-based text of the book. The Masoretic and English numberings are identical for this book, so the block is not reseated and the block header is unchanged. The translation was built in six reviser passes (Masoretic chapters 1–5; 6–10; 11–15; 16–20; 21–25; 26–31) from the Masoretic text as it stands in the Westminster Leningrad Codex and Sefaria's Miqra according to the Masorah — the two witnesses consonant-exact in 776 of the book's 915 verses, the 139 divergences classified in the build record (47 ketiv/qere presentations, the qere printed; 38 Bolls initial-letter drops, the dropped-shin defect dominant, the draft following Sefaria in every case; 27 spelling only; 17 mixed; 5 whole-word drops; 5 encoding only) — with JPS 1917 as the open control of record. Six independent checker passes that did not build the text read the draft against the Hebrew line and both controls: five returned PASS with zero errors, and one (chapters 16–20) returned PASS WITH CORRECTIONS — one error (19:7, the ketiv's sense printed over the qere), corrected with the checker's exact replacement text and the book re-verified by count before insertion. The divine name יהוה stands 87 times in the book — no banked benchmark existed, so the count was established from both witnesses before the build; the two witnesses agree at every verse — and prints "Yahweh" at every seat; no standalone יהּ seat stands in Proverbs. The woman-of-worth poem (31:10–31) was verified an alphabet acrostic from both witnesses before the build — the twenty-two verses' first letters run the alphabet in order in each witness — and prints in the Masoretic order exactly as it stands. חסד prints "loyal love" at 3:3, 14:22, 16:6, 19:22, 20:6, 20:28 (twice), 21:21, and 31:26, with one recorded exception at 14:34, where the word stands in its disgrace sense and both controls read "sin is a reproach to any people"; the exception stands on the record and was upheld by the checker. The difference ledger records 821 of the book's 915 verses as differing materially from the seated text or the open control, and 123 open seats stand recorded. The directed seats stand as the commission named them: 1:7 ("The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of knowledge", the Name without the article); 3:5–6 ("Trust in Yahweh with all your heart... and he will make your paths straight", the promise in its singular form as the Hebrew stands); 8:22 (קנני printed "Yahweh possessed me", the verb in its primary sense; "created me" and JPS's "made me" recorded in the build record and not printed); 8:30 (אמון printed "a master worker", the "nursling" fork recorded open); 9:10 ("the knowledge of the Holy One is understanding"); 16:1–9 (the Yahweh cluster at full force, chapter 16 carrying eleven Name seats, the book's densest chapter); 19:7 (printed at the qere, "they are his", as corrected); 25:21–22 ("If your enemy is hungry, give him bread to eat... you will heap coals of fire on his head", as the Hebrew stands); 30:5–6 ("Every word of God is refined... Do not add to his words"); 30:18–19 (the four things, עלמה printed "a young woman" as the Hebrew stands); and 31:10–31 entire, closing at 31:30 ("a woman who fears Yahweh, she shall be praised"). Build record: PACKET-PROVERBS.md, LEDGER-PROVERBS.md, CHECK-REPORT-PROVERBS.md, and OPEN-SEATS-PROVERBS.md in the desk's working folder (.tools/fresh-proverbs/). Proverbs 1

1 The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel:
2 to know wisdom and instruction, to discern the words of understanding;
3 to receive instruction in wise dealing, in righteousness, justice, and fairness;
4 to give prudence to the simple, knowledge and discretion to the young,
5 let the wise hear and increase in learning, and let the man of understanding obtain sound counsel,
6 to understand a proverb and a parable, the words of the wise and their riddles.
7 The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction.
8 My son, listen to your father's instruction, and do not forsake your mother's teaching,
9 for they will be a graceful garland for your head, and necklaces around your neck.
10 My son, if sinners entice you, do not consent.
11 If they say: Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us lurk secretly for the innocent without cause,
12 let us swallow them up alive like Sheol, and whole, like those who go down into the pit;
13 we will find all kinds of valuable wealth, we will fill our houses with plunder;
14 cast your lot among us, we will all have one purse —
15 my son, do not walk on the way with them, keep your foot from their path,
16 for their feet run to evil, and they hurry to shed blood.
17 For the net is spread in vain in the sight of any bird,
18 but these lie in wait for their own blood, they lurk secretly for their own lives.
19 So are the ways of everyone who is greedy for gain; it takes away the life of its owners.
20 Wisdom calls aloud in the street, she raises her voice in the public squares;
21 she calls at the head of the noisy places, at the entrances of the city gates she speaks her words:
22 How long, you simple ones, will you love simplicity? How long will mockers delight in mockery, and fools hate knowledge?
23 Turn at my reproof; see, I will pour out my spirit to you, I will make my words known to you.
24 Because I called and you refused, I stretched out my hand and no one paid attention,
25 and you ignored all my counsel and would have none of my reproof,
26 I also will laugh at your calamity, I will mock when your dread comes,
27 when your dread comes like a storm, and your calamity comes on like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish come on you.
28 Then they will call on me, but I will not answer; they will seek me earnestly, but they will not find me,
29 because they hated knowledge and did not choose the fear of Yahweh.
30 They would have none of my counsel, they despised all my reproof.
31 So they will eat of the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own schemes.
32 For the waywardness of the simple will kill them, and the complacency of fools will destroy them.
33 But whoever listens to me will dwell securely, and will be at ease without fear of harm.

Proverbs 2

1 My son, if you receive my words and store up my commandments within you,
2 turning your ear to wisdom and applying your heart to understanding;
3 yes, if you call out for discernment and lift up your voice for understanding;
4 if you seek her as silver and search for her as for hidden treasures;
5 then you will understand the fear of Yahweh, and find the knowledge of God.
6 For Yahweh gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding.
7 He stores up sound wisdom for the upright; he is a shield to those who walk in integrity,
8 guarding the paths of justice, and he preserves the way of his faithful ones.
9 Then you will understand righteousness and justice, equity and every good path.
10 For wisdom will enter your heart, and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.
11 Discretion will watch over you; understanding will guard you,
12 to deliver you from the way of evil, from the man who speaks perverse things,
13 those who forsake the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness,
14 those who rejoice to do evil and delight in the perverseness of evil,
15 whose paths are crooked, and who are devious in their ways,
16 to deliver you from the strange woman, from the foreign woman who makes her words smooth,
17 who forsakes the companion of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God;
18 for her house sinks down to death, and her paths to the departed spirits.
19 None who go to her return, nor do they attain the paths of life.
20 so that you may walk in the way of the good, and keep the paths of the righteous.
21 For the upright will dwell in the land, and the blameless will remain in it.
22 But the wicked will be cut off from the land, and the treacherous will be rooted out of it.

Proverbs 3

1 My son, do not forget my teaching, but let your heart keep my commandments,
2 for they will add to you length of days, years of life, and peace.
3 Do not let loyal love and truth forsake you; bind them around your neck, write them on the tablet of your heart.
4 Then you will find favor and good insight in the sight of God and man.
5 Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding.
6 In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight.
7 Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear Yahweh, and turn away from evil.
8 It will be health to your body, and nourishment to your bones.
9 Honor Yahweh with your wealth, and with the firstfruits of all your produce;
10 then your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will overflow with new wine.
11 My son, do not despise Yahweh's discipline, and do not be weary of his correction,
12 for Yahweh corrects the one he loves, even as a father the son in whom he delights.
13 Happy is the man who finds wisdom, and the man who gets understanding.
14 For her profit is better than the profit of silver, and her gain better than fine gold.
15 She is more precious than pearls, and all the things you desire cannot compare with her.
16 Length of days is in her right hand; in her left hand are riches and honor.
17 Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace.
18 She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her, and happy is everyone who holds her fast.
19 Yahweh founded the earth by wisdom; by understanding he established the heavens.
20 By his knowledge the depths were broken open, and the skies drop down dew.
21 My son, let them not depart from your eyes; keep sound wisdom and discretion,
22 and they will be life to your soul, and grace for your neck.
23 Then you will walk securely in your way, and your foot will not stumble.
24 When you lie down, you will not be afraid; you will lie down, and your sleep will be sweet.
25 Do not be afraid of sudden terror, nor of the ruin of the wicked when it comes,
26 for Yahweh will be your confidence, and will keep your foot from being caught.
27 Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in the power of your hand to do it.
28 Do not say to your neighbor: Go, and come again, and tomorrow I will give, when you have it with you.
29 Do not devise evil against your neighbor, while he dwells securely beside you.
30 Do not contend with a man without cause, if he has done you no harm.
31 Do not envy a man of violence, and choose none of his ways.
32 For the devious man is an abomination to Yahweh, but his intimate counsel is with the upright.
33 Yahweh's curse is on the house of the wicked, but he blesses the dwelling of the righteous.
34 He mocks mockers, but to the humble he gives favor.
35 The wise will inherit honor, but fools carry away shame.

Proverbs 4

1 Listen, sons, to a father's instruction, and pay attention to know understanding,
2 for I give you good learning; do not forsake my teaching.
3 For I was a son to my father, tender and an only child before my mother.
4 He taught me and said to me: Let your heart hold fast to my words; keep my commandments, and live.
5 Get wisdom, get understanding; do not forget, and do not turn away from the words of my mouth.
6 Do not forsake her, and she will preserve you; love her, and she will guard you.
7 The beginning of wisdom is this: get wisdom, and with all your getting, get understanding.
8 Prize her highly, and she will exalt you; she will honor you when you embrace her.
9 She will give your head a garland of grace; she will deliver to you a crown of splendor.
10 Listen, my son, and receive my sayings, and the years of your life will be many.
11 I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have led you in the paths of uprightness.
12 When you walk, your step will not be hampered, and if you run, you will not stumble.
13 Hold fast to instruction, do not let go; guard her, for she is your life.
14 Do not enter the path of the wicked, and do not walk in the way of evil people.
15 Avoid it, do not pass by it; turn from it, and pass on.
16 For they do not sleep unless they have done evil, and their sleep is taken away unless they make someone stumble.
17 For they eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of violence.
18 But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn, shining brighter and brighter until the full day.
19 The way of the wicked is like deep darkness; they do not know what they stumble over.
20 My son, pay attention to my words; incline your ear to my sayings.
21 Let them not depart from your eyes; keep them in the midst of your heart.
22 For they are life to those who find them, and health to all their flesh.
23 Guard your heart above all guarding, for from it flow the springs of life.
24 Put away from you a crooked mouth, and put devious lips far from you.
25 Let your eyes look straight ahead, and let your gaze be straight before you.
26 Make level the path of your foot, and let all your ways be established.
27 Do not turn to the right or to the left; remove your foot from evil.

Proverbs 5

1 My son, pay attention to my wisdom; incline your ear to my understanding,
2 that you may keep discretion, and your lips may guard knowledge.
3 For the lips of a strange woman drip honey, and her mouth is smoother than oil,
4 but her end is bitter as wormwood, and sharp as a two-edged sword.
5 Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold of Sheol.
6 She does not ponder the path of life; her ways wander, and she does not know it.
7 Now therefore, sons, listen to me, and do not depart from the words of my mouth.
8 Keep your way far from her, and do not go near the door of her house,
9 lest you give your vigor to others, and your years to the cruel one,
10 lest strangers be filled with your strength, and your labors be in the house of a foreigner,
11 and you groan at your end, when your flesh and your body are consumed,
12 and you say: How I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof!
13 I did not listen to the voice of my teachers, nor incline my ear to those who instructed me.
14 I was almost in utter ruin, in the midst of the congregation and assembly.
15 Drink water from your own cistern, and flowing water from your own well.
16 Should your springs be dispersed outside, and streams of water in the public squares?
17 Let them be for yourself alone, and not for strangers with you.
18 Let your fountain be blessed, and rejoice in the wife of your youth.
19 A loving doe and a graceful deer — let her breasts satisfy you at all times; be intoxicated always in her love.
20 Why then, my son, should you be intoxicated with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a foreign woman?
21 For a man's ways are before Yahweh's eyes, and he makes level all his paths.
22 His own iniquities will capture the wicked, and he will be held by the cords of his sin.
23 He will die for lack of instruction, and in the greatness of his folly he will go astray.

Proverbs 6

1 My son, if you have become surety for your neighbor, if you have struck hands for a stranger,
2 you are snared by the words of your mouth, you are caught by the words of your mouth.
3 Do this now, my son, and free yourself, since you have come into the hand of your neighbor: go, humble yourself, and press your neighbor.
4 Give no sleep to your eyes, and no slumber to your eyelids.
5 Free yourself like a gazelle from the hand, and like a bird from the hand of the trapper.
6 Go to the ant, you sluggard; see her ways, and be wise,
7 which has no chief, overseer, or ruler,
8 yet she prepares her bread in the summer, and gathers her food in the harvest.
9 How long will you lie down, sluggard? When will you rise from your sleep?
10 A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to lie down,
11 and your poverty will come like a traveler, and your want like a man with a shield.
12 A worthless person, a man of iniquity, walks with a crooked mouth,
13 winking with his eyes, scraping with his feet, pointing with his fingers,
14 with perversity in his heart; he devises evil at all times, he sends out strife.
15 Therefore his calamity will come suddenly; suddenly he will be broken, and there will be no healing.
16 There are six things that Yahweh hates, and seven that are an abomination to him:
17 haughty eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,
18 a heart that devises plans of iniquity, feet that hurry to run to evil,
19 a false witness who breathes out lies, and one who sends out strife among brothers.
20 My son, keep the commandment of your father, and do not forsake the teaching of your mother.
21 Bind them on your heart always; tie them around your neck.
22 When you walk, it will lead you; when you lie down, it will watch over you; and when you awake, it will talk with you.
23 For the commandment is a lamp, and the teaching is light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life,
24 to keep you from the evil woman, from the smoothness of the tongue of the foreign woman.
25 Do not desire her beauty in your heart, and do not let her capture you with her eyelids.
26 For on account of a prostitute one comes down to a loaf of bread, and the wife of a man hunts for a precious life.
27 Can a man scoop fire into his bosom, and his clothes not be burned?
28 Or can a man walk on the hot coals, and his feet not be scorched?
29 So is the one who goes in to the wife of his neighbor; everyone who touches her will not go unpunished.
30 People do not despise a thief if he steals to fill his appetite when he is hungry,
31 but if he is found, he will repay sevenfold; he will give all the wealth of his house.
32 A man who commits adultery with a woman lacks heart; the one who does it destroys his own life.
33 He will find wounds and dishonor, and his reproach will not be wiped away.
34 For jealousy is the fury of a man, and he will not spare in the day of vengeance.
35 He will not accept any ransom, and he will not consent even if you multiply gifts.

Proverbs 7

1 My son, keep my words, and store up my commandments with you.
2 Keep my commandments and live, and my teaching as the pupil of your eye.
3 Bind them on your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart.
4 Say to wisdom: You are my sister, and call understanding a close relative,
5 to keep you from the foreign woman, from the outsider who makes her words smooth.
6 For at the window of my house, through my lattice, I looked out,
7 and I saw among the simple ones, I discerned among the sons a young man who lacks heart,
8 passing through the street near her corner, and he steps on the way to her house,
9 in the twilight, in the evening of the day, in the depth of night and darkness.
10 And there, a woman meets him, with the dress of a prostitute and a guarded heart.
11 She is loud and rebellious; her feet do not stay in her house.
12 Now she is outside, now in the squares, and at every corner she lies in wait.
13 She seizes him and kisses him; she makes her face bold and says to him:
14 Sacrifices of peace offerings are with me; today I have paid my vows.
15 Therefore I have come out to meet you, to seek your face eagerly, and I have found you.
16 I have spread my couch with coverings, with striped cloth of Egyptian linen.
17 I have sprinkled my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.
18 Come, let us drink deeply of love until the morning; let us delight ourselves with loves.
19 For the man is not at home; he has gone on a journey far away.
20 He has taken the bag of money in his hand; at the day of the full moon he will come home.
21 She turns him aside with her great persuasion; with the smoothness of her lips she drives him on.
22 He goes after her suddenly, as an ox comes to the slaughter, and as one in fetters to the correction of a fool,
23 until an arrow pierces his liver, as a bird hurries to a snare and does not know that it is for his life.
24 And now, sons, listen to me, and pay attention to the words of my mouth.
25 Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not stray in her paths,
26 for she has cast down many wounded, and all her slain are strong men.
27 Her house is the ways of Sheol, going down to the chambers of death.

Proverbs 8

1 Does wisdom not call out, and does understanding not give her voice?
2 On the top of the heights, beside the way, at the house of the pathways, she has taken her stand.
3 Beside the gates, at the mouth of the city, at the entrance of the doors, she cries aloud:
4 To you, men, I call, and my voice is to the sons of man.
5 You simple ones, understand prudence, and you fools, understand with the heart.
6 Hear, for I will speak noble things, and the opening of my lips will be right things.
7 For my palate speaks truth, and wickedness is an abomination to my lips.
8 All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing twisted or crooked in them.
9 They are all plain to the one who understands, and right to those who find knowledge.
10 Take my instruction and not silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold.
11 For wisdom is better than jewels, and all desirable things cannot compare with her.
12 I, wisdom, dwell with prudence, and I find knowledge and discretion.
13 The fear of Yahweh is to hate evil; pride and arrogance and the evil way and a mouth of perversities I hate.
14 Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom; I am understanding, strength is mine.
15 By me kings reign, and rulers decree righteousness.
16 By me princes rule, and nobles, all who judge righteously.
17 I love those who love me, and those who seek me early will find me.
18 Riches and honor are with me, enduring wealth and righteousness.
19 My fruit is better than gold and than fine gold, and my yield than choice silver.
20 I walk in the path of righteousness, in the midst of the pathways of justice,
21 to give those who love me substance to inherit, and I will fill their treasuries.
22 Yahweh possessed me at the beginning of his way, before his works of old.
23 From of old I was set up, from the beginning, from the first times of the earth.
24 When there were no depths I was brought forth, when there were no springs heavy with water.
25 Before the mountains were sunk down, before the hills, I was brought forth,
26 while he had not yet made the earth and the open places, and the first of the dust of the world.
27 When he established the heavens, I was there; when he drew a circle on the face of the deep,
28 when he made the clouds firm above, when the springs of the deep grew strong,
29 when he set his limit for the sea, so that the waters would not cross his mouth, when he marked out the foundations of the earth,
30 then I was beside him as a master worker, and I was a delight day by day, playing before him at all times,
31 playing in the world of his earth, and my delight was with the sons of man.
32 And now, sons, listen to me, and blessed are those who keep my ways.
33 Hear instruction and be wise, and do not refuse it.
34 Blessed is the man who listens to me, watching daily at my gates, keeping watch at the posts of my doors.
35 For the one who finds me finds life, and obtains favor from Yahweh.
36 But the one who sins against me wrongs his own life; all who hate me love death.

Proverbs 9

1 Wisdom has built her house; she has hewn her seven pillars.
2 She has slaughtered her meat, she has mixed her wine, she has also set her table.
3 She has sent out her young women; she calls on the tops of the heights of the city:
4 Whoever is simple, let him turn in here. To the one who lacks heart she says:
5 Come, eat of my bread, and drink of the wine I have mixed.
6 Leave your simple ways, and live, and walk in the way of understanding.
7 The one who corrects a scoffer takes shame for himself, and the one who reproves a wicked man takes his blemish.
8 Do not reprove a scoffer, lest he hate you; reprove a wise man, and he will love you.
9 Give to a wise man, and he will become still wiser; make known to a righteous man, and he will increase in learning.
10 The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.
11 For by me your days will be many, and years of life will be added to you.
12 If you are wise, you are wise for yourself, and if you scoff, you alone will bear it.
13 The woman Folly is loud; she is simple and knows nothing.
14 She sits at the entrance of her house, on a seat on the heights of the city,
15 to call to those who pass on the way, who make their paths straight:
16 Whoever is simple, let him turn in here. And to the one who lacks heart she says:
17 Stolen waters are sweet, and bread of secret places is pleasant.
18 But he does not know that the shades are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol.

Proverbs 10

1 The proverbs of Solomon. A wise son makes a father glad, but a foolish son is the grief of his mother.
2 Treasures of wickedness profit nothing, but righteousness delivers from death.
3 Yahweh will not let the life of the righteous go hungry, but he thrusts away the craving of the wicked.
4 A slack hand makes poverty, but the hand of the diligent makes rich.
5 One who gathers in summer is a prudent son; one who sleeps in harvest is a son who brings shame.
6 Blessings are on the head of the righteous, but the mouth of the wicked covers violence.
7 The memory of the righteous is for a blessing, but the name of the wicked will rot.
8 The wise of heart will take commandments, but a fool of lips will fall.
9 One who walks in integrity walks securely, but one who twists his ways will be known.
10 One who winks the eye gives pain, and a fool of lips will fall.
11 The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, but the mouth of the wicked covers violence.
12 Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all transgressions.
13 On the lips of the discerning, wisdom is found, but a rod is for the back of one who lacks heart.
14 Wise men store up knowledge, but the mouth of a fool is ruin near at hand.
15 The wealth of a rich man is his strong city; the poverty of the poor is their ruin.
16 The wage of the righteous is to life; the yield of the wicked is to sin.
17 One who keeps instruction is on the path to life, but one who forsakes reproof leads astray.
18 One who covers hatred has lips of falsehood, and one who brings out slander is a fool.
19 In many words transgression does not cease, but one who holds back his lips is prudent.
20 The tongue of the righteous is choice silver; the heart of the wicked is worth little.
21 The lips of the righteous feed many, but fools die for lack of heart.
22 The blessing of Yahweh, it makes rich, and he adds no pain with it.
23 Doing a shameful plan is like sport for a fool, and wisdom is the same for a man of understanding.
24 What the wicked dreads will come on him, but the desire of the righteous will be granted.
25 When the whirlwind passes, the wicked is no more, but the righteous is a foundation forever.
26 Like vinegar to the teeth and like smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to those who send him.
27 The fear of Yahweh adds days, but the years of the wicked will be cut short.
28 The hope of the righteous is gladness, but the expectation of the wicked will perish.
29 The way of Yahweh is a stronghold for integrity, but ruin for workers of iniquity.
30 The righteous will never be shaken, but the wicked will not dwell in the land.
31 The mouth of the righteous bears the fruit of wisdom, but the tongue of perversities will be cut off.
32 The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable, but the mouth of the wicked is perversities.

Proverbs 11

1 A false balance is detestable to Yahweh, but a full weight is his delight.
2 When pride comes, then shame comes, but wisdom is with the humble.
3 The integrity of the upright will guide them, but the crookedness of the treacherous will destroy them.
4 Riches do not profit in the day of wrath, but righteousness delivers from death.
5 The righteousness of the blameless will make his way straight, but the wicked will fall by his own wickedness.
6 The righteousness of the upright will deliver them, but the treacherous will be trapped by their desire.
7 When a wicked man dies, hope perishes, and the expectation of strength perishes.
8 The righteous is delivered from trouble, and the wicked comes in his place.
9 With his mouth the godless man destroys his neighbor, but through knowledge the righteous will be delivered.
10 When it goes well with the righteous, the city rejoices, and when the wicked perish, there is joyful shouting.
11 By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted, but it is torn down by the mouth of the wicked.
12 One who despises his neighbor lacks sense, but a man of understanding keeps silent.
13 One who goes about as a slanderer reveals secrets, but one who is faithful in spirit conceals a matter.
14 Where there is no guidance, a people falls, but in a multitude of counselors there is deliverance.
15 One who gives a pledge for a stranger will suffer badly, but one who refuses to strike hands in pledge is secure.
16 A gracious woman holds on to honor, and violent men hold on to riches.
17 A man of loyal love does good to his own soul, but the cruel man troubles his own flesh.
18 The wicked earns deceitful wages, but one who sows righteousness has a true reward.
19 True righteousness leads to life, and one who pursues evil pursues it to his own death.
20 Those perverse in heart are detestable to Yahweh, but those blameless in their way are his delight.
21 Hand to hand, the evil man will not go unpunished, but the offspring of the righteous will escape.
22 Like a gold ring in the snout of a pig is a beautiful woman who turns away from discretion.

19 Listen, my son, and be wise, and direct your heart in the way.
20 Do not be among wine-drinkers, among gluttonous eaters of meat,
21 for the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe a man with rags.
22 Listen to your father who fathered you, and do not despise your mother when she is old.
23 Buy truth and do not sell it — wisdom, discipline, and understanding.
24 The father of a righteous man will greatly rejoice, and he who fathers a wise child will have joy in him.
25 Let your father and your mother be glad, and let her who bore you rejoice.
26 My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes watch my ways.
27 For a prostitute is a deep pit, and a strange woman is a narrow well.
28 She also lies in wait like a robber and increases the treacherous among men.
29 Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has complaining? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes?
30 Those who linger over wine, those who go to taste mixed wine.
31 Do not look at wine when it is red, when it sparkles in the cup, when it goes down smoothly.
32 In the end it bites like a serpent and stings like a viper.
33 Your eyes will see strange things, and your heart will utter perverse things.
34 You will be like one who lies down in the heart of the sea, like one who lies on the top of a mast.
35 They struck me, but I felt no pain; they beat me, but I did not know it. When will I awake? I will seek it again.

Proverbs 24

1 Do not envy evil men, and do not desire to be with them,
2 for their heart plots violence, and their lips speak trouble.
3 By wisdom a house is built, and by understanding it is established;
4 By knowledge the rooms are filled with all precious and pleasant riches.
5 A wise man is strong, and a man of knowledge increases power.
6 For by wise guidance you will wage your war, and in a multitude of counselors there is victory.
7 Wisdom is too high for a fool; at the gate he does not open his mouth.
8 One who plans to do evil will be called a master of schemes.
9 The scheme of folly is sin, and a scoffer is an abomination to men.
10 If you grow slack in the day of trouble, your strength is small.
11 Rescue those who are taken away to death, and hold back those who are stumbling to the slaughter.
12 If you say: We did not know this — does he who weighs hearts not consider it? He who guards your soul, does he not know it? And will he not repay a man according to his work?
13 Eat honey, my son, for it is good, and the honeycomb is sweet to your taste;
14 so know wisdom for your soul: if you find it, there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off.
15 Do not lie in wait, wicked man, against the dwelling of the righteous; do not destroy his resting place.
16 For a righteous man falls seven times and rises again, but the wicked stumble in calamity.
17 Do not rejoice when your enemy falls, and do not let your heart be glad when he stumbles,
18 lest Yahweh see it and it be evil in his eyes, and he turn his anger away from him.
19 Do not fret because of evildoers, and do not envy the wicked,
20 for the evil man has no future; the lamp of the wicked will go out.
21 Fear Yahweh, my son, and the king; do not join with those given to change,
22 for their calamity will rise suddenly, and who knows the ruin that comes from both of them?
23 These also are sayings of the wise. To show partiality in judgment is not good.
24 He who says to the wicked: You are righteous — peoples will curse him, nations will denounce him,
25 but those who rebuke will have delight, and a good blessing will come upon them.
26 One who answers with right words kisses the lips.
27 Prepare your work outside and make it ready for yourself in the field; afterward, build your house.
28 Do not be a witness against your neighbor without cause, and do not deceive with your lips.
29 Do not say: As he did to me, so I will do to him; I will repay the man according to his work.
30 I passed by the field of a sluggard, by the vineyard of a man without sense,
31 and it was all grown over with thorns; its surface was covered with nettles, and its stone wall was broken down.
32 Then I looked and set my heart on it; I saw and took a lesson.
33 A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest,
34 and your poverty will come like a robber, and your need like an armed man.

Proverbs 25

1 These also are proverbs of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah king of Judah copied out.
2 It is the glory of God to conceal a matter, and the glory of kings to search out a matter.
3 The heavens for height and the earth for depth, and the heart of kings is unsearchable.
4 Remove the dross from the silver, and a vessel comes out for the refiner;
5 Remove the wicked from before the king, and his throne will be established in righteousness.
6 Do not exalt yourself before the king, and do not stand in the place of the great;
7 for it is better to be told: Come up here, than to be lowered before a noble whom your eyes have seen.
8 Do not go out hastily to quarrel, lest in the end you not know what to do when your neighbor puts you to shame.
9 Argue your case with your neighbor, but do not reveal another's secret,
10 lest one who hears it shame you, and your bad reputation never depart.
11 A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in settings of silver.
12 A ring of gold and an ornament of fine gold is a wise reprover to a listening ear.
13 Like the cold of snow in the day of harvest is a faithful messenger to those who send him, for he refreshes the soul of his masters.
14 Clouds and wind and no rain is a man who boasts of a false gift.
15 By patience a ruler is persuaded, and a soft tongue breaks a bone.
16 If you find honey, eat only enough for you, lest you have your fill of it and vomit it.
17 Make your foot rare in your neighbor's house, lest he have his fill of you and hate you.
18 A club, a sword, and a sharp arrow is a man who testifies falsely against his neighbor.
19 A bad tooth and a stumbling foot is trust in a treacherous man in a day of trouble.
20 Like one who takes off a garment on a cold day, and like vinegar on soda, is one who sings songs to a troubled heart.
21 If your enemy is hungry, give him bread to eat, and if he is thirsty, give him water to drink,
22 for you will heap coals of fire on his head, and Yahweh will reward you.
23 The north wind brings forth rain, and a backbiting tongue brings an angry face.
24 It is better to live in a corner of the housetop than with a contentious woman in a shared house.
25 Like cold water to a weary soul is good news from a far country.
26 A muddied spring and a corrupted fountain is a righteous man who gives way before the wicked.
27 It is not good to eat much honey, and to search out one's own glory is not glory.
28 A city broken through, with no wall, is a man who has no restraint over his spirit.

Proverbs 26

1 Like snow in summer and rain in harvest, so honor is not fitting for a fool.
2 Like a sparrow in its wandering, like a swallow in its flying, so a causeless curse comes to him.
3 A whip for the horse, a bridle for the donkey, and a rod for the back of fools.
4 Do not answer a fool according to his folly, lest you also be like him.
5 Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own eyes.
6 One who sends a message by the hand of a fool cuts off his own feet and drinks violence.
7 Like the legs of a lame man that hang limp, so is a proverb in the mouth of fools.
8 Like one who binds a stone in a sling, so is one who gives honor to a fool.
9 Like a thorn that goes up into the hand of a drunkard, so is a proverb in the mouth of fools.
10 Like an archer who wounds everyone, so is one who hires a fool or hires passers-by.
11 As a dog that returns to his vomit, so is a fool who repeats his folly.
12 Do you see a man wise in his own eyes? There is more hope for a fool than for him.
13 The sluggard says: There is a lion in the road, a lion in the streets.
14 As the door turns on its hinges, so does the sluggard on his bed.
15 The sluggard buries his hand in the dish; he is too weary to bring it back to his mouth.
16 The sluggard is wiser in his own eyes than seven men who answer with discretion.
17 Like one who takes a dog by the ears is one who passes by and meddles in a quarrel that is not his own.
18 Like a madman who shoots firebrands, arrows, and death,
19 so is a man who deceives his neighbor and says: Was I not joking?
20 For lack of wood a fire goes out, and where there is no whisperer, strife falls silent.
21 Charcoal for embers and wood for fire, and a contentious man for kindling strife.
22 The words of a whisperer are like tasty morsels, and they go down into the innermost parts of the body.
23 Like silver dross coating an earthen vessel are burning lips and an evil heart.
24 He who hates disguises himself with his lips, and within him he harbors deceit.
25 When he makes his voice gracious, do not believe him, for there are seven abominations in his heart.
26 Hatred may be covered by deceit, but his wickedness will be revealed in the assembly.
27 Whoever digs a pit will fall into it, and whoever rolls a stone, it will return on him.
28 A lying tongue hates those crushed by it, and a flattering mouth works ruin.

Proverbs 27

1 Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring.
2 Let another praise you, and not your own mouth; a stranger, and not your own lips.
3 A stone is heavy and sand is weighty, but a fool's vexation is heavier than both.
4 Wrath is cruel and anger is a flood, but who can stand before jealousy?
5 Better is open rebuke than hidden love.
6 Trustworthy are the wounds of a friend, but the kisses of an enemy are profuse.
7 A full soul tramples on a honeycomb, but to a hungry soul every bitter thing is sweet.
8 Like a bird that wanders from its nest, so is a man who wanders from his place.
9 Oil and incense make the heart glad, and the sweetness of a friend comes from heartfelt counsel.
10 Do not forsake your friend and your father's friend, and do not go to your brother's house in the day of your calamity; better is a neighbor who is near than a brother who is far.
11 Be wise, my son, and make my heart glad, that I may answer the one who taunts me.
12 A prudent man sees evil and hides himself; the simple pass on and are punished.
13 Take his garment, for he has given surety for a stranger, and hold him in pledge for a foreign woman.
14 One who blesses his friend with a loud voice, rising early in the morning, it will be counted a curse to him.
15 A continual dripping on a day of driving rain and a contentious woman are alike,
16 restraining her is restraining the wind, and grasping oil with the right hand.
17 Iron sharpens iron, so a man sharpens the countenance of his friend.
18 One who tends a fig tree will eat its fruit, and one who guards his master will be honored.
19 As water reflects a face, so the heart of a man reflects the man.
20 Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied, and the eyes of man are never satisfied.
21 A crucible for silver and a furnace for gold, and a man is tested by his praise.
22 If you crush a fool in a mortar with a pestle among grain, his folly will not depart from him.
23 Know well the condition of your flocks; set your heart on your herds,
24 for wealth does not last forever, and does a crown endure from generation to generation?
25 The hay is gone and the new grass appears, and the herbs of the mountains are gathered in;
26 the lambs are for your clothing, and the goats are the price of a field,
27 and there will be enough goats' milk for your food, for the food of your household, and life for your servant girls.

Proverbs 28

1 The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a young lion.
2 Through the transgression of a land its princes are many, but by a man of understanding and knowledge, established order will long continue.
3 A poor man who oppresses the weak is like a sweeping rain that leaves no food.
4 Those who forsake the law praise the wicked, but those who keep the law contend with them.
5 Evil men do not understand justice, but those who seek Yahweh understand everything.
6 Better is a poor man who walks in his integrity than one who is perverse in his ways though he is rich.
7 One who keeps the law is a son of understanding, but a companion of gluttons shames his father.
8 One who increases his wealth by interest and increase gathers it for one who is gracious to the poor.
9 One who turns his ear away from hearing the law, even his prayer is an abomination.
10 One who leads the upright astray into an evil way will himself fall into his own pit, but the blameless will inherit good.
11 A rich man is wise in his own eyes, but a poor man who has understanding searches him through.
12 When the righteous exult, there is great glory, but when the wicked rise, people hide themselves.
13 One who covers his transgressions will not prosper, but one who confesses and forsakes them will find mercy.
14 Blessed is a man who is always fearful, but he who hardens his heart will fall into evil.
15 A roaring lion and a charging bear is a wicked ruler over a poor people.
16 A prince who lacks understanding is also a great oppressor, but one who hates unjust gain will prolong his days.
17 A man oppressed by the blood of a person will flee to the pit; let no one support him.
18 One who walks blamelessly will be saved, but one who is perverse in his ways will fall at once.
19 One who works his land will be satisfied with bread, but one who chases empty things will be satisfied with poverty.
20 A faithful man will abound with blessings, but one who hurries to be rich will not go unpunished.
21 To show partiality is not good, and for a piece of bread a man will transgress.
22 A man with an evil eye hurries after wealth, and does not know that want will come on him.
23 One who rebukes a man will afterward find more favor than one who flatters with the tongue.
24 One who robs his father or his mother and says: It is no transgression, is a companion of a destroyer.
25 One who is greedy of soul stirs up strife, but one who trusts in Yahweh will be made prosperous.
26 One who trusts in his own heart is a fool, but one who walks in wisdom will be delivered.
27 One who gives to the poor will have no lack, but one who hides his eyes will have many curses.
28 When the wicked rise, a man hides himself, but when they perish, the righteous increase.

Proverbs 29

1 A man who is often reproved and hardens his neck will suddenly be broken, and there will be no remedy.
2 When the righteous increase, the people rejoice, but when a wicked man rules, the people groan.
3 A man who loves wisdom makes his father glad, but a companion of prostitutes wastes wealth.
4 By justice a king makes the land stand, but a man who exacts gifts tears it down.
5 A man who flatters his neighbor spreads a net for his steps.
6 In the transgression of an evil man there is a snare, but the righteous will sing and be glad.
7 The righteous knows the cause of the poor, but the wicked does not understand knowledge.
8 Men of scorn set a city ablaze, but wise men turn back anger.
9 If a wise man goes to law with a foolish man, the fool rages or laughs, and there is no rest.
10 Men of blood hate the blameless, and they seek the life of the upright.
11 A fool lets out all his spirit, but a wise man quietly holds it back.
12 If a ruler listens to a word of falsehood, all his servants are wicked.
13 A poor man and a man of oppressions meet together; Yahweh gives light to the eyes of both.
14 A king who judges the poor faithfully, his throne will be established forever.
15 A rod and reproof give wisdom, but a child left to himself brings shame to his mother.
16 When the wicked increase, transgression increases, but the righteous will look on their fall.
17 Discipline your son, and he will give you rest, and he will give delight to your soul.
18 Where there is no vision, the people cast off restraint, but blessed is one who keeps the law.
19 A servant will not be corrected by words, for though he understands, there is no answer.
20 Do you see a man hasty in his words? There is more hope for a fool than for him.
21 One who pampers his servant from youth will have him become a son in the end.
22 An angry man stirs up strife, and a wrathful man abounds in transgression.
23 A man's pride will bring him low, but one who is lowly in spirit will attain honor.
24 One who shares with a thief hates his own soul; he hears the oath and tells nothing.
25 The fear of man sets a snare, but one who trusts in Yahweh will be set on high.
26 Many seek the face of a ruler, but a man's justice is from Yahweh.
27 An unjust man is an abomination to the righteous, and one upright in the way is an abomination to the wicked.

Proverbs 30

1 The words of Agur the son of Jakeh, the oracle. The man declares to Ithiel, to Ithiel and Ucal:
2 For I am brutish, unlike a man, and the understanding of a man is not mine,
3 and I have not learned wisdom, and I do not have the knowledge of the Holy One.
4 Who has gone up to heaven and come down? Who has gathered the wind in his fists? Who has bound the waters in a garment? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What is his name, and what is the name of his son? Surely you know.
5 Every word of God is refined; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him.
6 Do not add to his words, lest he reprove you and you be proved a liar.
7 Two things I have asked of you; do not deny them to me before I die:
8 Remove far from me falsehood and lies; give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with my allotted bread,
9 lest I be full and deny you and say: Who is Yahweh? or lest I be poor and steal and profane the name of my God.
10 Do not slander a servant to his master, lest he curse you and you be found guilty.
11 There is a generation that curses its father and does not bless its mother.
12 There is a generation that is pure in its own eyes and yet is not washed from its filth.
13 There is a generation, oh how lofty are its eyes, and its eyelids are lifted up.
14 There is a generation whose teeth are swords and its jaw teeth knives, to devour the poor from the earth and the needy from among men.
15 The leech has two daughters: Give, Give. Three things are never satisfied, four that do not say: Enough:
16 Sheol and a closed womb, an earth not satisfied with water, and fire that never says: Enough.
17 An eye that mocks a father and despises obedience to a mother, the ravens of the valley will pick it out and the young eagles will eat it.
18 Three things are too wonderful for me, four I do not know:
19 the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a serpent on a rock, the way of a ship in the heart of the sea, and the way of a man with a young woman.
20 So is the way of an adulterous woman: she eats and wipes her mouth and says: I have done no wrong.
21 Under three things the earth trembles, and under four it cannot bear up:
22 under a servant when he becomes king, and a fool when he is filled with bread,
23 under a hated woman when she is married, and a female servant when she displaces her mistress.
24 There are four things that are small on the earth, but they are exceedingly wise:
25 The ants are a people not strong, yet they prepare their food in the summer;
26 the hyraxes are a people not mighty, yet they make their houses in the rock;
27 the locusts have no king, yet they all go out in ranks;
28 the lizard you can take in your hands, yet it is in kings' palaces.
29 Three things are stately in their stride, four stately in their going:
30 a lion, mighty among beasts, that does not turn back before anything;
31 the greyhound, the male goat, and a king against whom there is no rising up.
32 If you have been foolish in exalting yourself, or if you have plotted, put your hand to your mouth.
33 For pressing milk produces curds, and pressing the nose produces blood, and pressing anger produces strife.

Proverbs 31

1 The words of Lemuel the king, an oracle that his mother taught him:
2 What, my son? What, son of my womb? What, son of my vows?
3 Do not give your strength to women, or your ways to those who destroy kings.
4 It is not for kings, Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine, nor for princes: Where is strong drink?
5 Lest he drink and forget what is decreed and change the judgment of all the sons of affliction.
6 Give strong drink to one who is perishing, and wine to those bitter in soul;
7 let him drink and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.
8 Open your mouth for the mute, for the cause of all who are perishing.
9 Open your mouth, judge in righteousness, and give justice to the poor and needy.
10 A woman of worth, who can find? Her price is far beyond rubies.
11 The heart of her husband trusts in her, and he will lack no gain.
12 She does him good and not evil all the days of her life.
13 She seeks wool and flax, and works willingly with her hands.
14 She is like the ships of a merchant; from afar she brings her food.
15 She rises while it is still night and gives food to her household and a portion to her servant girls.
16 She considers a field and buys it; with the fruit of her hands she plants a vineyard.
17 She girds her waist with strength and makes her arms strong.
18 She tastes that her trading is good; her lamp does not go out at night.
19 She puts her hands to the distaff, and her hands hold the spindle.
20 She opens her palm to the poor, and she stretches out her hands to the needy.
21 She does not fear for her household because of snow, for all her household are clothed in scarlet.
22 She makes coverings for herself; her clothing is fine linen and purple.
23 Her husband is known in the gates, when he sits among the elders of the land.
24 She makes linen garments and sells them, and she gives belts to the merchant.
25 Strength and dignity are her clothing, and she laughs at the day to come.
26 She opens her mouth with wisdom, and the teaching of loyal love is on her tongue.
27 She watches the ways of her household, and she does not eat the bread of idleness.
28 Her children rise and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praises her:
29 Many daughters have done worthily, but you have surpassed them all.
30 Charm is deceitful and beauty is fleeting, but a woman who fears Yahweh, she shall be praised.
31 Give her from the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her in the gates.
