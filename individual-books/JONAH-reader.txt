Jonah 1

1 And the word of Yahweh came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,

2 "Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and proclaim against it, for their evil has come up before me."

3 But Jonah arose to flee to Tarshish, away from the presence of Yahweh. He went down to Joppa and found a ship going to Tarshish, and he paid its fare and went down into it, to go with them to Tarshish, away from the presence of Yahweh.

4 But Yahweh hurled a great wind onto the sea, and there was a great storm on the sea, and the ship threatened to break up.

5 Then the sailors were afraid, and each man cried out to his god, and they hurled the goods that were in the ship into the sea, to lighten the load that was upon them. But Jonah had gone down into the innermost parts of the ship, and he lay down and fell fast asleep.

6 And the captain drew near to him and said to him, "What is the matter with you, sleeper? Arise, call on your God! Perhaps God will think of us, and we shall not perish."

7 And they said, each man to his neighbour, "Come, let us cast lots, that we may know on whose account this evil is upon us." And they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.

8 And they said to him, "Tell us, please: on whose account is this evil upon us? What is your work, and where do you come from? What is your land, and of what people are you?"

9 And he said to them, "I am a Hebrew, and I fear Yahweh, the God of the heavens, who made the sea and the dry land."

10 Then the men feared a great fear, and they said to him, "What is this you have done?" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of Yahweh, because he had told them.

11 And they said to him, "What shall we do to you, that the sea may be calm for us?" For the sea was growing more and more stormy.

12 And he said to them, "Lift me up and hurl me into the sea, and the sea will be calm for you; for I know that it is on my account that this great storm is upon you."

13 But the men rowed hard to bring the ship back to the dry land, and they could not, for the sea was growing more and more stormy against them.

14 And they cried to Yahweh and said, "Please, Yahweh, do not let us perish for this man's life, and do not put innocent blood upon us; for you, Yahweh, have done as you pleased."

15 And they lifted Jonah up and hurled him into the sea, and the sea stood still from its raging.

16 Then the men feared Yahweh with a great fear, and they offered a sacrifice to Yahweh, and made vows.

17 And Yahweh appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.

Jonah 2

1 Then Jonah prayed to Yahweh his God from the belly of the fish,

2 and he said: "I called out of my distress to Yahweh, and he answered me. From the belly of Sheol I cried for help, and you heard my voice.

3 For you cast me into the deep, into the heart of the seas, and the flood surrounded me; all your breakers and your waves passed over me.

4 And I said, 'I have been driven out from before your eyes; yet I will look again toward your holy temple.'

5 The waters closed around me, up to my very life; the deep surrounded me; seaweed was wrapped around my head.

6 I went down to the roots of the mountains. The earth — its bars closed behind me forever. Yet you brought up my life from the pit, Yahweh my God.

7 When my soul fainted within me, I remembered Yahweh, and my prayer came to you, into your holy temple.

8 Those who regard empty vanities forsake their own loving-kindness.

9 But I will sacrifice to you with the voice of thanksgiving; what I have vowed I will pay. Salvation belongs to Yahweh!"

10 And Yahweh spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah out onto the dry land.

Jonah 3

1 And the word of Yahweh came to Jonah a second time, saying,

2 "Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and proclaim to it the proclamation that I am speaking to you."

3 And Jonah arose and went to Nineveh, according to the word of Yahweh. Now Nineveh was a city great to God, a journey of three days.

4 And Jonah began to go into the city, a day's journey, and he proclaimed and said, "Forty days more, and Nineveh will be overthrown!"

5 And the people of Nineveh believed God, and they proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them to the least of them.

6 And the word reached the king of Nineveh, and he arose from his throne and took off his robe, and he covered himself with sackcloth and sat on the ashes.

7 And he had it cried out in Nineveh, by the decree of the king and his great men, saying, "Man and animal, herd and flock, shall not taste anything; they shall not graze, and they shall not drink water.

8 And let man and animal be covered with sackcloth, and let them cry to God mightily, and let each man turn from his evil way and from the violence that is in their hands.

9 Who knows? God may turn and relent, and turn back from his fierce anger, and we shall not perish."

10 And God saw their deeds, that they had turned from their evil way, and God relented concerning the evil that he had said he would do to them, and he did not do it.

Jonah 4

1 But it was evil to Jonah — a great evil — and he burned with anger.

2 And he prayed to Yahweh and said, "Please, Yahweh, was this not my word when I was still in my own land? That is why I hastened to flee to Tarshish beforehand, for I knew that you are a gracious God and compassionate, slow to anger and abounding in loving-kindness, and relenting concerning evil.

3 And now, Yahweh, please take my life from me, for my death is better than my life."

4 And Yahweh said, "Is it right for you to be angry?"

5 And Jonah went out of the city and sat down to the east of the city, and he made himself a booth there and sat under it in the shade, until he should see what would happen in the city.

6 And Yahweh God appointed a qiqayon plant, and it came up over Jonah to be a shade over his head, to deliver him from his evil. And Jonah rejoiced over the qiqayon with a great joy.

7 And God appointed a worm at the coming up of the dawn on the next day, and it struck the qiqayon, and it withered.

8 And when the sun rose, God appointed a scorching east wind, and the sun struck Jonah's head, and he fainted, and he asked that he might die, and he said, "My death is better than my life."

9 And God said to Jonah, "Is it right for you to be angry about the qiqayon?" And he said, "It is right for me to be angry, even to death."

10 And Yahweh said, "You had pity on the qiqayon, for which you did not labour and which you did not make grow, which came up in a night and perished in a night.

11 And should I not have pity on Nineveh, that great city, in which there are more than a hundred and twenty thousand people who do not know between their right hand and their left, and many animals?"
