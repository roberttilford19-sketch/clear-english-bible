Leviticus 1

1 Yahweh called to Moses, and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting, saying,

2 “Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, ‘When a man among you brings an offering to Yahweh, you shall bring your offering from the livestock, from the herd and from the flock.

3 “‘If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he shall offer a male without defect. He shall offer it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, that he may be accepted before Yahweh.

4 And he shall lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it shall be accepted for him to make atonement for him.

5 And he shall slaughter the young bull before Yahweh, and Aaron's sons, the priests, shall present the blood and sprinkle the blood around on the altar that is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

6 And he shall skin the burnt offering, and cut it into its pieces.

7 And the sons of Aaron the priest shall put fire on the altar, and arrange wood on the fire;

8 and Aaron's sons, the priests, shall arrange the pieces, the head, and the fat on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar;

9 and its innards and its legs he shall wash with water. And the priest shall burn the whole of it on the altar, a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

10 “‘And if his offering is from the flock, from the sheep or from the goats, for a burnt offering, he shall offer a male without defect.

11 And he shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before Yahweh, and Aaron's sons, the priests, shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.

12 And he shall cut it into its pieces, and its head and its fat, and the priest shall arrange them on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar,

13 and the innards and the legs he shall wash with water. And the priest shall offer the whole, and burn it on the altar. It is a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

14 “‘And if his offering to Yahweh is a burnt offering of birds, then he shall offer his offering from the turtledoves or from the young pigeons.

15 And the priest shall bring it to the altar, and pinch off its head, and burn it on the altar; and its blood shall be drained out on the wall of the altar;

16 and he shall remove its crop with its feathers, and throw it beside the altar on the east side, to the place of the ashes.

17 And he shall tear it by its wings; he shall not divide it. And the priest shall burn it on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire. It is a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

Leviticus 2

1 “‘And when a person offers a grain offering to Yahweh, his offering shall be fine flour, and he shall pour oil on it, and put frankincense on it.

2 And he shall bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests, and he shall take from there his handful from its fine flour and from its oil, with all its frankincense, and the priest shall burn its memorial portion on the altar, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

3 And what is left of the grain offering shall be Aaron's and his sons'. It is most holy, from the fire offerings of Yahweh.

4 “‘And when you offer a grain offering baked in the oven, it shall be unleavened cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, or unleavened wafers spread with oil.

5 And if your offering is a grain offering on the griddle, it shall be fine flour mixed with oil, unleavened.

6 You shall break it into pieces, and pour oil on it. It is a grain offering.

7 And if your offering is a grain offering of the frying pan, it shall be made of fine flour with oil.

8 And you shall bring the grain offering that is made from these things to Yahweh, and it shall be presented to the priest, and he shall bring it near to the altar.

9 And the priest shall lift out from the grain offering its memorial portion, and burn it on the altar, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

10 And what is left of the grain offering shall be Aaron's and his sons'. It is most holy, from the fire offerings of Yahweh.

11 “‘No grain offering that you offer to Yahweh shall be made leavened, for you shall burn no leaven and no honey from it as a fire offering to Yahweh.

12 You may offer them to Yahweh as an offering of first fruits, but they shall not go up on the altar for a pleasing aroma.

13 And every grain offering of yours you shall season with salt, and you shall not let the salt of the covenant of your God be lacking from your grain offering. With all your offerings you shall offer salt.

14 “‘And if you offer a grain offering of first fruits to Yahweh, you shall offer, for the grain offering of your first fruits, fresh ears roasted with fire, crushed grain of the fresh ear.

15 And you shall put oil on it, and lay frankincense on it. It is a grain offering.

16 And the priest shall burn its memorial portion, from its crushed grain and from its oil, with all its frankincense. It is a fire offering to Yahweh.

Leviticus 3

1 “‘And if his offering is a sacrifice of peace offerings, if he is offering from the herd, whether male or female, he shall offer it without defect before Yahweh.

2 And he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering, and slaughter it at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron's sons, the priests, shall sprinkle the blood around on the altar.

3 And he shall offer from the sacrifice of the peace offerings a fire offering to Yahweh: the fat that covers the innards, and all the fat that is on the innards,

4 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; with the kidneys he shall remove it.

5 And Aaron's sons shall burn it on the altar, on the burnt offering that is on the wood that is on the fire. It is a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

6 “‘And if his offering for a sacrifice of peace offerings to Yahweh is from the flock, male or female, he shall offer it without defect.

7 If he is offering a lamb as his offering, then he shall offer it before Yahweh,

8 and he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering, and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron's sons shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.

9 And he shall offer from the sacrifice of the peace offerings a fire offering to Yahweh: its fat, the whole fat tail — he shall remove it close to the backbone — and the fat that covers the innards, and all the fat that is on the innards,

10 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; with the kidneys he shall remove it.

11 And the priest shall burn it on the altar. It is food, a fire offering to Yahweh.

12 “‘And if his offering is a goat, then he shall offer it before Yahweh,

13 and he shall lay his hand on its head, and slaughter it before the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron's sons shall sprinkle its blood around on the altar.

14 And he shall offer from it his offering, a fire offering to Yahweh: the fat that covers the innards, and all the fat that is on the innards,

15 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; with the kidneys he shall remove it.

16 And the priest shall burn them on the altar. It is food, a fire offering, for a pleasing aroma. All the fat is Yahweh's.

17 It shall be a statute forever throughout your generations in all your dwellings: you shall eat no fat and no blood.’”

Leviticus 4

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 “Speak to the children of Israel, saying, ‘When a person sins unintentionally, in any of the commandments of Yahweh that shall not be done, and does any one of them,

3 if the anointed priest sins, so as to bring guilt on the people, then he shall offer for his sin that he has sinned a young bull without defect to Yahweh for a sin offering.

4 And he shall bring the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before Yahweh, and lay his hand on the head of the bull, and slaughter the bull before Yahweh.

5 And the anointed priest shall take from the blood of the bull, and bring it to the Tent of Meeting.

6 And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood, and sprinkle from the blood seven times before Yahweh, in front of the veil of the sanctuary.

7 And the priest shall put from the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense before Yahweh, which is in the Tent of Meeting, and all the blood of the bull he shall pour out at the base of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

8 And all the fat of the bull of the sin offering he shall lift out from it: the fat that covers the innards, and all the fat that is on the innards,

9 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; with the kidneys he shall remove it,

10 just as it is lifted out from the ox of the sacrifice of the peace offerings. And the priest shall burn them on the altar of the burnt offering.

11 And the skin of the bull, and all its flesh, with its head and with its legs, and its innards and its dung,

12 he shall carry out, the whole bull, outside the camp to a clean place, to the place where the ashes are poured out, and burn it on wood with fire. On the place where the ashes are poured out it shall be burned.

13 “‘And if the whole congregation of Israel errs, and a matter is hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they do any one of all the commandments of Yahweh that shall not be done, and are guilty,

14 when the sin that they have sinned is known, then the assembly shall offer a young bull for a sin offering, and bring it before the Tent of Meeting.

15 And the elders of the congregation shall lay their hands on the head of the bull before Yahweh, and the bull shall be slaughtered before Yahweh.

16 And the anointed priest shall bring from the blood of the bull to the Tent of Meeting.

17 And the priest shall dip his finger from the blood, and sprinkle seven times before Yahweh, in front of the veil.

18 And from the blood he shall put on the horns of the altar that is before Yahweh, which is in the Tent of Meeting, and all the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

19 And all its fat he shall lift out from it, and burn it on the altar.

20 And he shall do to the bull just as he did to the bull of the sin offering; so shall he do to it. And the priest shall make atonement for them, and they shall be forgiven.

21 And he shall carry the bull outside the camp, and burn it just as he burned the first bull. It is a sin offering for the assembly.

22 “‘When a ruler sins, and does unintentionally any one of all the commandments of Yahweh his God that shall not be done, and is guilty,

23 or his sin that he has sinned is made known to him, then he shall bring his offering, a male goat without defect.

24 And he shall lay his hand on the head of the goat, and slaughter it in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered before Yahweh. It is a sin offering.

25 And the priest shall take from the blood of the sin offering with his finger, and put it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering, and its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of the burnt offering.

26 And all its fat he shall burn on the altar, like the fat of the sacrifice of the peace offerings. And the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin, and he shall be forgiven.

27 “‘And if any one person of the people of the land sins unintentionally, in doing any one of the commandments of Yahweh that shall not be done, and is guilty,

28 or his sin that he has sinned is made known to him, then he shall bring his offering, a female goat without defect, for his sin that he has sinned.

29 And he shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering, and slaughter the sin offering in the place of the burnt offering.

30 And the priest shall take from its blood with his finger, and put it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

31 And all its fat he shall remove, just as fat is removed from the sacrifice of the peace offerings, and the priest shall burn it on the altar for a pleasing aroma to Yahweh. And the priest shall make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven.

32 “‘And if he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he shall bring a female without defect.

33 And he shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering, and slaughter it for a sin offering in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered.

34 And the priest shall take from the blood of the sin offering with his finger, and put it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering, and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

35 And all its fat he shall remove, just as the fat of the lamb is removed from the sacrifice of the peace offerings, and the priest shall burn them on the altar, on the fire offerings of Yahweh. And the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin that he has sinned, and he shall be forgiven.

Leviticus 5

1 “‘And when a person sins, and hears the voice of an oath, and he is a witness, whether he has seen or known, if he does not tell it, then he shall bear his iniquity.

2 Or when a person touches any unclean thing, whether the carcass of an unclean animal, or the carcass of unclean livestock, or the carcass of unclean swarming things, and it is hidden from him, and he is unclean and guilty,

3 or when he touches the uncleanness of a man, whatever his uncleanness by which he becomes unclean, and it is hidden from him, and he comes to know it, and is guilty,

4 or when a person swears, speaking rashly with the lips, to do evil or to do good, in all that a man may speak rashly with an oath, and it is hidden from him, and he comes to know it, and is guilty in one of these,

5 then when he is guilty in one of these, he shall confess that in which he has sinned,

6 and he shall bring his guilt offering to Yahweh for his sin that he has sinned, a female from the flock, a lamb or a female goat, for a sin offering, and the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin.

7 “‘And if his hand does not reach enough for a lamb, then he shall bring his guilt offering for that in which he has sinned, two turtledoves or two young pigeons, to Yahweh, one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering.

8 And he shall bring them to the priest, and he shall offer the one for the sin offering first, and pinch off its head from the back of its neck, and shall not divide it.

9 And he shall sprinkle from the blood of the sin offering on the wall of the altar, and what remains of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar. It is a sin offering.

10 And the second he shall make a burnt offering, according to the ordinance, and the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin that he has sinned, and he shall be forgiven.

11 “‘And if his hand does not reach two turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he shall bring his offering for that in which he has sinned, a tenth of an ephah of fine flour, for a sin offering. He shall put no oil on it, and he shall put no frankincense on it, for it is a sin offering.

12 And he shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take from it his handful, its memorial portion, and burn it on the altar, on the fire offerings of Yahweh. It is a sin offering.

13 And the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin that he has sinned in any one of these, and he shall be forgiven. And it shall be the priest's, like the grain offering.’”

14 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

15 “When a person acts unfaithfully, a breach of faith, and sins unintentionally in the holy things of Yahweh, then he shall bring his guilt offering to Yahweh, a ram without defect from the flock, at your valuation in silver shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary, for a guilt offering.

16 And for what he has sinned in the holy thing he shall make restitution, and he shall add a fifth of it to it, and give it to the priest. And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he shall be forgiven.

17 “And if a person sins, and does any one of all the commandments of Yahweh that shall not be done, and he did not know, and is guilty, and bears his iniquity,

18 then he shall bring a ram without defect from the flock, at your valuation, for a guilt offering, to the priest. And the priest shall make atonement for him for his error that he erred, and he did not know, and he shall be forgiven.

19 It is a guilt offering. He has surely become guilty before Yahweh.”

Leviticus 6

1 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

2 If a person sins and acts unfaithfully against Yahweh, and deals falsely with his neighbour about a deposit, or about a pledge, or about robbery, or has oppressed his neighbour,

3 or has found a lost thing and deals falsely about it and swears falsely — about any one of all the things that a man does, sinning by them —

4 then it shall be, when he sins and is guilty, that he shall restore what he took by robbery, or the thing that he got by oppression, or the deposit that was deposited with him, or the lost thing that he found,

5 or anything about which he swore falsely. He shall pay it back in full, and shall add a fifth part to it. To the one to whom it belongs he shall give it, on the day of his guilt.

6 And he shall bring his guilt offering to Yahweh: a ram without defect from the flock, according to your valuation, for a guilt offering, to the priest.

7 And the priest shall make atonement for him before Yahweh, and he shall be forgiven for any one of all the things that he does, becoming guilty by it.

8 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

9 Command Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the law of the burnt offering. The burnt offering shall be on its hearth on the altar all night until morning, and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it.

10 The priest shall put on his linen garment, and he shall put linen breeches on his flesh, and he shall take up the ashes to which the fire has consumed the burnt offering on the altar, and he shall put them beside the altar.

11 And he shall put off his garments and put on other garments, and he shall carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place.

12 And the fire on the altar shall be kept burning on it; it shall not go out. The priest shall burn wood on it morning by morning, and he shall arrange the burnt offering on it, and shall burn on it the fat of the peace offerings.

13 Fire shall be kept burning on the altar continually; it shall not go out.

14 And this is the law of the grain offering: the sons of Aaron shall offer it before Yahweh, in front of the altar.

15 And he shall take up from it, in his handful, some of the fine flour of the grain offering and some of its oil, and all the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and he shall burn its memorial portion on the altar, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

16 And what is left of it Aaron and his sons shall eat. It shall be eaten as unleavened cakes in a holy place; in the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting they shall eat it.

17 It shall not be baked leavened. I have given it as their portion from my offerings by fire. It is most holy, like the sin offering and like the guilt offering.

18 Every male among the sons of Aaron shall eat it. It is a statute forever throughout your generations, from Yahweh's offerings by fire. Whatever touches them shall be holy.

19 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

20 “This is the offering of Aaron and his sons, which they shall offer to Yahweh on the day he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.

21 On a griddle it shall be made with oil. Thoroughly mixed, you shall bring it in. You shall offer the baked pieces of the grain offering as a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

22 And the anointed priest in his place, from among his sons, shall make it. It is a statute forever to Yahweh; it shall be wholly burned.

23 And every grain offering of a priest shall be wholly burned; it shall not be eaten.”

24 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

25 “Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying: This is the law of the sin offering. In the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, the sin offering shall be slaughtered before Yahweh. It is most holy.

26 The priest who offers it for sin shall eat it. In a holy place it shall be eaten, in the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting.

27 Whatever touches its flesh shall be holy. And when any of its blood is sprinkled on a garment, you shall wash what was sprinkled on it in a holy place.

28 And an earthen vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken. And if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, it shall be scoured and rinsed in water.

29 Every male among the priests shall eat it. It is most holy.

30 And no sin offering from whose blood any is brought into the Tent of Meeting to make atonement in the holy place shall be eaten. It shall be burned with fire.”

Leviticus 7

1 And this is the law of the guilt offering. It is most holy.

2 In the place where they slaughter the burnt offering, they shall slaughter the guilt offering, and its blood he shall sprinkle on the altar all around.

3 And all its fat he shall offer from it: the fat tail, and the fat that covers the innards,

4 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe on the liver; with the kidneys he shall remove it.

5 And the priest shall burn them on the altar as an offering by fire to Yahweh. It is a guilt offering.

6 Every male among the priests shall eat it. In a holy place it shall be eaten. It is most holy.

7 As the sin offering, so the guilt offering: there is one law for them. The priest who makes atonement with it, his it shall be.

8 And the priest who offers a man's burnt offering — the skin of the burnt offering that he offered shall be the priest's, his.

9 And every grain offering that is baked in the oven, and everything made in a frying pan, and on a griddle, shall be the priest's who offers it; his it shall be.

10 And every grain offering mixed with oil, or dry, shall belong to all the sons of Aaron, a man as his brother.

11 And this is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which he shall offer to Yahweh.

12 If he offers it for thanksgiving, then he shall offer, with the sacrifice of thanksgiving, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and fine flour thoroughly mixed, cakes mixed with oil.

13 With cakes of leavened bread he shall offer his offering, with the sacrifice of thanksgiving of his peace offerings.

14 And he shall offer from it one from each offering as a contribution to Yahweh. It shall be the priest's who sprinkles the blood of the peace offerings; his it shall be.

15 And the flesh of the sacrifice of thanksgiving of his peace offerings shall be eaten on the day of his offering. He shall not leave any of it until morning.

16 And if the sacrifice of his offering is a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day he offers his sacrifice, and on the next day what is left of it shall be eaten.

17 And what is left of the flesh of the sacrifice on the third day shall be burned with fire.

18 And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings is eaten at all on the third day, it shall not be accepted. It shall not be credited to the one who offers it. It shall be an abhorrent thing, and the person who eats of it shall bear his iniquity.

19 And the flesh that touches any unclean thing shall not be eaten; it shall be burned with fire. And as for the flesh, everyone who is clean may eat flesh.

20 And the person who eats flesh from the sacrifice of peace offerings that belong to Yahweh, while his uncleanness is on him — that person shall be cut off from his people.

21 And a person who touches any unclean thing — the uncleanness of man, or an unclean animal, or any unclean detestable thing — and eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings that belong to Yahweh, that person shall be cut off from his people.

22 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

23 “Speak to the children of Israel, saying: All the fat of an ox, or a sheep, or a goat, you shall not eat.

24 And the fat of an animal that dies of itself, and the fat of an animal torn by beasts, may be used for any work, but you shall surely not eat it.

25 For whoever eats fat from the animal from which he offers an offering by fire to Yahweh — the person who eats it shall be cut off from his people.

26 And no blood at all shall you eat in any of your dwellings, whether of bird or of animal.

27 Any person who eats any blood at all — that person shall be cut off from his people.”

28 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

29 “Speak to the children of Israel, saying: The one who offers the sacrifice of his peace offerings to Yahweh shall bring his offering to Yahweh from the sacrifice of his peace offerings.

30 His own hands shall bring Yahweh's offerings by fire. The fat with the breast he shall bring — the breast, to wave it as a wave offering before Yahweh.

31 And the priest shall burn the fat on the altar, and the breast shall be Aaron's and his sons'.

32 And the right thigh you shall give as a contribution to the priest, from the sacrifices of your peace offerings.

33 The one among the sons of Aaron who offers the blood of the peace offerings and the fat — his shall be the right thigh as a portion.

34 For the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution I have taken from the children of Israel, from the sacrifices of their peace offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as a statute forever from the children of Israel.”

35 This is the anointing portion of Aaron and the anointing portion of his sons, from Yahweh's offerings by fire, on the day he presented them to serve as priests to Yahweh,

36 which Yahweh commanded to be given to them from the children of Israel on the day he anointed them. It is a statute forever throughout their generations.

37 This is the law for the burnt offering, for the grain offering, for the sin offering, for the guilt offering, for the consecration offering, and for the sacrifice of peace offerings,

38 which Yahweh commanded Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day he commanded the children of Israel to offer their offerings to Yahweh, in the wilderness of Sinai.

Leviticus 8

1 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

2 “Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and the bull of the sin offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread;

3 and gather all the congregation at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.”

4 Moses did as Yahweh commanded him, and the congregation was gathered at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

5 Moses said to the congregation, “This is the thing that Yahweh has commanded to do.”

6 Moses brought Aaron and his sons near, and washed them with water.

7 He put the tunic on him, and girded him with the sash, and clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him, and girded him with the woven band of the ephod, and fastened the ephod to him with it.

8 He placed the breastplate on him, and he put the Urim and the Thummim into the breastplate.

9 He set the turban on his head, and on the turban, in front of his face, he set the golden plate, the holy crown, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

10 Moses took the anointing oil, and anointed the tabernacle and all that was in it, and made them holy.

11 He sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its vessels, and the basin and its base, to make them holy.

12 He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head, and anointed him, to make him holy.

13 Moses brought Aaron's sons near, and clothed them with tunics, and girded them with sashes, and bound caps on them, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

14 He brought the bull of the sin offering near, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bull of the sin offering.

15 He slaughtered it, and Moses took the blood and put it on the horns of the altar all around with his finger, and purified the altar, and he poured the blood at the base of the altar, and made it holy, to make atonement for it.

16 He took all the fat that was on the innards, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and Moses burned them on the altar.

17 And the bull, and its skin, and its flesh, and its dung, he burned with fire outside the camp, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

18 He brought the ram of the burnt offering near, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.

19 He slaughtered it, and Moses sprinkled the blood on the altar all around.

20 He cut the ram into its pieces, and Moses burned the head and the pieces and the fat.

21 He washed the innards and the legs with water, and Moses burned the whole ram on the altar. It was a burnt offering for a pleasing aroma; it was a fire offering to Yahweh, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

22 He brought the second ram near, the ram of consecration, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.

23 He slaughtered it, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot.

24 He brought Aaron's sons near, and Moses put some of the blood on the lobe of their right ear, and on the thumb of their right hand, and on the great toe of their right foot, and Moses sprinkled the blood on the altar all around.

25 He took the fat and the fat tail, and all the fat that was on the innards, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and the right thigh;

26 and from the basket of unleavened bread that was before Yahweh he took one unleavened cake, and one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer, and he placed them on the fat portions and on the right thigh.

27 He put everything on Aaron's palms and on his sons' palms, and waved them as a wave offering before Yahweh.

Leviticus 22

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 “Speak to Aaron and to his sons, that they shall keep themselves separate from the holy things of the children of Israel, and they shall not profane my holy name — the things that they make holy to me. I am Yahweh.

3 Say to them: Throughout your generations, any man from all your offspring who draws near to the holy things that the children of Israel make holy to Yahweh, with his uncleanness on him, that person shall be cut off from before me. I am Yahweh.

4 Any man from the offspring of Aaron who has a defiling skin disease or has a discharge shall not eat of the holy things until he is clean. And one who touches anything made unclean by a dead person, or a man from whom an emission of semen goes out,

5 or a man who touches any swarming creature by which he becomes unclean, or a man by whom he becomes unclean, whatever his uncleanness —

6 a person who touches such a thing shall be unclean until the evening, and shall not eat of the holy things unless he has bathed his flesh in water.

7 And when the sun goes down he shall be clean, and afterwards he shall eat of the holy things, for it is his bread.

8 A carcass and an animal torn by beasts he shall not eat, to defile himself by it. I am Yahweh.

9 And they shall keep my charge, and they shall not bear sin for it and die in it when they profane it. I am Yahweh, who makes them holy.

10 And no outsider shall eat a holy thing. A sojourner of a priest, or a hired worker, shall not eat a holy thing.

11 But if a priest buys a person, the purchase of his money, he shall eat of it; and those born in his house — they shall eat of his bread.

12 And a priest's daughter, if she belongs to an outsider, she shall not eat of the contribution of the holy things.

13 And a priest's daughter, if she is a widow or divorced, and she has no offspring, and she returns to her father's house as in her youth, she shall eat from her father's bread; but no outsider shall eat of it.

14 And if a man eats a holy thing in error, he shall add its fifth to it and give the holy thing to the priest.

15 And they shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel, which they set apart for Yahweh,

16 and so make them bear the iniquity of guilt when they eat their holy things, for I am Yahweh, who makes them holy.

17 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

18 “Speak to Aaron and to his sons and to all the children of Israel, and say to them: Any man from the house of Israel, or from the foreigner in Israel, who brings near his offering for any of their vows or for any of their freewill offerings, which they bring near to Yahweh as a burnt offering —

19 for your acceptance it shall be a male without defect, from the cattle, from the sheep, or from the goats.

20 Anything in which there is a defect you shall not bring near, for it shall not be for acceptance for you.

21 And if a man brings near a sacrifice of peace offerings to Yahweh, to fulfil a vow or as a freewill offering, from the herd or from the flock, it shall be without defect, to be accepted; no defect shall be in it.

22 A blind animal, or a broken one, or a maimed one, or one with a growth, or one with scabs, or one with a skin eruption — you shall not bring these near to Yahweh, and you shall not put a fire offering from them on the altar to Yahweh.

23 And an ox or a sheep that has a limb too long or stunted, you may make it a freewill offering, but for a vow it shall not be accepted.

24 And an animal with testicles bruised, or crushed, or torn away, or cut off, you shall not bring near to Yahweh; and in your land you shall not do this.

25 And from the hand of a foreigner you shall not bring near the bread of your God from any of these, for their corruption is in them; a defect is in them. They shall not be accepted for you.

26 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

27 “An ox, or a sheep, or a goat, when it is born, it shall be seven days under its mother; and from the eighth day onwards it shall be accepted as an offering, a fire offering to Yahweh.

28 And an ox or a sheep — it and its young you shall not slaughter in one day.

29 And when you sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to Yahweh, you shall sacrifice it so that you are accepted.

30 On that same day it shall be eaten; you shall not leave any of it until morning. I am Yahweh.

31 And you shall keep my commandments and do them. I am Yahweh.

32 And you shall not profane my holy name, and I shall be made holy among the children of Israel. I am Yahweh, who makes you holy,

33 who brought you out from the land of Egypt to be God to you. I am Yahweh.

Leviticus 23

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 “Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: Yahweh's appointed times, which you shall proclaim as holy assemblies — these are my appointed times.

3 Six days work shall be done, and on the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, a holy assembly; you shall do no work. It is a Sabbath to Yahweh in all your dwellings.

4 These are Yahweh's appointed times, holy assemblies, which you shall proclaim at their appointed time.

5 In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, at twilight, is Yahweh's Passover.

6 And on the fifteenth day of this month is the feast of unleavened bread to Yahweh; seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

7 On the first day there shall be a holy assembly for you; you shall do no work of labour.

8 And you shall bring near a fire offering to Yahweh seven days. On the seventh day is a holy assembly; you shall do no work of labour.

9 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

10 “Speak to the children of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land that I give to you and you reap its harvest, you shall bring the sheaf of the first of your harvest to the priest.

11 And he shall wave the sheaf before Yahweh, for your acceptance; on the day after the Sabbath the priest shall wave it.

12 And on the day when you wave the sheaf you shall offer a lamb without defect, a year old, as a burnt offering to Yahweh.

13 And its grain offering shall be two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, a fire offering to Yahweh, a pleasing aroma; and its drink offering shall be wine, a quarter of a hin.

14 And you shall not eat bread, or roasted grain, or fresh grain until this very day, until you have brought the offering of your God. It is a statute forever throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

15 And you shall count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day when you brought the sheaf of the wave offering: seven Sabbaths shall be complete.

16 Until the day after the seventh Sabbath you shall count fifty days, and you shall bring near a new grain offering to Yahweh.

17 From your dwellings you shall bring bread for a wave offering: two loaves, of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour they shall be; they shall be baked with leaven, firstfruits to Yahweh.

18 And you shall bring near with the bread seven lambs without defect, a year old, and one young bull from the cattle, and two rams; they shall be a burnt offering to Yahweh, with their grain offering and their drink offerings, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

19 And you shall offer one male goat as a sin offering, and two lambs, a year old, as a sacrifice of peace offerings.

20 And the priest shall wave them with the bread of the firstfruits as a wave offering before Yahweh, with the two lambs; they shall be holy to Yahweh, for the priest.

21 And you shall proclaim on this very day: there shall be a holy assembly for you; you shall do no work of labour. It is a statute forever in all your dwellings throughout your generations.

22 And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not finish off the edge of your field when you reap, and you shall not gather the gleanings of your harvest; you shall leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am Yahweh your God.

23 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

24 “Speak to the children of Israel, saying: In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, there shall be a rest for you, a memorial of trumpet blasts, a holy assembly.

25 You shall do no work of labour, and you shall bring near a fire offering to Yahweh.

26 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

27 “But on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement; there shall be a holy assembly for you, and you shall humble yourselves, and you shall bring near a fire offering to Yahweh.

28 And you shall do no work on this very day, for it is a day of atonement, to make atonement for you before Yahweh your God.

29 For any person who is not humbled on this very day shall be cut off from his people.

30 And any person who does any work on this very day, I shall destroy that person from among his people.

31 You shall do no work. It is a statute forever throughout your generations in all your dwellings.

32 It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you shall humble yourselves. On the ninth day of the month, in the evening, from evening to evening, you shall rest on your Sabbath.

33 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

34 “Speak to the children of Israel, saying: On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the feast of booths, seven days to Yahweh.

35 On the first day is a holy assembly; you shall do no work of labour.

36 Seven days you shall bring near a fire offering to Yahweh. On the eighth day there shall be a holy assembly for you, and you shall bring near a fire offering to Yahweh; it is a solemn assembly; you shall do no work of labour.

37 These are Yahweh's appointed times, which you shall proclaim as holy assemblies, to bring near a fire offering to Yahweh — a burnt offering and a grain offering, a sacrifice and drink offerings, each day's matter on its day —

38 besides Yahweh's Sabbaths, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides all your freewill offerings, which you give to Yahweh.

39 But on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the produce of the land, you shall keep Yahweh's feast seven days; on the first day shall be a rest, and on the eighth day shall be a rest.

40 And you shall take for yourselves on the first day the fruit of a majestic tree, palm branches, and a bough of a leafy tree, and willows of the brook, and you shall rejoice before Yahweh your God seven days.

41 And you shall keep it as a feast to Yahweh seven days in the year; it is a statute forever throughout your generations. In the seventh month you shall keep it.

42 You shall dwell in booths seven days; every native-born person in Israel shall dwell in booths,

43 so that your generations may know that I made the children of Israel dwell in booths when I brought them out from the land of Egypt. I am Yahweh your God.

44 And Moses declared Yahweh's appointed times to the children of Israel.

Leviticus 24

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 “Command the children of Israel, and they shall bring to you clear olive oil, beaten, for the light, to set up a lamp continually.

3 Outside the veil of the testimony, in the tent of meeting, Aaron shall arrange it from evening to morning before Yahweh continually. It is a statute forever throughout your generations.

4 On the pure lampstand he shall arrange the lamps before Yahweh continually.

5 And you shall take fine flour and bake it into twelve loaves; two tenths of an ephah shall be in one loaf.

6 And you shall set them in two rows, six in a row, on the pure table before Yahweh.

7 And you shall put clear frankincense on the row, and it shall be for the bread as a memorial portion, a fire offering to Yahweh.

8 On the Sabbath day, on the Sabbath day, he shall arrange it before Yahweh continually, from the children of Israel, an everlasting covenant.

9 And it shall belong to Aaron and to his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place, for it is most holy to him from Yahweh's fire offerings, a statute forever.

10 And the son of an Israelite woman — and he was the son of an Egyptian man — went out among the children of Israel, and the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought in the camp.

11 And the son of the Israelite woman pronounced the Name and cursed, and they brought him to Moses. And his mother's name was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

12 And they put him in custody, until it should be made clear to them by the mouth of Yahweh.

13 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

14 “Bring out the one who cursed to outside the camp, and all who heard him shall lay their hands on his head, and all the congregation shall stone him.

15 And to the children of Israel you shall speak, saying: Any man who curses his God shall bear his sin.

16 And one who pronounces the name of Yahweh shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall surely stone him. As the foreigner, so the native-born: when he pronounces the Name, he shall be put to death.

17 And a man who strikes any human life shall surely be put to death.

18 And one who strikes an animal's life shall make it good, life for life.

19 And a man who inflicts a defect on his fellow: as he has done, so it shall be done to him —

20 fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he inflicts a defect on a man, so it shall be inflicted on him.

21 And one who strikes an animal shall make it good, and one who strikes a man shall be put to death.

22 One ordinance shall there be for you: as the foreigner, so shall the native-born be, for I am Yahweh your God.

23 And Moses spoke to the children of Israel, and they brought out the one who had cursed to outside the camp, and they stoned him with stone. And the children of Israel did as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

Leviticus 25

1 Yahweh spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying:

2 Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: When you come into the land which I give you, then the land shall keep a Sabbath to Yahweh.

3 Six years you shall sow your field, and six years you shall prune your vineyard, and you shall gather in its produce.

4 But in the seventh year there shall be a Sabbath of complete rest for the land, a Sabbath to Yahweh. You shall not sow your field, and you shall not prune your vineyard.

5 What grows of itself of your harvest you shall not reap, and the grapes of your undressed vine you shall not gather. It shall be a year of complete rest for the land.

6 And the Sabbath of the land shall be for you for food: for you, and for your slave, and for your female slave, and for your hired worker, and for your settler who sojourns with you.

7 And for your cattle, and for the beast that is in your land, all its produce shall be for food. {ס}

8 You shall count for yourself seven Sabbaths of years, seven years, seven times, and the days of seven Sabbaths of years shall be for you forty-nine years.

9 And you shall sound a horn, a blast, in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month. On the Day of Atonement you shall sound the horn throughout all your land.

10 And you shall make the fiftieth year holy, and proclaim liberty in the land to all its inhabitants. It shall be a jubilee for you, and you shall return, each man to his possession, and each man to his family you shall return.

11 It shall be a jubilee; the fiftieth year shall be for you. You shall not sow, and you shall not reap what grows of itself in it, and you shall not gather the grapes of its undressed vines.

12 For it is a jubilee; it shall be holy to you. From the field you shall eat its produce.

13 In this year of jubilee you shall return, each man to his possession.

14 And when you sell a sale to your fellow, or buy from the hand of your fellow, you shall not wrong, each man, his brother.

15 According to the number of years after the jubilee you shall buy from your fellow. According to the number of years of crops he shall sell to you.

16 According to the multitude of the years you shall increase its price, and according to the fewness of the years you shall decrease its price, for he is selling to you the number of the crops.

17 And you shall not wrong, each man, his fellow, and you shall fear your God, for I am Yahweh your God.

18 And you shall do my statutes, and you shall keep my ordinances and do them, and you shall dwell on the land in safety.

19 And the land shall give its fruit, and you shall eat to fullness, and dwell in safety on it.

20 And if you say, "What shall we eat in the seventh year? Look, we shall not sow, and we shall not gather in our produce,"

21 then I will command my blessing for you in the sixth year, and it shall make the produce for the three years.

22 And you shall sow the eighth year, and eat from the produce, the old, until the ninth year. Until its produce comes in, you shall eat the old.

23 And the land shall not be sold permanently, for the land is mine, for you are strangers and settlers with me.

24 And in all the land of your possession you shall grant a redemption for the land. {ס}

25 If your brother becomes poor, and sells from his possession, then his redeemer who is near to him shall come, and shall redeem what his brother has sold.

26 And if a man has no redeemer, and his hand attains, and he finds enough for his redemption,

27 then he shall count the years of his sale, and return the surplus to the man to whom he sold it, and he shall return to his possession.

28 But if his hand has not found enough to return to him, then what he has sold shall be in the hand of the one who bought it until the year of jubilee, and it shall go out in the jubilee, and he shall return to his possession.

29 And if a man sells a dwelling house in a city of a wall, then its redemption shall be until the completion of the year of its sale. For a full year shall be its redemption.

30 And if it is not redeemed until a full year is completed for him, then the house that is in the city that has a wall shall be established permanently for the one who bought it, throughout his generations. It shall not go out in the jubilee.

31 But the houses of the villages that have no wall around them shall be reckoned with the field of the land. Redemption shall be for it, and it shall go out in the jubilee.

32 And as for the cities of the Levites, the houses of the cities of their possession, a perpetual redemption shall be for the Levites.

33 And what is redeemed from the Levites — the sale of a house and the city of his possession shall go out in the jubilee, for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession in the midst of the children of Israel.

34 And the field of the pasture land of their cities shall not be sold, for it is a perpetual possession for them. {ס}

35 And if your brother becomes poor, and his hand falters with you, then you shall strengthen him. As a stranger and a settler shall he live with you.

36 You shall not take interest or increase from him, and you shall fear your God, and your brother shall live with you.

37 Your money you shall not give to him at interest, and at increase you shall not give your food.

38 I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, to give you the land of Canaan, to be God for you. {ס}

39 And if your brother becomes poor with you, and sells himself to you, you shall not make him serve the service of a slave.

40 As a hired worker, as a settler, he shall be with you. Until the year of jubilee he shall serve with you.

41 And he shall go out from with you, he and his children with him, and he shall return to his family, and to the possession of his fathers he shall return.

42 For they are my slaves, whom I brought out from the land of Egypt. They shall not be sold as the sale of a slave.

43 You shall not rule over him with harshness, and you shall fear your God.

44 And your slave and your female slave whom you may have — from the nations that are around you, from them you shall buy a slave and a female slave.

45 And also from the children of the settlers who sojourn with you, from them you shall buy, and from their family that is with you, which they have begotten in your land, and they shall be for you as a possession.

46 And you shall hand them down as an inheritance to your children after you, to inherit as a possession. For ever you shall make them serve, but over your brothers, the children of Israel, each man over his brother, you shall not rule over him with harshness. {ס}

47 And if the hand of a stranger and settler with you attains, and your brother beside him becomes poor, and he sells himself to the stranger, the settler with you, or to the stock of the family of a stranger,

48 after he is sold, redemption shall be for him. One of his brothers shall redeem him.

49 Or his uncle, or the son of his uncle, shall redeem him, or one from the flesh of his flesh, from his family, shall redeem him, or his hand attains and he redeems himself.

50 And he shall reckon with the one who bought him, from the year that he sold himself to him until the year of jubilee, and the money of his sale shall be according to the number of years. As the days of a hired worker he shall be with him.

51 If there are still many in the years, according to them he shall return his redemption price from the money of his purchase.

52 And if few remain in the years until the year of jubilee, then he shall reckon with him. According to his years he shall return his redemption price.

53 As a hired worker, year by year, he shall be with him. He shall not rule over him with harshness before your eyes.

54 And if he is not redeemed by these, then he shall go out in the year of jubilee, he and his children with him.

55 For to me the children of Israel are slaves. They are my slaves, whom I brought out from the land of Egypt. I am Yahweh your God.

Leviticus 26

1 You shall make no idols for yourselves, and a carved image and a pillar you shall not raise up for yourselves, and a figured stone you shall not place in your land, to bow down on it, for I am Yahweh your God.

2 You shall keep my Sabbaths, and you shall revere my sanctuary. I am Yahweh. {ס}

3 If you walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them,

4 then I will give your rains in their time, and the land shall give its produce, and the tree of the field shall give its fruit.

5 And the threshing shall reach to the vintage for you, and the vintage shall reach to the sowing, and you shall eat your bread to fullness, and dwell in safety in your land.

6 And I will give peace in the land, and you shall lie down, and no one shall make you tremble, and I will make the evil beast cease from the land, and a sword shall not pass through your land.

7 And you shall pursue your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.

8 And five of you shall pursue a hundred, and a hundred of you shall pursue ten thousand, and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.

9 And I will turn to you, and make you fruitful, and multiply you, and establish my covenant with you.

10 And you shall eat old, long stored, and you shall bring out the old before the new.

11 And I will set my dwelling in your midst, and my soul shall not abhor you.

12 And I will walk in your midst, and I will be God for you, and you shall be a people for me.

13 I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out from the land of Egypt, from being slaves to them, and I broke the bars of your yoke, and made you walk upright. {פ}

14 But if you do not listen to me, and do not do all these commandments,

15 and if you reject my statutes, and if your soul abhors my ordinances, so that you do not do all my commandments, by your breaking my covenant,

16 I also will do this to you: I will appoint over you terror, consumption and fever, wasting the eyes and pining the soul, and you shall sow your seed in vain, and your enemies shall eat it.

17 And I will set my face against you, and you shall be struck before your enemies, and those who hate you shall rule over you, and you shall flee when no one pursues you. {ס}

18 And if, up to these, you do not listen to me, then I will go on to discipline you sevenfold for your sins.

19 And I will break the pride of your power, and I will make your heavens like iron, and your land like bronze.

20 And your strength shall be spent in vain, and your land shall not give its produce, and the tree of the land shall not give its fruit.

21 And if you walk with me contrary, and are not willing to listen to me, then I will go on to bring a plague on you sevenfold, according to your sins.

22 And I will send among you the beast of the field, and it shall bereave you, and cut off your cattle, and make you few, and your roads shall be desolate.

23 And if by these you are not disciplined toward me, and you walk with me contrary,

24 then I also will walk with you contrary, and I will strike you, I also, sevenfold for your sins.

25 And I will bring on you a sword, avenging the vengeance of the covenant, and you shall be gathered into your cities, and I will send pestilence in your midst, and you shall be given into the hand of an enemy.

26 When I break for you the staff of bread, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven, and they shall return your bread by weight, and you shall eat and not be satisfied. {ס}

27 And if in spite of this you do not listen to me, and you walk with me contrary,

28 then I will walk with you in the wrath of contrary, and I will discipline you, I also, sevenfold for your sins.

29 And you shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you shall eat.

30 And I will destroy your high places, and cut down your sun-pillars, and put your carcasses on the carcasses of your idols, and my soul shall abhor you.

31 And I will make your cities a waste, and I will make your sanctuaries desolate, and I will not smell the scent of your soothing aroma.

32 And I myself will make the land desolate, and your enemies who dwell in it shall be appalled at it.

33 And you I will scatter among the nations, and I will draw out a sword after you, and your land shall be a desolation, and your cities shall be a waste.

34 Then the land shall make up its Sabbaths, all the days of its desolation, while you are in the land of your enemies. Then the land shall rest, and shall make up its Sabbaths.

35 All the days of its desolation it shall rest, the rest that it did not rest in your Sabbaths, when you dwelt on it.

36 And as for those who remain among you, I will bring faintness into their heart in the lands of their enemies, and the sound of a driven leaf shall pursue them, and they shall flee the flight of a sword, and fall when no one pursues.

37 And they shall stumble, each man on his brother, as before a sword, when no one pursues, and you shall have no standing before your enemies.

38 And you shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall eat you.

39 And those who remain among you shall rot away in their iniquity in the lands of your enemies, and also in the iniquities of their fathers, with them, they shall rot away.

40 And they shall confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers, in their treachery that they committed against me, and also that they walked with me contrary.

41 I also walked with them contrary, and brought them into the land of their enemies. Or then their uncircumcised heart shall be humbled, and then they shall make up their iniquity.

42 And I will remember my covenant with Jacob, and also my covenant with Isaac, and also my covenant with Abraham I will remember, and I will remember the land.

43 And the land shall be left by them, and shall make up its Sabbaths in its desolation apart from them, and they shall make up their iniquity, because, and because, they rejected my ordinances, and their soul abhorred my statutes.

44 And yet, even this: when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them, and I will not abhor them, to destroy them, to break my covenant with them, for I am Yahweh their God.

45 And I will remember for them the covenant of the former ones, whom I brought out from the land of Egypt before the eyes of the nations, to be God for them. I am Yahweh.

46 These are the statutes and the ordinances and the laws that Yahweh gave between himself and the children of Israel on Mount Sinai, by the hand of Moses. {פ}

Leviticus 27

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying:

2 Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: When a man makes a special vow, of persons to Yahweh, according to your valuation,

3 then your valuation shall be: for the male, from twenty years old to sixty years old, your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver, by the shekel of the sanctuary.

4 And if it is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels.

5 And if it is from five years old to twenty years old, then your valuation for the male shall be twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.

6 And if it is from a month old to five years old, then your valuation for the male shall be five shekels of silver, and for the female your valuation shall be three shekels of silver.

7 And if it is from sixty years old and upward, if it is a male, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels.

8 And if he is too poor for your valuation, then he shall be set before the priest, and the priest shall value him. According to what the hand of the one who vowed attains, the priest shall value him. {ס}

9 And if it is an animal of which they bring an offering to Yahweh, all that a man gives of it to Yahweh shall be holy.

10 He shall not exchange it, and he shall not substitute it, good for bad or bad for good. And if he surely substitutes animal for animal, then it and its substitute shall be holy.

11 And if it is any unclean animal, of which they do not bring an offering to Yahweh, then he shall set the animal before the priest,

12 and the priest shall value it, between good and bad. As your valuation, the priest's, so it shall be.

13 And if he surely redeems it, then he shall add its fifth to your valuation.

14 And when a man makes his house holy, a holy thing to Yahweh, then the priest shall value it, between good and bad. As the priest values it, so it shall stand.

15 And if the one who makes it holy redeems his house, then he shall add a fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall be his.

16 And if a man makes holy to Yahweh part of the field of his possession, then your valuation shall be according to its seed: the seed of a homer of barley at fifty shekels of silver.

17 If he makes his field holy from the year of jubilee, according to your valuation it shall stand.

18 And if he makes his field holy after the jubilee, then the priest shall reckon for him the money according to the years that remain until the year of jubilee, and it shall be deducted from your valuation.

19 And if the one who made the field holy surely redeems it, then he shall add a fifth of the money of your valuation to it, and it shall stand as his.

20 And if he does not redeem the field, and if he sells the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed again.

21 And the field, when it goes out in the jubilee, shall be holy to Yahweh, as a field devoted. Its possession shall be the priest's.

22 And if he makes holy to Yahweh a field of his purchase, which is not from the field of his possession,

23 then the priest shall reckon for him the amount of your valuation until the year of jubilee, and he shall give your valuation on that day, holy to Yahweh.

24 In the year of jubilee the field shall return to the one from whom he bought it, to the one to whom the possession of the land belongs.

25 And all your valuation shall be by the shekel of the sanctuary. Twenty gerahs shall be the shekel. {ס}

26 But a firstborn that is made a firstborn to Yahweh among the animals, no man shall make it holy. Whether ox or sheep, it is Yahweh's.

27 And if it is among the unclean animals, then he shall redeem it according to your valuation, and shall add its fifth to it. And if it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation.

28 But every devoted thing that a man devotes to Yahweh, from all that is his, from man and animal and from the field of his possession, shall not be sold and shall not be redeemed. Every devoted thing is a holy of holies to Yahweh.

29 Every devoted person who is devoted from among mankind shall not be ransomed. He shall surely be put to death.

30 And all the tithe of the land, from the seed of the land, from the fruit of the tree, is Yahweh's. It is holy to Yahweh.

31 And if a man surely redeems any of his tithe, he shall add its fifth to it.

32 And all the tithe of herd and flock, all that passes under the rod, the tenth shall be holy to Yahweh.

33 He shall not examine between good and bad, and he shall not substitute it. And if he surely substitutes it, then it and its substitute shall be holy. It shall not be redeemed.

34 These are the commandments that Yahweh commanded Moses for the children of Israel on Mount Sinai.
