Clear English Bible — Deuteronomy (Chapters 1–34): A Clear English Translation

COMPLETE — This book of the Clear English Bible is complete. This is a fresh translation made directly from the Hebrew: the Masoretic text of the Westminster Leningrad Codex, verified letter by letter against the Mechon Mamre edition. It was inserted into this edition on 23 November 2026, under the editor's ruling that the Torah of this Bible rests on the Hebrew, replacing the clear-English modernization of the World English Bible Classic that stood here before. This text was prepared with AI assistance. Robert Tilford is the editor.

Deuteronomy 1

1 These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the wilderness, in the Arabah opposite Suf, between Paran and Tophel, and Laban, and Hazeroth, and Dizahab.

2 It is eleven days from Horeb, by the way of Mount Seir, to Kadesh Barnea.

3 And it happened in the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, on the first day of the month, that Moses spoke to the children of Israel according to all that Yahweh had commanded him concerning them,

4 after he had struck Sihon the king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og the king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth, at Edrei.

5 Beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses undertook to explain this law, saying,

6 “Yahweh our God spoke to us in Horeb, saying, ‘You have lived long enough at this mountain.

7 Turn, and journey on, and go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all its neighbouring places: in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the lowland, in the Negev, and by the seashore, the land of the Canaanites, and Lebanon, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates.

8 See, I have set the land before you. Go in and possess the land that Yahweh swore to your fathers—to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob—to give to them and to their offspring after them.’ ”

9 And I spoke to you at that time, saying, “I am not able to bear you by myself alone.

10 Yahweh your God has multiplied you, and see, you are today as the stars of the sky for multitude.

11 May Yahweh, the God of your fathers, add to you a thousand times as many as you are, and bless you, as he has spoken to you!

12 How can I bear by myself alone your trouble, and your burden, and your strife?

13 Bring forward for yourselves wise men, and men of understanding, and men known among your tribes, and I will set them as heads over you.”

14 And you answered me, and said, “The thing that you have spoken is good to do.”

15 So I took the heads of your tribes, wise men and known men, and set them as heads over you, commanders of thousands, and commanders of hundreds, and commanders of fifties, and commanders of tens, and officers, for your tribes.

16 And I commanded your judges at that time, saying, “Hear between your brothers, and judge righteously between a man and his brother, and his sojourner.

17 You shall not show favour in judgment; you shall hear the small and the great alike. You shall not be afraid before any man, for the judgment is God’s. And the matter that is too hard for you, you shall bring near to me, and I will hear it.”

18 And I commanded you at that time all the things that you shall do.

19 And we journeyed from Horeb, and went through all that great and dreadful wilderness that you saw, by the way to the hill country of the Amorites, as Yahweh our God commanded us; and we came to Kadesh Barnea.

20 And I said to you, “You have come to the hill country of the Amorites, which Yahweh our God is giving to us.

21 See, Yahweh your God has set the land before you. Go up, possess it, as Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you. Do not fear, and do not be dismayed.”

22 And you all came near to me, and said, “Let us send men before us, and they shall search out the land for us, and bring back word to us of the way by which we shall go up, and the cities to which we shall come.”

23 And the thing was good in my eyes. And I took twelve men from you, one man for each tribe.

24 And they turned and went up into the hill country, and came to the Valley of Eshcol, and spied it out.

25 And they took in their hand some of the fruit of the land, and brought it down to us, and brought back word to us, and said, “The land that Yahweh our God is giving to us is good.”

26 Yet you were not willing to go up, but rebelled against the command of Yahweh your God.

27 And you murmured in your tents, and said, “Because Yahweh hated us, he has brought us out of the land of Egypt, to give us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us.

28 Where are we going up? Our brothers have melted our heart, saying, ‘The people are greater and taller than we. The cities are great and fortified up to the sky. And we have also seen the sons of the Anakim there!’ ”

29 Then I said to you, “Do not tremble, and do not be afraid of them.

30 Yahweh your God, who goes before you, he will fight for you, according to all that he did with you in Egypt before your eyes,

31 and in the wilderness, where you saw how Yahweh your God carried you, as a man carries his son, in all the way that you went, until you came to this place.”

32 Yet in this thing you do not believe Yahweh your God,

33 who goes before you on the way, to seek out a place for you to camp, in fire by night, to show you the way by which you shall go, and in the cloud by day.

34 And Yahweh heard the voice of your words, and was angry, and swore, saying,

35 “Not one of these men, this evil generation, shall see the good land that I swore to give to your fathers,

36 except Caleb the son of Jephunneh. He shall see it. And to him I will give the land on which he has trodden, and to his children, because he has fully followed Yahweh.”

37 Yahweh was also angry with me because of you, saying, “You also shall not go in there.

38 Joshua the son of Nun, who stands before you, he shall go in there. Strengthen him, for he will cause Israel to inherit it.

39 And your little ones, whom you said would be prey, and your children, who today do not know good or evil, they shall go in there. And to them I will give it, and they shall possess it.

40 But as for you, turn, and journey into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea.”

41 Then you answered and said to me, “We have sinned against Yahweh. We will go up and fight, according to all that Yahweh our God commanded us.” And each man of you girded on his weapons of war, and you thought it a light thing to go up into the hill country.

42 And Yahweh said to me, “Say to them, ‘Do not go up, and do not fight, for I am not among you, lest you be struck down before your enemies.’ ”

43 And I spoke to you, and you did not listen; but you rebelled against the command of Yahweh, and acted presumptuously, and went up into the hill country.

44 And the Amorites who lived in that hill country came out to meet you, and chased you as bees do, and crushed you in Seir as far as Hormah.

45 And you returned and wept before Yahweh, but Yahweh did not listen to your voice, nor give ear to you.

46 And you stayed in Kadesh many days, according to the days that you stayed there.

Deuteronomy 2

1 Then we turned, and journeyed into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea, as Yahweh had spoken to me; and we went around Mount Seir for many days.

2 And Yahweh spoke to me, saying,

3 “You have gone around this mountain long enough. Turn for yourselves northward.

4 And command the people, saying, ‘You are passing through the border of your brothers, the children of Esau, who live in Seir; and they will be afraid of you. So be very careful.

5 Do not contend with them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even a foot’s tread, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.

6 You shall buy food from them with money, and eat; and you shall also buy water from them with money, and drink.’ ”

7 For Yahweh your God has blessed you in all the work of your hand. He has known your walking through this great wilderness. These forty years Yahweh your God has been with you. You have lacked nothing.

8 So we passed by our brothers, the children of Esau, who live in Seir, away from the way of the Arabah, from Elath and from Ezion Geber. And we turned and passed by the way of the wilderness of Moab.

9 And Yahweh said to me, “Do not harass Moab, and do not contend with them in battle, for I will not give you any of his land as a possession, because I have given Ar to the children of Lot as a possession.”

10 (The Emim lived there before, a people great and many and tall, like the Anakim.

11 They too are counted as Rephaim, like the Anakim; but the Moabites call them Emim.

12 And in Seir the Horites lived before, but the children of Esau dispossessed them and destroyed them from before them, and lived in their place, just as Israel did to the land of his possession, which Yahweh gave to them.)

13 “Now rise up, and cross for yourselves the brook Zered.” And we crossed the brook Zered.

14 And the days that we went from Kadesh Barnea until we crossed the brook Zered were thirty-eight years, until all the generation, the men of war, had come to an end from the midst of the camp, as Yahweh had sworn to them.

15 Moreover, the hand of Yahweh was against them, to confound them from the midst of the camp, until they came to an end.

16 And it happened, when all the men of war had come to an end, dying from among the people,

17 that Yahweh spoke to me, saying,

18 “You are passing today over the border of Moab, at Ar.

19 And when you come near, opposite the children of Ammon, do not harass them, and do not contend with them, for I will not give you any of the land of the children of Ammon as a possession, because I have given it to the children of Lot as a possession.”

20 (That too is counted a land of Rephaim. Rephaim lived in it before, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,

21 a people great and many and tall, like the Anakim. And Yahweh destroyed them before them, and they dispossessed them, and lived in their place,

22 just as he did for the children of Esau who live in Seir, when he destroyed the Horites from before them; and they dispossessed them, and lived in their place, to this day.

23 And the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza—the Caphtorim, who came out of Caphtor, destroyed them, and lived in their place.)

24 “Rise up, journey on, and cross the valley of the Arnon. See, I have given into your hand Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to possess it, and contend with him in battle.

25 This day I will begin to put the dread of you and the fear of you on the peoples under all the sky, who will hear the report of you, and tremble, and writhe because of you.”

26 And I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon, with words of peace, saying,

27 “Let me pass through your land. I will go along the road, the road; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

28 You shall sell me food for money, and I will eat; and you shall give me water for money, and I will drink. Only let me pass through on my feet,

29 just as the children of Esau who live in Seir, and the Moabites who live in Ar, did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land that Yahweh our God is giving to us.”

30 But Sihon king of Heshbon was not willing to let us pass by him, for Yahweh your God hardened his spirit and made his heart firm, in order to give him into your hand, as it is this day.

31 And Yahweh said to me, “See, I have begun to give Sihon and his land before you. Begin to possess, so that you may possess his land.”

32 Then Sihon came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Jahaz.

33 And Yahweh our God gave him before us, and we struck him, and his sons, and all his people.

34 And we captured all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed every city—the men, and the women, and the little ones. We left no survivor.

35 Only the livestock we took as plunder for ourselves, and the spoil of the cities that we had captured.

36 From Aroer, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, and the city that is in the valley, as far as Gilead, there was no city too strong for us. Yahweh our God gave everything before us.

37 Only to the land of the children of Ammon you did not draw near—all the side of the river Jabbok, and the cities of the hill country, and all that Yahweh our God had commanded us.

Deuteronomy 3

1 Then we turned, and went up the way to Bashan. And Og the king of Bashan came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.

2 And Yahweh said to me, “Do not fear him, for I have given him into your hand, and all his people, and his land. And you shall do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.”

3 So Yahweh our God also gave Og, the king of Bashan, and all his people into our hand. And we struck him until no survivor was left to him.

4 And we captured all his cities at that time. There was no city that we did not take from them: sixty cities, all the region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

5 All these were fortified cities, with high walls, gates, and bars, besides the unwalled towns, very many.

6 And we utterly destroyed them, as we did to Sihon king of Heshbon, utterly destroying every city—the men, the women, and the little ones.

7 But all the livestock, and the spoil of the cities, we took as plunder for ourselves.

8 And we took at that time the land from the hand of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the valley of the Arnon as far as Mount Hermon.

9 (The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir.)

10 All the cities of the plain, and all Gilead, and all Bashan, as far as Salecah and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

11 (For only Og king of Bashan remained from the remnant of the Rephaim. See, his bed was a bed of iron. Is it not in Rabbah of the children of Ammon? Nine cubits was its length, and four cubits its width, by the cubit of a man.)

12 And this land we possessed at that time: from Aroer, which is by the valley of the Arnon, and half the hill country of Gilead and its cities, I gave to the Reubenites and to the Gadites;

13 and the rest of Gilead, and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh—all the region of Argob, all that Bashan; that is called the land of Rephaim.

14 Jair the son of Manasseh took all the region of Argob, as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and called them, Bashan itself, after his own name, Havvoth Jair, to this day.)

15 And to Machir I gave Gilead.

16 And to the Reubenites and to the Gadites I gave from Gilead as far as the valley of the Arnon—the middle of the valley as the border—as far as the river Jabbok, the border of the children of Ammon;

17 and the Arabah, and the Jordan as the border, from Chinnereth as far as the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, under the slopes of Pisgah eastward.

18 And I commanded you at that time, saying, “Yahweh your God has given you this land to possess it. You shall cross over armed before your brothers, the children of Israel—all you men of valour.

19 Only your wives, and your little ones, and your livestock—I know that you have much livestock—shall stay in your cities that I have given you,

20 until Yahweh gives rest to your brothers, as to you, and they too possess the land that Yahweh your God is giving them beyond the Jordan. Then you shall return, each man to his possession, which I have given you.”

21 And I commanded Joshua at that time, saying, “Your eyes have seen all that Yahweh your God has done to these two kings. So Yahweh will do to all the kingdoms to which you are crossing over there.

22 You shall not fear them, for Yahweh your God, he is the one fighting for you.”

23 And I pleaded with Yahweh at that time, saying,

24 “Lord Yahweh, you have begun to show your servant your greatness, and your strong hand. For what god is there in the sky or on the earth that can do works like yours, and mighty acts like yours?

25 Let me cross over, please, and see the good land that is beyond the Jordan, that good hill country, and Lebanon.”

26 But Yahweh was angry with me for your sake, and did not listen to me. And Yahweh said to me, “It is enough for you! Do not speak to me any more about this matter.

27 Go up to the top of Pisgah, and lift up your eyes westward, and northward, and southward, and eastward, and see with your eyes, for you shall not cross this Jordan.

28 But command Joshua, and strengthen him, and make him firm, for he shall cross over before this people, and he shall cause them to inherit the land that you will see.”

29 And we stayed in the valley, opposite Beth Peor.

Deuteronomy 4

1 And now, Israel, hear the statutes and the ordinances that I am teaching you to do, so that you may live, and go in and possess the land that Yahweh, the God of your fathers, is giving you.

2 You shall not add to the word that I am commanding you, and you shall not diminish from it, so that you keep the commandments of Yahweh your God that I am commanding you.

3 Your eyes have seen what Yahweh did at Baal Peor; for every man who went after Baal Peor, Yahweh your God destroyed him from your midst.

4 But you who clung to Yahweh your God are all alive today.

5 See, I have taught you statutes and ordinances, just as Yahweh my God commanded me, to do so in the midst of the land to which you are going in, to possess it.

6 And you shall keep them and do them, for that is your wisdom and your understanding in the eyes of the peoples, who will hear all these statutes and say, “Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.”

7 For what great nation is there that has God so near to it, as Yahweh our God is, whenever we call on him?

8 And what great nation is there that has statutes and ordinances as righteous as all this law that I am setting before you today?

9 Only take care for yourself, and guard your soul very much, lest you forget the things that your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart all the days of your life; and you shall make them known to your children and to your children’s children—

10 the day that you stood before Yahweh your God in Horeb, when Yahweh said to me, “Assemble the people to me, and I will make them hear my words, so that they may learn to fear me all the days that they live on the earth, and so that they may teach their children.”

11 And you came near and stood under the mountain. And the mountain was burning with fire up to the heart of the sky, with darkness, cloud, and thick darkness.

12 And Yahweh spoke to you from the midst of the fire: you were hearing the voice of words, but you were seeing no form; there was only a voice.

13 And he declared to you his covenant, which he commanded you to do, the Ten Words. And he wrote them on two tablets of stone.

14 And Yahweh commanded me at that time to teach you statutes and ordinances, for you to do them in the land to which you are crossing over, to possess it.

15 And you shall take great care for your souls, for you saw no form of any kind on the day that Yahweh spoke to you in Horeb from the midst of the fire,

16 lest you act corruptly, and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of any figure, the likeness of male or female,

17 the likeness of any animal that is on the earth, the likeness of any winged bird that flies in the sky,

18 the likeness of anything that creeps on the ground, the likeness of any fish that is in the water under the earth;

19 and lest you lift up your eyes to the sky, and see the sun and the moon and the stars, all the host of the sky, and be drawn away, and bow down to them, and serve them, which Yahweh your God has allotted to all the peoples under all the sky.

20 But you Yahweh has taken, and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be for him a people, an inheritance, as it is this day.

21 And Yahweh was angry with me because of your words, and swore that I should not cross the Jordan, and that I should not go in to the good land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance;

22 for I am dying in this land. I am not crossing the Jordan, but you are crossing, and you shall possess this good land.

23 Take care for yourselves, lest you forget the covenant of Yahweh your God, which he cut with you, and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of anything that Yahweh your God has commanded you against.

24 For Yahweh your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.

25 When you father children and children’s children, and you have grown old in the land, and you act corruptly, and make a carved image, the form of anything, and do what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh your God, to provoke him,

26 I call the sky and the earth to witness against you today, that you shall surely perish quickly from off the land to which you are crossing the Jordan, to possess it. You shall not prolong your days on it, for you shall surely be destroyed.

27 And Yahweh will scatter you among the peoples, and you will be left few in number among the nations to which Yahweh will drive you.

28 And there you will serve gods, the work of human hands, wood and stone, which do not see, and do not hear, and do not eat, and do not smell.

29 And from there you will seek Yahweh your God, and you will find him, when you search for him with all your heart and with all your soul.

30 In your distress, when all these things have found you, in the latter days, you will return to Yahweh your God and listen to his voice.

31 For Yahweh your God is a merciful God. He will not abandon you, and he will not destroy you, and he will not forget the covenant of your fathers, which he swore to them.

32 For ask now about the former days that were before you, from the day that God created man on the earth, and from one end of the sky to the other end of the sky: has anything been done like this great thing, or has anything like it been heard?

33 Has a people heard the voice of God speaking from the midst of the fire, as you have heard, and lived?

34 Or has God tried to go and take for himself a nation from the midst of a nation, by trials, by signs, and by wonders, and by war, and by a strong hand, and by an outstretched arm, and by great terrors, according to all that Yahweh your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?

35 You were shown it, so that you might know that Yahweh, he is God. There is no other besides him.

36 From the sky he made you hear his voice, to discipline you. And on the earth he showed you his great fire, and you heard his words from the midst of the fire.

37 And because he loved your fathers, and chose their offspring after them, he brought you out with his presence, with his great power, out of Egypt,

38 to dispossess nations greater and mightier than you from before you, to bring you in, to give you their land as an inheritance, as it is this day.

39 And you shall know today, and take it back to your heart, that Yahweh, he is God in the sky above and on the earth beneath. There is no other.

40 And you shall keep his statutes and his commandments, which I am commanding you today, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you, and so that you may prolong your days on the ground that Yahweh your God is giving you, all the days.

41 Then Moses set apart three cities beyond the Jordan, toward the rising of the sun,

42 for a killer to flee there, who kills his neighbour without knowledge, and who was not hating him yesterday or the day before; and he shall flee to one of these cities, and live:

43 Bezer in the wilderness, in the land of the plain, for the Reubenites; and Ramoth in Gilead, for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan, for the Manassites.

44 And this is the law that Moses set before the children of Israel.

45 These are the testimonies, and the statutes, and the ordinances, that Moses spoke to the children of Israel when they came out of Egypt,

46 beyond the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth Peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, whom Moses and the children of Israel struck when they came out of Egypt.

47 And they possessed his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites, who were beyond the Jordan, toward the rising of the sun,

48 from Aroer, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, as far as Mount Sion, which is Hermon,

49 and all the Arabah beyond the Jordan eastward, as far as the Sea of the Arabah, under the slopes of Pisgah.

Deuteronomy 5

1 And Moses called to all Israel, and said to them, “Hear, Israel, the statutes and the ordinances that I am speaking in your ears today, and you shall learn them, and keep them, to do them.

2 Yahweh our God cut a covenant with us in Horeb.

3 Yahweh did not cut this covenant with our fathers, but with us, we who are all of us here alive today.

4 Face to face Yahweh spoke with you on the mountain, from the midst of the fire,

5 —I was standing between Yahweh and you at that time, to declare to you the word of Yahweh, for you were afraid before the fire, and did not go up onto the mountain—saying,

6 “I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves.

7 You shall have no other gods before me.

8 You shall not make for yourself a carved image—any form that is in the sky above, or that is on the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth.

9 You shall not bow down to them, and you shall not serve them, for I, Yahweh your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children, and on the third generation, and on the fourth generation, of those who hate me,

10 and showing steadfast love to thousands, to those who love me and keep my commandments.

11 You shall not take the name of Yahweh your God in vain, for Yahweh will not hold him guiltless who takes his name in vain.

12 Keep the Sabbath day, to make it holy, as Yahweh your God commanded you.

13 Six days you shall labour, and do all your work;

14 but the seventh day is a Sabbath to Yahweh your God. You shall not do any work—you, or your son, or your daughter, or your slave, or your female slave, or your ox, or your donkey, or any of your livestock, or your sojourner who is within your gates—so that your slave and your female slave may rest as you do.

15 And you shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and Yahweh your God brought you out from there with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm. Therefore Yahweh your God commanded you to keep the Sabbath day.

16 Honour your father and your mother, as Yahweh your God commanded you, so that your days may be long, and so that it may go well with you, on the ground that Yahweh your God is giving you.

17 You shall not murder.

18 And you shall not commit adultery.

19 And you shall not steal.

20 And you shall not answer against your neighbour as a false witness.

21 And you shall not covet your neighbour’s wife. And you shall not desire your neighbour’s house, his field, or his slave, or his female slave, his ox, or his donkey, or anything that is your neighbour’s.”

22 These words Yahweh spoke to all your assembly on the mountain, from the midst of the fire, the cloud, and the thick darkness, with a great voice, and he added no more. And he wrote them on two tablets of stone, and gave them to me.

23 And it happened, when you heard the voice from the midst of the darkness, and the mountain was burning with fire, that you came near to me, all the heads of your tribes, and your elders;

24 and you said, “See, Yahweh our God has shown us his glory and his greatness, and we have heard his voice from the midst of the fire. This day we have seen that God speaks with man, and he lives.

25 And now, why should we die? For this great fire will consume us. If we go on hearing the voice of Yahweh our God any more, we shall die.

26 For who is there of all flesh who has heard the voice of the living God speaking from the midst of the fire, as we have, and lived?

27 You go near, and hear all that Yahweh our God will say; and you shall speak to us all that Yahweh our God will speak to you, and we will hear, and do it.”

28 And Yahweh heard the voice of your words, when you spoke to me; and Yahweh said to me, “I have heard the voice of the words of this people, which they have spoken to you. They have done well in all that they have spoken.

29 If only they had this heart in them, to fear me and to keep all my commandments all the days, so that it might go well with them and with their children forever!

30 Go, say to them, ‘Return for yourselves to your tents.’

31 But as for you, stand here with me, and I will speak to you all the commandment, and the statutes, and the ordinances, that you shall teach them, and they shall do them in the land that I am giving them to possess.”

32 And you shall keep to do as Yahweh your God has commanded you. You shall not turn aside to the right or to the left.

33 In all the way that Yahweh your God has commanded you, you shall walk, so that you may live, and so that it may be well with you, and so that you may prolong your days in the land that you shall possess.

Deuteronomy 6

1 And this is the commandment, the statutes and the ordinances, that Yahweh your God commanded to teach you, to do them in the land to which you are crossing over, to possess it,

2 so that you may fear Yahweh your God, to keep all his statutes and his commandments, which I am commanding you—you, and your son, and your son’s son, all the days of your life—and so that your days may be prolonged.

3 And you shall hear, Israel, and keep to do it, so that it may go well with you, and so that you may multiply greatly, as Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you, in a land flowing with milk and honey.

4 Hear, Israel: Yahweh our God, Yahweh is one.

5 And you shall love Yahweh your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your might.

6 And these words, which I am commanding you today, shall be on your heart;

7 and you shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk on the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up.

8 And you shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.

9 And you shall write them on the doorposts of your house, and on your gates.

10 And it shall be, when Yahweh your God brings you into the land that he swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give you—great and good cities that you did not build,

11 and houses full of all good things that you did not fill, and cisterns hewn out that you did not hew, vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant—and you eat and are satisfied,

12 take care for yourself, lest you forget Yahweh, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves.

13 Yahweh your God you shall fear, and him you shall serve, and by his name you shall swear.

14 You shall not go after other gods, from the gods of the peoples who are around you,

15 for Yahweh your God among you is a jealous God—lest the anger of Yahweh your God burn against you, and he destroy you from off the face of the ground.

16 You shall not test Yahweh your God, as you tested him at Massah.

17 You shall surely keep the commandments of Yahweh your God, and his testimonies, and his statutes, which he has commanded you.

18 And you shall do what is right and good in the eyes of Yahweh, so that it may go well with you, and you may go in and possess the good land that Yahweh swore to your fathers,

19 to thrust out all your enemies from before you, as Yahweh has spoken.

20 When your son asks you in time to come, saying, “What are the testimonies, and the statutes, and the ordinances, that Yahweh our God has commanded you?”

21 then you shall say to your son, “We were slaves to Pharaoh in Egypt, and Yahweh brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand;

22 and Yahweh gave signs and wonders, great and grievous, on Egypt, on Pharaoh, and on all his house, before our eyes;

23 and us he brought out from there, in order to bring us in, to give us the land that he swore to our fathers.

24 And Yahweh commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear Yahweh our God, for our good all the days, to keep us alive, as it is this day.

25 And it will be righteousness for us, when we keep to do all this commandment before Yahweh our God, as he has commanded us.”

Deuteronomy 7

1 When Yahweh your God brings you into the land that you are entering there to possess, and casts out many nations before you — the Hittite, the Girgashite, the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite, seven nations greater and mightier than you;

2 and Yahweh your God gives them before you and you strike them, you shall surely devote them to destruction. You shall make no covenant with them, and you shall show them no mercy.

3 You shall not make marriages with them; your daughter you shall not give to his son, and his daughter you shall not take for your son.

4 For he will turn your son away from following me, and they will serve other gods, and Yahweh's anger will be kindled against you, and he will destroy you quickly.

5 But this is what you shall do to them: you shall break down their altars, and break their pillars in pieces, and cut down their Asherim, and burn their carved images with fire.

6 For you are a holy people to Yahweh your God. Yahweh your God has chosen you to be for him a treasured people, out of all the peoples who are on the face of the earth.

7 Not because you were more in number than all the peoples did Yahweh set his love on you and choose you, for you were the fewest of all the peoples;

8 but because Yahweh loved you, and because he kept the oath that he swore to your fathers, Yahweh brought you out with a strong hand and redeemed you from the house of slaves, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

9 And you shall know that Yahweh your God, he is God, the faithful God, who keeps the covenant and the steadfast love with those who love him and keep his commandments, to a thousand generations,

10 and who repays those who hate him to their face, to destroy them. He will not delay for one who hates him; to his face he will repay him.

11 And you shall keep the commandment, the statutes, and the ordinances that I command you today, to do them.

12 And it shall be, because you listen to these ordinances and keep and do them, that Yahweh your God will keep for you the covenant and the steadfast love that he swore to your fathers.

13 And he will love you, and bless you, and multiply you, and he will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground, your grain and your new wine and your oil, the young of your cattle and the young of your flock, on the ground that he swore to your fathers to give you.

14 You shall be blessed above all the peoples; there shall not be a barren male or a barren female among you, or among your livestock.

15 And Yahweh will turn away all sickness from you, and all the evil diseases of Egypt that you knew he will not put on you, but will give them to all who hate you.

16 And you shall consume all the peoples that Yahweh your God gives you; your eye shall not pity them, and you shall not serve their gods, for that is a snare to you.

17 If you say in your heart, “These nations are more than I; how can I dispossess them?”

18 you shall not fear them; you shall surely remember what Yahweh your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt:

19 the great trials that your eyes saw, and the signs and the wonders, and the strong hand and the outstretched arm, with which Yahweh your God brought you out. So Yahweh your God will do to all the peoples of whom you are afraid.

20 And also the hornet Yahweh your God will send among them, until those who remain and those who hide themselves perish from before you.

21 You shall not be terrified before them, for Yahweh your God is in your midst, a great and awesome God.

22 And Yahweh your God will cast out these nations before you little by little; you will not be able to finish them quickly, lest the beasts of the field multiply against you.

23 And Yahweh your God will give them before you, and will throw them into great confusion, until they are destroyed.

24 And he will give their kings into your hand, and you shall make their name perish from under the heavens; no man shall stand before you, until you have destroyed them.

25 The carved images of their gods you shall burn with fire; you shall not covet the silver and the gold that is on them and take it for yourself, lest you be snared by it, for it is an abomination to Yahweh your God.

26 And you shall not bring an abomination into your house, and become a devoted thing like it; you shall surely detest it and you shall surely abhor it, for it is a devoted thing.

Deuteronomy 8

1 All the commandment that I command you today you shall keep to do, so that you may live and multiply and come in and possess the land that Yahweh swore to your fathers.

2 And you shall remember all the way that Yahweh your God led you these forty years in the wilderness, in order to humble you, to test you, to know what was in your heart, whether you would keep his commandments or not.

3 And he humbled you and made you hungry, and fed you the manna that you did not know and that your fathers did not know, in order to make you know that man does not live by bread alone, but by everything that comes out of Yahweh's mouth man lives.

4 Your garment did not wear out from on you, and your foot did not swell, these forty years.

5 And you shall know in your heart that as a man disciplines his son, Yahweh your God disciplines you.

6 And you shall keep the commandments of Yahweh your God, to walk in his ways and to fear him.

7 For Yahweh your God is bringing you into a good land, a land of streams of water, springs and deep waters coming out in the valley and in the hills;

8 a land of wheat and barley, and vine and fig tree and pomegranate, a land of olive oil and honey;

9 a land in which you will eat bread without scarcity; you will lack nothing in it; a land whose stones are iron, and from whose mountains you will dig copper.

10 And you will eat and be satisfied, and you will bless Yahweh your God for the good land that he has given you.

11 Guard yourself, lest you forget Yahweh your God, by not keeping his commandments and his ordinances and his statutes that I command you today;

12 lest you eat and be satisfied, and build good houses and dwell in them;

13 and your herd and your flock multiply, and silver and gold multiply for you, and all that is yours multiplies;

14 and your heart is lifted up, and you forget Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, from the house of slaves;

15 who led you through the great and awesome wilderness — serpent, fiery serpent and scorpion, and thirst where there was no water — who brought water out for you from the rock of flint;

16 who fed you manna in the wilderness that your fathers did not know, in order to humble you and in order to test you, to do you good in your end;

17 and you say in your heart, “My power and the strength of my hand made me this wealth.”

18 And you shall remember Yahweh your God, for he is the one who gives you power to make wealth, in order to establish his covenant that he swore to your fathers, as this day.

19 And it shall be, if you surely forget Yahweh your God and go after other gods and serve them and bow down to them, I testify against you today that you shall surely perish.

20 As the nations that Yahweh is destroying before you, so you shall perish, because you did not listen to the voice of Yahweh your God.

Deuteronomy 9

1 Hear, Israel: you are crossing the Jordan today, to come in to dispossess nations greater and mightier than you, cities great and fortified up to the heavens,

2 a people great and tall, the sons of the Anakim, whom you know and of whom you have heard, “Who can stand before the sons of Anak?”

3 And you shall know today that Yahweh your God, he is the one who crosses before you as a consuming fire; he will destroy them and he will subdue them before you, and you shall dispossess them and destroy them quickly, as Yahweh has spoken to you.

4 Do not say in your heart, when Yahweh your God thrusts them out from before you, saying, “For my righteousness Yahweh has brought me in to possess this land,” while for the wickedness of these nations Yahweh is dispossessing them from before you.

5 Not for your righteousness and not for the uprightness of your heart are you coming in to possess their land, but for the wickedness of these nations Yahweh your God is dispossessing them from before you, and in order to establish the word that Yahweh swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

6 And you shall know that not for your righteousness is Yahweh your God giving you this good land to possess, for you are a stiff-necked people.

7 Remember, do not forget, how you provoked Yahweh your God to wrath in the wilderness; from the day that you went out of the land of Egypt until you came to this place, you have been rebellious against Yahweh.

8 And at Horeb you provoked Yahweh to wrath, and Yahweh was angry with you, to destroy you,

9 when I went up the mountain to receive the tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant that Yahweh made with you, and I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights; I ate no bread and drank no water.

10 And Yahweh gave me the two tablets of stone written with the finger of God, and on them was written according to all the words that Yahweh spoke with you on the mountain from the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly.

11 And it was at the end of forty days and forty nights that Yahweh gave me the two tablets of stone, the tablets of the covenant.

12 And Yahweh said to me, “Arise, go down quickly from here, for your people whom you brought out of Egypt have dealt corruptly; they have quickly turned aside from the way that I commanded them; they have made themselves a cast image.”

13 And Yahweh said to me, saying, “I have seen this people, and behold, it is a stiff-necked people.

14 Leave me alone, and I will destroy them and blot out their name from under the heavens, and I will make you into a nation mightier and greater than they.”

15 And I turned and came down from the mountain, and the mountain was burning with fire, and the two tablets of the covenant were on my two hands.

16 And I saw, and behold, you had sinned against Yahweh your God; you had made yourselves a cast calf; you had quickly turned aside from the way that Yahweh commanded you.

17 And I took hold of the two tablets and threw them from my two hands, and broke them before your eyes.

18 And I fell down before Yahweh as at the first, forty days and forty nights; I ate no bread and drank no water, because of all your sin that you sinned, in doing what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh, to provoke him.

19 For I feared because of the anger and the wrath with which Yahweh was angry against you, to destroy you; and Yahweh listened to me that time also.

20 And with Aaron Yahweh was very angry, to destroy him; and I prayed also for Aaron at that time.

21 And I took your sin that you had made, the calf, and burned it with fire, and crushed it, grinding it well, until it was fine as dust, and I threw its dust into the stream that came down from the mountain.

22 And at Taberah, and at Massah, and at Kibroth Hattaavah, you provoked Yahweh to wrath.

23 And when Yahweh sent you from Kadesh Barnea, saying, “Go up and possess the land that I have given you,” you rebelled against the commandment of Yahweh your God, and you did not believe him and did not listen to his voice.

24 You have been rebellious against Yahweh from the day that I knew you.

25 And I fell down before Yahweh the forty days and the forty nights that I fell down, because Yahweh had said he would destroy you.

26 And I prayed to Yahweh and said, “Lord Yahweh, do not destroy your people and your inheritance, whom you redeemed in your greatness, whom you brought out of Egypt with a strong hand.

27 Remember your slaves, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; do not turn to the stubbornness of this people, or to its wickedness, or to its sin,

28 lest the land from which you brought us out say, ‘Because Yahweh was not able to bring them into the land that he promised them, and because he hated them, he brought them out to kill them in the wilderness.’

29 And they are your people and your inheritance, whom you brought out with your great power and with your outstretched arm.”

Deuteronomy 10

1 At that time Yahweh said to me, “Hew for yourself two tablets of stone like the first, and come up to me on the mountain, and make for yourself an ark of wood.

2 And I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke, and you shall put them in the ark.”

3 And I made an ark of acacia wood, and hewed two tablets of stone like the first, and went up the mountain, and the two tablets were in my hand.

4 And he wrote on the tablets according to the first writing, the ten words, which Yahweh spoke to you on the mountain from the midst of the fire on the day of the assembly, and Yahweh gave them to me.

5 And I turned and came down from the mountain, and put the tablets in the ark that I had made, and they were there, as Yahweh commanded me.

6 And the children of Israel journeyed from Beeroth Bene Jaakan to Moserah; there Aaron died, and there he was buried, and Eleazar his son served as priest in his place.

7 From there they journeyed to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land of streams of water.

8 At that time Yahweh set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, to stand before Yahweh to minister to him, and to bless in his name, to this day.

9 Therefore Levi had no portion or inheritance with his brothers; Yahweh is his inheritance, as Yahweh your God spoke to him.

10 And I stayed on the mountain as the first days, forty days and forty nights, and Yahweh listened to me that time also; Yahweh was not willing to destroy you.

11 And Yahweh said to me, “Arise, go, lead the journey before the people, and they shall come in and possess the land that I swore to their fathers to give them.”

12 And now, Israel, what does Yahweh your God ask of you, but to fear Yahweh your God, to walk in all his ways and to love him, and to serve Yahweh your God with all your heart and with all your soul,

13 to keep the commandments of Yahweh and his statutes, that I command you today, for your good?

14 Behold, to Yahweh your God belong the heavens and the heavens of heavens, the earth and all that is in it.

15 Only in your fathers did Yahweh delight, to love them, and he chose their seed after them, you, above all the peoples, as this day.

16 And you shall circumcise the foreskin of your heart, and your neck you shall stiffen no more.

17 For Yahweh your God, he is God of gods and Lord of lords, the great God, the mighty and the awesome, who shows no partiality and does not take a bribe,

18 who does justice for the fatherless and the widow, and loves the foreigner, to give him bread and clothing.

19 And you shall love the foreigner, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

20 Yahweh your God you shall fear; him you shall serve, and to him you shall cling, and by his name you shall swear.

21 He is your praise, and he is your God, who did for you these great and awesome things that your eyes saw.

22 With seventy persons your fathers went down to Egypt, and now Yahweh your God has made you as the stars of the heavens for multitude.

Deuteronomy 11

1 And you shall love Yahweh your God, and keep his charge, and his statutes and his ordinances and his commandments, all the days.

2 And you shall know today — for not with your children, who did not know and who did not see, the discipline of Yahweh your God, his greatness, his strong hand and his outstretched arm,

3 and his signs and his deeds, which he did in the midst of Egypt to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to all his land;

4 and what he did to the army of Egypt, to its horses and to its chariots, how he made the waters of the Sea of Reeds flow over their faces as they pursued after you, and Yahweh destroyed them to this day;

5 and what he did for you in the wilderness until you came to this place;

6 and what he did to Dathan and to Abiram, the sons of Eliab son of Reuben, how the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and their households and their tents, and all the living substance that was at their feet, in the midst of all Israel;

7 for your eyes saw all the great deed of Yahweh that he did.

8 And you shall keep all the commandment that I command you today, so that you may be strong and come in and possess the land that you are crossing there to possess,

9 and so that you may prolong days on the ground that Yahweh swore to your fathers to give to them and to their seed, a land flowing with milk and honey.

10 For the land that you are entering there to possess is not like the land of Egypt, from which you went out, where you sowed your seed and watered it with your foot, like a vegetable garden;

11 and the land that you are crossing there to possess is a land of mountains and valleys; from the rain of the heavens it drinks water,

12 a land that Yahweh your God seeks after; always the eyes of Yahweh your God are on it, from the beginning of the year to the end of the year.

13 And it shall be, if you surely listen to my commandments that I command you today, to love Yahweh your God and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul,

14 and I will give the rain of your land in its time, early rain and late rain, and you shall gather your grain and your new wine and your oil.

15 And I will give grass in your field for your livestock, and you will eat and be satisfied.

16 Guard yourselves, lest your heart be enticed, and you turn aside and serve other gods and bow down to them;

17 and Yahweh's anger will be kindled against you, and he will shut the heavens and there will be no rain, and the ground will not give its produce, and you will perish quickly from on the good land that Yahweh is giving you.

18 And you shall put these words of mine on your heart and on your soul, and bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.

19 And you shall teach them to your children, to speak of them when you sit in your house and when you walk on the way and when you lie down and when you rise up.

20 And you shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates,

21 so that your days and the days of your children may multiply on the ground that Yahweh swore to your fathers to give them, as the days of the heavens over the earth.

22 For if you surely keep all this commandment that I command you, to do it, to love Yahweh your God, to walk in all his ways and to cling to him,

23 then Yahweh will dispossess all these nations from before you, and you shall possess nations greater and mightier than you.

24 Every place on which the sole of your foot treads shall be yours; from the wilderness and the Lebanon, from the river, the river Euphrates, to the western sea shall be your border.

25 No man shall stand before you; Yahweh your God will put the fear of you and the dread of you on the face of all the land on which you tread, as he spoke to you.

26 See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse:

27 the blessing, if you listen to the commandments of Yahweh your God that I command you today;

28 and the curse, if you do not listen to the commandments of Yahweh your God and turn aside from the way that I command you today, to go after other gods that you have not known.

29 And it shall be, when Yahweh your God brings you into the land that you are entering there to possess, you shall set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.

30 Are they not beyond the Jordan, after the way of the setting of the sun, in the land of the Canaanite who dwells in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh?

31 For you are crossing the Jordan to come in to possess the land that Yahweh your God is giving you, and you shall possess it and dwell in it.

32 And you shall keep to do all the statutes and the ordinances that I am setting before you today.

Deuteronomy 12

1 These are the statutes and the ordinances that you shall keep to do in the land that Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has given you to possess, all the days that you live on the ground.

2 You shall surely destroy all the places where the nations that you are dispossessing served their gods, on the high mountains and on the hills and under every green tree;

3 And you shall break down their altars and break their pillars, and their Asherim you shall burn with fire, and the carved images of their gods you shall cut down, and you shall destroy their name from that place.

4 You shall not do so to Yahweh your God.

5 But to the place that Yahweh your God will choose out of all your tribes to put his name there, to his dwelling you shall seek, and there you shall come;

6 And there you shall bring your burnt offerings and your sacrifices and your tithes and the contribution of your hand, and your vows and your freewill offerings and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock;

7 And there you shall eat before Yahweh your God, and you shall rejoice in all the undertaking of your hand, you and your households, in which Yahweh your God has blessed you.

8 You shall not do according to all that we are doing here today, each man all that is right in his eyes;

9 for you have not yet come to the rest and to the inheritance that Yahweh your God is giving you.

10 And you shall cross the Jordan and dwell in the land that Yahweh your God is causing you to inherit, and he will give you rest from all your enemies around, and you shall dwell in safety;

11 And the place that Yahweh your God will choose to make his name dwell there — there you shall bring all that I command you: your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the contribution of your hand, and all the choice of your vows that you vow to Yahweh;

12 And you shall rejoice before Yahweh your God, you and your sons and your daughters and your slaves and your female slaves, and the Levite who is in your gates, for he has no portion or inheritance with you.

13 Guard yourself, lest you offer your burnt offerings in every place that you see;

14 but in the place that Yahweh will choose in one of your tribes, there you shall offer your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I command you.

15 Only, in all the desire of your soul you may slaughter and eat flesh within all your gates, according to the blessing of Yahweh your God that he has given you; the unclean and the clean may eat it, as the gazelle and as the deer.

16 Only the blood you shall not eat; on the earth you shall pour it like water.

17 You may not eat in your gates the tithe of your grain and your new wine and your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock, and all your vows that you vow, and your freewill offerings and the contribution of your hand;

18 but before Yahweh your God you shall eat it in the place that Yahweh your God will choose, you and your son and your daughter and your slave and your female slave, and the Levite who is in your gates, and you shall rejoice before Yahweh your God in all the undertaking of your hand.

19 Guard yourself, lest you forsake the Levite all your days on your ground.

20 When Yahweh your God enlarges your border, as he spoke to you, and you say, “I will eat flesh,” because your soul desires to eat flesh, in all the desire of your soul you may eat flesh.

21 If the place that Yahweh your God will choose to put his name there is far from you, you may slaughter from your herd and from your flock that Yahweh has given you, as I commanded you, and eat in your gates in all the desire of your soul.

22 Just as the gazelle and the deer are eaten, so you shall eat it; the unclean and the clean together may eat it.

23 Only be strong not to eat the blood, for the blood is the life, and you shall not eat the life with the flesh.

24 You shall not eat it; on the earth you shall pour it like water.

25 You shall not eat it, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you, when you do what is right in the eyes of Yahweh.

26 Only your holy things that you have, and your vows, you shall carry and come to the place that Yahweh will choose;

27 And you shall offer your burnt offerings, the flesh and the blood, on the altar of Yahweh your God, and the blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out on the altar of Yahweh your God, and the flesh you shall eat.

28 Keep and listen to all these words that I command you, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you forever, when you do what is good and right in the eyes of Yahweh your God.

29 When Yahweh your God cuts off the nations from before you, to which you are coming there to dispossess them, and you dispossess them and dwell in their land,

30 guard yourself, lest you be snared after them, after they are destroyed from before you, and lest you seek after their gods, saying, “How did these nations serve their gods? I will do so also, I too.”

31 You shall not do so to Yahweh your God, for every abomination to Yahweh that he hates they have done for their gods, for even their sons and their daughters they burn in fire for their gods.

32 All the word that I command you, you shall keep to do; you shall not add to it and you shall not take away from it.

Deuteronomy 13

1 If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams arises among you, and he gives you a sign or a wonder,

2 and the sign or the wonder comes to pass, of which he spoke to you, saying, “Let us go after other gods” (which you have not known) “and let us serve them,”

3 you shall not listen to the words of that prophet, or to that dreamer of dreams; for Yahweh your God is testing you, to know whether you love Yahweh your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

4 You shall walk after Yahweh your God, and you shall fear him, and you shall keep his commandments, and you shall listen to his voice, and you shall serve him, and you shall cling to him.

5 That prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you out of the house of slaves, to draw you aside from the way which Yahweh your God commanded you to walk in. So you shall remove the evil from among you.

6 If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son, or your daughter, or the wife of your bosom, or your neighbour who is as your own soul, entices you secretly, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods”—which you have not known, you, nor your fathers;

7 of the gods of the peoples who are around you, near to you, or far off from you, from the one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth—

8 you shall not consent to him nor listen to him; neither shall your eye pity him, neither shall you spare, neither shall you conceal him;

9 but you shall surely kill him. Your hand shall be first on him to put him to death, and afterwards the hand of all the people.

10 You shall stone him with stones, and he shall die, because he has sought to draw you away from Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves.

11 And all Israel shall hear, and fear, and shall not do any more such an evil thing as this among you.

12 If you hear in one of your cities, which Yahweh your God gives you to dwell there, saying,

13 certain worthless men have gone out from among you and have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, “Let us go and serve other gods,” which you have not known,

14 then you shall inquire, and search, and ask diligently. And see, if it is true, and the thing is certain, that such a detestable thing was done among you,

15 you shall surely strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword, devoting it to destruction, with all that is in it and its livestock, with the edge of the sword.

16 You shall gather all its plunder into the middle of its open place, and shall burn with fire the city, with all its plunder, wholly, to Yahweh your God. It shall be a heap forever. It shall not be built again.

17 Nothing of the devoted thing shall cling to your hand, that Yahweh may turn from the fierceness of his anger and give you mercy, and have compassion on you and multiply you, as he has sworn to your fathers,

18 when you listen to the voice of Yahweh your God, to keep all his commandments which I command you today, to do that which is right in the eyes of Yahweh your God.

Deuteronomy 14

1 You are the children of Yahweh your God. You shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.

2 For you are a holy people to Yahweh your God, and Yahweh has chosen you to be for him a treasured people, out of all peoples who are on the face of the earth.

3 You shall not eat any detestable thing.

4 These are the animals which you may eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat,

5 the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the chamois.

6 Every animal that parts the hoof, and has the hoof split in two and chews the cud, among the animals, that you may eat.

7 Nevertheless these you shall not eat of those that chew the cud, or of those that have the split hoof: the camel, the hare, and the rock badger. Because they chew the cud but do not part the hoof, they are unclean to you.

8 The pig, because it parts the hoof but does not chew the cud, is unclean to you. You shall not eat of their flesh. You shall not touch their carcasses.

9 These you may eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales, you may eat.

10 And whatever does not have fins and scales you shall not eat. It is unclean to you.

11 Every clean bird you may eat.

12 But these are they of which you shall not eat: the eagle, the vulture, and the osprey,

13 the red kite, the falcon, and the kite according to its kind,

14 and every raven according to its kind,

15 the ostrich, the owl, the seagull, and the hawk according to its kind,

16 the little owl, the great owl, and the horned owl,

17 the pelican, the vulture, and the cormorant,

18 the stork, and the heron according to its kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.

19 All winged swarming things are unclean to you. They shall not be eaten.

20 Every clean bird you may eat.

21 You shall not eat anything that dies of itself. You may give it to the sojourner who is within your gates, that he may eat it; or you may sell it to a foreigner; for you are a holy people to Yahweh your God. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

22 You shall surely tithe all the increase of your seed, that which comes out of the field year by year.

23 And you shall eat before Yahweh your God, in the place which he will choose to cause his name to dwell there, the tithe of your grain, of your new wine, and of your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock; that you may learn to fear Yahweh your God always.

24 If the way is too long for you, so that you are not able to carry it, because the place which Yahweh your God will choose to set his name there is too far from you, when Yahweh your God blesses you,

25 then you shall exchange it for money, and bind up the money in your hand, and go to the place which Yahweh your God will choose.

26 And you shall give the money for whatever your soul desires: for herd, or for flock, or for wine, or for strong drink, or for whatever your soul asks of you. And you shall eat there before Yahweh your God, and you shall rejoice, you and your household.

27 And the Levite who is within your gates you shall not forsake, for he has no portion nor inheritance with you.

28 At the end of every three years you shall bring forth all the tithe of your increase in that same year, and shall lay it up within your gates.

29 And the Levite, because he has no portion nor inheritance with you, and the sojourner, and the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates, shall come, and shall eat and be satisfied; that Yahweh your God may bless you in all the work of your hand which you do.

Deuteronomy 15

1 At the end of every seven years you shall make a release.

2 And this is the manner of the release: every creditor shall release what he has lent to his neighbour. He shall not press his neighbour and his brother, because a release to Yahweh has been proclaimed.

3 Of a foreigner you may press it; but whatever of yours is with your brother, your hand shall release.

4 Yet there shall be no needy among you, for Yahweh will surely bless you in the land which Yahweh your God gives you for an inheritance to possess it,

5 if only you surely listen to the voice of Yahweh your God, to keep to do all this commandment which I command you today.

6 For Yahweh your God has blessed you, as he promised you. You shall lend on pledge to many nations, but you shall not borrow on pledge. You shall rule over many nations, but they shall not rule over you.

7 If there is a needy man among you, one of your brothers, within any of your gates in your land which Yahweh your God gives you, you shall not harden your heart, nor shut your hand from your needy brother;

8 but you shall surely open your hand to him, and shall surely lend him on pledge sufficient for his need, whatever he lacks.

9 Beware for yourself, lest there be a worthless thought in your heart, saying, “The seventh year, the year of release, is at hand,” and your eye is evil against your needy brother and you give him nothing; and he cries to Yahweh against you, and it is sin in you.

10 You shall surely give to him, and your heart shall not be grieved when you give to him, because for this thing Yahweh your God will bless you in all your work and in all that you put your hand to.

11 For the needy will never cease out of the land. Therefore I command you, saying, you shall surely open your hand to your brother, to your poor, and to your needy, in your land.

12 If your brother, a Hebrew man, or a Hebrew woman, is sold to you, and he serves you six years, then in the seventh year you shall let him go free from you.

13 And when you let him go free from you, you shall not let him go empty.

14 You shall surely furnish him out of your flock, and out of your threshing floor, and out of your wine press. Of that with which Yahweh your God has blessed you, you shall give to him.

15 And you shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and Yahweh your God redeemed you. Therefore I command you this thing today.

16 And it shall be, if he says to you, “I will not go out from you,” because he loves you and your house, because he fares well with you,

17 then you shall take the awl, and thrust it through his ear and into the door, and he shall be your slave forever. And also to your female slave you shall do likewise.

18 It shall not seem hard in your eyes, when you let him go free from you, for at double the hire of a hired man he has served you six years. And Yahweh your God will bless you in all that you do.

19 Every firstborn male that is born of your herd and of your flock you shall sanctify to Yahweh your God. You shall do no work with the firstborn of your ox, nor shear the firstborn of your flock.

20 Before Yahweh your God you shall eat it year by year in the place which Yahweh will choose, you and your household.

21 And if there is a defect in it—is lame or blind, or any bad defect—you shall not sacrifice it to Yahweh your God.

22 Within your gates you shall eat it. The unclean and the clean alike shall eat it, as the gazelle and as the deer.

23 Only its blood you shall not eat. You shall pour it out on the ground like water.

Deuteronomy 16

1 Keep the month of Abib, and keep the Passover to Yahweh your God; for in the month of Abib Yahweh your God brought you out of Egypt by night.

2 And you shall sacrifice the Passover to Yahweh your God, from the flock and the herd, in the place which Yahweh will choose to cause his name to dwell there.

3 You shall eat no leavened bread with it. Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread with it, the bread of affliction (for you came out of the land of Egypt in haste), that you may remember the day when you came out of the land of Egypt all the days of your life.

4 And no leaven shall be seen with you in all your borders seven days; neither shall any of the flesh, which you sacrifice the first day at evening, remain all night until the morning.

5 You may not sacrifice the Passover within any of your gates which Yahweh your God gives you;

6 but at the place which Yahweh your God will choose to cause his name to dwell there, there you shall sacrifice the Passover at evening, at the going down of the sun, at the appointed time of your coming out of Egypt.

7 And you shall cook and eat it in the place which Yahweh your God will choose. In the morning you shall turn, and go to your tents.

8 Six days you shall eat unleavened bread. On the seventh day shall be a solemn assembly to Yahweh your God. You shall do no work.

9 You shall count for yourself seven weeks. From the time you begin to put the sickle to the standing grain you shall begin to count seven weeks.

10 And you shall keep the feast of weeks to Yahweh your God after the measure of the freewill offering of your hand, which you shall give, according as Yahweh your God blesses you.

11 And you shall rejoice before Yahweh your God: you, and your son, and your daughter, and your slave, and your female slave, and the Levite who is within your gates, and the sojourner, and the fatherless, and the widow who are among you, in the place which Yahweh your God will choose to cause his name to dwell there.

12 And you shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt. And you shall keep and do these statutes.

13 You shall keep the feast of booths seven days, when you have gathered in from your threshing floor and from your wine press.

14 And you shall rejoice in your feast: you, and your son, and your daughter, and your slave, and your female slave, and the Levite, and the sojourner, and the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates.

15 Seven days you shall keep a feast to Yahweh your God in the place which Yahweh will choose, because Yahweh your God will bless you in all your increase and in all the work of your hands, and you shall be altogether joyful.

16 Three times in a year all your males shall appear before Yahweh your God in the place which he will choose: in the feast of unleavened bread, and in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of booths. And they shall not appear before Yahweh empty.

17 Every man shall give according to the gift of his hand, according to the blessing of Yahweh your God which he has given you.

18 Judges and officers shall you appoint for yourself in all your gates, which Yahweh your God gives you, according to your tribes; and they shall judge the people with an ordinance of righteousness.

19 You shall not turn aside an ordinance. You shall not show partiality. You shall not take a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and perverts the words of the righteous.

20 Justice, justice shall you follow, that you may live and inherit the land which Yahweh your God gives you.

21 You shall not plant for yourself an Asherah — any tree — beside the altar of Yahweh your God, which you will make for yourself.

22 Neither shall you set up for yourself a standing stone, which Yahweh your God hates.

Deuteronomy 17

1 You shall not sacrifice to Yahweh your God an ox or a sheep in which there is a defect, any evil thing; for that is a detestable thing to Yahweh your God.

2 If there is found among you, within any of your gates which Yahweh your God gives you, a man or a woman who does that which is evil in the eyes of Yahweh your God, in transgressing his covenant,

3 and has gone and served other gods, and bowed down to them, or to the sun, or to the moon, or to any of the host of heaven, which I have not commanded,

4 and it is told to you, and you hear it, then you shall inquire diligently. And see, if it is true, and the thing is certain, that such a detestable thing is done in Israel,

5 then you shall bring out that man or that woman who has done this evil thing to your gates, that man or that woman; and you shall stone them with stones, and they shall die.

6 By the testimony of two witnesses, or three witnesses, he who is to die shall be put to death. By the testimony of one witness he shall not be put to death.

7 The hand of the witnesses shall be first on him to put him to death, and afterwards the hand of all the people. So you shall remove the evil from among you.

8 If a matter is too difficult for you for judgment, between blood and blood, between plea and plea, and between stroke and stroke, matters of controversy within your gates, then you shall arise, and go up to the place which Yahweh your God will choose.

9 And you shall come to the priests, the Levites, and to the judge who will be in those days. And you shall inquire, and they shall declare to you the sentence of the ordinance.

10 And you shall do according to the word which they declare to you from that place which Yahweh will choose. And you shall keep to do according to all that they teach you.

11 According to the teaching which they teach you, and according to the ordinance which they tell you, you shall do. You shall not turn aside from the word which they declare to you, to the right hand or to the left.

12 And the man who acts presumptuously, in not listening to the priest who stands to minister there before Yahweh your God, or to the judge, that man shall die. And you shall remove the evil from Israel.

13 And all the people shall hear, and fear, and shall do no more presumptuously.

14 When you come to the land which Yahweh your God gives you, and possess it and dwell in it, and say, “I will set a king over me, like all the nations that are around me,”

15 you shall surely set a king over you, whom Yahweh your God will choose. From among your brothers you shall set a king over you. You may not put a foreigner over you, who is not your brother.

16 Only he shall not multiply horses for himself, nor cause the people to return to Egypt, in order to multiply horses; for Yahweh has said to you, “You shall not go back that way again.”

17 And he shall not multiply wives for himself, that his heart does not turn away. And silver and gold he shall not greatly multiply for himself.

18 And it shall be, when he sits on the throne of his kingdom, that he shall write for himself a copy of this teaching in a book, from that which is before the priests, the Levites.

19 And it shall be with him, and he shall read in it all the days of his life, that he may learn to fear Yahweh his God, to keep all the words of this teaching and these statutes, to do them;

20 that his heart is not lifted up above his brothers, and that he does not turn aside from the commandment to the right hand or to the left, in order that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he and his children, among Israel.

Deuteronomy 18

6 And if a Levite comes from any of your gates out of all Israel, where he sojourns, and comes with all the desire of his soul to the place which Yahweh will choose,

Deuteronomy 19

1 When Yahweh your God cuts off the nations whose land Yahweh your God gives you, and you dispossess them and dwell in their cities and in their houses,

2 you shall set apart three cities for yourself in the midst of your land, which Yahweh your God gives you to possess.

3 You shall prepare the road for yourself, and you shall divide into three parts the territory of your land which Yahweh your God causes you to inherit, and it shall be for every slayer to flee there.

4 And this is the case of the slayer who shall flee there and live: whoever strikes his neighbour without knowledge, and he was not hating him in time past —

5 as when a man goes with his neighbour into the forest to cut wood, and his hand swings the axe to cut down the tree, and the iron slips from the wood and finds his neighbour and he dies — he shall flee to one of these cities and live;

6 lest the avenger of blood pursue after the slayer while his heart is hot, and overtake him because the road is long, and strike him mortally, though there is no judgment of death for him, because he was not hating him in time past.

7 Therefore I command you, saying, “You shall set apart three cities for yourself.”

8 And if Yahweh your God enlarges your territory, as he swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land which he spoke of giving to your fathers,

9 if you keep all this commandment to do it, which I command you today, to love Yahweh your God and to walk in his ways all the days, then you shall add for yourself three more cities besides these three,

10 so that innocent blood shall not be shed in the midst of your land which Yahweh your God gives you as an inheritance, and blood be upon you.

11 But if a man hates his neighbour, and lies in wait for him, and rises against him, and strikes him so that he dies, and flees to one of these cities,

12 then the elders of his city shall send and take him from there, and give him into the hand of the avenger of blood, and he shall die.

13 Your eye shall not pity him, and you shall purge the blood of the innocent from Israel, and it shall go well with you.

14 You shall not move your neighbour’s boundary mark, which the first ones set up, in your inheritance which you shall inherit in the land which Yahweh your God gives you to possess.

15 One witness shall not rise against a man for any iniquity or for any sin, in any sin that he sins. By the mouth of two witnesses, or by the mouth of three witnesses, a matter shall be established.

16 If a witness of violence rises against a man, to testify against him of a turning aside,

17 then the two men who have the dispute shall stand before Yahweh, before the priests and the judges who shall be in those days,

18 and the judges shall inquire well, and behold, if the witness is a false witness — he has testified falsely against his brother —

19 then you shall do to him as he plotted to do to his brother, and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

20 And those who remain shall hear and fear, and shall not again do any more such an evil thing as this in your midst.

21 And your eye shall not pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

Deuteronomy 20

1 When you go out to war against your enemies, and you see horse and chariot, a people more numerous than you, you shall not fear them, for Yahweh your God is with you, who brought you up from the land of Egypt.

2 And it shall be, when you draw near to the battle, the priest shall approach and speak to the people,

3 and say to them, “Hear, Israel: you draw near today to battle against your enemies. Let your heart not be soft. Do not fear, and do not hurry in alarm, and do not be terrified before them,

4 for Yahweh your God is the one who goes with you, to fight for you against your enemies, to save you.”

5 And the officers shall speak to the people, saying, “What man is there who has built a new house and has not dedicated it? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man dedicate it.

6 And what man is there who has planted a vineyard and has not begun to use it? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man begin to use it.

7 And what man is there who has betrothed a wife and has not taken her? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man take her.”

8 And the officers shall speak further to the people, and say, “What man is there who is fearful and soft of heart? Let him go and return to his house, so that he does not melt the heart of his brothers like his own heart.”

9 And it shall be, when the officers have finished speaking to the people, they shall appoint commanders of armies at the head of the people.

10 When you draw near to a city to fight against it, you shall call to it for peace.

11 And it shall be, if it answers you peace and opens to you, then all the people who are found in it shall be yours for forced labour, and they shall serve you.

12 And if it does not make peace with you, and makes war with you, then you shall besiege it.

13 And Yahweh your God shall give it into your hand, and you shall strike every male of it by the edge of the sword.

14 Only the women, and the little ones, and the livestock, and all that is in the city, all its spoil, you shall take as spoil for yourself, and you shall eat the spoil of your enemies, which Yahweh your God has given you.

15 So you shall do to all the cities that are very far from you, which are not of the cities of these nations.

16 Only from the cities of these peoples, which Yahweh your God gives you as an inheritance, you shall not let live anything that breathes,

17 for you shall surely devote them to destruction: the Hittite and the Amorite, the Canaanite and the Perizzite, the Hivite and the Jebusite, as Yahweh your God commanded you,

18 so that they do not teach you to do according to all their detestable things, which they have done for their gods, and you sin against Yahweh your God.

19 When you besiege a city many days, to fight against it, to seize it, you shall not destroy its trees by swinging an axe against them, for from it you eat, and it you shall not cut down — for is the tree of the field a man, that it should come before you in the siege?

20 Only a tree that you know is not a tree for food, that one you shall destroy and cut down, and you shall build siege works against the city that makes war with you, until it falls.

Deuteronomy 21

1 If a slain man is found in the land which Yahweh your God gives you to possess, fallen in the field, and it is not known who struck him,

2 then your elders and your judges shall go out, and they shall measure to the cities that are around the slain man.

3 And it shall be, the city nearest to the slain man — the elders of that city shall take a heifer of the herd, which has not been worked with, which has not drawn in a yoke,

4 and the elders of that city shall bring the heifer down to a valley that flows continually, which is not worked and not sown, and they shall break the heifer’s neck there in the valley.

5 And the priests, the sons of Levi, shall come near, for Yahweh your God has chosen them to serve him and to bless in the name of Yahweh, and by their mouth shall be every dispute and every stroke.

6 And all the elders of that city, those nearest to the slain man, shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley,

7 and they shall answer and say, “Our hands have not shed this blood, and our eyes have not seen it.

8 Make atonement, Yahweh, for your people Israel, whom you redeemed, and do not put innocent blood in the midst of your people Israel.” And the blood shall be atoned for them.

9 And you shall purge the innocent blood from your midst, when you do what is right in the eyes of Yahweh.

10 When you go out to war against your enemies, and Yahweh your God gives him into your hand, and you take his captives,

11 and you see among the captives a woman beautiful in form, and you desire her, and you take her for yourself as a wife,

12 then you shall bring her into the midst of your house, and she shall shave her head and do her nails,

13 and she shall remove the garment of her captivity from upon her, and she shall dwell in your house, and weep for her father and her mother a month of days, and after that you shall come in to her and be her husband, and she shall be your wife.

14 And it shall be, if you do not delight in her, you shall let her go where she desires, and you shall surely not sell her for money; you shall not treat her as a slave, because you have humbled her.

15 If a man has two wives, one loved and one hated, and they bear him sons, the loved and the hated, and the firstborn son is the hated one’s,

16 then it shall be, on the day he causes his sons to inherit what is his, he shall not be able to treat as firstborn the son of the loved one in preference to the son of the hated one, the firstborn.

17 For he shall acknowledge the firstborn, the son of the hated one, by giving him a double portion of all that is found to be his, for he is the first of his strength; the ordinance of the firstborn is his.

18 If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son, who does not listen to the voice of his father or to the voice of his mother, and they discipline him and he does not listen to them,

19 then his father and his mother shall seize him and bring him out to the elders of his city and to the gate of his place,

20 and they shall say to the elders of his city, “This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious; he does not listen to our voice; he is a glutton and a drunkard.”

21 And all the men of his city shall stone him with stones, and he shall die, and you shall purge the evil from your midst, and all Israel shall hear and fear.

22 And if a man has a sin worthy of death, and he is put to death, and you hang him on a tree,

23 his corpse shall not stay the night on the tree, for you shall surely bury him on that day — for a hanged man is a curse of God — and you shall not defile your land, which Yahweh your God gives you as an inheritance.

Deuteronomy 22

1 You shall not see your brother’s ox or his sheep straying and hide yourself from them; you shall surely bring them back to your brother.

2 And if your brother is not near to you, and you do not know him, then you shall gather it into the midst of your house, and it shall be with you until your brother seeks it, and you shall return it to him.

3 And so you shall do for his donkey, and so you shall do for his garment, and so you shall do for every lost thing of your brother’s, which is lost from him and you find it; you may not hide yourself.

4 You shall not see your brother’s donkey or his ox fallen on the road and hide yourself from them; you shall surely help him raise them up.

5 A man’s article shall not be on a woman, and a man shall not wear a woman’s garment, for everyone who does these things is a detestable thing to Yahweh your God.

6 If a bird’s nest happens before you on the road, in any tree or on the ground, with young ones or eggs, and the mother is crouching over the young ones or over the eggs, you shall not take the mother with the children.

7 You shall surely send the mother away, and the children you may take for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may lengthen your days.

8 When you build a new house, you shall make a parapet for your roof, and you shall not put blood on your house if any man falls from it.

9 You shall not sow your vineyard with two kinds, lest the full yield be set apart as holy — the seed which you sow and the produce of the vineyard.

10 You shall not plough with an ox and with a donkey together.

11 You shall not wear a mixed cloth, wool and linen together.

12 You shall make tassels for yourself on the four corners of your covering, with which you cover yourself.

13 If a man takes a wife, and comes in to her, and hates her,

14 and lays against her charges of things, and brings out a bad name against her, and says, “I took this woman, and I came near to her, and I did not find virginity in her,”

15 then the father of the young woman and her mother shall take and bring out the signs of the young woman’s virginity to the elders of the city, at the gate.

16 And the father of the young woman shall say to the elders, “I gave my daughter to this man as a wife, and he hates her.

17 And behold, he has laid charges of things, saying, ‘I did not find virginity in your daughter,’ and these are the signs of my daughter’s virginity.” And they shall spread the garment before the elders of the city.

18 And the elders of that city shall take the man and discipline him,

19 and they shall fine him a hundred shekels of silver, and give them to the father of the young woman, because he brought out a bad name against a virgin of Israel. And she shall be his wife; he shall not be able to send her away all his days.

20 But if this matter is true — virginity was not found in the young woman —

21 then they shall bring out the young woman to the entrance of her father’s house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones and she shall die, because she has done a disgraceful thing in Israel, to play the prostitute in her father’s house, and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

22 If a man is found lying with a woman who is married to a husband, then both of them shall die, the man who lay with the woman and the woman, and you shall purge the evil from Israel.

25 But if the man finds the betrothed young woman in the field, and the man seizes her and lies with her, then the man who lay with her alone shall die.

27 For he found her in the field; the betrothed young woman cried out, and there was no one to save her.

28 If a man finds a young woman, a virgin, who is not betrothed, and seizes her and lies with her, and they are found,

Deuteronomy 23

1 One whose testicles are crushed, or whose male organ is cut off, shall not enter into the assembly of Yahweh.

2 A mamzer shall not enter into the assembly of Yahweh; even to the tenth generation, none of his shall enter into the assembly of Yahweh.

3 An Ammonite or a Moabite shall not enter into the assembly of Yahweh; even to the tenth generation, none of theirs shall enter into the assembly of Yahweh, forever,

4 because they did not meet you with bread and with water on the road, when you came out of Egypt, and because Moab hired against you Balaam the son of Beor from Pethor of Aram Naharaim, to curse you.

5 And Yahweh your God was not willing to listen to Balaam, and Yahweh your God turned for you the curse into a blessing, because Yahweh your God loved you.

6 You shall not seek their peace or their good all your days, forever.

7 You shall not detest an Edomite, for he is your brother. You shall not detest an Egyptian, for you were a foreigner in his land.

8 Children who are born to them, the third generation, may enter into the assembly of Yahweh.

9 When you go out as a camp against your enemies, you shall keep yourself from every evil thing.

10 If there is among you a man who is not clean because of a happening of the night, he shall go out to outside the camp; he shall not come into the midst of the camp.

11 And it shall be, toward the turning of evening, he shall wash in water, and when the sun comes in, he shall come into the midst of the camp.

12 And you shall have a place outside the camp, and you shall go out there, outside.

13 And you shall have a peg among your equipment, and it shall be, when you sit down outside, you shall dig with it, and you shall turn back and cover your excrement.

14 For Yahweh your God walks in the midst of your camp, to deliver you and to give your enemies before you, and your camp shall be holy, and he shall not see in you the nakedness of a thing, and turn away from you.

15 You shall not hand over a slave to his master, who escapes to you from his master.

16 He shall dwell with you, in your midst, in the place that he chooses in one of your gates, where it is good for him; you shall not oppress him.

17 There shall be no cult prostitute from the daughters of Israel, and there shall be no cult prostitute from the sons of Israel.

18 You shall not bring the hire of a prostitute, or the price of a dog, into the house of Yahweh your God for any vow, for both of these also are a detestable thing to Yahweh your God.

19 You shall not charge your brother interest: interest on money, interest on food, interest on any thing that is lent at interest.

20 To the foreigner you may charge interest, but to your brother you shall not charge interest, so that Yahweh your God may bless you in every undertaking of your hand, in the land to which you are coming, there, to possess it.

21 When you vow a vow to Yahweh your God, you shall not delay to pay it, for Yahweh your God shall surely require it from you, and it shall be sin in you.

22 And if you refrain from vowing, it shall not be sin in you.

23 What comes out of your lips you shall keep and do, as you vowed to Yahweh your God — a freewill offering — which you spoke with your mouth.

24 When you come into your neighbour’s vineyard, you may eat grapes according to your soul, to your satisfaction, but you shall not put any into your vessel.

25 When you come into your neighbour’s standing grain, you may pluck ears with your hand, but you shall not swing a sickle over your neighbour’s standing grain.

Deuteronomy 24

1 When a man takes a wife and marries her, and it shall be, if she does not find favour in his eyes, because he has found in her the nakedness of a thing, then he shall write her a document of cutting off, and give it in her hand, and send her from his house.

2 And she shall go out from his house, and go, and be another man’s.

3 And if the latter man hates her, and writes her a document of cutting off, and gives it in her hand, and sends her from his house — or if the latter man dies, who took her for himself as a wife —

4 her first husband, who sent her away, shall not be able to return and take her to be his wife, after she has been defiled, for that is a detestable thing before Yahweh, and you shall not cause the land to sin, which Yahweh your God gives you as an inheritance.

5 When a man takes a new wife, he shall not go out in the army, and nothing shall be laid upon him for any matter; he shall be free for his house one year, and he shall make his wife, whom he took, glad.

6 No one shall take in pledge a mill or an upper millstone, for he takes a life in pledge.

7 If a man is found stealing a life from his brothers, from the children of Israel, and he treats him as a slave and sells him, then that thief shall die, and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

8 Take care in the plague of scale disease, to observe very diligently and to do according to all that the priests, the Levites, teach you; as I commanded them, you shall keep to do.

9 Remember what Yahweh your God did to Miriam on the road, when you came out of Egypt.

10 When you lend your neighbour any kind of loan, you shall not come into his house to take his pledge.

11 You shall stand outside, and the man to whom you lend shall bring the pledge out to you, outside.

12 And if he is a poor man, you shall not lie down with his pledge.

13 You shall surely return the pledge to him when the sun comes in, and he shall lie down in his garment and bless you, and it shall be righteousness for you before Yahweh your God.

14 You shall not oppress a hired worker who is poor and needy, from your brothers or from your foreigner who is in your land, in your gates.

15 On his day you shall give his wage, and the sun shall not come in upon it, for he is poor, and he sets his heart on it, and he shall not cry against you to Yahweh, and it be sin in you.

16 Fathers shall not be put to death for children, and children shall not be put to death for fathers; a man shall be put to death for his own sin.

17 You shall not turn aside the ordinance of a foreigner, of a fatherless one, and you shall not take in pledge a widow’s garment.

18 And you shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and Yahweh your God redeemed you from there; therefore I command you to do this thing.

19 When you reap your harvest in your field, and you forget a sheaf in the field, you shall not return to take it; it shall be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow, so that Yahweh your God may bless you in all the work of your hands.

20 When you beat your olive tree, you shall not go over the boughs again; it shall be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow.

21 When you gather the grapes of your vineyard, you shall not glean after you; it shall be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow.

22 And you shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt; therefore I command you to do this thing.

Deuteronomy 25

1 When there is a dispute between men, and they come near to judgment, and they judge them, they shall justify the righteous and condemn the wicked.

2 And if the wicked man is deserving of beating, the judge shall make him lie down and be beaten before his face, according to his wickedness, by number.

3 Forty stripes he may give him; he shall not add more, lest he add to beat him beyond these with a great beating, and your brother be degraded in your eyes.

4 You shall not muzzle an ox in its threshing.

5 When brothers dwell together, and one of them dies and has no son, the wife of the dead man shall not be given outside to a stranger. Her husband's brother shall go in to her, and take her for himself as a wife, and perform the duty of a husband's brother to her.

6 And the firstborn whom she bears shall stand in the name of his dead brother, so that his name shall not be blotted out from Israel.

7 And if the man does not desire to take his brother's wife, his brother's wife shall go up to the gate, to the elders, and say, “My husband's brother refuses to raise up a name for his brother in Israel; he will not perform the duty of a husband's brother to me.”

8 And the elders of his city shall call him and speak to him. And if he stands and says, “I do not desire to take her,”

9 then his brother's wife shall come near to him in the eyes of the elders, and remove his sandal from his foot, and spit in his face, and answer and say, “So shall it be done to the man who does not build his brother's house.”

10 And his name shall be called in Israel, “The house of the one whose sandal was removed.”

11 When men fight together, a man and his brother, and the wife of one comes near to rescue her husband from the hand of the one striking him, and puts out her hand and seizes him by his private parts,

12 you shall cut off her hand; your eye shall not pity.

13 You shall not have in your bag a stone and a stone, a great and a small.

14 You shall not have in your house an ephah and an ephah, a great and a small.

15 A complete and just stone you shall have; a complete and just ephah you shall have, so that your days may be long on the ground that Yahweh your God is giving you.

16 For everyone who does these things, everyone who does injustice, is an abomination to Yahweh your God.

17 Remember what Amalek did to you on the way, when you came out of Egypt,

18 how he met you on the way and cut down behind you all who were feeble behind you, when you were faint and weary; and he did not fear God.

19 And when Yahweh your God gives you rest from all your enemies all around, in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance to possess it, you shall blot out the memory of Amalek from under the heavens. You shall not forget.

18 lest there be among you a man or a woman, or a family or a tribe, whose heart turns away today from Yahweh our God, to go to serve the gods of those nations; lest there be among you a root bearing gall and wormwood,

19 and when he hears the words of this oath-curse, he blesses himself in his heart, saying, “Peace shall be mine, though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart,” so as to sweep away the watered with the thirsty.

20 Yahweh will not be willing to forgive him, for then the anger of Yahweh and his jealousy will smoke against that man, and all the oath-curse written in this book will lie on him, and Yahweh will blot out his name from under the heavens.

21 And Yahweh will separate him for evil from all the tribes of Israel, according to all the oath-curses of the covenant written in this book of the law.

22 And the later generation, your children who will rise after you, and the foreigner who will come from a far land, shall say — and they will see the plagues of that land and its sicknesses with which Yahweh has made it sick —

23 “Sulphur and salt, a burning, is all its land; it is not sown, and it does not sprout, and no herb rises in it, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which Yahweh overthrew in his anger and in his wrath.”

24 And all the nations shall say, “For what has Yahweh done thus to this land? What is the heat of this great anger?”

25 And they shall say, “Because they forsook the covenant of Yahweh, the God of their fathers, which he cut with them when he brought them out of the land of Egypt,

26 and they went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods that they did not know and that he had not allotted to them.

27 And the anger of Yahweh burned against that land, to bring on it all the curse written in this book.

28 And Yahweh uprooted them from on their ground in anger, in wrath, and in great fury, and cast them into another land, as this day.”

29 The hidden things belong to Yahweh our God, and the revealed things belong to us and to our children forever, to do all the words of this law.

Deuteronomy 30

1 And it shall happen, when all these things come upon you, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before you, and you take them back to your heart among all the nations where Yahweh your God has driven you,

2 and you return to Yahweh your God and listen to his voice, according to all that I command you today, you and your children, with all your heart and with all your soul,

3 then Yahweh your God shall restore your fortunes and have compassion on you, and he shall return and gather you from all the peoples where Yahweh your God has scattered you.

4 If your outcast is at the end of the heavens, from there Yahweh your God shall gather you, and from there he shall take you.

5 And Yahweh your God shall bring you to the land which your fathers possessed, and you shall possess it, and he shall do good to you and multiply you more than your fathers.

6 And Yahweh your God shall circumcise your heart and the heart of your seed, to love Yahweh your God with all your heart and with all your soul, so that you may live.

7 And Yahweh your God shall put all these curses on your enemies and on those who hate you, who pursued you.

8 And you shall return and listen to the voice of Yahweh, and do all his commandments which I command you today.

9 And Yahweh your God shall make you abundant in all the work of your hand, in the fruit of your body, and in the fruit of your livestock, and in the fruit of your ground, for good, for Yahweh shall again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your fathers,

10 if you listen to the voice of Yahweh your God, to keep his commandments and his statutes which are written in this book of the Torah, if you return to Yahweh your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

11 For this commandment which I command you today is not too wonderful for you, and it is not far away.

12 It is not in heaven, so that you should say, “Who shall go up for us to heaven, and take it for us, and make us hear it, that we may do it?”

13 And it is not beyond the sea, so that you should say, “Who shall cross over for us to the other side of the sea, and take it for us, and make us hear it, that we may do it?”

14 For the word is very near to you, in your mouth and in your heart, to do it.

15 See, I have set before you today life and good, and death and evil,

16 in that I command you today to love Yahweh your God, to walk in his ways, and to keep his commandments and his statutes and his ordinances, and you shall live and multiply, and Yahweh your God shall bless you in the land to which you are going there to possess it.

17 But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are drawn away and bow down to other gods and serve them,

18 I declare to you today that you shall surely perish. You shall not prolong days on the ground to which you are crossing the Jordan to go in to possess it.

19 I call heaven and earth to witness against you today: life and death I have set before you, the blessing and the curse. Choose life, so that you may live, you and your seed,

20 to love Yahweh your God, to listen to his voice, and to cling to him, for he is your life and the length of your days, to dwell on the ground which Yahweh swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them.

Deuteronomy 31

1 And Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel.

2 And he said to them, “I am a hundred and twenty years old today. I can no longer go out and come in, and Yahweh has said to me, ‘You shall not cross this Jordan.’

3 Yahweh your God, he is crossing over before you. He shall destroy these nations from before you, and you shall dispossess them. Joshua, he is crossing over before you, as Yahweh has spoken.

4 And Yahweh shall do to them as he did to Sihon and to Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land, whom he destroyed.

5 And Yahweh shall give them before you, and you shall do to them according to all the commandment which I have commanded you.

6 Be strong and courageous. Do not fear and do not be terrified before them, for Yahweh your God, he is the one who goes with you. He shall not abandon you and he shall not forsake you.”

7 And Moses called to Joshua and said to him in the eyes of all Israel, “Be strong and courageous, for you shall come with this people to the land which Yahweh swore to their fathers to give to them, and you shall cause them to inherit it.

8 And Yahweh, he is the one who goes before you. He shall be with you. He shall not abandon you and he shall not forsake you. Do not fear and do not be dismayed.”

9 And Moses wrote this Torah and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carry the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, and to all the elders of Israel.

10 And Moses commanded them, saying, “At the end of seven years, at the appointed time of the year of release, at the Festival of Booths,

11 when all Israel comes to appear before Yahweh your God in the place which he shall choose, you shall read this Torah before all Israel in their ears.

12 Assemble the people, the men and the women and the little ones, and your stranger who is in your gates, so that they may hear and so that they may learn and fear Yahweh your God and keep them, to do all the words of this Torah,

13 and their children, who have not known, shall hear and learn to fear Yahweh your God all the days which you live on the ground which you are crossing the Jordan there to possess it.”

14 And Yahweh said to Moses, “See, your days have drawn near to die. Call Joshua, and stand in the Tent of Meeting, and I shall commission him.” And Moses and Joshua went and stood in the Tent of Meeting.

15 And Yahweh appeared in the Tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood at the entrance of the Tent.

16 And Yahweh said to Moses, “See, you are about to sleep with your fathers, and this people shall rise and go astray after the foreign gods of the land to which it is going there, in its midst, and it shall forsake me and break my covenant which I have made with it.

17 And my anger shall burn against it in that day, and I shall forsake them and hide my face from them, and it shall be for devouring, and many evils and troubles shall find it, and it shall say in that day, ‘Is it not because my God is not in my midst that these evils have found me?’

18 And I shall surely hide my face in that day, on account of all the evil which it has done, for it has turned to other gods.

19 “And now, write for yourselves this song and teach it to the children of Israel. Put it in their mouth, so that this song may be for me a witness against the children of Israel.

20 For I shall bring it to the ground which I swore to its fathers, flowing with milk and honey, and it shall eat and be full and grow fat, and turn to other gods and serve them, and spurn me and break my covenant.

21 And it shall happen, when many evils and troubles find it, that this song shall answer before it as a witness, for it shall not be forgotten from the mouth of its seed, for I know its inclination — what it is doing even today — before I bring it to the land which I swore.”

22 And Moses wrote this song on that day, and taught it to the children of Israel.

23 And he commissioned Joshua son of Nun and said, “Be strong and courageous, for you shall bring the children of Israel to the land which I swore to them, and I shall be with you.”

24 And it happened, when Moses finished writing the words of this Torah on a book, until they were complete,

25 that Moses commanded the Levites who carry the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, saying,

26 “Take this book of the Torah and put it beside the ark of the covenant of Yahweh your God, and it shall be there against you as a witness.

27 For I know your rebellion and your stiff neck. See, while I am still alive with you today, you have been rebellious against Yahweh — and how much more after my death!

28 Assemble to me all the elders of your tribes and your officers, and I shall speak these words in their ears, and I shall call heaven and earth to witness against them.

29 For I know that after my death you shall surely act corruptly and turn from the way which I have commanded you, and evil shall meet you in the latter days, for you shall do what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh, to provoke him by the work of your hands.”

30 And Moses spoke in the ears of all the assembly of Israel the words of this song, until they were complete.

Deuteronomy 32

1 Give ear, heavens, and I shall speak, and let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

2 My teaching shall drop like the rain, my speech shall flow like the dew, like showers on young grass and like downpours on the herb.

3 For I shall proclaim the name of Yahweh. Give greatness to our God.

4 The Rock, his work is without defect, for all his ways are ordinance. A God of faithfulness and no wrong, righteous and upright is he.

5 They have acted corruptly toward him; they are not his children — their defect. A crooked and twisted generation.

6 Is this how you repay Yahweh, foolish people and not wise? Is he not your father who made you his own? He made you and established you.

7 Remember the days of old, consider the years of generation after generation. Ask your father and he shall tell you, your elders and they shall say to you.

8 When the Most High gave the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of man, he set the borders of the peoples according to the number of the children of Israel.

9 For Yahweh's portion is his people, Jacob the measure of his inheritance.

10 He found him in a desert land, and in a waste, a howling wilderness. He surrounded him, he gave him understanding, he guarded him like the pupil of his eye.

11 Like an eagle that stirs up its nest, that hovers over its young, he spread his wings, he took him, he carried him on his pinions.

12 Yahweh alone led him, and there was no foreign god with him.

13 He made him ride on the high places of the land, and he ate the produce of the field, and he made him suck honey from a rock and oil from a flinty rock,

14 curds of the herd and milk of the flock, with fat of lambs and rams, sons of Bashan, and he-goats, with the kidney-fat of wheat, and the blood of the grape you drank as foaming wine.

15 And Jeshurun grew fat and kicked — you grew fat, you grew thick, you grew sleek — and he forsook God who made him, and treated as foolish the Rock of his salvation.

16 They made him jealous with strange gods, with abominations they provoked him.

17 They sacrificed to demons, not God, gods they had not known, new ones that came recently, which your fathers did not dread.

18 The Rock who bore you, you neglected, and you forgot God who brought you forth in labour.

19 And Yahweh saw and spurned, from the provocation of his sons and his daughters.

20 And he said, “I shall hide my face from them, I shall see what their end is, for they are a generation of reversals, children in whom there is no faithfulness.

21 They made me jealous with a no-god, they provoked me with their empty things, and I shall make them jealous with a no-people, with a foolish nation I shall provoke them.

22 For a fire has burned in my anger and shall burn to the lowest Sheol, and shall devour the land and its produce, and set ablaze the foundations of the mountains.

23 “I shall heap evils upon them, I shall spend my arrows on them.

24 Wasted by hunger and devoured by burning heat and bitter destruction, and the teeth of beasts I shall send against them, with the venom of crawlers in the dust.

25 Outside, the sword shall bereave, and in the inner rooms, terror — both young man and virgin, the nursing child with the man of grey hair.

26 I said, I shall scatter them, I shall make their memory cease from mankind,

27 were it not that I feared the provocation of the enemy, lest their adversaries misjudge, lest they say, ‘Our hand is high, and Yahweh has not done all this.’”

28 For they are a nation that has lost counsel, and there is no understanding in them.

29 If they were wise, they would understand this, they would discern their end.

30 How shall one pursue a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock has sold them and Yahweh has handed them over?

31 For their rock is not like our Rock, and our enemies are judges.

32 For their vine is from the vine of Sodom and from the fields of Gomorrah. Their grapes are grapes of poison, their clusters are bitter to them.

33 Their wine is the venom of serpents, and the cruel poison of asps.

34 “Is it not stored up with me, sealed in my treasuries?

35 Vengeance is mine, and repayment, at the time when their foot slips, for the day of their calamity is near, and the things to come hurry upon them.”

36 For Yahweh shall judge his people, and on his slaves he shall relent, when he sees that strength has gone, and there is none left, restrained or released.

37 And he shall say, “Where are their gods, the rock in which they took refuge,

38 who ate the fat of their sacrifices, drank the wine of their drink offering? Let them rise and help you, let them be a hiding place over you.

39 “See now that I, I am he, and there is no god beside me. I put to death and I make alive, I have wounded and I shall heal, and there is no one who delivers from my hand.

40 For I lift my hand to heaven and say, ‘As I live forever,

41 if I sharpen the lightning of my sword and my hand takes hold of ordinance, I shall return vengeance to my adversaries, and to those who hate me I shall repay.

42 I shall make my arrows drunk with blood, and my sword shall devour flesh, from the blood of slain and captive, from the head of the long-haired leaders of the enemy.’”

43 Sing aloud, nations, for his people, for he shall avenge the blood of his slaves, and he shall return vengeance to his adversaries, and he shall make atonement for his land, his people.

44 And Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the ears of the people, he and Hoshea son of Nun.

45 And Moses finished speaking all these words to all Israel.

46 And he said to them, “Set your heart to all the words which I testify against you today, which you shall command your children to keep, to do all the words of this Torah.

47 For it is not an empty word for you, for it is your life, and by this word you shall prolong days on the ground which you are crossing the Jordan there to possess it.”

48 And Yahweh spoke to Moses on this very day, saying,

49 “Go up to this mountain of Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, which is opposite Jericho, and see the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the children of Israel as a possession.

50 And die on the mountain to which you are going up there, and be gathered to your people, as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people,

51 because you broke faith with me in the midst of the children of Israel at the waters of Meribah of Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin, because you did not treat me as holy in the midst of the children of Israel.

52 For from opposite you shall see the land, but there you shall not go, to the land which I am giving to the children of Israel.”

Deuteronomy 33

1 And this is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the children of Israel before his death.

2 And he said, “Yahweh came from Sinai, and rose up from Seir to them, he shone forth from Mount Paran, and he came from the myriads of holy ones. From his right hand, a fiery law for them.

3 Indeed, he loves the peoples. All his holy ones are in your hand, and they sat down at your feet, receiving from your words.

4 Moses commanded us a Torah, a possession for the assembly of Jacob.

5 And there was a king in Jeshurun, when the heads of the people gathered, together the tribes of Israel.

6 “Let Reuben live and not die, and let his men be few.”

7 And this is for Judah, and he said, “Hear, Yahweh, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people. His hands contend for him, and you shall be a help against his adversaries.”

8 And of Levi he said, “Your Thummim and your Urim are with your faithful man, whom you tested at Massah, with whom you contended at the waters of Meribah,

9 who says to his father and to his mother, ‘I have not seen him,’ and his brothers he does not acknowledge, and his children he does not know, for they have kept your word and they guard your covenant.

10 They shall teach your ordinances to Jacob and your Torah to Israel. They shall put incense in your nostrils and a whole offering on your altar.

11 Bless, Yahweh, his strength, and accept the work of his hands. Strike the loins of those who rise against him and those who hate him, that they may not rise.”

12 Of Benjamin he said, “The beloved of Yahweh shall dwell in safety beside him. He hovers over him all the day, and between his shoulders he dwells.”

13 And of Joseph he said, “Blessed by Yahweh is his land, from the choicest things of heaven, from the dew, and from the deep crouching beneath,

14 and from the choicest produce of the sun, and from the choicest yield of the months,

15 and from the head of the ancient mountains, and from the choicest things of the everlasting hills,

16 and from the choicest things of the land and its fullness, and the favour of the one who dwelt in the bush. Let it come to the head of Joseph, and to the crown of the one set apart from his brothers.

17 His firstborn ox, majesty is his, and his horns are the horns of a wild ox. With them he shall gore peoples together to the ends of the land. And they are the myriads of Ephraim, and they are the thousands of Manasseh.”

18 And of Zebulun he said, “Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents.

19 They shall call peoples to the mountain. There they shall sacrifice sacrifices of righteousness, for they shall suck the abundance of the seas and the hidden hoards of the sand.”

20 And of Gad he said, “Blessed is the one who enlarges Gad. Like a lion he dwells, and tears arm, even the crown of the head.

21 And he chose the first part for himself, for there the portion of the lawgiver was reserved, and he came with the heads of the people. He did the righteousness of Yahweh, and his ordinances with Israel.”

22 And of Dan he said, “Dan is a lion's cub, he leaps from Bashan.”

23 And of Naphtali he said, “Naphtali, satisfied with favour and full of the blessing of Yahweh, possess sea and south.”

24 And of Asher he said, “Blessed above sons is Asher. Let him be the favoured one of his brothers, and dip his foot in oil.

25 Iron and bronze shall be your bars, and as your days, so shall your strength be.

26 “There is none like God, Jeshurun, riding the heavens to your help, and in his majesty the skies.

27 A dwelling place is the God of old, and underneath are the everlasting arms. And he drove out the enemy from before you, and said, ‘Destroy!’

28 And Israel dwelt in safety, alone, the spring of Jacob, in a land of grain and new wine. Indeed, his heavens drop dew.

29 Happy are you, Israel. Who is like you, a people saved by Yahweh, the shield of your help and the sword of your majesty? And your enemies shall cringe to you, and you shall tread on their high places.”

Deuteronomy 34

1 And Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, the head of Pisgah, which is opposite Jericho, and Yahweh showed him all the land, Gilead as far as Dan,

2 and all Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah as far as the hinder sea,

3 and the Negev, and the Plain, the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar.

4 And Yahweh said to him, “This is the land which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, saying, ‘To your seed I shall give it.’ I have made you see it with your eyes, but there you shall not cross over.”

5 And Moses, the slave of Yahweh, died there in the land of Moab, at the command of Yahweh.

6 And he buried him in the valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth Peor, and no man knows his burial place to this day.

7 And Moses was a hundred and twenty years old at his death. His eye had not dimmed, and his freshness had not fled.

8 And the children of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days, and the days of weeping, the mourning for Moses, were complete.

9 And Joshua son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands on him, and the children of Israel listened to him and did as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

10 And there has not arisen again a prophet in Israel like Moses, whom Yahweh knew face to face,

11 for all the signs and the wonders which Yahweh sent him to do in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh and to all his slaves and to all his land,

12 and for all the strong hand and for all the great terror which Moses did in the eyes of all Israel.
