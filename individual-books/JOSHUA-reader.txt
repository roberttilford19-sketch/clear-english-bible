Joshua 1

1 After Moses, the servant of Yahweh, had died, Yahweh said to Joshua son of Nun, who had attended Moses:

2 "Moses my servant is dead. So now get up and cross this Jordan, you and all this people, into the land I am giving them, to the children of Israel.

3 Every place where the sole of your foot steps, I have given it to you, just as I promised Moses.

4 From the wilderness and this Lebanon as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, and as far as the Great Sea where the sun goes down, that will be your territory.

5 No one will be able to stand against you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you; I will not let you go, and I will not abandon you.

6 Be strong and courageous, for you will give this people possession of the land that I swore to their fathers to give them.

7 Only be strong and very courageous, taking care to act according to all the Torah that Moses my servant commanded you. Do not turn aside from it to the right or to the left, so that you may succeed wherever you go.

8 This book of the Torah must not leave your mouth; you shall recite it quietly day and night, so that you take care to do all that is written in it. For then you will make your way prosper, and then you will succeed.

9 Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not tremble and do not be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go."

10 Then Joshua gave orders to the officers of the people:

11 "Go through the camp and order the people: Prepare provisions for yourselves, for in three days you will cross this Jordan, to enter and take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess."

12 And to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh, Joshua said:

13 "Remember what Moses, the servant of Yahweh, commanded you: Yahweh your God is giving you rest, and he will give you this land.

14 Your wives, your little ones, and your livestock will stay in the land Moses gave you beyond the Jordan. But you will cross ahead of your brothers, armed for battle, all you fighting men, and you will help them,

15 until Yahweh gives your brothers rest, as he has given you, and they too take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving them. Then you may return to the land of your possession and possess it, the land Moses, the servant of Yahweh, gave you beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise."

16 They answered Joshua: "All that you command us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.

17 Just as we obeyed Moses in everything, so we will obey you. Only may Yahweh your God be with you, as he was with Moses.

18 Anyone who defies your word and does not obey your commands in all that you order him shall be put to death. Only be strong and courageous."

Joshua 2

1 Joshua son of Nun sent two men secretly from Shittim as spies, saying, "Go and look over the land, and Jericho." They went and entered the house of a prostitute named Rahab, and spent the night there.

2 The king of Jericho was told: "Look, men from the children of Israel came here tonight to search out the land."

3 So the king of Jericho sent word to Rahab: "Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house, for they have come to search out the whole land."

4 But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. She said, "Yes, the men did come to me, but I did not know where they were from.

5 When the gate was about to be shut, at dark, the men went out. I do not know where they went. Chase after them quickly, and you will catch them."

6 In fact she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them among the stalks of flax that she had laid out on the roof.

7 The men pursued them on the road to the Jordan, as far as the fords, and the gate was shut as soon as the pursuers had gone out after them.

8 Before the spies lay down to sleep, she came up to them on the roof

9 and said to the men: "I know that Yahweh has given you the land, that dread of you has fallen on us, and that all who live in the land melt away before you.

10 For we have heard how Yahweh dried up the waters of the Sea of Reeds before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites beyond the Jordan, to Sihon and to Og, whom you devoted to destruction.

11 When we heard it, our hearts melted, and no spirit remained in anyone because of you, for Yahweh your God — he is God in heaven above and on earth below.

12 So now, please swear to me by Yahweh: as I have shown you loyal love, you in turn will show loyal love to my father's house, and you will give me a trustworthy sign,

13 and you will keep alive my father and my mother, my brothers and my sisters, and all who belong to them, and you will rescue our lives from death."

14 The men said to her, "Our lives in place of yours, even to death, if you do not tell this business of ours. And when Yahweh gives us the land, we will show you loyal love and faithfulness."

15 Then she lowered them by a rope through the window, for her house was set into the city wall, and she lived within the wall.

16 She told them, "Go into the hill country, so the pursuers do not come upon you. Hide there for three days, until the pursuers return, and afterward go on your way."

17 The men said to her, "We will be free of this oath of yours that you made us swear:

18 When we come into the land, you must tie this cord of scarlet thread in the window through which you lowered us, and gather your father, your mother, your brothers, and all your father's household to you in the house.

19 Anyone who goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood will be on his own head, and we will be guiltless. But anyone who stays with you in the house, his blood will be on our head if a hand is laid on him.

20 And if you tell this business of ours, we will be free of your oath that you made us swear."

21 She said, "Let it be as you say." She sent them away, and they left, and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

22 They went and reached the hill country, and stayed there three days until the pursuers returned. The pursuers searched all along the road and did not find them.

23 Then the two men turned back, came down from the hill country, crossed over, and came to Joshua son of Nun, and told him everything that had happened to them.

24 They said to Joshua, "Surely Yahweh has given the whole land into our hands, and all who live in the land melt away before us."

Joshua 3

1 Joshua rose early in the morning, and they set out from Shittim and came to the Jordan, he and all the children of Israel, and they lodged there before crossing.

2 At the end of three days, the officers went through the camp

3 and ordered the people: "When you see the ark of the covenant of Yahweh your God, with the Levitical priests carrying it, you shall set out from your place and follow it.

4 But keep a distance between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure. Do not come near it, so that you may know the way you must go, for you have not passed this way before."

5 Joshua said to the people, "Make yourselves holy, for tomorrow Yahweh will do wonders among you."

6 Then Joshua said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant and cross ahead of the people." So they took up the ark of the covenant and went ahead of the people.

7 Yahweh said to Joshua, "Today I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel, so that they may know that as I was with Moses, so I will be with you.

8 You shall order the priests who carry the ark of the covenant: When you reach the edge of the waters of the Jordan, stand still in the Jordan."

9 Joshua said to the children of Israel, "Come here and listen to the words of Yahweh your God."

10 And Joshua said, "By this you will know that the living God is among you, and that he will surely drive out before you the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, and the Jebusites.

11 See, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing ahead of you into the Jordan.

12 So now choose twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one man for each tribe.

13 And when the soles of the feet of the priests who carry the ark of Yahweh, the Lord of all the earth, come to rest in the waters of the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan will be cut off — the waters flowing down from above — and they will stand in a single heap."

14 So when the people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, the priests carrying the ark of the covenant were ahead of the people.

15 When those carrying the ark reached the Jordan and the feet of the priests carrying the ark dipped into the edge of the water — now the Jordan overflows all its banks throughout the days of harvest —

16 the waters flowing down from above stopped and rose in a single heap a long way off, at Adam, the city beside Zarethan, while the waters flowing down to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, were completely cut off. And the people crossed opposite Jericho.

17 The priests who carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, while all Israel crossed on dry ground, until the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan.

Joshua 4

1 When the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan, Yahweh said to Joshua:

2 "Take twelve men from the people, one man from each tribe,

3 and order them: Take up twelve stones from here, from the middle of the Jordan, from the place where the priests' feet stood firm, carry them across with you, and set them down at the camp where you will spend this night."

4 So Joshua called the twelve men he had appointed from the children of Israel, one man from each tribe,

5 and Joshua said to them, "Cross ahead of the ark of Yahweh your God into the middle of the Jordan, and each of you lift a stone onto his shoulder, according to the number of the tribes of the children of Israel,

6 so that this may be a sign among you. When your children ask in days to come, 'What are these stones to you?'

7 you shall tell them: The waters of the Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of Yahweh. When it crossed the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off. And these stones will be a memorial for the children of Israel forever."

8 The children of Israel did as Joshua commanded. They took up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, as Yahweh had told Joshua, according to the number of the tribes of the children of Israel, carried them across with them to the camp, and set them down there.

9 Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, in the place where the feet of the priests who carried the ark of the covenant had stood, and they are there to this day.

10 The priests who carried the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until everything was completed that Yahweh had commanded Joshua to tell the people, according to all that Moses had commanded Joshua. And the people hurried across.

11 When all the people had finished crossing, the ark of Yahweh and the priests crossed over in front of the people.

12 The children of Reuben, the children of Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh crossed, armed for battle, ahead of the children of Israel, as Moses had told them.

13 About forty thousand men equipped for the army crossed before Yahweh for battle, to the plains of Jericho.

14 On that day Yahweh made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel, and they held him in awe, as they had held Moses in awe, all the days of his life.

15 Yahweh said to Joshua:

16 "Order the priests who carry the ark of the testimony to come up out of the Jordan."

17 So Joshua ordered the priests: "Come up out of the Jordan."

18 When the priests who carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came up from the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of the priests' feet were lifted onto dry ground, the waters of the Jordan returned to their place and flowed over all its banks as before.

19 The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month and camped at Gilgal, on the eastern edge of Jericho.

20 And those twelve stones that they had taken from the Jordan, Joshua set up at Gilgal.

21 He said to the children of Israel, "When your children ask their fathers in days to come, 'What are these stones?'

22 you shall make it known to your children: Israel crossed this Jordan on dry land.

23 For Yahweh your God dried up the waters of the Jordan before you until you had crossed, just as Yahweh your God did to the Sea of Reeds, which he dried up before us until we had crossed,

24 so that all the peoples of the earth may know the hand of Yahweh, that it is mighty, and so that you may fear Yahweh your God all your days."

Joshua 5

1 When all the kings of the Amorites who were west of the Jordan and all the kings of the Canaanites who were by the sea heard that Yahweh had dried up the waters of the Jordan before the children of Israel until they had crossed over, their hearts melted, and there was no spirit left in them because of the children of Israel.

2 At that time Yahweh said to Joshua, "Make knives of flint for yourself, and circumcise the children of Israel again, a second time."

3 So Joshua made knives of flint and circumcised the children of Israel at the Hill of the Foreskins.

4 This is the reason Joshua circumcised them: all the people who came out of Egypt, the males, all the men of war, had died in the wilderness on the way after they came out of Egypt.

5 For all the people who came out had been circumcised, but all the people who were born in the wilderness on the way as they came out of Egypt had not been circumcised.

6 For the children of Israel walked in the wilderness for forty years, until the whole nation, the men of war who came out of Egypt, had perished, because they did not listen to the voice of Yahweh. Yahweh had sworn to them that he would not let them see the land that Yahweh had sworn to their fathers to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey.

7 And he raised up their children in their place; Joshua circumcised them, for they were uncircumcised, because they had not been circumcised on the way.

8 When the whole nation had finished being circumcised, they stayed where they were in the camp until they had recovered.

9 And Yahweh said to Joshua, "Today I have rolled the reproach of Egypt away from you." So the name of that place was called Gilgal, and it is so to this day.

10 The children of Israel camped at Gilgal, and they kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, on the plains of Jericho.

11 On the day after the Passover, on that very day, they ate from the produce of the land, unleavened cakes and parched grain.

12 The manna stopped the next day, when they ate from the produce of the land, and the children of Israel had manna no longer; that year they ate from the yield of the land of Canaan.

13 When Joshua was near Jericho, he lifted his eyes and looked, and there a man was standing in front of him with his sword drawn in his hand. Joshua went to him and said to him, "Are you for us, or for our enemies?"

14 He said, "No; I have now come as commander of the host of Yahweh." Joshua fell on his face to the ground and bowed down, and he said to him, "What message does my lord have for his servant?"

15 The commander of the host of Yahweh said to Joshua, "Take your sandal off your foot, for the place where you stand is holy." And Joshua did so.

Joshua 6

1 Now Jericho was shut up tight because of the children of Israel; no one went out and no one came in.

2 Yahweh said to Joshua, "See, I have given Jericho into your hand, and its king, and its mighty men of valor."

3 "You shall march around the city, all the men of war, going around the city once; this you shall do for six days."

4 "Seven priests shall carry seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark, and on the seventh day you shall march around the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the trumpets."

5 "When they sound a long blast on the ram's horn, when you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people shall shout with a great shout; then the wall of the city will fall down flat, and the people shall go up, each man straight ahead."

6 Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said to them, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark of Yahweh."

7 And he said to the people, "Move forward and march around the city, and let the armed force pass on before the ark of Yahweh."

8 When Joshua had spoken to the people, the seven priests carrying the seven trumpets of rams' horns before Yahweh passed on and blew the trumpets, and the ark of the covenant of Yahweh followed after them.

9 The armed force went before the priests who blew the trumpets, and the rear guard went after the ark, going on and blowing the trumpets as they went.

10 Joshua had commanded the people, "You shall not shout and you shall not let your voice be heard, and no word shall come out of your mouth, until the day I say to you, 'Shout!' Then you shall shout."

11 So he had the ark of Yahweh go around the city, circling it once; then they came into the camp and spent the night in the camp.

12 Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of Yahweh.

13 The seven priests carrying the seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark of Yahweh went on continually and blew the trumpets; the armed force went before them, and the rear guard came after the ark of Yahweh, going on and blowing the trumpets as they went.

14 On the second day they marched around the city once and returned to the camp; so they did for six days.

15 On the seventh day they rose early, at the rising of the dawn, and marched around the city in the same way seven times; only on that day did they march around the city seven times.

16 At the seventh time, when the priests blew the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, "Shout, for Yahweh has given you the city!"

17 "The city and all that is in it shall be devoted to destruction to Yahweh. Only Rahab the prostitute shall live, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers we sent."

18 "But you, keep yourselves from the things devoted to destruction, lest you take from what is devoted to destruction and so make the camp of Israel itself a thing devoted to destruction and bring trouble on it."

19 "All the silver and gold and the vessels of bronze and iron are holy to Yahweh; they shall go into the treasury of Yahweh."

20 So the people shouted, and the trumpets were blown. When the people heard the sound of the trumpet, the people shouted with a great shout, and the wall fell down flat, and the people went up into the city, each man straight ahead, and they captured the city.

21 They devoted to destruction all that was in the city, both man and woman, young and old, and ox and sheep and donkey, with the edge of the sword.

22 To the two men who had spied out the land Joshua said, "Go into the house of the prostitute and bring out from there the woman and all who belong to her, as you swore to her."

23 The young men, the spies, went in and brought out Rahab and her father and her mother and her brothers and all who belonged to her; they brought out all her relatives, and they placed them outside the camp of Israel.

24 They burned the city with fire, and all that was in it; only the silver and the gold and the vessels of bronze and iron they put into the treasury of the house of Yahweh.

25 Rahab the prostitute and her father's household and all who belonged to her Joshua kept alive, and she has lived in the midst of Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

26 Joshua swore an oath at that time, saying, "Cursed before Yahweh is the man who rises up and rebuilds this city, Jericho; at the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest son he shall set up its gates."

27 Yahweh was with Joshua, and his fame spread through all the land.

Joshua 7

1 The children of Israel broke faith in regard to the things devoted to destruction: Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of the things devoted to destruction, and the anger of Yahweh burned against the children of Israel.

2 Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is beside Beth-aven, east of Bethel, and he said to them, "Go up and spy out the land." The men went up and spied out Ai.

3 They returned to Joshua and said to him, "Do not let all the people go up; let about two thousand or three thousand men go up and strike Ai. Do not weary all the people there, for they are few."

4 So about three thousand men of the people went up there, and they fled before the men of Ai.

5 The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them, and chased them from before the gate as far as Shebarim and struck them on the slope; and the heart of the people melted and became like water.

6 Joshua tore his clothes and fell on his face to the ground before the ark of Yahweh until the evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they put dust on their heads.

7 Joshua said, "Ah, Lord Yahweh, why did you bring this people across the Jordan at all, to give us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us? If only we had been content to stay on the other side of the Jordan!"

8 "Please, Lord, what can I say, now that Israel has turned its back before its enemies?"

9 "The Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land will hear of it and will surround us and cut off our name from the earth; and what will you do for your great name?"

10 Yahweh said to Joshua, "Get up! Why are you fallen on your face like this?"

11 "Israel has sinned; they have also transgressed my covenant that I commanded them. They have also taken from the things devoted to destruction, and they have also stolen, and they have also deceived, and they have also put it among their own belongings."

12 "Therefore the children of Israel cannot stand before their enemies; they turn their backs before their enemies, because they have become a thing devoted to destruction. I will no longer be with you unless you destroy the things devoted to destruction from among you."

13 "Get up, consecrate the people, and say: Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow, for thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: There is a thing devoted to destruction in your midst, Israel; you cannot stand before your enemies until you remove the thing devoted to destruction from among you."

14 "In the morning you shall come near by your tribes; the tribe that Yahweh takes shall come near by families, the family that Yahweh takes shall come near by households, and the household that Yahweh takes shall come near man by man."

15 "The one who is taken with the things devoted to destruction shall be burned with fire, he and all that belongs to him, because he has transgressed the covenant of Yahweh and because he has done a disgraceful thing in Israel."

16 Joshua rose early in the morning and brought Israel near by its tribes, and the tribe of Judah was taken.

17 He brought near the family of Judah, and the family of the Zerahites was taken; he brought near the family of the Zerahites man by man, and Zabdi was taken.

18 He brought near his household man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.

19 Joshua said to Achan, "My son, please give glory to Yahweh, the God of Israel, and give him confession; tell me now what you have done; do not hide it from me."

20 Achan answered Joshua and said, "Truly, I have sinned against Yahweh, the God of Israel, and this is what I did."

21 "When I saw among the spoil a fine mantle from Shinar, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a bar of gold weighing fifty shekels, I coveted them and took them; and see, they are hidden in the ground in the middle of my tent, with the silver under it."

22 Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent; and see, it was hidden in his tent, with the silver under it.

23 They took them from the middle of the tent and brought them to Joshua and to all the children of Israel, and they laid them out before Yahweh.

24 Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan son of Zerah, and the silver and the mantle and the bar of gold, and his sons and his daughters, and his oxen and his donkeys and his sheep, and his tent and all that belonged to him, and they brought them up to the Valley of Achor.

25 Joshua said, "How you have troubled us! Yahweh will trouble you this day." And all Israel stoned him with stones; they burned them with fire and stoned them with stones.

26 They raised over him a great heap of stones, which stands to this day, and Yahweh turned from the fierceness of his anger. Therefore the name of that place has been called the Valley of Achor to this day.

Joshua 8

1 Yahweh said to Joshua, "Do not fear and do not be dismayed. Take with you all the people of war, and arise, go up to Ai. See, I have given into your hand the king of Ai and his people and his city and his land."

2 "You shall do to Ai and its king as you did to Jericho and its king, except that its spoil and its livestock you may take as plunder for yourselves. Place an ambush for the city behind it."

3 Joshua arose, and all the people of war with him, to go up to Ai; Joshua chose thirty thousand men, mighty men of valor, and sent them out by night.

4 He commanded them, "See, you are to lie in ambush against the city, behind the city; do not go very far from the city, but all of you be ready."

5 "I and all the people who are with me will approach the city; when they come out against us as they did the first time, we will flee before them."

6 "They will come out after us until we have drawn them away from the city, for they will say, 'They are fleeing before us as the first time.' So we will flee before them."

7 "Then you shall rise from the ambush and take possession of the city, for Yahweh your God will give it into your hand."

8 "When you have seized the city, you shall set the city on fire; you shall do according to the word of Yahweh. See, I have commanded you."

9 Joshua sent them out, and they went to the place of ambush and stayed between Bethel and Ai, west of Ai, while Joshua spent that night among the people.

Joshua 9

1 When all the kings on the other side of the Jordan heard of it — in the hill country, in the lowland, and along the whole shore of the Great Sea facing Lebanon: the Hittite, the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite —

5 with worn-out, patched sandals on their feet and worn-out clothes on their backs, and all the bread they took for the journey was dry and crumbled.

6 They came to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, "We have come from a distant land, so now make a covenant with us."

7 Then the man of Israel said to the Hivite, "Perhaps you are living right among us; how then can I make a covenant with you?"

8 They said to Joshua, "We are your servants." And Joshua said to them, "Who are you, and where do you come from?"

9 They answered him, "Your servants have come from a very distant land because of the name of Yahweh your God, for we have heard the report of him and all that he did in Egypt,

10 and all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan — to Sihon king of Heshbon and to Og king of Bashan, who was at Ashtaroth.

11 Our elders and all the inhabitants of our land said to us, 'Take provisions in hand for the journey, go to meet them, and say to them: We are your servants; now make a covenant with us.'

12 This is our bread: we took it warm from our houses as our provision on the day we set out to come to you, and now, see, it is dry and crumbled.

13 And these wineskins, which we filled when they were new — see, they have burst. And these clothes and sandals of ours have worn out because the journey was so very long."

14 So the men took some of their provisions, but they did not ask for the word of Yahweh.

15 Joshua made peace with them and made a covenant with them, to let them live, and the leaders of the congregation swore an oath to them.

16 Three days after they had made the covenant with them, they heard that these people were near them and were living among them.

17 The children of Israel set out and on the third day reached their cities; their cities were Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath-jearim.

18 But the children of Israel did not strike them down, because the leaders of the congregation had sworn to them by Yahweh, the God of Israel, and the whole congregation grumbled against the leaders.

21 The leaders said to them, "Let them live." So they became woodcutters and water carriers for the whole congregation, as the leaders had told them.

24 They answered Joshua and said, "Because your servants were told, and told plainly, what Yahweh your God commanded Moses his servant: to give you the whole land and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land before you. We were very much afraid for our lives because of you, and so we did this thing.

25 Now, see, we are in your hand; do to us whatever seems good and right in your eyes."

26 So he did that to them: he rescued them from the hand of the children of Israel, and they did not kill them.

27 That day Joshua made them woodcutters and water carriers for the congregation and for the altar of Yahweh, to this day, in the place that he would choose.

23 If we built ourselves an altar to turn away from following Yahweh, or to offer on it burnt offering and grain offering, or to make on it sacrifices of peace offerings, may Yahweh himself call us to account.

24 Rather, we did this out of concern for a reason, saying: In time to come your children may say to our children, 'What have you to do with Yahweh, the God of Israel?

25 For Yahweh has made the Jordan a border between us and you, you children of Reuben and children of Gad; you have no portion in Yahweh.' So your children would make our children stop fearing Yahweh.

26 So we said, 'Let us prepare to build the altar, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice,

27 but it is a witness between us and you, and between our generations after us, that we may perform the service of Yahweh before him with our burnt offerings, our sacrifices, and our peace offerings, and your children may not say to our children in time to come, "You have no portion in Yahweh."'

28 And we said, 'If they say this to us or to our generations in time to come, we will say: See the pattern of the altar of Yahweh that our fathers made, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice, for it is a witness between us and you.'

29 Far be it from us to rebel against Yahweh and turn away today from following Yahweh by building an altar for burnt offering, for grain offering, and for sacrifice, besides the altar of Yahweh our God that stands before his tabernacle."

30 When Phinehas the priest and the leaders of the congregation, the heads of the thousands of Israel who were with him, heard the words that the children of Reuben, the children of Gad, and the children of Manasseh spoke, it was good in their eyes.

31 Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the children of Reuben, to the children of Gad, and to the children of Manasseh, "Today we know that Yahweh is in our midst, because you have not committed this treachery against Yahweh; now you have delivered the children of Israel from the hand of Yahweh."

32 Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned from the children of Reuben and from the children of Gad, from the land of Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the children of Israel, and brought them back word.

33 The word was good in the eyes of the children of Israel, and the children of Israel blessed God and said no more of going up against them for war, to destroy the land in which the children of Reuben and the children of Gad lived.

34 The children of Reuben and the children of Gad called the altar — for it is a witness between us that Yahweh is God.

Joshua 23

1 It came to pass, after many days, after Yahweh had given Israel rest from all their enemies all around, that Joshua was old and advanced in years.

2 Joshua called all Israel, its elders, its heads, its judges, and its officers, and said to them, "I am old and advanced in years,

3 and you have seen everything that Yahweh your God has done to all these nations because of you, for Yahweh your God is the one who has fought for you.

4 See, I have allotted to you as an inheritance for your tribes these nations that remain, from the Jordan, together with all the nations that I cut off, as far as the Great Sea toward the setting of the sun.

5 Yahweh your God himself will thrust them out before you and drive them out from before you, and you will possess their land, as Yahweh your God promised you.

6 So be very strong to keep and to do everything written in the book of the Torah of Moses, without turning aside from it to the right or to the left,

7 so that you do not mix with these nations, those that remain among you, and do not mention the name of their gods, or swear by them, or serve them, or bow down to them.

8 Rather, cling to Yahweh your God, as you have done to this day.

9 For Yahweh has driven out before you nations great and strong, and as for you, no man has stood against you to this day.

10 One man of you chases a thousand, for Yahweh your God is the one who fights for you, as he promised you.

11 So take great care for yourselves to love Yahweh your God.

12 For if you turn back at all and cling to the rest of these nations, those that remain among you, and intermarry with them, and go in among them and they among you,

13 know for certain that Yahweh your God will no longer drive out these nations from before you; they will be a snare and a trap for you, a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you.

14 Look, today I am going the way of all the earth, and you know with all your heart and with all your soul that not one word has failed of all the good words that Yahweh your God spoke concerning you; all of them have come to pass for you, not one word of them has failed.

15 But just as every good word that Yahweh your God spoke to you has come upon you, so Yahweh will bring upon you every evil word, until he has destroyed you from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you,

16 when you transgress the covenant of Yahweh your God, which he commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them; then the anger of Yahweh will burn against you, and you will perish quickly from the good land that he has given you."

Joshua 24

1 Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem and called the elders of Israel, its heads, its judges, and its officers, and they presented themselves before God.

2 Joshua said to all the people, "Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: 'Beyond the River your fathers lived long ago, Terah, the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor, and they served other gods.

3 I took your father Abraham from beyond the River and led him through all the land of Canaan, and I multiplied his seed and gave him Isaac.

4 To Isaac I gave Jacob and Esau, and to Esau I gave Mount Seir to possess, while Jacob and his children went down to Egypt.

5 I sent Moses and Aaron and struck Egypt with plagues, as I did in its midst, and afterward I brought you out.

6 I brought your fathers out of Egypt, and you came to the sea, and Egypt pursued your fathers with chariots and horsemen to the Sea of Reeds.

7 They cried out to Yahweh, and he put darkness between you and the Egyptians and brought the sea upon them and covered them, and your eyes saw what I did in Egypt; then you lived in the wilderness many days.

8 I brought you into the land of the Amorites, who lived beyond the Jordan, and they fought with you, and I gave them into your hand and you possessed their land, and I destroyed them before you.

9 Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and fought against Israel, and he sent and called Balaam son of Beor to curse you.

10 But I would not listen to Balaam, so he blessed you again and again, and I delivered you from his hand.

11 You crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho, and the lords of Jericho fought against you — the Amorite, the Perizzite, the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Girgashite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite — and I gave them into your hand.

12 I sent the hornet before you, and it drove them out before you, the two kings of the Amorites; it was not by your sword and not by your bow.

13 I gave you a land on which you had not laboured, and cities that you had not built, and you settled in them; you are eating from vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant.'

14 Now therefore fear Yahweh and serve him in wholeness and in truth, and put away the gods that your fathers served beyond the River and in Egypt, and serve Yahweh.

15 And if it is evil in your eyes to serve Yahweh, choose for yourselves today whom you will serve: whether the gods that your fathers served beyond the River, or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you are living; but as for me and my house, we will serve Yahweh."

16 The people answered and said, "Far be it from us to forsake Yahweh, to serve other gods,

17 for Yahweh our God is the one who brought us and our fathers up from the land of Egypt, from the house of slaves, and who did these great signs before our eyes and kept us along all the way that we went and among all the peoples through whose midst we passed.

18 Yahweh drove out before us all the peoples, and the Amorites who lived in the land; we too will serve Yahweh, for he is our God."

19 Joshua said to the people, "You cannot serve Yahweh, for he is a holy God; he is a jealous God; he will not bear your transgression and your sins.

20 If you forsake Yahweh and serve foreign gods, he will turn and do you harm and finish you, after he has done you good."

21 The people said to Joshua, "No, for we will serve Yahweh."

22 Joshua said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you yourselves have chosen Yahweh, to serve him." And they said, "We are witnesses."

23 "Now therefore put away the foreign gods that are in your midst, and incline your heart to Yahweh, the God of Israel."

24 The people said to Joshua, "Yahweh our God we will serve, and his voice we will obey."

25 So Joshua cut a covenant with the people that day, and set for them a statute and an ordinance at Shechem.

26 Joshua wrote these words in the book of the Torah of God, and he took a large stone and set it up there under the oak that was in the sanctuary of Yahweh.

27 Joshua said to all the people, "Look, this stone will be a witness against us, for it has heard all the words of Yahweh that he spoke with us; it will be a witness against you, lest you deny your God."

28 Then Joshua sent the people away, each to his inheritance.

29 It came to pass after these things that Joshua son of Nun, the servant of Yahweh, died, one hundred and ten years old.

30 They buried him within the border of his inheritance at Timnath-serah, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

31 Israel served Yahweh all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who outlived Joshua and who had known all the work of Yahweh that he had done for Israel.

32 The bones of Joseph, which the children of Israel had brought up from Egypt, they buried at Shechem, in the portion of the field that Jacob bought from the children of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred kesitah, and they became an inheritance for the children of Joseph.

33 Eleazar son of Aaron died, and they buried him at Gibeah of Phinehas his son, which had been given to him in the hill country of Ephraim.
