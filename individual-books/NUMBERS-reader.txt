Numbers 1

1 Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the Tent of Meeting, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt, saying,

2 “Take the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, every male, head by head,

3 from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host in Israel. You and Aaron shall count them by their hosts.

4 And with you there shall be a man of every tribe, each man the head of his fathers’ house.

5 And these are the names of the men who shall stand with you: Of Reuben: Elizur the son of Shedeur.

6 Of Simeon: Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

7 Of Judah: Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

8 Of Issachar: Nethanel the son of Zuar.

9 Of Zebulun: Eliab the son of Helon.

10 Of the children of Joseph: of Ephraim: Elishama the son of Ammihud; of Manasseh: Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.

11 Of Benjamin: Abidan the son of Gideoni.

12 Of Dan: Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.

13 Of Asher: Pagiel the son of Ochran.

14 Of Gad: Eliasaph the son of Deuel.

15 Of Naphtali: Ahira the son of Enan.”

16 These are the summoned ones of the congregation, the princes of the tribes of their fathers; they are the heads of the thousands of Israel.

17 And Moses and Aaron took these men who were designated by name.

18 And they assembled all the congregation together on the first day of the second month, and they registered their descent by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, head by head.

19 As Yahweh commanded Moses, so he counted them in the wilderness of Sinai.

20 And the children of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn, were these: their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

21 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Reuben, were forty-six thousand five hundred.

22 Of the children of Simeon, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, those who were counted of it, according to the number of the names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

23 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Simeon, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

24 Of the children of Gad, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

25 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Gad, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

26 Of the children of Judah, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

27 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Judah, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.

28 Of the children of Issachar, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

29 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Issachar, were fifty-four thousand four hundred.

30 Of the children of Zebulun, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

31 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Zebulun, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

32 Of the children of Joseph, of the children of Ephraim, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

33 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Ephraim, were forty thousand five hundred.

34 Of the children of Manasseh, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

35 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Manasseh, were thirty-two thousand two hundred.

36 Of the children of Benjamin, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

37 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Benjamin, were thirty-five thousand four hundred.

38 Of the children of Dan, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

39 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Dan, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

40 Of the children of Asher, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

41 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Asher, were forty-one thousand five hundred.

42 The children of Naphtali, their generations, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host:

43 those who were counted of them, of the tribe of Naphtali, were fifty-three thousand four hundred.

44 These are those who were counted, whom Moses and Aaron counted, and the princes of Israel, twelve men; each one was for his fathers’ house.

45 And all those who were counted of the children of Israel by their fathers’ houses, from twenty years old and upward, all who go out to the host in Israel, were—

46 all those who were counted were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

47 But the Levites, by the tribe of their fathers, were not counted among them.

48 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

49 “Only the tribe of Levi you shall not count, and their sum you shall not take among the children of Israel;

50 but you shall appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, and over all its vessels, and over all that belongs to it. They shall carry the tabernacle and all its vessels, and they shall minister to it, and they shall encamp around the tabernacle.

51 And when the tabernacle sets out, the Levites shall take it down; and when the tabernacle encamps, the Levites shall set it up. And the outsider who comes near shall be put to death.

52 And the children of Israel shall encamp, every man by his own camp, and every man by his own standard, by their hosts.

53 But the Levites shall encamp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that there shall be no wrath on the congregation of the children of Israel. And the Levites shall keep the charge of the tabernacle of the testimony.”

54 And the children of Israel did so. According to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, so they did.

Numbers 2

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

2 “The children of Israel shall encamp, every man by his own standard, with the signs of their fathers’ houses. Opposite, around the Tent of Meeting, they shall encamp.

3 And those who encamp in front, eastward, shall be the standard of the camp of Judah, by their hosts. And the prince of the children of Judah is Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

4 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.

5 And those who encamp next to him are the tribe of Issachar. And the prince of the children of Issachar is Nethanel the son of Zuar.

6 And his host, and those who were counted of it, were fifty-four thousand four hundred.

7 The tribe of Zebulun: and the prince of the children of Zebulun is Eliab the son of Helon.

8 And his host, and those who were counted of it, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

9 All who were counted of the camp of Judah were one hundred and eighty-six thousand four hundred, by their hosts. They shall set out first.

10 The standard of the camp of Reuben shall be southward, by their hosts. And the prince of the children of Reuben is Elizur the son of Shedeur.

11 And his host, and those who were counted of it, were forty-six thousand five hundred.

12 And those who encamp next to him are the tribe of Simeon. And the prince of the children of Simeon is Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.

13 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

14 And the tribe of Gad: and the prince of the children of Gad is Eliasaph the son of Reuel.

15 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

16 All who were counted of the camp of Reuben were one hundred and fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty, by their hosts. And they shall set out second.

17 Then the Tent of Meeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites in the middle of the camps. As they encamp, so they shall set out, every man in his place, by their standards.

18 The standard of the camp of Ephraim shall be westward, by their hosts. And the prince of the children of Ephraim is Elishama the son of Ammihud.

19 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were forty thousand five hundred.

20 And next to him is the tribe of Manasseh. And the prince of the children of Manasseh is Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.

21 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were thirty-two thousand two hundred.

22 And the tribe of Benjamin: and the prince of the children of Benjamin is Abidan the son of Gideoni.

23 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were thirty-five thousand four hundred.

24 All who were counted of the camp of Ephraim were one hundred and eight thousand one hundred, by their hosts. And they shall set out third.

25 The standard of the camp of Dan shall be northward, by their hosts. And the prince of the children of Dan is Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.

26 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

27 And those who encamp next to him are the tribe of Asher. And the prince of the children of Asher is Pagiel the son of Ochran.

28 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were forty-one thousand five hundred.

29 And the tribe of Naphtali: and the prince of the children of Naphtali is Ahira the son of Enan.

30 And his host, and those who were counted of them, were fifty-three thousand four hundred.

31 All who were counted of the camp of Dan were one hundred and fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall set out last, by their standards.”

32 These are those who were counted of the children of Israel by their fathers’ houses. All who were counted of the camps, by their hosts, were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

33 But the Levites were not counted among the children of Israel, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

34 And the children of Israel did so. According to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, so they encamped by their standards, and so they set out, every man by his families, according to his fathers’ house.

Numbers 3

1 And these are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day Yahweh spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.

2 And these are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

3 These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whose hand he filled to serve as priests.

4 And Nadab and Abihu died before Yahweh when they brought near strange fire before Yahweh in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children. And Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests before Aaron their father.

5 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

6 “Bring the tribe of Levi near, and you shall make them stand before Aaron the priest, and they shall minister to him.

7 And they shall keep his charge and the charge of the whole congregation before the Tent of Meeting, to do the service of the tabernacle.

8 And they shall keep all the vessels of the Tent of Meeting and the charge of the children of Israel, to do the service of the tabernacle.

9 And you shall give the Levites to Aaron and to his sons. They are given, wholly given to him from the children of Israel.

10 And Aaron and his sons you shall appoint, and they shall keep their priesthood. And the outsider who comes near shall be put to death.”

11 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

12 “And I, behold, I have taken the Levites from among the children of Israel in place of every firstborn who opens the womb among the children of Israel, and the Levites shall be mine,

13 for every firstborn is mine. On the day I struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I made holy to me every firstborn in Israel, from man to animal. They shall be mine. I am Yahweh.”

14 And Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,

15 “Count the children of Levi by their fathers’ houses, by their families. Every male from a month old and upward you shall count.”

16 And Moses counted them at the mouth of Yahweh, as he was commanded.

17 And these were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari.

18 And these are the names of the sons of Gershon by their families: Libni and Shimei.

19 And the sons of Kohath by their families: Amram, and Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

20 And the sons of Merari by their families: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites by their fathers’ houses.

21 Of Gershon: the family of the Libnites and the family of the Shimeites. These are the families of the Gershonites.

22 Those who were counted of them, in the number of every male from a month old and upward, those who were counted of them were seven thousand five hundred.

23 The families of the Gershonites shall encamp behind the tabernacle, westward.

24 And the prince of the fathers’ house of the Gershonites is Eliasaph the son of Lael.

25 And the charge of the sons of Gershon in the Tent of Meeting is the tabernacle and the tent, its covering, and the screen for the entrance of the Tent of Meeting,

26 and the hangings of the court, and the screen for the entrance of the court, which is around the tabernacle and around the altar, and its cords, for all its service.

27 And of Kohath: the family of the Amramites, and the family of the Izharites, and the family of the Hebronites, and the family of the Uzzielites. These are the families of the Kohathites.

28 In the number of every male from a month old and upward, eight thousand six hundred, keeping the charge of the sanctuary.

29 The families of the sons of Kohath shall encamp on the side of the tabernacle, southward.

30 And the prince of the fathers’ house of the families of the Kohathites is Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.

31 And their charge is the ark, and the table, and the lampstand, and the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary with which they minister, and the screen, and all its service.

32 And the prince of the princes of the Levites is Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, appointed over those who keep the charge of the sanctuary.

33 Of Merari: the family of the Mahlites and the family of the Mushites. These are the families of Merari.

34 And those who were counted of them, in the number of every male from a month old and upward, were six thousand two hundred.

35 And the prince of the fathers’ house of the families of Merari is Zuriel the son of Abihail. On the side of the tabernacle they shall encamp, northward.

36 And the appointed charge of the sons of Merari is the boards of the tabernacle, and its bars, and its pillars, and its sockets, and all its vessels, and all its service,

37 and the pillars of the court around, and their sockets, and their pins, and their cords.

38 And those who encamp before the tabernacle, in front, before the Tent of Meeting eastward, are Moses, and Aaron, and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the children of Israel. And the outsider who comes near shall be put to death.

39 All who were counted of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the mouth of Yahweh, by their families, every male from a month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand.

40 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Count every firstborn male of the children of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the number of their names.

41 And you shall take the Levites for me—I am Yahweh—in place of every firstborn among the children of Israel, and the animals of the Levites in place of every firstborn among the animals of the children of Israel.”

42 And Moses counted, as Yahweh commanded him, every firstborn among the children of Israel.

43 And every firstborn male, in the number of names from a month old and upward, of those who were counted of them, was twenty-two thousand two hundred and seventy-three.

44 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

45 “Take the Levites in place of every firstborn among the children of Israel, and the animals of the Levites in place of their animals, and the Levites shall be mine. I am Yahweh.

46 And for the redeemed of the two hundred and seventy-three who are over and above the Levites, from the firstborn of the children of Israel,

47 you shall take five shekels for each head; by the shekel of the sanctuary you shall take it—twenty gerahs is the shekel.

48 And you shall give the silver to Aaron and to his sons, the redeemed of those who are over among them.”

49 And Moses took the silver of the redemption from those who were over and above those redeemed by the Levites;

50 from the firstborn of the children of Israel he took the silver, one thousand three hundred and sixty-five, by the shekel of the sanctuary;

51 and Moses gave the silver of the redeemed to Aaron and to his sons, at the mouth of Yahweh, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

Numbers 4

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

2 “Take the sum of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, by their families, by their fathers’ houses,

3 from thirty years old and upward, and until fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, to do work in the Tent of Meeting.

4 This is the service of the sons of Kohath in the Tent of Meeting: the most holy things.

5 And Aaron and his sons shall come, when the camp sets out, and they shall take down the veil, the screen, and they shall cover the ark of the testimony with it,

6 and they shall put on it a covering of tachash skin, and they shall spread a cloth wholly of blue above, and they shall set its poles.

7 And on the table of the Presence they shall spread a cloth of blue, and they shall put on it the dishes, and the spoons, and the bowls, and the jars for pouring out, and the continual bread shall be on it.

8 And they shall spread on them a cloth of scarlet worm, and they shall cover it with a covering of tachash skin, and they shall set its poles.

9 And they shall take a cloth of blue and cover the lampstand of the light, and its lamps, and its tongs, and its trays, and all its oil vessels with which they minister to it.

10 And they shall put it and all its vessels into a covering of tachash skin, and they shall put it on the carrying bar.

11 And on the golden altar they shall spread a cloth of blue, and they shall cover it with a covering of tachash skin, and they shall set its poles.

12 And they shall take all the vessels of ministry with which they minister in the sanctuary, and they shall put them into a cloth of blue, and they shall cover them with a covering of tachash skin, and they shall put them on the carrying bar.

13 And they shall remove the fat ashes from the altar, and they shall spread on it a cloth of purple.

14 And they shall put on it all its vessels with which they minister on it—the trays, the forks, and the shovels, and the basins, all the vessels of the altar—and they shall spread on it a covering of tachash skin, and they shall set its poles.

15 And when Aaron and his sons have finished covering the sanctuary and all the vessels of the sanctuary, when the camp sets out, after that the sons of Kohath shall come to carry, and they shall not touch the sanctuary, lest they die. These are the burden of the sons of Kohath in the Tent of Meeting.

16 And the charge of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest is the oil for the light, and the incense of spices, and the continual grain offering, and the anointing oil—the charge of the whole tabernacle and all that is in it, in the sanctuary and in its vessels.”

17 And Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

18 “Do not cut off the tribe of the families of the Kohathites from among the Levites;

19 but do this for them, and they shall live and not die, when they come near the most holy things: Aaron and his sons shall come and set them, every man to his service and to his burden;

20 and they shall not come in to see, as the sanctuary is swallowed up, and die.”

21 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

22 “Take the sum of the sons of Gershon also, by their fathers’ houses, by their families;

23 from thirty years old and upward until fifty years old you shall count them, everyone who comes to serve the service, to do service in the Tent of Meeting.

24 This is the service of the families of the Gershonites, to serve and to bear burdens:

25 they shall carry the curtains of the tabernacle, and the Tent of Meeting, its covering, and the covering of tachash that is on it above, and the screen for the entrance of the Tent of Meeting,

26 and the hangings of the court, and the screen for the entrance of the gate of the court, which is around the tabernacle and around the altar, and their cords, and all the vessels of their service, and all that is done for them, and they shall serve.

27 At the mouth of Aaron and his sons shall be all the service of the sons of the Gershonites, for all their burden and for all their service; and you shall appoint over them, as a charge, all their burden.

28 This is the service of the families of the sons of the Gershonites in the Tent of Meeting, and their charge is under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.”

29 “The sons of Merari, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, you shall count them;

30 from thirty years old and upward and until fifty years old you shall count them, everyone who comes to the host, to do the service of the Tent of Meeting.

31 And this is the charge of their burden, for all their service in the Tent of Meeting: the boards of the tabernacle, and its bars, and its pillars, and its sockets,

32 and the pillars of the court around, and their sockets, and their pins, and their cords, for all their vessels and for all their service. And by names you shall appoint the vessels of the charge of their burden.

33 This is the service of the families of the sons of Merari, for all their service in the Tent of Meeting, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.”

34 And Moses and Aaron and the princes of the congregation counted the sons of the Kohathites, by their families and by their fathers’ houses,

35 from thirty years old and upward and until fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, to do service in the Tent of Meeting.

36 And those who were counted of them, by their families, were two thousand seven hundred and fifty.

37 These are those who were counted of the families of the Kohathites, everyone who serves in the Tent of Meeting, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the mouth of Yahweh, by the hand of Moses.

38 And those who were counted of the sons of Gershon, by their families and by their fathers’ houses,

39 from thirty years old and upward and until fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, to do service in the Tent of Meeting,

40 those who were counted of them, by their families, by their fathers’ houses, were two thousand six hundred and thirty.

41 These are those who were counted of the families of the sons of Gershon, everyone who serves in the Tent of Meeting, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the mouth of Yahweh.

42 And those who were counted of the families of the sons of Merari, by their families, by their fathers’ houses,

43 from thirty years old and upward and until fifty years old, everyone who comes to the host, to do service in the Tent of Meeting,

44 those who were counted of them, by their families, were three thousand two hundred.

45 These are those who were counted of the families of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron counted at the mouth of Yahweh, by the hand of Moses.

46 All those who were counted, whom Moses and Aaron and the princes of Israel counted—the Levites—by their families and by their fathers’ houses,

47 from thirty years old and upward and until fifty years old, everyone who comes to do the work of service and the work of bearing burdens in the Tent of Meeting,

48 those who were counted of them were eight thousand five hundred and eighty.

49 At the mouth of Yahweh they counted them, by the hand of Moses, every man to his service and to his burden, and so they were counted, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

Numbers 5

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 “Command the children of Israel, and they shall send out from the camp everyone who has a scaly skin disease, and everyone who has a discharge, and everyone who is unclean by a dead person.

3 From male to female you shall send them out; to outside the camp you shall send them, so that they do not defile their camp, in the midst of which I dwell.”

4 And the children of Israel did so, and sent them out to outside the camp; as Yahweh spoke to Moses, so the children of Israel did.

5 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

6 “Speak to the children of Israel: When a man or a woman commits any of all the sins of mankind, acting unfaithfully against Yahweh, and that person is guilty,

7 then they shall confess their sin that they have done, and he shall make restitution for his guilt in full, and shall add a fifth of it to it, and shall give it to the one against whom he has been guilty.

8 But if the man has no redeemer to whom restitution may be made for the guilt, the restitution for guilt that is restored to Yahweh shall be the priest’s, besides the ram of the atonement, by which atonement shall be made for him.

9 And every contribution of all the holy things of the children of Israel, which they bring near to the priest, shall be his.

10 And a man’s holy things shall be his; whatever a man gives to the priest, it shall be his.”

11 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

12 “Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: If any man’s wife goes astray, and acts unfaithfully against him,

13 and a man lies with her, a lying of seed, and it is hidden from the eyes of her husband, and she is concealed, and she has been defiled, and there is no witness against her, and she has not been caught,

14 and a spirit of jealousy passes over him, and he is jealous of his wife, and she has been defiled; or a spirit of jealousy passes over him, and he is jealous of his wife, and she has not been defiled;

15 then the man shall bring his wife to the priest, and shall bring her offering for her: a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He shall pour no oil on it, and shall put no frankincense on it, for it is a grain offering of jealousy, a grain offering of remembrance, bringing iniquity to remembrance.

16 And the priest shall bring her near, and shall set her before Yahweh.

17 And the priest shall take holy water in an earthen vessel; and from the dust that is on the floor of the tabernacle the priest shall take, and shall put it into the water.

18 And the priest shall set the woman before Yahweh, and shall loosen the hair of the woman’s head, and shall put into her hands the grain offering of remembrance, which is the grain offering of jealousy. And in the priest’s hand shall be the bitter water that brings the curse.

19 And the priest shall make her swear, and shall say to the woman, ‘If no man has lain with you, and if you have not gone astray into uncleanness under your husband, be clear from this bitter water that brings the curse.

20 But you, if you have gone astray under your husband, and if you have been defiled, and a man has given his lying with you, besides your husband—’

21 then the priest shall make the woman swear with the oath of the curse, and the priest shall say to the woman, ‘Yahweh shall make you a curse and an oath among your people, when Yahweh makes your thigh fall away and your belly swell;

22 and this water that brings the curse shall go into your inward parts, to make the belly swell and the thigh fall away.’ And the woman shall say, ‘Amen, Amen.’

23 ‘And the priest shall write these curses in the book, and shall wipe them out into the bitter water.

24 And he shall make the woman drink the bitter water that brings the curse; and the water that brings the curse shall enter into her, and become bitter.

25 And the priest shall take the grain offering of jealousy from the woman’s hand, and shall wave the grain offering before Yahweh, and shall bring it near to the altar.

26 And the priest shall take a handful from the grain offering, its remembrance portion, and shall burn it on the altar, and afterwards shall make the woman drink the water.

27 And when he has made her drink the water, then it shall be, if she has been defiled and has acted unfaithfully against her husband, that the water that brings the curse shall enter into her and become bitter, and her belly shall swell, and her thigh shall fall away; and the woman shall be a curse in the midst of her people.

28 And if the woman has not been defiled, but is clean, then she shall be clear, and shall be sown with seed.

29 ‘This is the law of jealousy, when a wife goes astray under her husband, and has been defiled,

30 or when a spirit of jealousy passes over a man, and he is jealous of his wife; then he shall set the woman before Yahweh, and the priest shall do to her all this law.

31 And the man shall be clear from iniquity, and that woman shall bear her iniquity.’”

Numbers 6

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 “Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: When a man or a woman makes a special vow, the vow of a Nazirite, to consecrate himself to Yahweh,

3 he shall abstain from wine and strong drink. He shall drink no vinegar of wine and no vinegar of strong drink, and he shall drink no liquor of grapes, and he shall eat no grapes, fresh or dried.

21 ‘This is the law of the Nazirite who vows, his offering to Yahweh for his Nazirite vow, besides what his hand can reach. According to his vow that he vows, so shall he do, according to the law of his Nazirite vow.’”

23 At the mouth of Yahweh they encamped, and at the mouth of Yahweh they journeyed; they kept the charge of Yahweh, at the mouth of Yahweh by the hand of Moses.

1 And the people were as complainers, evil in the ears of Yahweh; and Yahweh heard, and his anger burned, and the fire of Yahweh burned among them and consumed at the edge of the camp.

2 And the people cried out to Moses, and Moses prayed to Yahweh, and the fire sank down.

3 And he called the name of that place Taberah, because the fire of Yahweh burned among them.

4 And the mixed crowd that was among them craved a craving, and the children of Israel also wept again and said, “Who will feed us meat?

5 We remember the fish that we ate in Egypt for nothing, the cucumbers and the melons and the leeks and the onions and the garlic;

6 and now our soul is dried up; there is nothing at all; only to the manna are our eyes.”

7 And the manna was like coriander seed, and its appearance was like the appearance of bdellium.

8 The people went about and gathered and ground in the mills, or crushed in the mortar, and boiled in the pot, and made it into cakes, and its taste was like the taste of a cake of oil.

9 And when the dew came down on the camp at night, the manna came down on it.

10 And Moses heard the people weeping by their families, each man at the entrance of his tent, and Yahweh’s anger burned greatly, and in Moses’ eyes it was evil.

11 And Moses said to Yahweh, “Why have you done evil to your slave, and why have I not found favour in your eyes, to put the burden of all this people on me?

12 Did I conceive all this people? Did I give birth to him, that you say to me, ‘Carry him in your bosom, as the nurse carries the sucking child,’ to the ground that you swore to their fathers?

13 From where have I meat to give to all this people? For they weep against me, saying, ‘Give us meat, and let us eat.’

14 I alone cannot carry all this people, because it is too heavy for me.

15 And if you are doing thus to me, please kill me outright, if I have found favour in your eyes, and let me not see my evil.”

16 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Gather for me seventy men from the elders of Israel, whom you know that they are elders of the people and its officers, and take them to the Tent of Meeting, and they shall stand there with you.

17 And I will come down and speak with you there, and I will take from the spirit that is on you and put on them, and they will bear with you the burden of the people, and you will not bear it, you alone.

18 And to the people you shall say, ‘Sanctify yourselves for tomorrow, and you shall eat meat, for you wept in the ears of Yahweh, saying, “Who will feed us meat? For it was good for us in Egypt.” And Yahweh will give you meat, and you shall eat.

19 You shall not eat one day, and not two days, and not five days, and not ten days, and not twenty days,

20 but a month of days, until it comes out from your nostrils and becomes loathsome to you, because you rejected Yahweh who is among you and wept before him, saying, ‘Why did we come out of Egypt?’ ”

21 And Moses said, “Six hundred thousand on foot are the people among whom I am, and you have said, ‘I will give meat to them, and they shall eat a month of days.’

22 Shall flocks and herds be slaughtered for them and suffice for them? Or shall all the fish of the sea be gathered for them and suffice for them?”

23 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Is Yahweh’s hand short? Now you shall see whether my word happens to you or not.”

24 And Moses went out and spoke to the people the words of Yahweh, and he gathered seventy men from the elders of the people and set them around the Tent.

25 And Yahweh came down in the cloud and spoke to him, and took from the spirit that was on him and gave on the seventy men, the elders; and when the spirit rested on them, they prophesied, and did not continue.

26 And two men remained in the camp; the name of the one was Eldad, and the name of the second was Medad, and the spirit rested on them — and they were among those written, but they had not gone out to the Tent — and they prophesied in the camp.

27 And the young man ran and told Moses and said, “Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp.”

28 And Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ attendant from his youth, answered and said, “My lord Moses, restrain them.”

29 And Moses said to him, “Are you jealous for me? Would that all Yahweh’s people were prophets, that Yahweh would put his spirit on them!”

Numbers 19

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

2 "This is the statute of the teaching which Yahweh has commanded, saying: Speak to the children of Israel, and they shall take to you a red cow without defect, in which there is no defect, upon which no yoke has come.

3 And you shall give her to Eleazar the priest, and he shall bring her out outside the camp, and she shall be slaughtered before him.

4 And Eleazar the priest shall take from her blood with his finger, and shall sprinkle from her blood toward the front of the Tent of Meeting seven times.

5 And he shall burn the cow in his sight; her skin and her flesh and her blood, with her dung, he shall burn.

6 And the priest shall take cedar wood and hyssop and scarlet of worm, and shall cast it into the midst of the burning of the cow.

7 And the priest shall wash his garments and shall bathe his flesh in water, and afterwards he shall come into the camp, and the priest shall be unclean until the evening.

8 And the one who burns her shall wash his garments in water and shall bathe his flesh in water, and shall be unclean until the evening.

9 And a clean man shall gather the ashes of the cow and shall lay them outside the camp in a clean place, and it shall be kept for the congregation of the children of Israel for the water of impurity; it is a sin offering.

10 And the one who gathers the ashes of the cow shall wash his garments and shall be unclean until the evening, and it shall be for the children of Israel and for the sojourner who sojourns among them as a statute forever.

11 The one who touches a dead person, any human life, shall be unclean seven days.

12 He shall purify himself with it on the third day, and on the seventh day he shall be clean. And if he does not purify himself on the third day and on the seventh day, he shall not be clean.

13 Everyone who touches a dead person, the life of the man who has died, and does not purify himself, has defiled the tabernacle of Yahweh, and that person shall be cut off from Israel, because the water of impurity was not dashed on him; he shall be unclean; his uncleanness is still on him.

14 This is the teaching when a man dies in a tent: everyone who comes into the tent and everything that is in the tent shall be unclean seven days.

15 And every open vessel on which there is no tied covering is unclean.

16 And everyone who in the open field touches one slain by the sword, or a dead person, or a bone of a man, or a grave, shall be unclean seven days.

17 And for the unclean they shall take from the dust of the burning of the sin offering, and he shall put on it living water in a vessel.

18 And a clean man shall take hyssop and dip it in the water and sprinkle on the tent and on all the vessels and on the persons who were there, and on the one who touched the bone, or the slain, or the dead, or the grave.

19 And the clean one shall sprinkle on the unclean on the third day and on the seventh day, and he shall purify him on the seventh day, and he shall wash his garments and bathe in water and shall be clean in the evening.

20 And a man who is unclean and does not purify himself, that person shall be cut off from the midst of the assembly, because he has defiled the sanctuary of Yahweh; the water of impurity was not dashed on him; he is unclean.

21 And it shall be for them as a statute forever, and the one who sprinkles the water of impurity shall wash his garments, and the one who touches the water of impurity shall be unclean until the evening.

22 And everything that the unclean person touches shall be unclean, and the person who touches it shall be unclean until the evening."

6 And if she does come to be married to a husband, while her vows are on her, or the utterance of her lips with which she has bound herself,

7 and her husband hears it and keeps silent to her on the day he hears it, then her vows shall stand, and her bonds with which she has bound herself shall stand.

8 But if, on the day her husband hears it, he forbids her and makes void her vow that is on her, and the utterance of her lips with which she has bound herself, Yahweh shall forgive her.

9 But the vow of a widow, or of a divorced woman — everything with which she has bound herself shall stand on her.

10 And if she vowed in her husband's house, or bound a bond on herself with an oath,

12 But if her husband surely makes them void on the day he hears them, then everything that goes out of her lips, concerning her vows or concerning the bond on herself, shall not stand. Her husband has made them void, and Yahweh shall forgive her.

13 Every vow, and every oath of a bond to afflict herself, her husband may let it stand, or her husband may make it void.

14 But if her husband keeps altogether silent to her from day to day, then he lets all her vows stand, or all her bonds that are on her. He has let them stand, because he kept silent to her on the day he heard them.

15 But if he surely makes them void after he has heard them, then he shall bear her guilt."

18 We shall not return to our houses until the children of Israel have inherited, every man his inheritance.

19 For we shall not inherit with them across the Jordan and beyond, because our inheritance has come to us across the Jordan eastward."

20 And Moses said to them, "If you do this thing, if you arm yourselves before Yahweh for the war,

21 and every armed man of you crosses the Jordan before Yahweh, until he has driven out his enemies from before him,

22 and the land is subdued before Yahweh, and afterward you return, then you shall be clear before Yahweh and before Israel, and this land shall be yours for a possession before Yahweh.

23 But if you do not do so, behold, you have sinned against Yahweh. And know your sin, which will find you.

24 Build cities for your little ones, and folds for your flock, and do what goes out of your mouth."

25 And the children of Gad and the children of Reuben spoke to Moses, saying, "Your slaves shall do as my lord commands.

26 Our little ones, our wives, our livestock, and all our beasts shall be there in the cities of Gilead,

27 and your slaves shall cross, every man armed for the army, before Yahweh for the war, as my lord says."

28 And Moses commanded concerning them to Eleazar the priest, and to Joshua the son of Nun, and to the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel.

29 And Moses said to them, "If the children of Gad and the children of Reuben cross the Jordan with you, every man armed for the war before Yahweh, and the land is subdued before you, then you shall give them the land of Gilead for a possession.

30 But if they do not cross armed with you, then they shall have a possession among you in the land of Canaan."

31 And the children of Gad and the children of Reuben answered, saying, "What Yahweh has spoken to your slaves, so we shall do.

32 We shall cross armed before Yahweh into the land of Canaan, and with us the possession of our inheritance across the Jordan."

33 And Moses gave to them — to the children of Gad, and to the children of Reuben, and to half the tribe of Manasseh the son of Joseph — the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land by its cities with their borders, the cities of the land all around.

34 And the children of Gad built Dibon, and Ataroth, and Aroer,

35 and Atroth Shophan, and Jazer, and Jogbehah,

36 and Beth Nimrah, and Beth Haran — fortified cities, and folds for the flock.

37 And the children of Reuben built Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Kiriathaim,

38 and Nebo, and Baal Meon — their names changed — and Sibmah. And they called the names of the cities that they built by names.

39 And the children of Machir the son of Manasseh went to Gilead and took it, and dispossessed the Amorites who were in it.

40 And Moses gave Gilead to Machir the son of Manasseh, and he dwelt in it.

41 And Jair the son of Manasseh went and took their villages, and called them Havvoth Jair.

42 And Nobah went and took Kenath and its villages, and called it Nobah, by his own name.

Numbers 33

1 These are the journeys of the children of Israel, who went out from the land of Egypt by their armies, under the hand of Moses and Aaron.

2 And Moses wrote their starting points according to their journeys, by the command of Yahweh; and these are their journeys according to their starting points.

3 And they set out from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the day after the Passover the children of Israel went out with a high hand, in the sight of all the Egyptians,

4 while the Egyptians were burying those whom Yahweh had struck among them, all the firstborn; and on their gods Yahweh executed judgments.

5 And the children of Israel set out from Rameses, and encamped at Succoth.

6 And they set out from Succoth, and encamped at Etham, which is at the edge of the wilderness.

7 And they set out from Etham, and turned back to Pihahiroth, which is before Baal Zephon, and they encamped before Migdol.

8 And they set out from before Hahiroth, and crossed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness, and they went a journey of three days in the wilderness of Etham, and encamped at Marah.

9 And they set out from Marah, and came to Elim; and in Elim there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they encamped there.

10 And they set out from Elim, and encamped by the Red Sea.

11 And they set out from the Red Sea, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin.

12 And they set out from the wilderness of Sin, and encamped at Dophkah.

13 And they set out from Dophkah, and encamped at Alush.

14 And they set out from Alush, and encamped at Rephidim, and there was no water there for the people to drink.

15 And they set out from Rephidim, and encamped at the wilderness of Sinai.

16 And they set out from the wilderness of Sinai, and encamped at Kibroth Hattaavah.

17 And they set out from Kibroth Hattaavah, and encamped at Hazeroth.

18 And they set out from Hazeroth, and encamped at Rithmah.

19 And they set out from Rithmah, and encamped at Rimmon Perez.

20 And they set out from Rimmon Perez, and encamped at Libnah.

21 And they set out from Libnah, and encamped at Rissah.

22 And they set out from Rissah, and encamped at Kehelathah.

23 And they set out from Kehelathah, and encamped at Mount Shepher.

24 And they set out from Mount Shepher, and encamped at Haradah.

25 And they set out from Haradah, and encamped at Makheloth.

26 And they set out from Makheloth, and encamped at Tahath.

27 And they set out from Tahath, and encamped at Terah.

28 And they set out from Terah, and encamped at Mithkah.

29 And they set out from Mithkah, and encamped at Hashmonah.

30 And they set out from Hashmonah, and encamped at Moseroth.

31 And they set out from Moseroth, and encamped at Bene Jaakan.

32 And they set out from Bene Jaakan, and encamped at Hor Haggidgad.

33 And they set out from Hor Haggidgad, and encamped at Jotbathah.

34 And they set out from Jotbathah, and encamped at Abronah.

35 And they set out from Abronah, and encamped at Ezion Geber.

36 And they set out from Ezion Geber, and encamped in the wilderness of Zin — that is, Kadesh.

37 And they set out from Kadesh, and encamped at Mount Hor, at the edge of the land of Edom.

38 And Aaron the priest went up to Mount Hor by the command of Yahweh, and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel had come out of the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first of the month.

39 And Aaron was a hundred and twenty-three years old when he died at Mount Hor.

40 And the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who dwelt in the Negev in the land of Canaan, heard of the coming of the children of Israel.

45 And they set out from Iyim, and encamped at Dibon Gad.

47 And they set out from Almon Diblathaim, and encamped in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.

48 And they set out from the mountains of Abarim, and encamped in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

49 And they encamped by the Jordan, from Beth Jeshimoth to Abel Shittim, in the plains of Moab.

50 And Yahweh spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

55 But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you, then those of them whom you let remain shall be as pricks in your eyes and as thorns in your sides, and they shall harass you in the land in which you dwell.

56 And it shall be that as I thought to do to them, so I will do to you.

Numbers 35

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

2 Command the children of Israel, and they shall give to the Levites, from the inheritance of their possession, cities to dwell in; and pasture land around the cities you shall give to the Levites.

3 And the cities shall be theirs to dwell in, and their pasture lands shall be for their cattle, and for their goods, and for all their animals.

4 And the pasture lands of the cities, which you shall give to the Levites, shall be from the wall of the city and outward, a thousand cubits all around.

5 And you shall measure outside the city: the east side, two thousand cubits; and the south side, two thousand cubits; and the west side, two thousand cubits; and the north side, two thousand cubits, with the city in the middle. This shall be theirs, the pasture lands of the cities.

6 And the cities that you shall give to the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge, which you shall give for the manslayer to flee there; and besides them you shall give forty-two cities.

7 All the cities that you shall give to the Levites shall be forty-eight cities — them, and their pasture lands.

8 And the cities that you shall give from the possession of the children of Israel: from the many you shall take many, and from the few you shall take few; each man, according to his inheritance that he inherits, shall give of his cities to the Levites.

9 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

10 Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: When you cross over the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

11 you shall appoint for yourselves cities; they shall be cities of refuge for you, and a manslayer who strikes a person unwittingly shall flee there.

12 And the cities shall be for you as a refuge from the avenger, so that the manslayer shall not die until he stands before the congregation for judgment.

13 And the cities that you shall give shall be six cities of refuge for you.

14 Three of the cities you shall give beyond the Jordan, and three of the cities you shall give in the land of Canaan; they shall be cities of refuge.

15 For the children of Israel, and for the stranger, and for the settler in their midst, these six cities shall be for a refuge, for everyone who strikes a person unwittingly to flee there.

16 And if he struck him with an instrument of iron, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

17 And if he struck him with a hand-stone, by which a man may die, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

18 Or if he struck him with a hand-weapon of wood, by which a man may die, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.

19 The avenger of blood, he shall put the murderer to death; when he meets him, he shall put him to death.

20 And if he pushed him in hatred, or hurled at him, lying in wait, and he died,

21 or struck him with his hand in enmity, and he died, the one who struck him shall surely be put to death; he is a murderer; the avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death when he meets him.

22 But if he pushed him suddenly, without enmity, or hurled any object at him without lying in wait,

23 or with any stone, by which a man may die, without seeing him, and made it fall on him, and he died, and he was not an enemy to him and was not seeking his harm,

24 then the congregation shall judge between the one who struck and the avenger of blood, according to these ordinances.

25 And the congregation shall rescue the manslayer from the hand of the avenger of blood, and the congregation shall restore him to the city of his refuge, to which he had fled, and he shall dwell in it until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil.

26 But if the manslayer at any time goes out beyond the border of the city of his refuge, to which he flees,

27 and the avenger of blood finds him outside the border of the city of his refuge, and the avenger of blood kills the manslayer, there is no bloodguilt for him,

28 because in the city of his refuge he shall remain until the death of the high priest; and after the death of the high priest the manslayer shall return to the land of his possession.

29 And these things shall be for you as a statute of judgment, throughout your generations, in all your dwellings.

30 Everyone who strikes a person dead: on the testimony of witnesses the murderer shall be put to death; and one witness shall not testify against a person, for him to die.

31 And you shall take no ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death, for he shall surely be put to death.

32 And you shall take no ransom for one who has fled to the city of his refuge, to return to dwell in the land before the death of the priest.

33 And you shall not pollute the land in which you are, for the blood — it pollutes the land; and no atonement shall be made for the land, for the blood that was shed in it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

34 And you shall not defile the land in which you dwell, in whose midst I dwell; for I, Yahweh, dwell in the midst of the children of Israel.

Numbers 36

1 And the heads of the fathers of the family of the children of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, of the families of the children of Joseph, came near and spoke before Moses and before the princes, the heads of the fathers of the children of Israel,

2 and they said: Yahweh commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the children of Israel, and my lord was commanded by Yahweh to give the inheritance of Zelophehad our brother to his daughters.

3 And if they become wives to one of the sons of the tribes of the children of Israel, their inheritance shall be taken away from the inheritance of our fathers, and shall be added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they shall belong, and from the lot of our inheritance it shall be taken away.

4 And when the jubilee of the children of Israel comes, their inheritance shall be added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they shall belong, and from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers their inheritance shall be taken away.

5 And Moses commanded the children of Israel by the command of Yahweh, saying: The tribe of the children of Joseph speaks rightly.

6 This is the word that Yahweh commanded concerning the daughters of Zelophehad, saying: They shall become wives to one who is good in their eyes; only, to the family of the tribe of their father shall they become wives.

7 And no inheritance of the children of Israel shall turn from tribe to tribe, for each man shall cleave to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers — the children of Israel.

8 And every daughter who inherits an inheritance from the tribes of the children of Israel shall become a wife to one of the family of the tribe of her father, so that the children of Israel may inherit, each man, the inheritance of his fathers.

9 And no inheritance shall turn from one tribe to another tribe, for each man shall cleave to his own inheritance — the tribes of the children of Israel.

10 As Yahweh commanded Moses, so the daughters of Zelophehad did:

11 and Mahlah, Tirzah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, became wives to the sons of their uncles.

12 To men of the families of the children of Manasseh the son of Joseph they became wives, and their inheritance remained with the tribe of the family of their father.

13 These are the commandments and the ordinances that Yahweh commanded, by the hand of Moses, to the children of Israel, in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.
