Habakkuk 1

1 The burden which Habakkuk the prophet saw.
2 How long, Yahweh, will I cry out, and you will not hear? I cry out to you: Violence! and you will not save.
3 Why do you show me iniquity and look on trouble? Destruction and violence are before me; strife is there, and contention rises up.
4 Therefore the law is paralyzed, and justice never goes forth; for the wicked surround the righteous; therefore justice goes forth perverted.
5 Look among the nations and watch, and be utterly amazed; for I am working a work in your days that you will not believe, though it is told.
6 For see, I am raising up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hasty nation, who march through the breadth of the earth to possess dwelling places that are not theirs.
7 They are dreadful and fearsome; their judgment and their dignity proceed from themselves.
8 Their horses are swifter than leopards and fiercer than the evening wolves. Their horsemen gallop onward; their horsemen come from far away; they fly like an eagle hastening to devour.
9 All of them come for violence; their faces are set forward; they gather captives like sand.
10 They scoff at kings, and rulers are a laughingstock to them. They laugh at every stronghold; they heap up earth and take it.
11 Then they sweep by like the wind and go on, and are guilty; their strength is their god.
12 Are you not from everlasting, Yahweh my God, my Holy One? We shall not die. Yahweh, you have appointed him for judgment; you, Rock, have established him for correction.
13 Your eyes are too pure to look on evil, and you cannot look on trouble. Why do you look on the treacherous and keep silent when the wicked swallows up the one who is more righteous than he?
14 You make people like the fish of the sea, like the creeping things that have no ruler over them.
15 He takes up all of them with a hook; he drags them in his net and gathers them in his dragnet; therefore he rejoices and is glad.
16 Therefore he sacrifices to his net and burns incense to his dragnet, because by them his portion is rich and his food is plentiful.
17 Will he keep on emptying his net, and continually slaughter nations without mercy?

Habakkuk 2

1 I will stand at my watchpost and station myself on the rampart; I will look out to see what he will say to me, and what I shall answer concerning my complaint.
2 Yahweh answered me and said: Write the vision and make it plain on tablets, so that the one who reads it may run.
3 For the vision is still for the appointed time; it hastens toward the end and will not lie. If it delays, wait for it, for it will surely come; it will not be late.
4 Behold, his soul is puffed up; it is not upright in him. But the righteous shall live by his faithfulness.
5 Moreover, wine is treacherous; the arrogant man does not rest. He has enlarged his appetite like Sheol, and like death he is never satisfied; he gathers to himself all the nations and collects to himself all the peoples.
6 Will not all of these take up a taunt against him and a mocking riddle against him, and say: Woe to him who increases what is not his — how long? — and loads himself down with pledges!
7 Will not your creditors rise up suddenly, and those who shake you awaken, and you will become plunder for them?
8 Because you have plundered many nations, all the remnant of the peoples will plunder you, because of human blood and the violence done to the land, to the city and to all who dwell in it.
9 Woe to him who gains evil gain for his house, to set his nest on high, to be delivered from the hand of evil.
10 You have devised shame for your house, cutting off many peoples, and you have sinned against your own life.
11 For the stone will cry out from the wall, and the beam from the woodwork will answer it.
12 Woe to him who builds a city with blood and establishes a town by injustice.
13 Behold, is it not from Yahweh of Armies that the peoples labor for the fire, and the nations weary themselves for nothing?
14 For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of Yahweh, as the waters cover the sea.
15 Woe to him who gives his neighbor drink, adding your poison and making him drunk, so that you may gaze on their nakedness.
16 You are filled with shame instead of glory. Drink, you also, and be exposed; the cup of Yahweh's right hand will turn upon you, and utter disgrace will be upon your glory.
17 For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you, and the destruction of the animals will terrify you, because of human blood and the violence done to the land, to the city and to all who dwell in it.
18 What profit is a carved image, that its maker has carved it, or a cast image, a teacher of lies, that the maker of its form trusts in it, to make mute idols?
19 Woe to him who says to the wood: Awake! and to the silent stone: Arise! Can it teach? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all in it.
20 But Yahweh is in his holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before him.

Habakkuk 3

1 A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, set to Shigionoth.
2 Yahweh, I have heard the report of you, and I am afraid. Yahweh, revive your work in the midst of the years; in the midst of the years make it known; in wrath remember mercy.
3 God comes from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah. His glory covered the heavens, and the earth is full of his praise.
4 His splendor is like the light; rays shine from his hand, where his power is hidden.
5 Before him goes plague, and fiery bolts go out at his feet.
6 He stood, and shook the earth; he looked, and made the nations tremble. The everlasting mountains were dashed in pieces, the ancient hills bowed down; his goings are everlasting.
7 I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction; the curtains of the land of Midian tremble.
8 Was Yahweh angry with the rivers? Was your anger against the rivers, or your wrath against the sea, that you ride on your horses, on your chariots of salvation?
9 Your bow is laid bare; the rods of the word are sworn. Selah. You split the earth with rivers.
10 The mountains saw you, and trembled; a storm of waters passed over; the deep gave its voice, and lifted its hands on high.
11 The sun and moon stood still in their dwelling; at the light of your arrows as they go, at the gleam of your glittering spear.
12 In indignation you march through the earth; in anger you thresh the nations.
13 You went out for the salvation of your people, for the salvation of your anointed. You crushed the head from the house of the wicked, laying bare the foundation to the neck. Selah.
14 You pierced the head of his warriors with his own rods; they storm to scatter me, their rejoicing like one devouring the poor in secret.
15 You trod the sea with your horses, the foaming of mighty waters.
16 I heard, and my belly trembled; my lips quivered at the sound; rottenness enters into my bones, and I tremble where I stand, as I wait quietly for the day of trouble, when the people come up who invade us.
17 For though the fig tree does not blossom, and there is no fruit on the vines, the produce of the olive fails, and the fields yield no food, the flock is cut off from the fold, and there is no herd in the stalls;
18 Yet I will rejoice in Yahweh; I will exult in the God of my salvation.
19 The Lord Yahweh is my strength, and he makes my feet like the feet of a deer, and makes me walk on my high places. For the music director, on my stringed instruments.
