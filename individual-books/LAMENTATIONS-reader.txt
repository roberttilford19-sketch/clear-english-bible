Lamentations 1

1 How the city sits solitary, that was full of people! She has become like a widow, she who was great among the nations. She who was a princess among the provinces has become a slave.
2 She weeps bitterly in the night, and her tears are on her cheeks. Among all her lovers she has no one to comfort her. All her friends have dealt treacherously with her; they have become her enemies.
3 Judah has gone into exile because of affliction and because of great servitude. She dwells among the nations; she finds no rest. All her pursuers overtook her between the straits.
4 The roads to Zion mourn, because no one comes to the appointed festival. All her gates are desolate; her priests sigh; her virgins are afflicted, and she herself is in bitterness.
5 Her adversaries have become the head; her enemies are at ease, for Yahweh has afflicted her for the multitude of her transgressions. Her young children have gone into captivity before the adversary.
6 All her splendor has departed from the daughter of Zion. Her princes have become like deer that find no pasture, and they have gone without strength before the pursuer.
7 Jerusalem remembers in the days of her affliction and her wanderings all her treasures that were from the days of old. When her people fell into the hand of the adversary and there was no one to help her, the adversaries saw her; they mocked at her downfall.
8 Jerusalem has sinned grievously; therefore she has become an unclean thing. All who honored her despise her, because they have seen her nakedness. She herself also sighs and turns backward.
9 Her uncleanness was in her skirts; she did not remember her end, and she has come down astoundingly. She has no comforter. See, Yahweh, my affliction, for the enemy has magnified himself.
10 The adversary has spread out his hand over all her treasures, for she has seen nations enter her sanctuary, those concerning whom you commanded that they should not enter into your assembly.
11 All her people sigh; they seek bread. They have given their treasures for food, to revive their soul. See, Yahweh, and look, for I have become despised.
12 Is it nothing to you, all you who pass by? Look and see if there is any sorrow like my sorrow, which was dealt out to me, with which Yahweh has afflicted me in the day of his fierce anger.
13 From on high he sent fire into my bones, and it has prevailed against them. He has spread a net for my feet; he has turned me back. He has made me desolate, faint all the day.
14 The yoke of my transgressions is bound by his hand; they are knit together, they have come up on my neck; he has made my strength fail. The Lord has delivered me into hands I am not able to stand against.
15 The Lord has spurned all my mighty men in my midst; he has called an appointed assembly against me to crush my young men. The Lord has trodden the virgin, the daughter of Judah, as in a winepress.
16 For these things I weep; my eye, my eye runs down with water, because the comforter who should revive my soul is far from me. My children are desolate, because the enemy has prevailed.
17 Zion spreads out her hands; there is no one to comfort her. Yahweh has commanded concerning Jacob that those around him should be his adversaries. Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them.
18 Yahweh is righteous, for I have rebelled against his command. Hear now, all you peoples, and see my sorrow. My virgins and my young men have gone into captivity.
19 I called for my lovers; they deceived me. My priests and my elders perished in the city, while they sought food for themselves to revive their soul.
20 See, Yahweh, for I am in distress. My insides churn; my heart turns over within me, for I have grievously rebelled. Outside, the sword bereaves; at home, it is like death.
21 They have heard that I sigh; there is no one to comfort me. All my enemies have heard of my trouble; they are glad that you have done it. You will bring the day that you proclaimed, and they will be like me.
22 Let all their wickedness come before you, and do to them as you have done to me for all my transgressions, for my sighs are many and my heart is faint.

Lamentations 2

1 How the Lord has covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger! He has cast down from heaven to earth the beauty of Israel, and has not remembered his footstool in the day of his anger.
2 The Lord has swallowed up and did not spare all the dwellings of Jacob. In his wrath he has thrown down the strongholds of the daughter of Judah; he has brought them down to the ground; he has profaned the kingdom and its princes.
3 He has cut off in fierce anger all the horn of Israel. He has drawn back his right hand from before the enemy. He has burned in Jacob like a flaming fire that devours all around.
4 He has bent his bow like an enemy; his right hand is set like an adversary, and he has killed all that were pleasant to the eye. In the tent of the daughter of Zion he has poured out his wrath like fire.
5 The Lord has become like an enemy; he has swallowed up Israel, he has swallowed up all her palaces, he has destroyed his strongholds, and he has multiplied mourning and lamentation in the daughter of Judah.
6 He has torn down his booth like a garden; he has destroyed his appointed place. Yahweh has caused appointed festival and Sabbath to be forgotten in Zion, and in the indignation of his anger he has despised king and priest.
7 The Lord has cast off his altar; he has abhorred his sanctuary. He has delivered the walls of her palaces into the hand of the enemy. They have made a noise in the house of Yahweh as on the day of an appointed festival.
8 Yahweh purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion. He stretched out the line; he did not withdraw his hand from destroying. He made rampart and wall mourn; together they languish.
9 Her gates have sunk into the ground; he has destroyed and broken her bars. Her king and her princes are among the nations; there is no instruction. Her prophets also find no vision from Yahweh.
10 The elders of the daughter of Zion sit on the ground in silence. They have cast dust on their heads; they have girded themselves with sackcloth. The virgins of Jerusalem bow their heads to the ground.
11 My eyes are spent with tears; my insides churn; my liver is poured out on the earth because of the destruction of the daughter of my people, because young children and infants faint in the streets of the city.
12 They say to their mothers: Where is grain and wine? as they faint like the wounded in the streets of the city, as their soul is poured out into their mothers' bosom.
13 What can I testify for you? What can I liken to you, daughter of Jerusalem? What can I compare to you, that I may comfort you, virgin daughter of Zion? For your breach is great like the sea; who can heal you?
14 Your prophets have seen for you false and worthless visions; they have not uncovered your iniquity, to restore your fortunes, but they have seen for you false oracles and delusions.
15 All who pass by clap their hands at you; they hiss and shake their head at the daughter of Jerusalem: Is this the city that they called the perfection of beauty, the joy of the whole earth?
16 All your enemies have opened their mouth against you; they hiss and gnash their teeth; they say: We have swallowed her up. Surely this is the day we waited for; we have found it, we have seen it.
17 Yahweh has done what he planned; he has fulfilled his word that he commanded in the days of old. He has thrown down and has not pitied, and he has made the enemy rejoice over you; he has exalted the horn of your adversaries.
18 Their heart cried out to the Lord. Wall of the daughter of Zion, let tears run down like a river day and night. Give yourself no rest; let the pupil of your eye not be still.
19 Arise, cry out in the night, at the beginning of the watches. Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord. Lift up your hands to him for the life of your young children, who faint from hunger at the head of every street.
20 See, Yahweh, and look: to whom have you done this? Should women eat their offspring, the children they have nursed? Should priest and prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?
21 Young and old lie on the ground in the streets. My virgins and my young men have fallen by the sword. You have killed in the day of your anger; you have slaughtered and have not pitied.
22 You have summoned, as on a day of appointed festival, my terrors from all around, and on the day of Yahweh's anger there was no escapee or survivor. Those I nursed and raised, my enemy has finished off.

Lamentations 3

1 I am the man who has seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.
2 He has led me and made me walk in darkness, and not in light.
3 Surely against me he turns his hand again and again all day long.
4 He has worn out my flesh and my skin; he has broken my bones.
5 He has built against me, and surrounded me with gall and hardship.
6 He has made me dwell in dark places, like those who have been long dead.
7 He has walled me in, so that I cannot go out; he has made my chain heavy.
8 Even when I cry out and call for help, he shuts out my prayer.
9 He has walled up my ways with cut stone; he has made my paths crooked.
10 He is to me a bear lying in wait, a lion in hiding places.
11 He has turned aside my ways and torn me in pieces; he has made me desolate.
12 He has bent his bow, and set me as a target for the arrow.
13 He has caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my kidneys.
14 I have become a laughingstock to all my people, their song all day long.
15 He has filled me with bitterness; he has sated me with wormwood.
16 He has broken my teeth with gravel; he has made me crouch in the ashes.
17 My soul is cast off from peace; I have forgotten good.
18 I said: My strength has perished, and my hope from Yahweh.
19 Remember my affliction and my wandering, the wormwood and the gall.
20 My soul surely remembers, and it is bowed down within me.
21 This I bring back to my heart; therefore I will hope.
22 The loyal loves of Yahweh: for we are not consumed, for his compassions do not fail.
23 They are new every morning; great is your faithfulness.
24 Yahweh is my portion, says my soul; therefore I will hope in him.
25 Yahweh is good to those who wait for him, to the soul that seeks him.
26 It is good that one wait and be silent for the salvation of Yahweh.
27 It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.
28 Let him sit alone and be silent, for he has laid it on him.
29 Let him put his mouth in the dust; perhaps there is hope.
30 Let him give his cheek to the one who strikes him; let him be filled with reproach.
31 For the Lord will not cast off forever;
32 for though he causes grief, he will have compassion according to the multitude of his loyal loves;
33 for he does not afflict from his heart, nor grieve the children of men.
34 To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,
35 to turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most High,
36 to subvert a man in his cause: the Lord does not approve.
37 Who is this who speaks, and it comes to pass, when the Lord has not commanded it?
38 Do not evils and the good come out from the mouth of the Most High?
39 Why should a living man complain, a man over his sins?
40 Let us search our ways and examine them, and let us return to Yahweh.
41 Let us lift up our heart with our hands to God in the heavens.
42 We have transgressed and rebelled; you have not pardoned.
43 You have covered us with anger and pursued us; you have killed, you have not pitied.
44 You have covered yourself with a cloud, so that no prayer can pass through.
45 You have made us offscouring and refuse among the peoples.
46 All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.
47 Terror and the pit have come upon us, devastation and destruction.
48 My eye runs down with streams of water, over the destruction of the daughter of my people.
49 My eye pours out and does not cease, without any rest,
50 until Yahweh looks down and sees from heaven.
51 My eye afflicts my soul, because of all the daughters of my city.
52 My enemies have hunted me like a bird, without cause.
53 They cut off my life in the pit, and cast a stone on me.
54 Waters flowed over my head; I said: I am cut off.
55 I called on your name, Yahweh, from the lowest pit.
56 You heard my voice: do not hide your ear from my sighing, from my cry.
57 You drew near on the day I called on you; you said: Do not fear.
58 Lord, you have pleaded the causes of my soul; you have redeemed my life.
59 You have seen, Yahweh, my wrong; judge my cause.
60 You have seen all their vengeance, all their plans against me.
61 You have heard their reproach, Yahweh, all their plans against me,
62 the lips of those who rise against me and their muttering against me all day long.
63 Look at their sitting down and their rising up; I am their song.
64 You will repay them a recompense, Yahweh, according to the work of their hands.
65 You will give them hardness of heart, your curse upon them.
66 You will pursue them in anger and destroy them from under the heavens of Yahweh.

Lamentations 4

1 How the gold has grown dim! How the fine gold has changed! The holy stones are poured out at the head of every street.
2 The precious sons of Zion, weighed against fine gold — how they are counted as clay jars, the work of a potter's hands!
3 Even the jackals bare the breast and nurse their young; the daughter of my people has become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness.
4 The tongue of the nursing child sticks to the roof of its mouth in thirst; the little children ask for bread, and no one breaks it for them.
5 Those who ate delicacies are desolate in the streets; those reared on scarlet embrace dunghills.
6 The guilt of the daughter of my people is greater than the sin of Sodom, which was overthrown in a moment, and no hands were laid on her.
7 Her consecrated ones were purer than snow, whiter than milk; their bodies were redder than coral, their form like sapphire.
8 Their appearance is darker than blackness; they are not recognized in the streets; their skin shrivels on their bones; it has dried up, it has become like wood.
9 Better off were those slain by the sword than those slain by hunger, for these waste away, pierced through, for lack of the produce of the field.
10 The hands of compassionate women have boiled their own children; they became their food in the breaking of the daughter of my people.
11 Yahweh has spent his wrath; he has poured out his fierce anger; he has kindled a fire in Zion, and it has devoured its foundations.
12 The kings of the earth did not believe, nor all the inhabitants of the world, that an adversary and enemy would enter the gates of Jerusalem.
13 It is because of the sins of her prophets and the guilt of her priests, who shed the blood of the righteous in her midst.
14 They wandered blind in the streets; they were defiled with blood, so that no one could touch their garments.
15 Away, unclean! they cried to them. Away, away, do not touch! When they fled away and wandered, it was said among the nations: They can no longer sojourn here.
16 The face of Yahweh has scattered them; he will no longer look on them. They did not honor the faces of priests, and they showed no favor to elders.
17 Still our eyes fail, looking in vain for our help; in our watching we watched for a nation that would not save.
18 They hunted our steps, so that we could not walk in our streets; our end drew near, our days were fulfilled, for our end had come.
19 Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles of the heavens; on the mountains they chased us hotly, in the wilderness they lay in wait for us.
20 The breath of our nostrils, the anointed of Yahweh, was caught in their pits, of whom we said: In his shadow we will live among the nations.
21 Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom, who dwell in the land of Uz; the cup will pass to you also; you will get drunk and strip yourself bare.
22 Your guilt is ended, daughter of Zion; he will no longer exile you. He has visited your guilt, daughter of Edom; he has uncovered your sins.

Lamentations 5

1 Remember, Yahweh, what has happened to us; look and see our disgrace.
2 Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers, our houses to foreigners.
3 We have become orphans, and there is no father; our mothers are like widows.
4 Our water we drink for silver; our wood comes to us at a price.
5 Our pursuers are on our necks; we labor, and we are given no rest.
6 We have given a hand to Egypt, and to Assyria, to be satisfied with bread.
7 Our fathers sinned, and they are no more, and we bear their guilt.
8 Slaves rule over us; there is no one to deliver us from their hand.
9 At the risk of our lives we bring in our bread, because of the sword of the wilderness.
10 Our skin is scorched like an oven from the burning heat of famine.
11 They violated the women in Zion, the virgins in the cities of Judah.
12 Princes were hanged up by their hands; the faces of elders were not honored.
13 Young men bear the millstone, and boys stagger under the wood.
14 Elders have ceased from the gate, young men from their music.
15 The joy of our heart has ceased; our dancing has turned to mourning.
16 The crown has fallen from our head. Woe to us now, for we have sinned!
17 For this our heart is faint, and for these things our eyes are dim,
18 for the mountain of Zion, which is desolate; foxes walk on it.
19 You, Yahweh, remain forever; your throne is from generation to generation.
20 Why do you forget us forever, and forsake us for length of days?
21 Restore us to yourself, Yahweh, that we may be restored; renew our days as of old.
22 Unless you have utterly rejected us and are angry with us beyond measure.
