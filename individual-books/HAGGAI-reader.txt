Haggai 1

1 In the second year of Darius the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of Yahweh came by the hand of Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, saying:
2 Thus says Yahweh of hosts: This people have said: The time has not come, the time for the house of Yahweh to be built.
3 Then the word of Yahweh came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying:
4 Is it a time for you yourselves to live in your paneled houses, while this house lies in ruins?
5 Now therefore, thus says Yahweh of hosts: Consider your ways.
6 You have sown much and harvested little; you eat, but there is no satisfaction; you drink, but there is no fullness; you clothe yourselves, but there is no warmth; and the one who earns wages earns wages for a bag with holes.
7 Thus says Yahweh of hosts: Consider your ways.
8 Go up to the mountain and bring wood and build the house, and I will take pleasure in it and I will be glorified, says Yahweh.
9 You looked for much, and see, it came to little; and when you brought it home, I blew on it. Why? The declaration of Yahweh of hosts: Because of my house, which lies in ruins, while you run, each one, to his own house.
10 Therefore, because of you, the heavens have withheld dew, and the earth has withheld its produce.
11 And I called a drought on the land and on the mountains, and on the grain and on the new wine and on the oil, and on whatever the ground brings forth, and on people and on livestock, and on all the labor of hands.
12 Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people obeyed the voice of Yahweh their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, as Yahweh their God had sent him, and the people feared before Yahweh.
13 Then Haggai, the messenger of Yahweh, spoke the message of Yahweh to the people, saying: I am with you — the declaration of Yahweh.
14 And Yahweh stirred the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people, and they came and did work on the house of Yahweh of hosts, their God,
15 on the twenty-fourth day of the month, in the sixth month, in the second year of Darius the king.

Haggai 2

1 In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the word of Yahweh came through Haggai the prophet, saying:
2 Speak now to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying:
3 Who among you is left who saw this house in its former glory? And how do you see it now? Is it not like nothing in your eyes?
4 And now be strong, Zerubbabel, the declaration of Yahweh, and be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and be strong, all you people of the land, the declaration of Yahweh, and work, for I am with you, the declaration of Yahweh of hosts.
5 The word that I covenanted with you when you came out of Egypt, and my Spirit stands among you: do not be afraid.
6 For thus says Yahweh of hosts: Once more, and it is only a little while, and I will shake the heavens and the earth and the sea and the dry land;
7 and I will shake all nations, and the treasure of all nations shall come, and I will fill this house with glory, says Yahweh of hosts.
8 The silver is mine and the gold is mine, the declaration of Yahweh of hosts.
9 The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former, says Yahweh of hosts, and in this place I will give peace, the declaration of Yahweh of hosts.
10 On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of Yahweh came to Haggai the prophet, saying:
11 Thus says Yahweh of hosts: Ask the priests now for a ruling, saying:
12 If a man carries holy flesh in the fold of his garment, and his fold touches the bread or the stew or the wine or the oil or any food, will it become holy? And the priests answered and said: No.
13 Then Haggai said: If a person who is unclean by a dead body touches any of these, will it become unclean? And the priests answered and said: It will become unclean.
14 Then Haggai answered and said: So is this people, and so is this nation before me, the declaration of Yahweh, and so is every work of their hands, and what they offer there is unclean.
15 And now, please consider from this day onward, before a stone was laid on a stone in the temple of Yahweh,
16 in that time, when one came to a heap of twenty, there were only ten; when one came to the winepress to draw out fifty, there were only twenty;
17 I struck you with scorching and with mildew and with hail, all the work of your hands, and still you did not turn to me, the declaration of Yahweh.
18 Please consider from this day onward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day the foundation of the temple of Yahweh was laid: consider it.
19 Is the seed still in the barn? As yet the vine and the fig tree and the pomegranate and the olive tree have not borne fruit. From this day I will bless you.
20 And the word of Yahweh came a second time to Haggai on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying:
21 Speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, saying: I will shake the heavens and the earth;
22 and I will overthrow the throne of kingdoms and destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations, and I will overthrow the chariots and their riders, and the horses and their riders will fall, each by the sword of his brother.
23 On that day, the declaration of Yahweh of hosts, I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant, the declaration of Yahweh, and I will make you like a signet ring, for I have chosen you, the declaration of Yahweh of hosts.
