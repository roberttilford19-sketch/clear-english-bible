Ecclesiastes 1

1 The words of Qoheleth, the son of David, king in Jerusalem.
2 Breath of breaths, says Qoheleth; breath of breaths, all is breath.
3 What gain does a man have from all his toil at which he toils under the sun?
4 A generation goes, and a generation comes, but the earth stands forever.
5 The sun rises, and the sun goes down, and hurries to its place, where it rises.
6 The wind goes toward the south and turns around to the north; turning, turning, the wind goes, and the wind returns on its circuits.
7 All the streams go to the sea, yet the sea is not full; to the place where the streams go, there they go again.
8 All things are wearisome; a man cannot speak it. The eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor is the ear filled with hearing.
9 What has been is what will be, and what has been done is what will be done, and there is nothing new under the sun.
10 Is there a thing of which it is said: See, this is new? It has already been, in the ages that were before us.
11 There is no memory of those who came before, and for those who come later there will be no memory either, among those who come after them.
12 I, Qoheleth, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.
13 And I set my heart to seek and to search out by wisdom all that is done under heaven; it is a grievous task that God has given to the sons of men to be afflicted with.
14 I saw all the works that are done under the sun, and see, all is breath and a striving after wind.
15 What is crooked cannot be made straight, and what is lacking cannot be counted.
16 I spoke with my heart, saying: See, I have gained great wisdom, and added more, beyond all who were before me over Jerusalem, and my heart has seen much wisdom and knowledge.
17 And I set my heart to know wisdom, and to know madness and folly; I perceived that this also is a striving after wind.
18 For in much wisdom is much grief, and one who increases knowledge increases sorrow.

Ecclesiastes 2

1 I said in my heart: Come now, I will try you with mirth, and enjoy pleasure; and see, this also was breath.
2 I said of laughter: It is mad; and of mirth: What does this do?
3 I searched in my heart how to cheer my flesh with wine, my heart guiding me with wisdom, and how to lay hold on folly, until I could see what is good for the sons of men to do under heaven during the number of the days of their life.
4 I made great works. I built houses for myself. I planted vineyards for myself.
5 I made gardens and orchards for myself, and planted in them trees of every kind of fruit.
6 I made pools of water for myself, to water from them a forest growing with trees.
7 I bought male servants and female servants, and I had servants born in my house; also I had great possessions of herds and flocks, more than all who were before me in Jerusalem.
8 I gathered for myself also silver and gold, and the treasure of kings and the provinces; I got for myself male singers and female singers, and the delights of the sons of men, women very many.
9 And I became great and increased more than all who were before me in Jerusalem; also my wisdom stood by me.
10 And all that my eyes asked for, I did not withhold from them; I did not hold back my heart from any joy, for my heart had joy from all my toil, and this was my portion from all my toil.
11 And I turned to all my works that my hands had made, and to the toil that I had toiled to do, and see, all was breath and a striving after wind, and there was no gain under the sun.
12 And I turned to see wisdom and madness and folly; for what can the man do who comes after the king, but what has already been done?
13 And I saw that wisdom has an advantage over folly, as light has an advantage over darkness.
14 The wise man has his eyes in his head, but the fool walks in darkness; and I also knew that one fate befalls them all.
15 And I said in my heart: The fate of the fool will befall me also; then why have I become so very wise? And I spoke in my heart that this also is breath.
16 For there is no memory of the wise man, any more than of the fool, forever; in the days to come all will already have been forgotten, and how the wise man dies — just like the fool!
17 And I hated life, for the work that is done under the sun was grievous to me, for all is breath and a striving after wind.
18 And I hated all my toil at which I toil under the sun, because I must leave it to the man who will be after me.
19 And who knows whether he will be wise or a fool? Yet he will rule over all my toil at which I toiled and at which I acted wisely under the sun. This also is breath.
20 And I turned to make my heart despair over all the toil at which I had toiled under the sun.
21 For there is a man whose toil is with wisdom and with knowledge and with skill, and to a man who has not toiled for it he must give it as his portion. This also is breath and a great evil.
22 For what does a man get from all his toil and from the striving of his heart at which he toils under the sun?
23 For all his days are pains, and his task is grief; even at night his heart does not rest. This also is breath.
24 There is nothing better for a man than to eat and drink, and to make his soul enjoy good in his toil. This also I saw: it is from the hand of God.
25 For who can eat, and who can have enjoyment, apart from me?
26 For God gives wisdom and knowledge and joy to the man who is good before him; but to the sinner he gives the task of gathering and heaping up, to give to the one who is good before God. This also is breath and a striving after wind.

Ecclesiastes 3

1 For everything there is a season, and a time for every matter under heaven:
2 a time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to uproot what is planted;
3 a time to kill, and a time to heal; a time to break down, and a time to build up;
4 a time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to dance;
5 a time to throw away stones, and a time to gather stones; a time to embrace, and a time to keep far from embracing;
6 a time to seek, and a time to lose; a time to keep, and a time to throw away;
7 a time to tear, and a time to sew; a time to keep silent, and a time to speak;
8 a time to love, and a time to hate; a time for war, and a time for peace.
9 What gain does the worker have from that at which he toils?
10 I saw the task that God has given to the sons of men to be afflicted with.
11 He has made everything beautiful in its time; also he has set eternity in their heart, yet so that man cannot find out the work that God has done from the beginning to the end.
12 I know that there is nothing good for them except to rejoice and to do good in their lives.
13 And also, that every man should eat and drink and see good in all his toil — it is the gift of God.
14 I know that all that God does will be forever; nothing can be added to it and nothing taken from it, and God has done it so that they fear before him.
15 What is has already been, and what is to be has already been, and God seeks that which is pursued.
16 And again I saw under the sun: in the place of justice, there was wickedness, and in the place of righteousness, there was wickedness.
17 I said in my heart: God will judge the righteous and the wicked, for there is a time there for every matter and for every work.
18 I said in my heart concerning the sons of men: God tests them, so that they may see that they themselves are animals.
19 For the fate of the sons of men and the fate of the beast — one fate is theirs: as this one dies, so dies that one, and they all have one breath, and man has no advantage over the beast, for all is breath.
20 All go to one place; all were from the dust, and all return to the dust.
21 Who knows if the spirit of the sons of men goes upward, and the spirit of the beast goes downward to the earth?
22 And I saw that there is nothing better than that a man should rejoice in his works, for that is his portion; for who will bring him to see what will be after him?

Ecclesiastes 4

1 And I turned again and saw all the oppressions that are done under the sun, and see, the tears of the oppressed, and they had no comforter; and from the hand of their oppressors there was power, and they had no comforter.
2 And I praised the dead, who have already died, more than the living, who are still alive,
3 and better than both of them is the one who has not yet been, who has not seen the evil work that is done under the sun.
4 And I saw all toil and all skill in work, that it is a man's rivalry with his neighbor. This also is breath and a striving after wind.
5 The fool folds his hands and eats his own flesh.
6 Better is a handful of quietness than two handfuls of toil and a striving after wind.
7 And I turned again and saw a breath under the sun.
8 There is one who is alone, and there is no second; he has neither son nor brother, yet there is no end to all his toil, and his eye is not satisfied with riches. For whom, then, do I toil and deprive my soul of good? This also is breath, and it is a grievous task.
9 Two are better than one, because they have a good reward for their toil.
10 For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow; but woe to the one who falls alone and has no second to lift him up.
11 Also, if two lie together, they are warm; but how can one be warm alone?
12 And if one prevails against him, two will stand against him; and a threefold cord is not quickly torn apart.
13 Better is a poor and wise youth than an old and foolish king who no longer knows how to take warning.
14 For he came out of prison to be king, although in his kingdom he was born poor.
15 I saw all the living who walk under the sun with the second youth, who will stand in his place.
16 There was no end to all the people, to all over whom he was; yet those who come after will not rejoice in him. Surely this also is breath and a striving after wind.
17 Guard your foot when you go to the house of God, and draw near to listen, rather than to give the sacrifice of fools, for they do not know that they do evil.

Ecclesiastes 5

1 Do not be rash with your mouth, and do not let your heart be hasty to bring out a word before God; for God is in heaven, and you are on earth; therefore let your words be few.
2 For a dream comes through a multitude of business, and a fool's voice through a multitude of words.
3 When you vow a vow to God, do not delay to pay it; for he has no pleasure in fools; pay what you vow.
4 It is better not to vow than to vow and not pay.
5 Do not let your mouth make your flesh sin, and do not say before the messenger that it was an error. Why should God be angry at your voice, and destroy the work of your hands?
6 For in a multitude of dreams there are breaths and many words; but fear God.
7 If you see the oppression of the poor, and the violent taking away of justice and righteousness in a province, do not marvel at the matter; for one higher than the high watches, and there are higher ones over them.
8 And the profit of a land in everything is a king who is served by the field.
9 He who loves silver will not be satisfied with silver, nor he who loves abundance, with increase; this also is breath.
10 When goods increase, those who eat them increase; and what advantage is there to their owner, except the seeing of his eyes?
11 Sweet is the sleep of the laboring man, whether he eats little or much; but the satiety of the rich man does not let him sleep.
12 There is a grievous evil that I have seen under the sun: riches kept by their owner to his harm;
13 and those riches perish by an evil venture, and he has fathered a son, and there is nothing in his hand.
14 As he came out of his mother's womb, naked he will go back, going just as he came, and he will take nothing for his toil that he can carry away in his hand.
15 And this also is a grievous evil: exactly as he came, so he will go; and what profit has he who toils for the wind?
16 All his days also he eats in darkness, and he has much vexation, and sickness and wrath.
17 See, what I have seen is good: it is fitting to eat and to drink, and to see good in all one's toil in which one toils under the sun, the number of the days of his life that God has given him; for this is his portion.
18 Also, every man to whom God has given riches and wealth, and has given him power to eat of it, and to take his portion, and to rejoice in his toil—this is the gift of God.
19 For he will not much remember the days of his life, because God answers him in the joy of his heart.

Ecclesiastes 6

1 There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, and it is heavy upon men:
2 a man to whom God gives riches, wealth, and honor, so that he lacks nothing for his soul of all that he desires, yet God does not give him power to eat of it, but a stranger eats it; this is breath, and it is an evil disease.
3 If a man fathers a hundred children and lives many years, and the days of his years are many, but his soul is not satisfied with good, and moreover he has no burial, I say that a stillborn child is better than he;
4 for it comes in breath, and departs in darkness, and its name is covered with darkness;
5 moreover it has not seen the sun nor known it; this has rest rather than the other;
6 yes, even if he lives a thousand years twice over and sees no good, do not all go to one place?
7 All a man's toil is for his mouth, and yet the appetite is not filled.
8 For what advantage has the wise more than the fool? What has the poor man who knows how to walk before the living?
9 Better is the seeing of the eyes than the wandering of the appetite; this also is breath and a striving after wind.
10 What has been, its name was already given, and it is known what man is; and he cannot contend in judgment with him who is mightier than he.
11 For there are many words that increase breath; what advantage has man?
12 For who knows what is good for man in life, all the days of his life of breath, which he spends like a shadow? For who can tell a man what will be after him under the sun?

Ecclesiastes 7

1 A good name is better than precious oil, and the day of death than the day of one's birth.
2 It is better to go to the house of mourning than to go to the house of feasting, for that is the end of all men, and the living will take it to heart.
3 Vexation is better than laughter, for by the sadness of the face the heart is made good.
4 The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning, but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth.
5 It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise than for a man to hear the song of fools.
6 For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of the fool; this also is breath.
7 Surely oppression drives a wise man mad, and a gift destroys the heart.
8 Better is the end of a thing than its beginning, and the patient in spirit is better than the proud in spirit.
9 Do not be hasty in your spirit to be angry, for anger rests in the bosom of fools.
10 Do not ask why the former days were better than these, for it is not out of wisdom that you ask concerning this.
11 Wisdom is good with an inheritance, and a profit to those who see the sun.
12 For wisdom is a defense, as money is a defense; but the advantage of knowledge is that wisdom gives life to those who have it.
13 Consider the work of God, for who can make straight what he has made crooked?
14 In the day of prosperity be joyful, and in the day of adversity consider; God has made the one alongside the other, so that man should find nothing after him.
15 All this I have seen in the days of my breath: there is a righteous man who perishes in his righteousness, and there is a wicked man who prolongs his life in his evil.
16 Do not be righteous overmuch; neither make yourself overwise; why should you destroy yourself?
17 Do not be overly wicked, neither be a fool; why should you die before your time?
18 It is good that you take hold of this, and also from that do not withdraw your hand; for the one who fears God will come out of them all.
19 Wisdom gives strength to the wise more than ten rulers who are in a city.
20 For there is not a righteous man on earth who does good and does not sin.
21 Also, do not give your heart to all the words that are spoken, lest you hear your servant curse you;
22 for many times also your heart knows that you yourself have cursed others.
23 All this I have tested by wisdom; I said: I will be wise; but it was far from me.
24 That which is, is far off and exceedingly deep; who can find it out?
25 I turned, I and my heart, to know and to search out, and to seek wisdom and the account, and to know that wickedness is folly, and that foolishness is madness;
26 and I find more bitter than death the woman whose heart is snares and nets, and her hands are bands; whoever pleases God will escape from her, but the sinner will be caught by her.
27 See, this I have found, says Qoheleth, one thing added to another, to find out the account;
28 which my soul still sought, but I did not find; one man among a thousand I found, but a woman among all these I did not find.
29 See, this only I have found: that God made man upright, but they have sought out many schemes.

Ecclesiastes 8

1 Who is like the wise man, and who knows the interpretation of a thing? A man's wisdom makes his face shine, and the boldness of his face is changed.
2 I say: keep the king's command, and that because of the oath of God.
3 Do not be hasty to go out of his presence; do not persist in an evil thing, for he does whatever pleases him.
4 Since a king's word has power, who can say to him, What are you doing?
5 Whoever keeps the command will know no evil thing, and a wise man's heart will know time and judgment.
6 For to every matter there is a time and judgment; for the evil of man is great upon him.
7 For he does not know what will be; for even when it comes to pass, who will declare it to him?
8 There is no man who has power over the wind to retain the wind; neither has he power over the day of death; and there is no discharge in war; neither will wickedness deliver the one who is given to it.
9 All this I have seen, and I applied my heart to every work that is done under the sun, at a time when one man has power over another to his hurt.
10 And so I saw the wicked buried; they had come and gone from the holy place, and they were forgotten in the city where they did this; this also is breath.
11 Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil;
12 though a sinner does evil a hundred times and prolongs his days, yet I also know that it will be well with those who fear God, who fear before him;
13 but it will not be well with the wicked, and he will not prolong his days, which are as a shadow, because he does not fear before God.
14 There is a breath which is done upon the earth, that there are righteous men to whom it happens according to the work of the wicked; again, there are wicked men to whom it happens according to the work of the righteous—I said that this also is breath.
15 So I commended mirth, that a man has no better thing under the sun than to eat, and to drink, and to be merry, and that this should accompany him in his toil all the days of his life which God has given him under the sun.
16 When I applied my heart to know wisdom, and to see the business that is done upon the earth, for neither day nor night do his eyes see sleep,
17 then I saw all the work of God, that man cannot find out the work that is done under the sun, because though a man toils to seek it out, he will not find it; yes, and though a wise man says he would know it, he cannot find it.

Ecclesiastes 9

1 For I laid all this to my heart, to make clear all this: that the righteous and the wise and their works are in the hand of God; whether love or hatred, man does not know; all is before them.
2 All things come alike to all: one event for the righteous and for the wicked, for the good and for the clean and for the unclean, for the one who sacrifices and for the one who does not sacrifice; as the good, so the sinner; the one who swears, as the one who fears an oath.
3 This is an evil in all that is done under the sun, that there is one event for all; and also the heart of the sons of men is full of evil, and madness is in their heart while they live, and after that they go to the dead.
4 For whoever is joined to all the living, there is hope; for a living dog is better than a dead lion.
5 For the living know that they will die, but the dead know nothing, and they have no more reward, for their memory is forgotten.
6 Also their love, also their hatred, also their envy have already perished, and they have no portion any more, forever, in anything that is done under the sun.
7 Go, eat your bread with joy, and drink your wine with a good heart, for God has already accepted your works.
8 At all times let your garments be white, and let oil on your head not be lacking.
9 Enjoy life with the wife whom you love, all the days of your life of breath that he has given you under the sun, all your days of breath; for that is your portion in life and in your toil in which you toil under the sun.
10 All that your hand finds to do, do with your strength; for there is no work or reckoning or knowledge or wisdom in Sheol, where you are going.
11 I turned and saw under the sun that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, and also not bread to the wise, and also not riches to the discerning, and also not favor to the knowing; for time and chance happen to them all.
12 For man also does not know his time: like the fish that are taken in an evil net, and like the birds caught in the snare, so the sons of men are snared at an evil time, when it falls on them suddenly.
13 This also I saw: wisdom under the sun, and it was great to me:
14 there was a little city, and few men in it; and a great king came against it and surrounded it and built great siege-works against it;
15 and there was found in it a poor wise man, and he delivered the city by his wisdom; yet no man remembered that same poor man.
16 And I said: Wisdom is better than strength; yet the poor man's wisdom is despised, and his words are not heard.
17 The words of the wise heard in quiet are better than the cry of a ruler among fools.
18 Wisdom is better than weapons of war, but one sinner destroys much good.

Ecclesiastes 10

1 Dead flies make the perfumer's oil stink and bubble; so a little folly outweighs wisdom and honor.
2 A wise man's heart is to his right, and a fool's heart to his left.
3 Also, when a fool walks on the road, his heart fails him, and he tells everyone that he is a fool.
4 If the spirit of the ruler rises against you, do not leave your place; for gentleness settles great offenses.
5 There is an evil I have seen under the sun, like an error that goes out from before the ruler:
6 Folly is set on great heights, and the rich sit in a low place.
7 I have seen servants on horses, and princes walking like servants on the earth.
8 Whoever digs a pit will fall into it, and whoever breaks through a wall, a snake will bite him.
9 Whoever quarries stones will be hurt by them; whoever splits wood will be endangered by it.
10 If the iron is blunt and one does not sharpen the edge, he must use more strength; but wisdom gives the advantage of success.
11 If the snake bites when there is no charming, the master of the tongue has no advantage.
12 The words of a wise man's mouth are gracious, but the lips of a fool will swallow him up.
13 The beginning of the words of his mouth is folly, and the end of his mouth is evil madness.
14 A fool multiplies words; no man knows what will be, and what will be after him, who can tell him?
15 The toil of fools wearies him, for he does not know how to go to the city.
16 Woe to you, O land, whose king is a youth, and whose princes eat in the morning!
17 Happy are you, O land, whose king is a son of nobles, and whose princes eat at the proper time, for strength and not for drunkenness.
18 Through sloth the rafters sink in, and through slackness of the hands the house leaks.
19 Bread is made for laughter, and wine gladdens life, and money answers all things.
20 Do not curse the king, even in your thought, and do not curse the rich in your bedroom; for a bird of the sky will carry the voice, and that which has wings will tell the matter.

Ecclesiastes 11

1 Cast your bread upon the waters, for after many days you will find it.
2 Give a portion to seven, and also to eight, for you do not know what evil will be on the earth.
3 If the clouds are full of rain, they empty themselves on the earth; and if a tree falls to the south or to the north, in the place where the tree falls, there it will be.
4 Whoever watches the wind will not sow, and whoever looks at the clouds will not reap.
5 As you do not know what is the way of the wind, or how the bones grow in the womb of her who is with child, even so you do not know the work of God who does all.
6 In the morning sow your seed, and in the evening do not withhold your hand; for you do not know which will prosper, this or that, or whether both alike will be good.
7 The light is sweet, and it is good for the eyes to see the sun.
8 For if a man lives many years, let him rejoice in them all, and remember the days of darkness, for they will be many. All that comes is breath.
9 Rejoice, young man, in your youth, and let your heart cheer you in the days of your youth, and walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes; but know that for all these things God will bring you into judgment.
10 Remove vexation from your heart, and put away evil from your flesh; for childhood and the dawn of life are breath.

Ecclesiastes 12

1 Remember your Creator in the days of your youth, before the evil days come and the years draw near when you will say: I have no pleasure in them;
2 Before the sun, and the light, and the moon, and the stars are darkened, and the clouds return after the rain;
3 In the day when the keepers of the house tremble, and the strong men bow themselves, and the grinders cease because they are few, and those who look out of the windows are darkened,
4 And the doors are shut in the street, when the sound of the grinding is low; and one rises up at the voice of a bird, and all the daughters of song are brought low;
5 Also, they are afraid of a height, and terrors are in the way; and the almond tree blossoms, and the grasshopper drags itself along, and the caper-berry fails; for man goes to his eternal home, and the mourners go about the street;
6 Before the silver cord is snapped, and the golden bowl is shattered, and the pitcher is broken at the spring, and the wheel is shattered into the pit;
7 And the dust returns to the earth as it was, and the spirit returns to God who gave it.
8 Breath of breaths, says Qoheleth; all is breath.
9 Moreover, Qoheleth was wise; he still taught the people knowledge, and he pondered and sought out and set in order many proverbs.
10 Qoheleth sought to find words of delight, and what was written uprightly, words of truth.
11 The words of the wise are like goads, and like nails firmly planted are the masters of collections; they are given from one shepherd.
12 And furthermore, my son, be warned: the making of many books has no end, and much study is a weariness of the flesh.
13 The end of the matter, all having been heard: fear God, and keep his commandments; for this is the whole man.
14 For God will bring every work into judgment, concerning every hidden thing, whether it is good or whether it is evil.
