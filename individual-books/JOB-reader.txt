Clear English Bible — Job (Chapters 1–42): A Clear English Translation

This completes the Masoretic book of Job in this edition. The remaining books of the Old Testament stand in this edition as a clear-English modernization of the World English Bible (WEB) Classic text named in the front matter, except the books the front matter names as fresh translations made directly from the Hebrew; the New Testament stands on the Greek text named there. The book as printed here stands as PUBLISHED.

Publication — 25 November 2026. By the editor's ruling of 25 November 2026, this book of the Clear English Bible stands published. Until that ruling it stood on this shelf complete and landed, but unpublished; the editor, Robert Tilford, is the checker of last resort, and his ruling is the final check under which the book goes out. The records that follow stand exactly as written at their dates.

Job 1

1 There was a man in the land of Uz, whose name was Job; and that man was blameless and upright, and one who feared God, and turned away from evil.
2 And there were born to him seven sons and three daughters.
3 His possessions also were seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen, five hundred female donkeys, and a very great household; so that this man was the greatest of all the children of the east.
4 And his sons used to go and hold a feast in the house of each one on his day; and they would send and invite their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.
5 And it was so, when the days of their feasting had run their course, that Job sent and sanctified them, and rose up early in the morning, and offered burnt offerings according to the number of them all; for Job said: It may be that my sons have sinned, and renounced God in their hearts. Job did so continually.
6 Now it fell on a day, that the sons of God came to present themselves before Yahweh, and the Adversary came also among them.
7 And Yahweh said to the Adversary: From where have you come? Then the Adversary answered Yahweh, and said: From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.
8 And Yahweh said to the Adversary: Have you considered my servant Job, that there is no one like him in the earth, a blameless and upright man, one who fears God, and turns away from evil?
9 Then the Adversary answered Yahweh, and said: Does Job fear God for nothing?
10 Have you not made a hedge about him, and about his house, and about all that he has, on every side? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his possessions have increased in the land.
11 But put out your hand now, and touch all that he has, and he will renounce you to your face.
12 And Yahweh said to the Adversary: See, all that he has is in your power; only do not put out your hand on himself. So the Adversary went out from the presence of Yahweh.
13 And it fell on a day when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother's house,
14 that a messenger came to Job, and said: The oxen were plowing, and the donkeys feeding beside them,
15 and the Sabeans made a raid, and took them away; yes, they struck the servants with the edge of the sword; and I alone have escaped to tell you.
16 While he was still speaking, another came also, and said: A fire of God has fallen from heaven, and has burned up the sheep and the servants, and consumed them; and I alone have escaped to tell you.
17 While he was still speaking, another came also, and said: The Chaldeans set themselves in three bands, and fell on the camels, and have taken them away, yes, and struck the servants with the edge of the sword; and I alone have escaped to tell you.
18 While he was still speaking, another came also, and said: Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother's house,
19 and see, there came a great wind from across the wilderness, and struck the four corners of the house, and it fell on the young people, and they died; and I alone have escaped to tell you.
20 Then Job arose, and tore his robe, and shaved his head, and fell down on the ground, and worshiped.
21 And he said: Naked I came out of my mother's womb, and naked I shall return there; Yahweh gave, and Yahweh has taken away; blessed be the name of Yahweh.
22 In all this Job did not sin, nor charge God with wrongdoing.

Job 2

1 Again it fell on a day, that the sons of God came to present themselves before Yahweh, and the Adversary came also among them to present himself before Yahweh.
2 And Yahweh said to the Adversary: From where have you come? And the Adversary answered Yahweh, and said: From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.
3 And Yahweh said to the Adversary: Have you considered my servant Job, that there is no one like him in the earth, a blameless and upright man, one who fears God, and turns away from evil? And he still holds fast to his integrity, although you moved me against him, to destroy him without cause.
4 And the Adversary answered Yahweh, and said: Skin for skin; yes, all that a man has he will give for his life.
5 But put out your hand now, and touch his bone and his flesh, and he will renounce you to your face.
6 And Yahweh said to the Adversary: See, he is in your hand; only spare his life.
7 So the Adversary went out from the presence of Yahweh, and struck Job with severe boils from the sole of his foot to his crown.
8 And he took for himself a potsherd to scrape himself with it; and he sat among the ashes.
9 Then his wife said to him: Do you still hold fast to your integrity? Renounce God, and die.
10 But he said to her: You speak as one of the foolish women speaks. Shall we receive good from God, and shall we not receive evil? In all this Job did not sin with his lips.
11 Now when Job's three friends heard of all this evil that had come on him, they came each one from his own place, Eliphaz the Temanite, and Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite; and they agreed together to come to mourn with him and to comfort him.
12 And when they lifted up their eyes from afar, and did not know him, they lifted up their voice, and wept; and they tore each one his robe, and threw dust on their heads toward heaven.
13 So they sat down with him on the ground seven days and seven nights, and no one spoke a word to him; for they saw that his grief was very great.

Job 3

1 After this Job opened his mouth, and cursed his day.
2 And Job answered, and said:
3 Let the day perish in which I was born, and the night in which it was said: A man-child is conceived.
4 Let that day be darkness; let not God seek it from above, neither let light shine on it.
5 Let darkness and the shadow of death claim it for their own; let a cloud dwell on it; let all that makes black the day terrify it.
6 As for that night, let thick darkness seize it; let it not rejoice among the days of the year; let it not come into the number of the months.
7 See, let that night be barren; let no joyful voice come into it.
8 Let those who curse the day curse it, who are ready to rouse up Leviathan.
9 Let the stars of its twilight be dark; let it look for light, but have none; neither let it behold the eyelids of the morning;
10 because it did not shut up the doors of my womb, nor hide trouble from my eyes.
11 Why did I not die from the womb? Why did I not perish when I came out of the belly?
12 Why did the knees receive me? And why the breasts, that I should suck?
13 For now I would have lain still and been quiet; I would have slept; then I would have been at rest,
14 with kings and counsellors of the earth, who built up waste places for themselves;
15 or with princes who had gold, who filled their houses with silver;
16 or as a hidden untimely birth I would not have been; as infants who never saw light.
17 There the wicked cease from troubling; and there the weary are at rest.
18 There the prisoners are at ease together; they do not hear the voice of the taskmaster.
19 The small and the great are there; and the servant is free from his master.
20 Why is light given to him who is in misery, and life to the bitter in soul,
21 who long for death, but it does not come; and dig for it more than for hidden treasures,
22 who rejoice to exultation, and are glad, when they find the grave?
23 To a man whose way is hidden, and whom God has hedged in?
24 For my sighing comes before my food, and my roarings are poured out like water.
25 For the thing I feared has come on me, and what I dreaded has come to me.
26 I was not at ease, neither was I quiet, neither did I rest; but trouble came.

Job 4

1 Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered, and said:
2 If one ventures a word to you, will you be weary? But who can hold back from speaking?
3 See, you have instructed many, and you have strengthened the weak hands.
4 Your words have upheld him who was falling, and you have strengthened the feeble knees.
5 But now it has come to you, and you are weary; it touches you, and you are troubled.
6 Is not your fear of God your confidence, your hope the integrity of your ways?
7 Remember, I ask you, who ever perished, being innocent? Or where were the upright cut off?
8 As I have seen, those who plow iniquity, and sow trouble, reap the same.
9 By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of his anger they are consumed.
10 The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions are broken.
11 The old lion perishes for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.
12 Now a word was secretly brought to me, and my ear received a whisper of it.
13 In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falls on men,
14 fear came on me, and trembling, and made most of my bones shake.
15 Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up.
16 It stood still, but I could not discern its appearance; a form was before my eyes; silence, and I heard a voice:
17 Shall mortal man be just before God? Shall a man be pure before his Maker?
18 See, he puts no trust in his servants, and his angels he charges with folly;
19 how much more those who dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed before the moth!
20 Between morning and evening they are shattered; they perish forever without anyone regarding it.
21 Is not their tent-cord plucked up in them? They die, and that without wisdom.

Job 5

1 Call now; is there anyone who will answer you? And to which of the holy ones will you turn?
2 For anger kills the foolish man, and jealousy kills the simple one.
3 I have seen the foolish taking root; but suddenly I cursed his habitation.
4 His children are far from safety, and are crushed in the gate, with no one to deliver them.
5 Whose harvest the hungry eats up, and takes it even out of the thorns, and the thirsty pant for their wealth.
6 For affliction does not come out of the dust, neither does trouble spring out of the ground;
7 but man is born to trouble, as the sparks fly upward.
8 But as for me, I would seek God, and to God I would commit my cause;
9 who does great things and unsearchable, marvelous things without number;
10 who gives rain on the earth, and sends waters on the fields;
11 to set up on high those who are low, and mourners are exalted to safety.
12 He frustrates the plans of the crafty, so that their hands can perform no enterprise.
13 He takes the wise in their own craftiness; and the counsel of the wily is carried headlong.
14 They meet with darkness in the daytime, and grope at noonday as in the night.
15 But he saves the needy from the sword of their mouth, and from the hand of the mighty.
16 So the poor has hope, and iniquity shuts her mouth.
17 See, happy is the man whom God corrects; therefore do not despise the chastening of the Almighty.
18 For he makes sore, and binds up; he wounds, and his hands make whole.
19 He will deliver you in six troubles; yes, in seven no evil shall touch you.
20 In famine he will redeem you from death; and in war from the power of the sword.
21 You shall be hidden from the scourge of the tongue; neither shall you be afraid of destruction when it comes.
22 At destruction and famine you shall laugh; neither shall you be afraid of the beasts of the earth.
23 For you shall be in league with the stones of the field; and the beasts of the field shall be at peace with you.
24 And you shall know that your tent is in peace; and you shall visit your habitation, and shall miss nothing.
25 You shall know also that your offspring shall be many, and your offspring as the grass of the earth.
26 You shall come to your grave in a ripe age, like a shock of grain that comes in in its season.
27 See, this we have searched out; so it is; hear it, and know it for yourself.

Job 6

1 Then Job answered, and said:
2 Oh that my vexation were weighed, and my calamity laid in the balances together!
3 For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas; therefore my words are rash.
4 For the arrows of the Almighty are with me, whose poison my spirit drinks up; the terrors of God set themselves in array against me.
5 Does the wild ass bray when he has grass? Or does the ox low over his fodder?
6 Can that which is tasteless be eaten without salt? Or is there any taste in the white of an egg?
7 My soul refuses to touch them; they are like loathsome food to me.
8 Oh that I might have my request, and that God would grant me what I long for!
9 Even that God would be pleased to crush me; that he would let loose his hand, and cut me off!
10 Then I would still have comfort; yes, I would exult in pain that does not spare; for I have not denied the words of the Holy One.
11 What is my strength, that I should wait? And what is my end, that I should prolong my life?
12 Is my strength the strength of stones? Or is my flesh of bronze?
13 Is it not that I have no help in me, and that sound wisdom is driven from me?
14 For the one who is melting away, loyal love is due from his friend, even if he forsakes the fear of the Almighty.
15 My brothers have dealt deceitfully as a brook, as the channel of brooks that pass away,
16 which are dark by reason of the ice, and in which the snow hides itself;
17 when they warm, they vanish; when it is hot, they are consumed out of their place.
18 The paths of their course wind; they go up into the waste, and perish.
19 The caravans of Tema looked, the companies of Sheba waited for them;
20 they were ashamed because they had hoped; they came there, and were confounded.
21 For now you have become his; you see a terror, and are afraid.
22 Did I say: Give to me? Or: Offer a present for me from your substance?
23 Or: Deliver me from the adversary's hand? Or: Redeem me from the hand of the oppressors?
24 Teach me, and I will hold my peace; and make me understand in what I have erred.
25 How forcible are words of uprightness! But what does your reproof reprove?
26 Do you intend to reprove words, since the speeches of one who is desperate are as wind?
27 Yes, you would cast lots on the fatherless, and dig a pit for your friend.
28 Now therefore be pleased to look on me; for surely I shall not lie to your face.
29 Return, I ask you, let there be no injustice; yes, return again; my righteousness is in it.
30 Is there injustice on my tongue? Can my palate not discern perverse things?

Job 7

1 Is there not a time of service for man on earth? And are not his days like the days of a hireling?
2 As a servant who eagerly longs for the shadow, and as a hireling who looks for his wages;
3 so I am made to possess months of vanity, and wearisome nights are appointed to me.
4 When I lie down, I say: When shall I arise? But the evening is drawn out, and I am full of tossings to and fro until the dawning of the day.
5 My flesh is clothed with worms and clods of dust; my skin closes up and breaks out afresh.
6 My days are swifter than a weaver's shuttle, and are spent without hope.
7 Remember that my life is a breath; my eye shall no more see good.
8 The eye of him who sees me shall behold me no more; while your eyes are on me, I am gone.
9 As the cloud is consumed and vanishes away, so he who goes down to Sheol shall come up no more.
10 He shall return no more to his house, neither shall his place know him any more.
11 Therefore I will not refrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.
12 Am I a sea, or a sea-monster, that you set a watch over me?
13 When I say: My bed shall comfort me, my couch shall ease my complaint;
14 then you scare me with dreams, and terrify me through visions;
15 so that my soul chooses strangling, and death rather than my bones.
16 I loathe it; I shall not live forever; let me alone; for my days are vanity.
17 What is man, that you should magnify him, and that you should set your heart on him,
18 and that you should visit him every morning, and try him every moment?
19 How long will you not look away from me, nor let me alone till I swallow down my spittle?
20 If I have sinned, what do I do to you, O watcher of men? Why have you set me as a mark for you, so that I am a burden to myself?
21 And why do you not pardon my transgression, and take away my iniquity? For now I shall lie down in the dust; and you will seek me, but I shall not be.

Job 8

1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:
2 How long will you speak these things, and the words of your mouth be a mighty wind?
3 Does God pervert judgment? Or does the Almighty pervert justice?
4 If your children sinned against him, he delivered them into the hand of their transgression.
5 If you would seek God earnestly, and make your supplication to the Almighty,
6 if you are pure and upright, surely now he would awake for you, and make the dwelling of your righteousness whole.
7 And though your beginning was small, yet your latter end will greatly increase.
8 For inquire, please, of the former generation, and apply yourself to what their fathers searched out;
9 for we are only of yesterday, and know nothing, because our days on earth are a shadow;
10 Will they not teach you and tell you, and bring out words from their heart?
11 Can the papyrus shoot up without a marsh? Can the reed-grass grow without water?
12 While it is still in its greenness, and not cut down, it withers before any other grass.
13 So are the paths of all who forget God; and the hope of the godless man will perish,
14 whose confidence is cut off, and whose trust is a spider's web.
15 He leans on his house, but it will not stand; he holds fast to it, but it will not endure.
16 He is lush before the sun, and his shoots go out over his garden.
17 His roots are wrapped about the heap; he looks on the place of stones.
18 If he is destroyed from his place, then it will deny him: I have not seen you.
19 See, this is the joy of his way, and out of the dust others will spring.
20 See, God will not reject a blameless man, nor will he uphold evildoers;
21 until he fills your mouth with laughter, and your lips with shouting.
22 Those who hate you will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more.

Job 9

1 Then Job answered and said:
2 Truly I know that it is so; but how can a man be right with God?
3 If he wanted to contend with him, he could not answer him one time in a thousand.
4 He is wise in heart and mighty in strength; who has hardened himself against him, and prospered?
5 He moves the mountains, and they do not know it, when he overturns them in his anger.
6 He shakes the earth out of its place, and its pillars tremble.
7 He commands the sun, and it does not rise; and he seals up the stars.
8 He alone stretches out the heavens, and treads on the waves of the sea.
9 He makes the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the chambers of the south.
10 He does great things beyond searching out, and marvelous things without number.
11 See, he passes by me, and I do not see him; he moves on, and I do not perceive him.
12 See, he snatches away — who can turn him back? Who will say to him: What are you doing?
13 God will not withdraw his anger; the helpers of Rahab bowed down under him.
14 How much less can I answer him, and choose my words with him?
15 Though I were righteous, I would not answer; I would make supplication to my judge.
16 If I called, and he answered me, I would not believe that he had listened to my voice;
17 for he crushes me with a tempest, and multiplies my wounds without cause;
18 he will not let me draw my breath, but fills me with bitterness.
19 If it is a matter of strength — see, he is mighty! And if of judgment, who will summon me?
20 Though I am righteous, my own mouth will condemn me; though I am blameless, it will prove me perverse.
21 I am blameless; I do not regard myself; I despise my life.
22 It is all one; therefore I say: he destroys the blameless and the wicked.
23 If the scourge kills suddenly, he mocks at the trial of the innocent.
24 The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; he covers the faces of its judges; if it is not he, then who is it?
25 Now my days are swifter than a runner; they flee away, they see no good.
26 They have passed by like reed boats, like an eagle swooping on its prey.
27 If I say: I will forget my complaint, I will put off my face and be cheerful,
28 I dread all my pains; I know that you will not hold me innocent.
29 I will be condemned; why then do I labor in vain?
30 If I wash myself in snow water, and cleanse my hands with lye,
31 yet you will plunge me in the pit, and my own clothes will abhor me.
32 For he is not a man, as I am, that I should answer him, that we should come together in judgment.
33 There is no arbiter between us, who might lay his hand on us both.
34 Let him take his rod away from me, and let his terror not make me afraid;
35 then I would speak, and not fear him; for I am not so with myself.

Job 10

1 My soul is weary of my life. I will give free course to my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.
2 I will say to God: Do not condemn me; make me know why you contend with me.
3 Is it good for you that you oppress, that you despise the work of your hands, and shine on the counsel of the wicked?
4 Do you have eyes of flesh? Or do you see as a man sees?
5 Are your days as the days of a mortal, or your years as a man's days,
6 that you inquire after my iniquity, and search after my sin,
7 though you know that I am not wicked, and there is no deliverer from your hand.
8 Your hands have shaped me and made me, all together round about; yet you destroy me.
9 Remember, please, that you made me as clay; and will you return me to dust?
10 Did you not pour me out like milk, and curdle me like cheese?
11 You have clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and sinews.
12 You have granted me life and loyal love, and your care has preserved my spirit.
13 Yet these things you hid in your heart; I know that this is with you:
14 If I sin, you watch me, and you will not acquit me of my iniquity.
15 If I am wicked, woe to me; and if I am righteous, yet I will not lift my head, being filled with disgrace and looking on my affliction.
16 And if it exalts itself, you hunt me like a lion, and again you show yourself marvelous upon me.
17 You renew your witnesses against me, and increase your anger toward me; fresh troops keep coming against me.
18 Why then did you bring me out of the womb? I would have died, and no eye would have seen me.
19 I would have been as though I had not been; I would have been carried from the womb to the grave.
20 Are not my days few? Cease, and turn away from me, that I may brighten up a little,
21 before I go, and do not return, to the land of darkness and deep darkness;
22 a land of gloom like darkness itself, of deep darkness without any order, where the light is like darkness.

Job 11

1 Then Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:
2 Should the multitude of words go unanswered? And should a man full of talk be justified?
3 Should your boastings make men hold their peace? You mock, and no one puts you to shame;
4 And you said: My teaching is pure, and I am clean in your eyes.
5 But oh, that God would speak, and open his lips against you,
6 and that he would tell you the secrets of wisdom — for sound wisdom is twofold. Know that God has remitted part of your iniquity.
7 Can you find out the deep things of God? Can you attain to the limit of the Almighty?
8 It is higher than heaven — what can you do? Deeper than Sheol — what can you know?
9 Its measure is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.
10 If he passes by and shuts up, or assembles the court, who can hinder him?
11 For he knows worthless men; he sees iniquity — will he not then consider it?
12 But a hollow man will get understanding, when a wild donkey's colt is born a man.
13 If you set your heart right, and stretch out your hands toward him —
14 if iniquity is in your hand, put it far away, and do not let unrighteousness dwell in your tents —
15 surely then you will lift up your face without spot; you will be firm, and will not fear;
16 for you will forget your misery; you will remember it as waters that have passed away;
17 And your life will be brighter than the noonday; though there is darkness, it will be as the morning.
18 And you will be secure, because there is hope; you will look around, and take your rest in safety.
19 And you will lie down, and no one will make you afraid; and many will seek your favor.
20 But the eyes of the wicked will fail, and flight will perish from them, and their hope is the breathing out of the soul.

Job 12

1 Then Job answered and said:
2 No doubt you are the people, and wisdom will die with you.
3 But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you; yes, who does not know such things as these?
4 I am a laughingstock to my neighbor, I who called on God, and he answered me — the righteous, blameless man is a laughingstock,
5 A torch of contempt in the thought of him who is at ease, ready for those whose foot slips.
6 The tents of robbers prosper, and those who provoke God are secure, into whose hand God has brought it.
7 But ask now the animals, and they will teach you; and the birds of the heavens, and they will tell you;
8 or speak to the earth, and it will teach you; and the fish of the sea will declare to you;
9 Who does not know among all these that the hand of Yahweh has done this,
10 in whose hand is the life of every living thing, and the breath of all mankind?

Job 13

2 What you know, I know also; I am not inferior to you.
3 But I would speak to the Almighty, and I desire to reason with God.
4 But you are plasterers of lies; you are all physicians of no value.

7 Will you speak unrighteousness for God, and speak deceit for him?
8 Will you show him partiality? Will you contend for God?
9 Would it be good if he searched you out? Or will you mock him, as one mocks a man?

11 Will not his majesty terrify you, and his dread fall on you?

14 Why do I take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life in my hand?

Job 14

1 Man, born of a woman, is of few days, and full of trouble.
2 He comes forth like a flower, and is cut down; he flees also like a shadow, and does not stand.
3 And on such a one do you open your eyes, and bring me into judgment with you?
4 Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? Not one.
5 Seeing his days are determined, the number of his months is with you, and you have appointed his bounds, which he cannot pass;
6 Look away from him, that he may rest, until he completes his day like a hired worker.
7 For there is hope for a tree, if it is cut down, that it will sprout again, and its tender shoot will not cease.
8 Though its root grows old in the earth, and its stump dies in the dust,
9 yet at the scent of water it will bud, and put forth boughs like a plant.
10 But man dies, and is laid low; man breathes his last, and where is he?
11 The waters vanish from the sea, and the river runs parched and dry;
12 so man lies down and does not rise; until the heavens are no more, they will not awake, nor be roused from their sleep.
13 Oh that you would hide me in Sheol, keep me hidden until your anger turns back, set me a limit, and remember me!
14 If a man dies, will he live again? All the days of my hard service I would wait, until my relief comes.
15 You would call, and I would answer you; you would long for the work of your hands.
16 For now you number my steps; do you not watch over my sin?
17 My transgression is sealed up in a bag, and you plaster over my iniquity.
18 But the falling mountain crumbles away, and the rock is moved from its place;
19 The waters wear away the stones; its floods wash away the dust of the earth; so you destroy the hope of man.
20 You prevail against him forever, and he goes; you change his face, and send him away.
21 His sons come to honor, and he does not know it; they are brought low, and he does not perceive it.
22 But his flesh aches over him, and his soul mourns over him.

8 Have you heard the secret counsel of God? Do you limit wisdom to yourself?

12 Why does your heart carry you away? Why do your eyes flash,

18 what wise men declare, receiving it from their fathers and not concealing it,

29 He will not be rich, neither will his substance continue, neither will their possessions be extended on the earth.
30 He will not depart out of darkness. The flame will dry up his branches. He will go away by the breath of his mouth.
31 Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself, for emptiness will be his reward.
32 It will be accomplished before his time. His branch will not be green.
33 He will shake off his unripe grape as the vine, and will cast off his flower as the olive tree.
34 For the company of the godless will be barren, and fire will consume the tents of bribery.

10 They have gaped on me with their mouth. They have struck me on the cheek reproachfully. They gather themselves together against me.
11 God delivers me to the ungodly, and casts me into the hands of the wicked.

19 He lies down rich, but will do so no more; he opens his eyes, and he is no more.
20 Terrors overtake him like waters; a storm steals him away in the night.
21 The east wind lifts him up and he is gone, and it whirls him out of his place.

Job 28

1 Surely there is a mine for silver, and a place for gold that they refine.
2 Iron is taken from the earth, and copper is smelted from the stone.
3 Man puts an end to darkness, and searches to the far limit the stones of gloom and deep darkness.
4 He breaks open a shaft away from where people live; they are forgotten by the foot that passes; they hang far from men, they swing back and forth.
5 The earth, from it comes bread, and underneath it is turned up as by fire.
6 Its stones are the place of sapphires, and it has dust of gold.
7 That path no bird of prey knows, and the eye of the falcon has not seen it.
8 The proud beasts have not trodden it, and no lion has passed over it.
9 He puts his hand to the flinty rock; he overturns mountains by the roots.
10 He cuts channels through the rocks, and his eye sees every precious thing.
11 He dams the streams so they do not trickle, and brings what is hidden out to light.
12 But where shall wisdom be found, and where is the place of understanding?
13 Man does not know its value, and it is not found in the land of the living.
14 The deep says: It is not in me, and the sea says: It is not with me.
15 It cannot be bought with solid gold, and silver cannot be weighed out as its price.
16 It cannot be valued with the gold of Ophir, with precious onyx or sapphire.
17 Gold and glass cannot equal it, and it cannot be exchanged for vessels of fine gold.
18 Coral and crystal are not to be mentioned; the price of wisdom is above pearls.
19 The topaz of Cush cannot equal it, and it cannot be valued with pure gold.
20 From where, then, does wisdom come, and where is the place of understanding?
21 It is hidden from the eyes of all living, and concealed from the birds of the heavens.
22 Abaddon and Death say: With our ears we have heard a report of it.
23 God understands its way, and he knows its place.
24 For he looks to the ends of the earth, and sees under all the heavens,
25 when he made a weight for the wind, and measured out the waters by measure,
26 when he made a decree for the rain, and a way for the lightning of the thunder,
27 then he saw it and declared it; he established it, and also searched it out.
28 And he said to man: See, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom, and to turn away from evil is understanding.

Job 29

1 And Job again took up his discourse, and said:

3 when his lamp shone above my head, and by his light I walked through darkness,
4 as I was in the days of my prime, when the friendship of God was over my tent,
5 when the Almighty was still with me, and my children were around me,
6 when my steps were washed with butter, and the rock poured out streams of oil for me,
7 when I went out to the gate of the city, when I took my seat in the square,
8 the young men saw me and hid themselves, and the aged rose and stood,
9 the princes held back from speaking, and laid their hand on their mouth,
10 the voice of the nobles was hushed, and their tongue stuck to the roof of their mouth.
11 For when the ear heard me, it blessed me, and when the eye saw me, it bore witness to me,
12 because I rescued the poor who cried out, and the fatherless, who had no one to help him.
13 The blessing of the one about to perish came on me, and I made the heart of the widow sing for joy.
14 I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; my justice was like a robe and a turban.
15 I was eyes to the blind, and I was feet to the lame.
16 I was a father to the needy, and I searched out the cause of the one I did not know.

18 Then I said: I will die in my nest, and I will multiply my days like the sand,

20 my glory is fresh within me, and my bow is renewed in my hand.

24 I smiled on them when they did not believe it, and they did not cast down the light of my face.
25 I chose their way and sat as chief, and I lived like a king among the troops, like one who comforts mourners.

Job 30

1 But now those younger than I laugh at me, men whose fathers I disdained to put with the dogs of my flock.

3 Gaunt with want and hunger, they gnaw the dry ground, in the gloom of waste and desolation.
4 They pluck salt herbs among the bushes, and the roots of the broom tree are their food.
5 They are driven out from among men; men shout after them as after a thief.
6 They must live in the gullies of the valleys, in holes of the earth and the rocks.
7 Among the bushes they bray; under the nettles they huddle together.
8 Sons of fools, yes, sons of nameless men, they were whipped out of the land.
9 And now I have become their song, and I am a byword to them.
10 They abhor me, they keep far from me, and they do not hold back from spitting in my face.
11 For he has loosed my cord and afflicted me, and they have cast off the bridle before me.
12 On my right hand the brood rises; they push away my feet, and they heap up their paths of destruction against me.
13 They break up my path, they advance my calamity, with no helper for them.
14 As through a wide breach they come; amid the ruin they roll on.
15 Terrors are turned against me; they chase my honor like the wind, and my welfare has passed away like a cloud.
16 And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction have seized me.
17 At night my bones are pierced from me, and my gnawing pains take no rest.
18 With great force my garment is disfigured; it binds me like the collar of my tunic.

20 I cry out to you, and you do not answer me; I stand, and you gaze at me.
21 You have turned cruel to me; with the strength of your hand you oppose me.

23 For I know that you will bring me back to death, and to the house appointed for all living.
24 Yet will a man not stretch out his hand in a ruin? Or in his calamity is there not a cry for help for these?
25 Did I not weep for the one whose day was hard? Did my soul not grieve for the needy?
26 When I hoped for good, evil came, and when I waited for light, darkness came.
27 My insides boil and do not rest; days of affliction have met me.
28 I go about blackened, but not by the sun; I stand in the assembly and cry for help.
29 I have become a brother to jackals, and a companion to ostriches.
30 My skin has turned black and falls from me, and my bones burn with heat.
31 My lyre has turned to mourning, and my pipe to the voice of those who weep.

Job 31

1 I made a covenant with my eyes; how then could I look on a virgin?
2 And what would be the portion of God from above, and the heritage of the Almighty from on high?
3 Is it not calamity for the unrighteous, and disaster for the workers of iniquity?
4 Does he not see my ways, and count all my steps?
5 If I have walked with falsehood, and my foot has hurried to deceit,
6 let me be weighed in a just balance, so that God may know my integrity,
7 if my step has turned from the way, and my heart has gone after my eyes, and any spot has stuck to my hands,
8 then let me sow, and let another eat, and let my offspring be rooted out.
9 If my heart has been enticed by a woman, and I have lain in wait at the door of my neighbor,
10 then let my wife grind for another, and let others kneel over her.
11 For that would be a shameful crime, and an iniquity for the judges,
12 for it is a fire that consumes to Abaddon, and it would root out all my increase.
13 If I have despised the cause of my male servant or my female servant, when they contended with me,
14 what then shall I do when God rises up? And when he visits, what shall I answer him?
15 Did not he who made me in the womb also make him? And did not one fashion us in the womb?
16 If I have withheld what the poor desired, or caused the eyes of the widow to fail,
17 or eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless has not eaten of it,
18 no, from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, and from the womb of my mother I guided her,
19 if I have seen anyone perishing for lack of clothing, or the needy with no covering,
20 if his loins have not blessed me, and he was not warmed with the fleece of my sheep,
21 if I have lifted my hand against the fatherless, because I saw my help in the gate,
22 then let my shoulder fall from its blade, and let my arm be broken from its socket.
23 For calamity from God was a terror to me, and because of his majesty I could do nothing.
24 If I have made gold my hope, and said to fine gold: You are my confidence,
25 if I rejoiced because my wealth was great, and because my hand had gained much,
26 if I looked at the sun when it shone, or the moon moving in brightness,
27 and my heart was secretly enticed, and my mouth kissed my hand,
28 that also would be an iniquity for the judges, for I would have denied God above.
29 If I rejoiced at the ruin of the one who hated me, or stirred myself up when evil found him,
30 no, I did not let my mouth sin by asking for his life with a curse.
31 If the men of my tent did not say: Who can find anyone who has not been filled with his meat?
32 The stranger did not lodge in the street; I opened my doors to the traveler.
33 If I covered my transgressions like Adam, by hiding my iniquity in my bosom,
34 because I feared the great multitude, and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I kept silence and did not go out of the door,
35 Oh that I had someone to hear me! Here is my signature; let the Almighty answer me, and let my adversary write a scroll!
36 Surely I would carry it on my shoulder; I would bind it on me as a crown.
37 I would declare to him the number of my steps; like a prince I would approach him.
38 If my land cries out against me, and its furrows weep together,
39 if I have eaten its strength without payment, or caused its owners to breathe out their life,
40 let thistles grow instead of wheat, and stinking weeds instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.

Job 32

1 So these three men stopped answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.
2 Then the anger of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, was kindled; his anger was kindled against Job, because he justified himself rather than God.
3 His anger was also kindled against his three friends, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job.
4 Now Elihu had waited to speak to Job, because they were older in days than he.
5 And when Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, his anger was kindled.
6 And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said: I am young in days, and you are aged; therefore I shrank back and feared to declare my knowledge to you.
7 I said: Days should speak, and a multitude of years should make wisdom known.
8 But it is a spirit in a man, and the breath of the Almighty, that gives them understanding.
9 The great are not always wise, nor do the aged understand justice.
10 Therefore I say: Listen to me; I also will declare my knowledge.
11 See, I waited for your words; I listened for your insights, while you searched out words.
12 Yes, I paid attention to you, and see, there was no one among you who refuted Job or answered his words.
13 Beware, so that you do not say: We have found wisdom; God will thrust him away, not man.
14 For he has not arranged his words against me, and I will not answer him with your speeches.
15 They are dismayed, they answer no more; words have moved away from them.
16 And shall I wait, because they do not speak, because they stand still and answer no more?
17 I also will answer my part; I also will declare my knowledge.
18 For I am full of words; the spirit within me presses me.
19 See, my belly is like wine that has no vent; like new wineskins, it is ready to burst.
20 I will speak, so that I may find relief; I will open my lips and answer.
21 Let me not show favor to any man, and I will not give flattering titles to any man.
22 For I do not know how to flatter; my Maker would soon carry me away.

Job 33

1 But now, Job, please hear my speech, and listen to all my words.
2 See now, I have opened my mouth; my tongue has spoken in my mouth.
3 My words are from the uprightness of my heart, and my lips speak knowledge in purity.
4 The spirit of God has made me, and the breath of the Almighty gives me life.
5 If you can, answer me; set yourself in order before me, take your stand.
6 See, I am like you before God; I also was pinched off from clay.
7 See, no dread of me should terrify you, and my pressure will not be heavy on you.
8 Surely you have spoken in my ears, and I have heard the sound of words:
9 I am clean, without transgression; I am innocent, and there is no iniquity in me.
10 See, he finds occasions against me; he counts me as his enemy.
11 He puts my feet in the stocks; he watches all my paths.
12 See, in this you are not right, I answer you, for God is greater than man.
13 Why have you contended against him? For he does not answer on any of his matters.
14 For God speaks in one way, and in two, though a man does not perceive it.
15 In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls on men, in slumbers on the bed,
16 then he opens the ear of men, and seals their instruction,
17 to turn man away from his deed, and to hide pride from man.
18 He holds back his soul from the pit, and his life from crossing by the sword.
19 He is also reproved with pain on his bed, and the strife in his bones is constant.
20 And his life makes bread loathsome to him, and his soul dainty food.
21 His flesh wastes away out of sight, and his bones that were not seen stick out.
22 His soul draws near to the pit, and his life to those who bring death.
23 If there is for him an angel, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to declare to a man his uprightness,
24 then he is gracious to him and says: Deliver him from going down to the pit; I have found a ransom.
25 His flesh becomes fresher than in youth; he returns to the days of his vigor.
26 He prays to God, and he accepts him, and he sees his face with a shout of joy, and he restores to man his righteousness.
27 He sings before men and says: I sinned and perverted what was right, and it was not repaid to me.
28 He has redeemed his soul from crossing into the pit, and his life will see the light.
29 See, God does all these things, twice, three times, with a man,
30 to bring back his soul from the pit, to be lit with the light of the living.
31 Pay attention, Job, listen to me; be silent, and I will speak.
32 If you have words, answer me; speak, for I desire to justify you.
33 If not, you listen to me; be silent, and I will teach you wisdom.

24 With trembling and rage he swallows the ground; he cannot stand still at the sound of the trumpet.
25 At each blast of the trumpet he says: Aha! And from afar he smells the battle, the thunder of the captains and the war cry.
26 Is it by your understanding that the hawk soars, and spreads his wings toward the south?
27 Is it at your command that the eagle mounts up, and makes his nest on high?
28 He dwells on the rock and spends the night there, on the crag of the rock and the stronghold.
29 From there he spies out food; his eyes see it from afar.
30 His young ones suck up blood, and where the slain are, there he is.

Job 40

1 And Yahweh answered Job, and said:
2 Will the one who contends with the Almighty correct him? Let the one who argues with God answer it.
3 Then Job answered Yahweh, and said:
4 See, I am of small account; what can I answer you? I lay my hand on my mouth.
5 I have spoken once, and I will not answer; twice, and I will add no more.
6 Then Yahweh answered Job out of the whirlwind, and said:
7 Brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you will answer me.
8 Will you indeed annul my judgment? Will you condemn me, so that you may be in the right?
9 Do you have an arm like God, and can you thunder with a voice like his?
10 Dress yourself, then, in majesty and splendor, and clothe yourself with glory and beauty.
11 Pour out the overflowings of your anger, and look on everyone who is proud, and bring him low.
12 Look on everyone who is proud and humble him, and crush the wicked where they stand.
13 Hide them in the dust together; bind their faces in the hidden place.
14 Then I too will praise you, because your own right hand has saved you.
15 See now Behemoth, which I made along with you; he eats grass like an ox.
16 See now, his strength is in his loins, and his power in the muscles of his belly.
17 He makes his tail stiff like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are knit together.
18 His bones are tubes of bronze; his limbs are like bars of iron.
19 He is the first of the ways of God; only his Maker can bring his sword near him.
20 For the mountains bring him food, and all the animals of the field play there.
21 He lies under the lotus trees, in the cover of the reeds and the marsh.
22 The lotus trees cover him with their shade; the willows of the brook surround him.
23 See, if a river rages, he does not tremble; he is confident, though the Jordan rushes to his mouth.
24 Can anyone take him by his eyes, or pierce his nose with snares?
25 Can you draw out Leviathan with a fishhook, or press down his tongue with a cord?
26 Can you put a reed rope in his nose, or pierce his jaw with a hook?
27 Will he make many pleas to you, or speak soft words to you?
28 Will he make a covenant with you, to take him as a servant forever?
29 Will you play with him as with a bird, or bind him for your girls?
30 Will traders barter for him? Will they divide him among the merchants?
31 Can you fill his skin with barbed spears, or his head with fish harpoons?
32 Lay your hand on him; remember the battle — you will not do it again.

Job 41

1 See, hope in him is disappointed; is one not cast down even at the sight of him?
2 No one is so fierce that he dares stir him up; who then can stand before me?
3 Who has given to me first, that I should repay him? Whatever is under the whole heaven is mine.
4 I will not keep silent about his limbs, or the matter of his mighty strength, or the grace of his proportions.
5 Who can strip off his outer garment? Who can come within his double bridle?
6 Who can open the doors of his face? Around his teeth is terror.
7 Rows of shields are his pride, shut up with a close seal.
8 One touches the other, and no air can come between them.
9 They stick to one another; they clasp each other and cannot be separated.
10 His sneezes flash forth light, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the dawn.
11 Out of his mouth go flaming torches; sparks of fire leap out.
12 Out of his nostrils comes smoke, like a boiling pot over burning reeds.
13 His breath kindles coals, and a flame comes out of his mouth.
14 In his neck lodges strength, and dismay leaps before him.
15 The folds of his flesh stick together, cast firm on him, unmovable.
16 His heart is cast hard like a stone, yes, hard like the lower millstone.
17 When he rises up, the mighty are afraid; at his breakings they are beside themselves.
18 The sword that reaches him does not avail, nor spear, dart, or javelin.
19 He counts iron as straw, and bronze as rotten wood.
20 The arrow cannot make him flee; sling stones are turned to stubble for him.
21 Clubs are counted as stubble; he laughs at the rattling of the javelin.
22 Beneath him are sharp potsherds; he spreads a threshing sledge on the mud.
23 He makes the deep boil like a pot; he makes the sea like a pot of ointment.
24 Behind him he makes a path shine; one would think the deep had white hair.
25 On earth there is nothing like him, made to be without fear.
26 He looks on everything that is high; he is king over all the sons of pride.

Job 42

1 Then Job answered Yahweh, and said:
2 I know that you can do all things, and that no purpose of yours can be thwarted.
3 Who is this who hides counsel without knowledge? Therefore I declared what I did not understand, things too wonderful for me, which I did not know.
4 Listen, please, and I will speak; I will question you, and you will answer me.
5 I had heard of you by the hearing of the ear, but now my eye sees you.
6 Therefore I despise myself, and repent in dust and ashes.
7 And it was so, after Yahweh had spoken these words to Job, that Yahweh said to Eliphaz the Temanite: My anger burns against you and against your two friends, for you have not spoken of me what is right, as my servant Job has.
8 Now therefore take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer up a burnt offering for yourselves, and my servant Job will pray for you; for I will accept him, so that I do not deal with you according to your folly, for you have not spoken of me what is right, as my servant Job has.
9 So Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite went and did as Yahweh had spoken to them, and Yahweh accepted Job.
10 Yahweh restored the fortunes of Job, when he prayed for his friends, and Yahweh gave Job twice as much as he had before.
11 Then all his brothers and all his sisters and all who had known him before came to him, and ate bread with him in his house, and they sympathized with him and comforted him over all the evil that Yahweh had brought on him; and each one gave him a qesitah, and each one a ring of gold.
12 And Yahweh blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning, and he had fourteen thousand sheep, six thousand camels, a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand female donkeys.
13 And he had seven sons and three daughters.
14 And he called the name of the first Jemimah, and the name of the second Keziah, and the name of the third Keren Happuch.
15 And in all the land no women were found as beautiful as the daughters of Job, and their father gave them an inheritance among their brothers.
16 And Job lived after this a hundred and forty years, and saw his sons and his sons' sons, four generations.
17 And Job died, old and full of days.
