Hosea 1

1 The word of Yahweh that came to Hosea son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

2 When Yahweh first spoke through Hosea, Yahweh said to Hosea: Go, take for yourself a wife of prostitution and children of prostitution, for the land commits great prostitution, turning away from Yahweh.

3 So he went and took Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.

4 Yahweh said to him: Call his name Jezreel, for in a little while I will punish the house of Jehu for the blood of Jezreel, and I will bring the kingdom of the house of Israel to an end.

5 On that day I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel.

6 She conceived again and bore a daughter. He said to him: Call her name Lo-Ruhamah, for I will no longer have compassion on the house of Israel, that I should pardon them at all.

7 But I will have compassion on the house of Judah, and I will save them by Yahweh their God; I will not save them by bow, or by sword, or by battle, or by horses, or by horsemen.

8 When she had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son.

9 He said: Call his name Lo-Ammi, for you are not my people, and I will not be yours.

Hosea 2

1 The number of the children of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or counted; and in the place where it was said to them: You are not my people, it will be said to them: Children of the living God.

2 The children of Judah and the children of Israel will be gathered together, and they will appoint one head for themselves, and they will go up from the land, for great is the day of Jezreel.

3 Say to your brothers: Ammi, and to your sisters: Ruhamah.

4 Plead with your mother, plead, for she is not my wife and I am not her husband. Let her put away her prostitution from her face and her adultery from between her breasts,

5 lest I strip her naked and set her out as on the day she was born, and make her like a wilderness, and set her like a dry land, and kill her with thirst.

6 I will have no compassion on her children, for they are children of prostitution.

7 For their mother has played the prostitute; she who conceived them has acted shamefully, for she said: I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink.

8 Therefore I will hedge up her way with thorns, and I will wall her in, so that she cannot find her paths.

9 She will pursue her lovers but not overtake them; she will seek them but not find them. Then she will say: I will go and return to my first husband, for it was better for me then than now.

10 She did not know that it was I who gave her the grain, the new wine, and the oil, and multiplied silver for her, and gold, which they used for Baal.

11 Therefore I will take back my grain in its time and my new wine in its season, and I will snatch away my wool and my flax, which were to cover her nakedness.

12 Now I will uncover her shame in the sight of her lovers, and no one will rescue her from my hand.

13 I will bring all her joy to an end: her feasts, her new moons, her Sabbaths, and all her appointed festivals.

14 I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees, of which she said: These are my hire, which my lovers have given me. I will make them a forest, and the animals of the field will eat them.

15 I will punish her for the days of the Baals, when she burned incense to them and adorned herself with her earrings and her jewels and went after her lovers and forgot me, declares Yahweh.

16 Therefore I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak to her heart.

17 I will give her her vineyards from there, and the Valley of Achor as a door of hope. She will respond there as in the days of her youth, as on the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt.

18 On that day, declares Yahweh, you will call me my husband, and you will no longer call me my Baal.

19 I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and they will no longer be remembered by their name.

20 On that day I will make a covenant for them with the animals of the field, with the birds of the sky, and with the creeping things of the ground; and I will break bow and sword and war from the land, and I will make them lie down in safety.

21 I will betroth you to me forever; I will betroth you to me in righteousness and in justice, in loyal love and in compassion.

22 I will betroth you to me in faithfulness, and you will know Yahweh.

23 On that day I will respond, declares Yahweh; I will respond to the heavens, and they will respond to the earth,

24 and the earth will respond to the grain, the new wine, and the oil, and they will respond to Jezreel.

25 I will sow her for myself in the land, and I will have compassion on Lo-Ruhamah, and I will say to Lo-Ammi: You are my people, and he will say: My God.

Hosea 3

1 Yahweh said to me: Go again, love a woman who is loved by a companion and is an adulteress, just as Yahweh loves the children of Israel, though they turn to other gods and love raisin cakes.

2 So I bought her for myself for fifteen pieces of silver, and a homer of barley and a lethech of barley.

3 I said to her: You will stay with me many days; you will not play the prostitute, and you will not belong to a man, and I also will be the same toward you.

4 For the children of Israel will stay many days without king and without prince, without sacrifice and without sacred pillar, without ephod and household gods.

5 Afterward the children of Israel will return and seek Yahweh their God and David their king, and they will come trembling to Yahweh and to his goodness in the last days.

Hosea 4

1 Hear the word of Yahweh, children of Israel, for Yahweh has a case against the inhabitants of the land, because there is no truth and no loyal love and no knowledge of God in the land.

2 Swearing and lying, murder and stealing and adultery: they break out, and bloodshed follows bloodshed.

3 Therefore the land mourns, and everyone who lives in it languishes, together with the animals of the field and the birds of the sky; even the fish of the sea are taken away.

4 Yet let no one bring a charge, and let no one accuse, for your people are like those who bring a charge against a priest.

5 You will stumble by day, and the prophet will also stumble with you by night, and I will destroy your mother.

6 My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I will reject you from being a priest to me; and because you have forgotten the teaching of your God, I also will forget your children.

7 The more they increased, the more they sinned against me; I will change their glory into shame.

8 They feed on the sin of my people, and they set their heart on their iniquity.

9 It will be like people, like priest; I will punish him for his ways and repay him for his deeds.

10 They will eat and not be satisfied; they will play the prostitute and not increase, because they have stopped taking heed of Yahweh.

11 Prostitution, wine, and new wine take away the heart.

12 My people consult their wood, and their staff declares to them, for a spirit of prostitution has led them astray, and they play the prostitute away from their God.

13 They sacrifice on the tops of the mountains and burn incense on the hills, under oak, poplar, and terebinth, because their shade is good. Therefore your daughters play the prostitute and your daughters-in-law commit adultery.

14 I will not punish your daughters when they play the prostitute, or your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery, for the men themselves go aside with prostitutes and sacrifice with the sacred prostitutes, and a people without understanding comes to ruin.

15 Though you, Israel, play the prostitute, do not let Judah become guilty. Do not come to Gilgal, do not go up to Beth Aven, and do not swear: As Yahweh lives.

16 For Israel is stubborn like a stubborn heifer; now will Yahweh feed them like a lamb in a wide pasture?

17 Ephraim is joined to idols; leave him alone.

18 Their drink is sour; they continually play the prostitute; her rulers deeply love shame.

19 The wind has wrapped her in its wings, and they will be ashamed because of their sacrifices.

Hosea 5

1 Hear this, priests, and listen, house of Israel, and give ear, house of the king, for the judgment is for you, because you have been a snare at Mizpah and a net spread on Tabor.

2 The rebels have dug deep in slaughter, but I will discipline all of them.

3 I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hidden from me, for now you, Ephraim, have played the prostitute; Israel is defiled.

4 Their deeds do not allow them to return to their God, for a spirit of prostitution is within them, and they do not know Yahweh.

5 The pride of Israel testifies to his face; Israel and Ephraim will stumble in their iniquity; Judah also will stumble with them.

6 With their flocks and their herds they will go to seek Yahweh, but they will not find him; he has withdrawn from them.

7 They have dealt treacherously against Yahweh, for they have borne foreign children. Now the new moon will devour them together with their portions.

8 Blow the horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah; sound the alarm at Beth Aven: Behind you, Benjamin!

9 Ephraim will become a desolation on the day of rebuke; among the tribes of Israel I have made known what is sure.

10 The princes of Judah have become like those who move a boundary; I will pour out my wrath on them like water.

11 Ephraim is oppressed, crushed in judgment, because he was determined to go after filth.

12 I am like a moth to Ephraim and like rottenness to the house of Judah.

13 When Ephraim saw his sickness and Judah his wound, Ephraim went to Assyria and sent to King Jareb, but he cannot heal you, and he will not cure you of your wound.

14 For I am like a lion to Ephraim and like a young lion to the house of Judah. I, I myself, will tear and go away; I will carry off, and there will be no one to rescue.

15 I will go and return to my place until they acknowledge their guilt and seek my face; in their distress they will seek me eagerly.

Hosea 6

1 Come, let us return to Yahweh, for he has torn and he will heal us; he has struck and he will bind us up.

2 After two days he will revive us; on the third day he will raise us up, and we will live before him.

3 Let us know, let us press on to know Yahweh. His going forth is as sure as the dawn; he will come to us like the rain, like the spring rain that waters the earth.

4 What shall I do with you, Ephraim? What shall I do with you, Judah? Your loyal love is like a morning cloud and like the dew that goes away early.

5 Therefore I have hewn them by the prophets; I have killed them by the words of my mouth, and your judgments go forth like the light.

6 For I desire loyal love and not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings.

7 But they, like Adam, have transgressed the covenant; there they dealt treacherously against me.

8 Gilead is a city of evildoers, tracked with blood.

9 As robbers lie in wait for a man, so the company of priests murder on the way to Shechem; indeed, they commit a shameful crime.

10 In the house of Israel I have seen a horrible thing; there prostitution is in Ephraim; Israel is defiled.

11 Also for you, Judah, a harvest is appointed, when I restore the fortunes of my people.

Hosea 7

1 When I would heal Israel, the iniquity of Ephraim is uncovered and the evils of Samaria, for they practice falsehood; the thief enters, and a band of robbers raids outside.

2 They do not say to their heart that I remember all their evil. Now their deeds surround them; they are before my face.

3 With their evil they make the king glad, and with their lies the princes.

4 They are all adulterers, like an oven heated by the baker, who stops stirring from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened.

5 On the day of our king, the princes became sick with the heat of wine; he stretched out his hand with mockers.

6 For they draw their heart near like an oven in their plotting; all night their baker sleeps; in the morning it burns like a flaming fire.

7 All of them are hot like an oven, and they devour their judges; all their kings have fallen; there is none among them who calls to me.

8 Ephraim mixes himself among the peoples; Ephraim is a cake not turned.

9 Strangers have devoured his strength, and he does not know it; gray hairs are sprinkled on him, and he does not know it.

10 The pride of Israel testifies to his face, but they have not returned to Yahweh their God, nor sought him, for all this.

11 Ephraim has become like a dove, easily deceived, without heart; they call to Egypt, they go to Assyria.

12 As they go, I will spread my net over them; I will bring them down like the birds of the sky; I will discipline them, as their congregation has heard.

13 Woe to them, for they have strayed from me; destruction to them, for they have rebelled against me. I would redeem them, but they have spoken lies against me.

14 They have not cried to me with their heart, though they wail on their beds; for grain and new wine they assemble; they turn away from me.

15 I disciplined them and strengthened their arms, but they devise evil against me.

16 They return, but not upward; they have become like a treacherous bow. Their princes will fall by the sword because of the rage of their tongue; this is their derision in the land of Egypt.

Hosea 8

1 Put a trumpet to your lips. Like an eagle over the house of Yahweh, because they have broken my covenant and rebelled against my law.

2 They cry to me: My God, we know you, Israel.

3 Israel has rejected what is good; the enemy will pursue him.

4 They set up kings, but not by me; they made princes without my knowledge. From their silver and their gold they made idols for themselves, so that they would be cut off.

5 Your calf, Samaria, is cast off; my anger burns against them. How long will they be unable to attain purity?

6 For it is from Israel; a craftsman made it, and it is not God. The calf of Samaria will be broken to pieces.

7 For they sow wind and will reap the whirlwind. There is no standing grain; the sprout will yield no flour. If it yields any, strangers will swallow it up.

8 Israel is swallowed up; now they are among the nations like a vessel no one values.

9 For they have gone up to Assyria, like a wild donkey wandering alone. Ephraim has hired lovers.

10 Though they hire among the nations, now I will gather them, and they will begin to waste away under the burden of king and princes.

11 Ephraim has multiplied altars to sin; they have become altars to sin for him.

12 I wrote for him the many things of my law, but they were regarded as a strange thing.

13 They sacrifice flesh as my gift-offerings and eat it, but Yahweh does not accept them. Now he will remember their iniquity and punish their sins; they will return to Egypt.

14 Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces, and Judah has multiplied fortified cities; but I will send fire on his cities, and it will devour its palaces.

Hosea 9

1 Do not rejoice, Israel, with exultation like the peoples, for you have prostituted yourself, forsaking your God; you have loved a prostitute's wages on every threshing floor of grain.

2 The threshing floor and the winepress will not feed them, and the new wine will fail her.

3 They will not dwell in the land of Yahweh; Ephraim will return to Egypt, and in Assyria they will eat unclean food.

4 They will not pour out wine to Yahweh, and their sacrifices will not please him. Their sacrifices will be like the bread of mourners to them; all who eat of it will be defiled, for their bread is only for their appetite; it will not come into the house of Yahweh.

5 What will you do on the day of the appointed feast, and on the day of the feast of Yahweh?

6 For see, they have fled from destruction; Egypt will gather them, Memphis will bury them. Nettles will possess their treasured things of silver; thorns will be in their tents.

7 The days of punishment have come; the days of recompense have come; Israel will know it. The prophet is a fool; the man of the spirit is mad, because of your great iniquity and great hostility.

8 Ephraim is a watchman with my God; as for the prophet, a fowler's snare is on all his ways, and hostility in the house of his God.

9 They have deeply corrupted themselves, as in the days of Gibeah. He will remember their iniquity; he will punish their sins.

10 I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness; I saw your fathers as the first fruit on the fig tree in its first season. But they came to Baal Peor and consecrated themselves to the shameful thing, and became detestable like the thing they loved.

11 Ephraim's glory will fly away like a bird: no birth, no one with child, no conception.

12 Even if they raise their children, I will bereave them until no one is left. Indeed, woe to them when I depart from them.

13 Ephraim, as I have seen, is like Tyre planted in a meadow; but Ephraim will bring his children out to the slayer.

14 Give them, Yahweh — what will you give? Give them a womb that miscarries and dry breasts.

15 All their wickedness is in Gilgal, for there I came to hate them. Because of the wickedness of their deeds I will drive them out of my house; I will love them no more. All their princes are rebels.

16 Ephraim is struck; their root has dried up; they will bear no fruit. Even if they bear children, I will kill the beloved offspring of their womb.

17 My God will reject them, because they did not listen to him, and they will be wanderers among the nations.

Hosea 10

1 Israel is a luxuriant vine that yields its fruit. The more his fruit increased, the more altars he built; the better his land, the better they made their pillars.

2 Their heart is divided; now they will bear their guilt. He will break down their altars; he will destroy their pillars.

3 For now they will say: We have no king, for we did not fear Yahweh; and the king, what can he do for us?

4 They speak mere words, swearing falsely and making a covenant; so judgment springs up like a poisonous plant in the furrows of the field.

5 The inhabitants of Samaria fear for the calves of Beth Aven; its people mourn over it, and its priests tremble over it, over its glory, because it has gone into exile from them.

6 It too will be carried to Assyria as a present to King Jareb. Ephraim will receive shame, and Israel will be ashamed of his counsel.

7 Samaria's king is cut off, like foam on the surface of the water.

8 The high places of Aven, the sin of Israel, will be destroyed. Thorn and thistle will come up on their altars, and they will say to the mountains: Cover us, and to the hills: Fall on us.

9 From the days of Gibeah you have sinned, Israel. There they stood; battle in Gibeah against the sons of iniquity will not overtake them.

10 When I desire, I will discipline them, and peoples will be gathered against them when they are bound for their two iniquities.

11 Ephraim is a trained heifer that loves to thresh, but I spared her fair neck. I will set a rider on Ephraim; Judah will plow; Jacob will break the clods for himself.

12 Sow for yourselves in righteousness; reap according to loyal love. Break up your fallow ground, for it is time to seek Yahweh, until he comes and rains righteousness on you.

13 You have plowed wickedness, reaped injustice, and eaten the fruit of lies, because you trusted in your way, in the multitude of your warriors.

14 A tumult will arise among your people, and all your fortresses will be destroyed, as Shalman destroyed Beth Arbel on the day of battle, when a mother was dashed in pieces together with her children.

15 So Bethel has done to you because of your great wickedness. At dawn the king of Israel will be utterly cut off.

Hosea 11

1 When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called my son.

2 They called them, and so they went away from them; they sacrificed to the Baals and made offerings to carved images.

3 Yet I taught Ephraim to walk, taking them by their arms, but they did not know that I healed them.

4 I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love, and I was to them like those who lift the yoke from their jaws, and I bent down to him and fed him.

5 He will not return to the land of Egypt, but Assyria will be his king, because they refused to return.

6 The sword will fall on his cities, destroy his bars, and devour them, because of their own counsels.

7 My people are determined to turn from me. Though they call to the Most High, he will not exalt them at all.

8 How can I give you up, Ephraim? How can I hand you over, Israel? How can I make you like Admah? How can I treat you like Zeboiim? My heart is turned over within me; my compassions are kindled together.

9 I will not act on the fierceness of my anger; I will not return to destroy Ephraim, for I am God and not a man, the Holy One in your midst, and I will not come in wrath.

10 They will walk after Yahweh; he will roar like a lion, for when he roars, children will come trembling from the west.

11 They will come trembling like a bird from Egypt and like a dove from the land of Assyria, and I will settle them in their homes, declares Yahweh.

Hosea 12

1 Ephraim has surrounded me with lies, and the house of Israel with deceit; and Judah is still wayward toward God, and toward the Holy One, who is faithful.

2 Ephraim feeds on wind and pursues the east wind; all day he multiplies lies and violence. They make a covenant with Assyria, and oil is carried to Egypt.

3 Yahweh has a dispute with Judah, and he will punish Jacob according to his ways; he will repay him according to his deeds.

4 In the womb he grasped his brother's heel, and in his strength he strove with God.

5 He strove with the angel and prevailed; he wept and sought his favor. He will find him at Bethel, and there he will speak with us.

6 Yahweh is the God of hosts; Yahweh is his memorial.

7 And you, return to your God; keep loyal love and justice, and wait for your God continually.

8 The trader has scales of deceit in his hand; he loves to oppress.

9 Ephraim said: Surely I have become rich; I have found wealth for myself. In all my labors they will find in me no iniquity that is sin.

10 But I am Yahweh your God from the land of Egypt; I will again make you dwell in tents, as in the days of the appointed feast.

11 I spoke to the prophets, and I multiplied visions, and through the prophets I gave parables.

12 If Gilead is iniquity, they have become sheer emptiness; in Gilgal they sacrifice bulls; indeed their altars are like stone heaps on the furrows of the field.

13 Jacob fled to the field of Aram, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.

14 By a prophet Yahweh brought Israel up from Egypt, and by a prophet he was kept.

15 Ephraim has provoked bitter anger; his bloodshed will be left on him, and his Lord will repay him his reproach.

Hosea 13

1 When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling; he was exalted in Israel, but he became guilty through Baal and died.

2 Now they continue to sin and make for themselves a molten image from their silver, idols according to their own understanding, all of it the work of craftsmen. Of them they say: Those who sacrifice human beings kiss calves.

3 Therefore they will be like the morning cloud and like the dew that goes away early, like chaff driven by a whirlwind from the threshing floor, and like smoke from a window.

4 But I am Yahweh your God from the land of Egypt; you shall know no god besides me, and there is no savior apart from me.

5 I knew you in the wilderness, in a land of burning drought.

6 When they were pastured, they were satisfied; they were satisfied, and their heart was lifted up; therefore they forgot me.

7 So I have become to them like a lion; like a leopard I will watch beside the way.

8 I will meet them like a bear bereaved of her cubs and tear open the enclosure of their heart; there I will devour them like a lion; the wild beast of the field will tear them to pieces.

9 You are destroyed, Israel, because you are against me, against your helper.

10 Where is your king now, that he may save you in all your cities, and your judges, of whom you said: Give me a king and princes?

11 I gave you a king in my anger, and I took him away in my wrath.

12 Ephraim's iniquity is bound up; his sin is stored away.

13 The pains of a woman in labor will come upon him. He is an unwise son, for when the time comes, he does not stand at the place where children break forth.

14 I will ransom them from the power of Sheol; I will redeem them from death. Death, where are your plagues? Sheol, where is your destruction? Compassion will be hidden from my eyes.

15 Though he is fruitful among brothers, an east wind will come, the wind of Yahweh rising from the wilderness, and his spring will dry up and his fountain will be parched. He will plunder the treasury of every precious vessel.

Hosea 14

1 Samaria will bear her guilt, for she has rebelled against her God. They will fall by the sword; their infants will be dashed in pieces, and their pregnant women will be ripped open.

2 Return, Israel, to Yahweh your God, for you have stumbled in your iniquity.

3 Take words with you and return to Yahweh. Say to him: Forgive all iniquity and receive what is good; then we will render as bulls the offering of our lips.

4 Assyria will not save us; we will not ride on horses, and we will no longer say to the work of our hands: Our gods, for in you the fatherless finds compassion.

5 I will heal their turning away; I will love them freely, for my anger has turned away from him.

6 I will be like the dew to Israel; he will blossom like the lily and strike his roots like Lebanon.

7 His shoots will spread out, and his splendor will be like the olive tree, and his fragrance like Lebanon.

8 They who dwell in his shadow will return and revive grain and blossom like the vine; his renown will be like the wine of Lebanon.

9 Ephraim, what have I to do with idols any more? I have answered him and I watch over him. I am like a luxuriant cypress; from me your fruit is found.

10 Who is wise, let him understand these things; who is discerning, let him know them. For the ways of Yahweh are right, and the righteous walk in them, but transgressors stumble in them.
