Exodus 1

1 Now these are the names of the sons of Israel who came into Egypt with Jacob; each man came with his household:

2 Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,

3 Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,

4 Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

5 And all the persons who came out of Jacob's loins were seventy persons, and Joseph was in Egypt.

6 And Joseph died, and all his brothers, and all that generation.

7 And the sons of Israel were fruitful, and swarmed, and multiplied, and grew very, very strong, and the land was filled with them.

8 And a new king arose over Egypt who did not know Joseph.

9 And he said to his people, “Look, the people of the sons of Israel are more numerous and stronger than we are.

10 Come, let us deal wisely with them, lest they multiply, and it happen, when war befalls us, that they also join our enemies, and fight against us, and go up from the land.”

11 And they set taskmasters over them to afflict them with their burdens. And they built store cities for Pharaoh: Pithom and Raamses.

12 And as they afflicted them, so they multiplied and so they spread, and they dreaded the sons of Israel.

13 And Egypt made the sons of Israel serve with rigour.

14 And they made their lives bitter with hard service, in mortar and in brick, and in all service in the field — all their service in which they made them serve with rigour.

15 And the king of Egypt said to the Hebrew midwives, of whom the name of the one was Shiphrah and the name of the second Puah.

16 And he said, “When you help the Hebrew women to give birth, and you see them on the birthstool, if it is a son, you shall kill him, and if it is a daughter, she shall live.”

17 And the midwives feared God, and did not do as the king of Egypt had spoken to them, and they let the children live.

18 And the king of Egypt called for the midwives and said to them, “Why have you done this thing, and let the children live?”

19 And the midwives said to Pharaoh, “Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women, for they are vigorous; before the midwife comes to them, they have given birth.”

20 And God dealt well with the midwives, and the people multiplied and grew very strong.

21 And it happened, because the midwives feared God, that he made houses for them.

22 And Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, “Every son who is born you shall throw into the Nile, and every daughter you shall let live.”

Exodus 2

1 And a man from the house of Levi went and took a daughter of Levi.

2 And the woman conceived and bore a son, and she saw him, that he was good, and she hid him three months.

3 And she could no longer hide him, and she took for him a basket of papyrus, and daubed it with bitumen and with pitch, and she put the child in it, and put it in the reeds on the bank of the Nile.

4 And his sister stationed herself at a distance, to know what would be done to him.

5 And Pharaoh's daughter went down to bathe at the Nile, and her young women were walking along the side of the Nile. And she saw the basket in the middle of the reeds, and she sent her female slave, and she took it.

6 And she opened it, and saw him, the child, and look, a boy crying. And she had compassion on him and said, “This is one of the children of the Hebrews.”

7 And his sister said to Pharaoh's daughter, “Shall I go and call for you a nursing woman from the Hebrew women, so that she may nurse the child for you?”

8 And Pharaoh's daughter said to her, “Go.” And the girl went and called the child's mother.

9 And Pharaoh's daughter said to her, “Take this child away and nurse him for me, and I will give you your wages.” And the woman took the child and nursed him.

10 And the child grew, and she brought him to Pharaoh's daughter, and he became a son to her. And she called his name Moses, and said, “Because I drew him out of the water.”

11 And it happened in those days that Moses grew up and went out to his brothers and saw their burdens. And he saw an Egyptian man striking a Hebrew man, one of his brothers.

12 And he turned this way and that way, and saw that there was no man, and he struck down the Egyptian and hid him in the sand.

13 And he went out on the second day, and look, two Hebrew men were fighting. And he said to the wicked one, “Why do you strike your neighbour?”

14 And he said, “Who set you as a ruler and a judge over us? Do you mean to kill me, as you killed the Egyptian?” And Moses feared and said, “Surely the matter is known.”

15 And Pharaoh heard this matter and sought to kill Moses. And Moses fled from before Pharaoh and lived in the land of Midian, and he sat down by the well.

16 And the priest of Midian had seven daughters. And they came and drew water and filled the troughs to water their father's flock.

17 And the shepherds came and drove them away, and Moses arose and saved them and watered their flock.

18 And they came to Reuel their father, and he said, “Why have you hurried to come today?”

19 And they said, “An Egyptian man saved us from the hand of the shepherds, and he even drew water for us, and watered the flock.”

20 And he said to his daughters, “And where is he? Why is this — you have left the man? Call him, so that he may eat bread.”

21 And Moses was willing to live with the man, and he gave Zipporah, his daughter, to Moses.

22 And she bore a son, and he called his name Gershom, for he said, “I have been a sojourner in a foreign land.”

23 And it happened in those many days that the king of Egypt died, and the sons of Israel groaned because of the service and cried out, and their cry for help went up to God because of the service.

24 And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob.

25 And God saw the sons of Israel, and God knew.

Exodus 3

1 And Moses was shepherding the flock of Jethro, his father-in-law, the priest of Midian, and he led the flock behind the wilderness and came to the mountain of God, to Horeb.

2 And the angel of Yahweh appeared to him in a flame of fire from the middle of the bush. And he looked, and look, the bush was burning with fire, and the bush was not consumed.

3 And Moses said, “Let me turn aside now and see this great sight, why the bush does not burn.”

4 And Yahweh saw that he had turned aside to see, and God called to him from the middle of the bush and said, “Moses, Moses.” And he said, “Here I am.”

5 And he said, “Do not come near here. Take your sandals off your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground.”

6 And he said, “I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.” And Moses hid his face, for he was afraid to look at God.

7 And Yahweh said, “I have surely seen the affliction of my people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their cry because of their taskmasters, for I know their pains.”

8 “And I have come down to deliver them from the hand of Egypt, and to bring them up from that land to a good and wide land, to a land flowing with milk and honey, to the place of the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite.

9 And now, look, the cry of the sons of Israel has come to me, and I have also seen the oppression with which Egypt oppresses them.

10 And now go, and I will send you to Pharaoh, and bring my people, the sons of Israel, out of Egypt.”

11 And Moses said to God, “Who am I, that I should go to Pharaoh, and that I should bring the sons of Israel out of Egypt?”

12 And he said, “For I will be with you, and this will be the sign for you that I have sent you: when you bring the people out of Egypt, you shall serve God on this mountain.”

13 And Moses said to God, “Look, I am coming to the sons of Israel, and I will say to them, ‘The God of your fathers has sent me to you,’ and they will say to me, ‘What is his name?’ What shall I say to them?”

14 And God said to Moses, “I AM WHO I AM.” And he said, “Thus you shall say to the sons of Israel: ‘I AM has sent me to you.’”

15 And God said again to Moses, “Thus you shall say to the sons of Israel: ‘Yahweh, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.’ This is my name forever, and this is my memorial from generation to generation.

16 “Go and gather the elders of Israel, and say to them, ‘Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has appeared to me — the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob — saying, “I have surely taken notice of you, and of what is done to you in Egypt.

17 And I have said, I will bring you up from the affliction of Egypt to the land of the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Amorite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite, to a land flowing with milk and honey.”’

18 And they will listen to your voice, and you shall come, you and the elders of Israel, to the king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, ‘Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews, has met us. And now, let us go, please, a journey of three days into the wilderness, and let us sacrifice to Yahweh, our God.’

19 And I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go, except by a strong hand.

20 And I will send out my hand and strike Egypt with all my wonders that I will do in its midst, and after that he will send you away.

21 And I will give this people favour in the eyes of Egypt, and it will happen that, when you go, you will not go empty.

22 And a woman shall ask from her neighbour, and from the woman who sojourns in her house, articles of silver and articles of gold and garments, and you shall put them on your sons and on your daughters, and you shall plunder Egypt.”

Exodus 4

1 And Moses answered and said, “But look, they will not believe me, and will not listen to my voice, for they will say, ‘Yahweh has not appeared to you.’”

2 And Yahweh said to him, “What is this in your hand?” And he said, “A staff.”

3 And he said, “Throw it to the ground.” And he threw it to the ground, and it became a serpent, and Moses fled from before it.

4 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Send out your hand and grasp its tail.” And he sent out his hand and took hold of it, and it became a staff in his palm.

5 “So that they may believe that Yahweh, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has appeared to you.”

6 And Yahweh said to him again, “Please bring your hand into your bosom.” And he brought his hand into his bosom and took it out, and look, his hand was diseased, like snow.

7 And he said, “Put your hand back into your bosom.” And he put his hand back into his bosom and took it out from his bosom, and look, it had returned like his flesh.

8 “And it will be, if they do not believe you and do not listen to the voice of the first sign, they will believe the voice of the latter sign.

9 And it will be, if they do not believe even these two signs and do not listen to your voice, you shall take from the waters of the Nile and pour them on the dry ground, and the waters that you take from the Nile will become blood on the dry ground.”

10 And Moses said to Yahweh, “Please, my Lord, I am not a man of words, neither in the past nor since you have spoken to your slave, for I am heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue.”

11 And Yahweh said to him, “Who put a mouth in man? Or who makes mute, or deaf, or seeing, or blind? Is it not I, Yahweh?

12 And now go, and I will be with your mouth, and I will teach you what you shall speak.”

13 And he said, “Please, my Lord, send, please, by the hand of whom you will send.”

14 And Yahweh's anger burned against Moses, and he said, “Is there not Aaron, your brother, the Levite? I know that he will surely speak. And also, look, he is coming out to meet you, and he will see you and rejoice in his heart.

15 And you shall speak to him and put the words in his mouth, and I will be with your mouth and with his mouth, and I will teach you what you shall do.

16 And he shall speak for you to the people, and it will be that he will be a mouth for you, and you shall be as God to him.

17 And you shall take this staff in your hand, with which you shall do the signs.”

18 And Moses went and returned to Jether, his father-in-law, and said to him, “Let me go, please, and return to my brothers who are in Egypt, and see whether they are still alive.” And Jethro said to Moses, “Go in peace.”

19 And Yahweh said to Moses in Midian, “Go, return to Egypt, for all the men who sought your life have died.”

20 And Moses took his wife and his sons and set them on the donkey, and he returned to the land of Egypt, and Moses took the staff of God in his hand.

21 And Yahweh said to Moses, “When you go to return to Egypt, see that you do before Pharaoh all the wonders that I have put in your hand, and I will strengthen his heart, and he will not send the people away.

22 And you shall say to Pharaoh, ‘Thus says Yahweh: Israel is my son, my firstborn.

23 And I have said to you, “Send my son away, so that he may serve me,” and you have refused to send him away. Look, I am killing your son, your firstborn.’”

24 And it happened on the way, at the lodging place, that Yahweh met him and sought to kill him.

25 And Zipporah took a flint and cut off the foreskin of her son, and made it touch his feet, and said, “For you are a bridegroom of blood to me.”

26 And he let him alone. Then she said, “A bridegroom of blood,” with regard to the circumcision.

27 And Yahweh said to Aaron, “Go to meet Moses, into the wilderness.” And he went and met him at the mountain of God and kissed him.

28 And Moses told Aaron all the words of Yahweh with which he had sent him, and all the signs that he had commanded him.

29 And Moses and Aaron went and gathered all the elders of the sons of Israel.

30 And Aaron spoke all the words that Yahweh had spoken to Moses, and he did the signs in the eyes of the people.

31 And the people believed, and they heard that Yahweh had taken notice of the sons of Israel and that he had seen their affliction, and they bowed down and prostrated themselves.

Exodus 5

1 And afterwards Moses and Aaron came and said to Pharaoh, “Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: ‘Send my people away, so that they may hold a feast to me in the wilderness.’”

2 And Pharaoh said, “Who is Yahweh, that I should listen to his voice, to send Israel away? I do not know Yahweh, and also I will not send Israel away.”

3 And they said, “The God of the Hebrews has met us. Let us go, please, a journey of three days into the wilderness, and let us sacrifice to Yahweh, our God, lest he fall on us with pestilence or with the sword.”

4 And the king of Egypt said to them, “Why, Moses and Aaron, do you let the people loose from their work? Go to your burdens.”

5 And Pharaoh said, “Look, the people of the land are now many, and you would make them rest from their burdens.”

6 And Pharaoh commanded, on that day, the taskmasters of the people and its officers, saying,

7 “You shall no longer give straw to the people to make the bricks, as before. They shall go and gather straw for themselves.

8 And the quota of bricks that they made before you shall lay on them; you shall not lessen any of it, for they are idle. Therefore they cry, saying, ‘Let us go, let us sacrifice to our God.’

9 Let the work be heavy on the men, and let them do it, and let them not pay attention to words of falsehood.”

10 And the taskmasters of the people and its officers went out and said to the people, saying, “Thus says Pharaoh: ‘I am not giving you straw.

11 You, go, take straw for yourselves from wherever you find it, for nothing will be lessened from your work.’”

12 And the people were scattered in all the land of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.

13 And the taskmasters pressed them, saying, “Complete your work, a day's amount on its day, as when there was straw.”

14 And the officers of the sons of Israel, whom Pharaoh's taskmasters had set over them, were beaten, saying, “Why have you not completed your quota in making bricks as before, both yesterday and also today?”

15 And the officers of the sons of Israel came and cried out to Pharaoh, saying, “Why do you do this to your slaves?

16 Straw is not given to your slaves, and they say to us, ‘Make bricks,’ and look, your slaves are beaten, and your own people are at fault.”

17 And he said, “Idle you are, idle! Therefore you say, ‘Let us go, let us sacrifice to Yahweh.’

18 And now go, work, and straw will not be given to you, and you shall deliver the quota of bricks.”

19 And the officers of the sons of Israel saw that they were in trouble, when it was said, “You shall not lessen anything from your bricks, a day's amount on its day.”

20 And they met Moses and Aaron, who were standing to meet them, as they came out from Pharaoh.

21 And they said to them, “May Yahweh look on you and judge, because you have made our scent stink in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of his slaves, to put a sword in their hand to kill us.”

22 And Moses returned to Yahweh and said, “Lord, why have you done evil to this people? Why is this — you have sent me?

23 For since I came to Pharaoh to speak in your name, he has done evil to this people, and you have not delivered your people at all.”


Exodus 6

1 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh, for with a strong hand he shall let them go, and with a strong hand he shall drive them out of his land.”

2 And God spoke to Moses, and said to him, “I am Yahweh.

3 And I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob as God Almighty, and by my name Yahweh I was not known to them.

4 And I also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their sojournings, in which they sojourned.

5 And I also have heard the groaning of the sons of Israel, whom the Egyptians enslave, and I have remembered my covenant.

6 Therefore say to the sons of Israel, ‘I am Yahweh, and I shall bring you out from under the burdens of Egypt, and I shall deliver you from their slavery, and I shall redeem you with an outstretched arm and with great judgments.

7 And I shall take you to me as a people, and I shall be God to you, and you shall know that I am Yahweh your God, who brings you out from under the burdens of Egypt.

8 And I shall bring you to the land that I lifted my hand to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, and I shall give it to you as a heritage. I am Yahweh.’ ”

9 And Moses spoke so to the sons of Israel, but they did not listen to Moses, because of shortness of spirit and because of hard service.

10 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

11 “Go, speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he let the sons of Israel go from his land.”

12 And Moses spoke before Yahweh, saying, “See, the sons of Israel have not listened to me, and how shall Pharaoh listen to me, and I am of uncircumcised lips?”

13 And Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, and commanded them concerning the sons of Israel and concerning Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

14 These are the heads of their fathers’ houses: the sons of Reuben, Israel’s firstborn — Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi. These are the families of Reuben.

15 And the sons of Simeon: Jemuel and Jamin and Ohad and Jachin and Zohar, and Shaul the son of the Canaanite woman. These are the families of Simeon.

16 And these are the names of the sons of Levi according to their generations: Gershon and Kohath and Merari. And the years of Levi’s life were one hundred and thirty-seven years.

17 The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, according to their families.

18 And the sons of Kohath: Amram and Izhar and Hebron and Uzziel. And the years of Kohath’s life were one hundred and thirty-three years.

19 And the sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of Levi according to their generations.

20 And Amram took Jochebed, his father’s sister, for himself as wife, and she bore him Aaron and Moses. And the years of Amram’s life were one hundred and thirty-seven years.

21 And the sons of Izhar: Korah and Nepheg and Zichri.

22 And the sons of Uzziel: Mishael and Elzaphan and Sithri.

23 And Aaron took Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab, sister of Nahshon, for himself as wife, and she bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

24 And the sons of Korah: Assir and Elkanah and Abiasaph. These are the families of the Korahites.

25 And Eleazar, Aaron’s son, took for himself one of the daughters of Putiel as wife, and she bore him Phinehas. These are the heads of the fathers of the Levites according to their families.

26 This is Aaron and Moses, to whom Yahweh said, “Bring out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt according to their hosts.”

27 They are those who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring out the sons of Israel from Egypt. This is Moses and Aaron.

28 And it was on the day Yahweh spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,

29 and Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying, “I am Yahweh. Speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I speak to you.”

30 And Moses said before Yahweh, “See, I am of uncircumcised lips, and how shall Pharaoh listen to me?”

Exodus 7

1 And Yahweh said to Moses, “See, I have made you God to Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother shall be your prophet.

2 You shall speak all that I command you, and Aaron your brother shall speak to Pharaoh, that he let the sons of Israel go from his land.

3 And I shall harden Pharaoh’s heart, and I shall multiply my signs and my wonders in the land of Egypt.

4 And Pharaoh shall not listen to you, and I shall put my hand on Egypt, and I shall bring out my hosts, my people the sons of Israel, from the land of Egypt with great judgments.

5 And Egypt shall know that I am Yahweh, when I stretch out my hand over Egypt and bring out the sons of Israel from among them.”

6 And Moses and Aaron did so; as Yahweh commanded them, so they did.

7 And Moses was eighty years old, and Aaron was eighty-three years old, when they spoke to Pharaoh.

8 And Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

9 “When Pharaoh speaks to you, saying, ‘Give a wonder for yourselves,’ then you shall say to Aaron, ‘Take your staff and throw it before Pharaoh; it shall become a serpent.’ ”

10 And Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh, and they did so, as Yahweh had commanded, and Aaron threw his staff before Pharaoh and before his slaves, and it became a serpent.

11 And Pharaoh also called for the wise men and for the sorcerers, and they also, the magicians of Egypt, did so with their secret arts.

12 And each man threw his staff, and they became serpents, and Aaron’s staff swallowed their staffs.

13 And Pharaoh’s heart was strong, and he did not listen to them, as Yahweh had spoken.

14 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Pharaoh’s heart is heavy. He refuses to let the people go.

15 Go to Pharaoh in the morning. See, he is going out to the water, and you shall stand to meet him on the bank of the Nile, and the staff that was turned into a serpent you shall take in your hand.

16 And you shall say to him, ‘Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews, sent me to you, saying, “Let my people go, that they may serve me in the wilderness.” And see, you have not listened until now.’

17 “Thus says Yahweh: By this you shall know that I am Yahweh. See, I am striking with the staff that is in my hand on the waters that are in the Nile, and they shall be turned to blood.

18 And the fish that are in the Nile shall die, and the Nile shall stink, and Egypt shall be weary of drinking water from the Nile.” ’ ”

19 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Say to Aaron, ‘Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, over their rivers, over their canals, and over their pools, and over all their gatherings of water, that they become blood, and there shall be blood in all the land of Egypt, and in wood and in stone.’ ”

20 And Moses and Aaron did so, as Yahweh commanded, and he lifted up with the staff and struck the waters that were in the Nile before Pharaoh’s eyes and before his slaves’ eyes, and all the waters that were in the Nile were turned to blood.

21 And the fish that were in the Nile died, and the Nile stank, and Egypt could not drink water from the Nile, and the blood was in all the land of Egypt.

22 And the magicians of Egypt did so with their secret arts, and Pharaoh’s heart was strong, and he did not listen to them, as Yahweh had spoken.

23 And Pharaoh turned and went to his house, and he did not set his heart even to this.

24 And all Egypt dug around the Nile for water to drink, for they could not drink from the waters of the Nile.

25 And seven days were fulfilled after Yahweh struck the Nile.

Exodus 8

1 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Go to Pharaoh, and you shall say to him, ‘Thus says Yahweh: Let my people go, that they may serve me.

2 And if you refuse to let them go, see, I am striking all your territory with frogs.

3 And the Nile shall swarm with frogs, and they shall go up and come into your house and into your bedroom and onto your bed, and into the house of your slaves and among your people, and into your ovens and into your kneading troughs.

4 And on you and on your people and on all your slaves the frogs shall come up.’ ”

5 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Say to Aaron, ‘Stretch out your hand with your staff over the rivers, over the canals, and over the pools, and bring up the frogs on the land of Egypt.’ ”

6 And Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frog came up and covered the land of Egypt.

7 And the magicians did so with their secret arts, and they brought up the frogs on the land of Egypt.

8 And Pharaoh called for Moses and for Aaron and said, “Pray to Yahweh, that he remove the frogs from me and from my people, and I shall let the people go, that they may sacrifice to Yahweh.”

9 And Moses said to Pharaoh, “Have the honour over me: for when shall I pray for you and for your slaves and for your people, to cut off the frogs from you and from your houses, that they remain only in the Nile?”

10 And he said, “For tomorrow.” And he said, “According to your word, so that you may know that there is none like Yahweh our God.

11 And the frogs shall depart from you and from your houses and from your slaves and from your people; only in the Nile shall they remain.”

12 And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried out to Yahweh concerning the frogs that he had set on Pharaoh.

13 And Yahweh did according to Moses’ word, and the frogs died from the houses, from the courts, and from the fields.

14 And they gathered them in heaps, heaps, and the land stank.

15 And Pharaoh saw that there was relief, and he made his heart heavy, and he did not listen to them, as Yahweh had spoken.

16 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Say to Aaron, ‘Stretch out your staff and strike the dust of the land, that it become lice in all the land of Egypt.’ ”

17 And they did so, and Aaron stretched out his hand with his staff and struck the dust of the land, and the lice were on man and on beast; all the dust of the land became lice in all the land of Egypt.

18 And the magicians did so with their secret arts to bring out the lice, and they could not, and the lice were on man and on beast.

19 And the magicians said to Pharaoh, “It is the finger of God.” And Pharaoh’s heart was strong, and he did not listen to them, as Yahweh had spoken.

20 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh. See, he is going out to the water, and you shall say to him, ‘Thus says Yahweh: Let my people go, that they may serve me.

21 For if you do not let my people go, see, I am sending among you and among your slaves and among your people and into your houses the swarm, and the houses of Egypt shall be filled with the swarm, and also the ground on which they are.

22 And on that day I shall set apart the land of Goshen, on which my people stand, so that there shall be no swarm there, so that you may know that I am Yahweh in the midst of the land.

23 And I shall put a redemption between my people and your people. Tomorrow this sign shall be.’ ”

24 And Yahweh did so, and a heavy swarm came into Pharaoh’s house and into his slaves’ house, and in all the land of Egypt the land was ruined because of the swarm.

25 And Pharaoh called for Moses and for Aaron and said, “Go, sacrifice to your God in the land.”

Exodus 9

2 For if you refuse to let them go and you still hold them,

3 see, the hand of Yahweh is on your livestock that are in the field, on the horses, on the donkeys, on the camels, on the herd, and on the flock — a very heavy plague.

4 And Yahweh shall make a distinction between the livestock of Israel and the livestock of Egypt, and nothing of all that belongs to the sons of Israel shall die.’ ”

5 And Yahweh set an appointed time, saying, “Tomorrow Yahweh shall do this thing in the land.”

6 And Yahweh did this thing the next day, and all the livestock of Egypt died, and from the livestock of the sons of Israel not one died.

7 And Pharaoh sent, and see, not even one of Israel’s livestock had died. And Pharaoh’s heart was heavy, and he did not let the people go.

8 And Yahweh said to Moses and to Aaron, “Take for yourselves handfuls full of soot of the furnace, and Moses shall throw it toward heaven before Pharaoh’s eyes.

9 And it shall become dust over all the land of Egypt, and it shall become, on man and on beast, boils breaking out with blisters in all the land of Egypt.”

10 And they took the soot of the furnace and stood before Pharaoh, and Moses threw it toward heaven, and it became boils with blisters breaking out on man and on beast.

11 And the magicians could not stand before Moses because of the boils, for the boils were on the magicians and on all Egypt.

12 And Yahweh made Pharaoh’s heart strong, and he did not listen to them, as Yahweh had spoken to Moses.

13 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Rise early in the morning and stand before Pharaoh, and say to him, ‘Thus says Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews: Let my people go, that they may serve me.

14 For this time I am sending all my plagues to your heart, and on your slaves, and on your people, so that you may know that there is none like me in all the earth.

15 For now I could have stretched out my hand and struck you and your people with the plague, and you would have been cut off from the earth.

16 But indeed for this reason I have made you stand, to show you my power, and so that my name may be told in all the earth.

17 You still set yourself up against my people, by not letting them go.

18 See, tomorrow about this time I am raining down very heavy hail, such as has not been in Egypt from the day it was founded until now.

19 And now send, bring your livestock and all that you have in the field to safety. Every man and beast that is found in the field and is not gathered home — the hail shall come down on them, and they shall die.’ ”

20 The one among Pharaoh’s slaves who feared Yahweh’s word made his slaves and his livestock flee to the houses.

21 And the one who did not set his heart to Yahweh’s word left his slaves and his livestock in the field.

22 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over heaven, that there be hail in all the land of Egypt, on man and on beast and on every plant of the field in the land of Egypt.”

23 And Moses stretched out his staff over heaven, and Yahweh gave thunder and hail, and fire went down to the earth, and Yahweh rained hail on the land of Egypt.

24 And there was hail, and fire flashing in the midst of the hail, very heavy, such as had not been in all the land of Egypt since it became a nation.

25 And the hail struck in all the land of Egypt all that was in the field, from man to beast, and the hail struck every plant of the field, and it shattered every tree of the field.

26 Only in the land of Goshen, where the sons of Israel were, there was no hail.

27 And Pharaoh sent and called for Moses and for Aaron and said to them, “I have sinned this time. Yahweh is the righteous one, and I and my people are the wicked.

28 Pray to Yahweh, and let there be enough of God’s thunder and hail, and I shall let you go, and you shall not continue to stay.”

29 And Moses said to him, “When I go out of the city, I shall spread out my palms to Yahweh. The thunder shall cease, and the hail shall be no more, so that you may know that the earth is Yahweh’s.

30 And you and your slaves — I know that you do not yet fear before Yahweh God.”

31 And the flax and the barley were struck, for the barley was in ear and the flax was in bud.

32 And the wheat and the spelt were not struck, for they are late.

33 And Moses went out from Pharaoh, out of the city, and spread out his palms to Yahweh, and the thunder and the hail ceased, and rain was not poured out on the earth.

34 And Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunder had ceased, and he continued to sin, and he made his heart heavy, he and his slaves.

35 And Pharaoh’s heart was strong, and he did not let the sons of Israel go, as Yahweh had spoken by Moses’ hand.

Exodus 10

5 and it shall cover the eye of the land, and no one shall be able to see the land, and it shall eat the rest of what escaped, what was left for you from the hail, and it shall eat every tree that grows for you from the field.

6 And your houses shall be filled, and the houses of all your slaves, and the houses of all Egypt, such as your fathers and your fathers’ fathers have not seen from the day they were on the ground until this day.” And he turned and went out from Pharaoh.

11 “Not so. Go now, the men, and serve Yahweh, for that is what you seek.” And they drove them out from Pharaoh’s presence.

12 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over the land of Egypt for the locust, that it come up over the land of Egypt and eat every plant of the land, all that the hail left.”

21 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Stretch out your hand over heaven, that there be darkness over the land of Egypt, and darkness shall be felt.”

22 And Moses stretched out his hand over heaven, and there was thick darkness in all the land of Egypt for three days.

23 No man saw his brother, and no man rose from under himself for three days, and for all the sons of Israel there was light in their dwellings.

24 And Pharaoh called to Moses and said, “Go, serve Yahweh. Only your flock and your herd shall be left behind. Your little ones also shall go with you.”

25 And Moses said, “You also shall give into our hand sacrifices and burnt offerings, and we shall make them for Yahweh our God.

26 And our livestock also shall go with us. Not a hoof shall remain, for from it we shall take to serve Yahweh our God, and we ourselves do not know what we shall serve Yahweh with until we come there.”

27 And Yahweh made Pharaoh’s heart strong, and he was not willing to let them go.

28 And Pharaoh said to him, “Go from me. Guard yourself: do not continue to see my face, for on the day you see my face you shall die.”

29 And Moses said, “You have spoken rightly. I shall not continue again to see your face.”

Exodus 12

1 Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,

2 “This month shall be for you the head of the months. It shall be for you the first of the months of the year.

3 Speak to all the congregation of Israel, saying, ‘On the tenth day of this month they shall take for themselves, each man a lamb according to their fathers’ houses, a lamb for a house.

4 And if the house is too small for a lamb, then he and his neighbour who is near to his house shall take one according to the number of the persons. Each man according to his eating, you shall count for the lamb.

5 A lamb without defect, a male a year old, shall be for you. You shall take it from the sheep or from the goats.

6 And it shall be kept by you until the fourteenth day of this month, and the whole assembly of the congregation of Israel shall slaughter it between the evenings.

7 They shall take some of the blood, and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel, on the houses in which they eat it.

8 They shall eat the flesh in that night, roasted with fire, and unleavened bread; with bitter herbs they shall eat it.

9 Do not eat any of it raw, or boiled at all in water, but only roasted with fire — its head on its legs and on its inner parts.

10 You shall not leave any of it until the morning, and what remains of it until the morning you shall burn with fire.

11 And in this way you shall eat it: your loins girded, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand. You shall eat it in haste. It is Yahweh’s Passover.

12 For I shall pass through the land of Egypt in that night, and shall strike all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from man to livestock, and against all the gods of Egypt I shall carry out judgments. I am Yahweh.

13 The blood shall be a sign for you on the houses where you are. When I see the blood, I shall pass over you, and there shall be no plague among you to destroy you when I strike the land of Egypt.

14 This day shall be a memorial for you, and you shall keep it as a feast to Yahweh. Throughout your generations you shall keep it as a feast, a statute forever.

15 “For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread. But on the first day you shall remove leaven from your houses, for whoever eats leavened bread from the first day until the seventh day, that soul shall be cut off from Israel.

16 On the first day there shall be a holy convocation for you, and on the seventh day a holy convocation. No work shall be done on them, except what every person must eat — that alone may be done by you.

17 You shall keep the unleavened bread, for on this very day I brought your hosts out of the land of Egypt. So you shall keep this day throughout your generations, a statute forever.

18 In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at evening, you shall eat unleavened bread, until the twenty-first day of the month at evening.

19 For seven days leaven shall not be found in your houses, for whoever eats what is leavened, that soul shall be cut off from the congregation of Israel, whether a sojourner or a native of the land.

20 You shall eat nothing leavened. In all your dwellings you shall eat unleavened bread.’”

21 Then Moses called for all the elders of Israel, and said to them, “Draw out and take for yourselves small livestock according to your families, and slaughter the Passover.

22 You shall take a bunch of hyssop, and dip it in the blood that is in the basin, and touch the lintel and the two doorposts with the blood that is in the basin. And you — no man shall go out from the door of his house until the morning.

23 For Yahweh shall pass through to strike Egypt. When he sees the blood on the lintel and on the two doorposts, Yahweh shall pass over the door, and shall not allow the destroyer to come into your houses to strike.

24 You shall keep this word as a statute for you and for your sons forever.

25 And it shall be, when you come to the land which Yahweh shall give you, as he has spoken, that you shall keep this service.

26 And it shall be, when your sons say to you, ‘What is this service to you?’

27 that you shall say, ‘It is a Passover sacrifice to Yahweh, who passed over the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt, when he struck Egypt, and delivered our houses.’” The people bowed their heads and worshipped.

28 The children of Israel went and did so. As Yahweh had commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did.

29 And it was at midnight that Yahweh struck all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sat on his throne to the firstborn of the captive who was in the dungeon, and all the firstborn of the livestock.

30 Pharaoh rose up in the night — he, and all his slaves, and all Egypt — and there was a great cry in Egypt, for there was not a house where there was not one dead.

31 He called for Moses and Aaron by night, and said, “Rise up, go out from among my people, both you and the children of Israel. Go, serve Yahweh, as you have said!

32 Take both your flocks and your herds, as you have said, and go. And bless me also!”

33 Egypt pressed hard on the people, to send them out of the land in haste, for they said, “We are all dead.”

34 The people took their dough before it was leavened, their kneading bowls bound up in their clothes on their shoulders.

35 The children of Israel did according to the word of Moses, and they asked of the Egyptians articles of silver, articles of gold, and clothing.

36 Yahweh gave the people favour in the eyes of the Egyptians, and they granted them what they asked. And they plundered Egypt.

37 The children of Israel journeyed from Rameses to Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, the men besides the children.

38 A mixed multitude also went up with them, and flock and herd, very much livestock.

39 They baked unleavened cakes of the dough which they brought out of Egypt, for it was not leavened, because they were driven out of Egypt and could not delay, and they had not made any provisions for themselves.

40 Now the dwelling of the children of Israel, who dwelt in Egypt, was four hundred and thirty years.

41 And it was at the end of four hundred and thirty years, and it was on this very day, that all the hosts of Yahweh went out from the land of Egypt.

42 It is a night of watchings for Yahweh, to bring them out from the land of Egypt. This is that night for Yahweh, watchings for all the children of Israel throughout their generations.

43 Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, “This is the statute of the Passover. No foreigner shall eat of it,

44 but every man’s slave who is bought for money, when you have circumcised him, then he shall eat of it.

45 A settler and a hired worker shall not eat of it.

46 It shall be eaten in one house. You shall not carry any of the flesh outside the house, and you shall not break a bone of it.

47 All the congregation of Israel shall do it.

48 When a sojourner lives with you and keeps the Passover to Yahweh, let all his males be circumcised, and then let him come near and keep it. He shall be as a native of the land, but no uncircumcised person shall eat of it.

49 One law shall be for the native and for the sojourner who lives among you.”

50 All the children of Israel did so. As Yahweh commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did.

51 And it was on this very day that Yahweh brought the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their hosts.

Exodus 15

4 He has thrown Pharaoh’s chariots and his host into the sea. His chosen officers are sunk in the Sea of Reeds.

5 The deeps cover them. They went down into the depths like a stone.

6 Your right hand, Yahweh, is glorious in power. Your right hand, Yahweh, crushes the enemy.

7 In the greatness of your majesty you overthrow those who rise up against you. You send out your burning anger. It eats them up as stubble.

8 With the breath of your nostrils, the waters were piled up. The flowing waters stood upright as a heap. The deeps were congealed in the heart of the sea.

9 The enemy said, ‘I will pursue. I will overtake. I will divide the spoil. My soul shall be filled with them. I will draw my sword. My hand shall dispossess them.’

Exodus 21

1 And these are the ordinances that you shall set before them.

2 When you buy a Hebrew slave, he shall serve six years, and in the seventh he shall go out free, for nothing.

3 If he comes in alone, he shall go out alone. If he is the husband of a wife, then his wife shall go out with him.

4 If his master gives him a wife and she bears him sons or daughters, the wife and her children shall belong to her master, and he shall go out alone.

5 But if the slave truly says, "I love my master, my wife, and my children; I will not go out free,"

6 then his master shall bring him to God, and shall bring him to the door or to the doorpost, and his master shall bore his ear with the awl, and he shall serve him forever.

7 And when a man sells his daughter as a female slave, she shall not go out as the slaves go out.

8 If she is displeasing in the eyes of her master, who designated her for himself, then he shall let her be redeemed. He shall have no power to sell her to a foreign people, because he has betrayed her.

9 And if he designates her for his son, he shall do for her according to the ordinance of the daughters.

10 If he takes another for himself, he shall not lessen her food, her clothing, or her marital rights.

11 And if he does not do these three things for her, she shall go out for nothing, with no money.

12 One who strikes a man and he dies shall surely be put to death.

13 But as for one who did not lie in wait, but God let it happen to his hand, I will set a place for you to which he shall flee.

14 And when a man acts wilfully against his neighbour, to kill him with cunning, you shall take him from my altar to die.

15 And one who strikes his father and his mother shall surely be put to death.

16 And one who steals a man and sells him, and he is found in his hand, shall surely be put to death.

17 And one who curses his father and his mother shall surely be put to death.

18 And when men quarrel and one strikes his neighbour with a stone or with a fist, and he does not die but falls to bed,

19 if he gets up and walks outside on his staff, then the one who struck him shall be clear. Only he shall pay for his loss of time, and he shall have him fully healed.

20 And when a man strikes his slave or his female slave with the rod, and his slave or his female slave dies under his hand, he shall surely be punished.

21 But if he remains a day or two days, he shall not be punished, because he is his money.

22 And when men fight and strike a pregnant woman and her children come out, and there is no harm, he shall surely be fined, as the woman's husband imposes on him, and he shall pay as the judges determine.

23 And if there is harm, then you shall give life for life,

24 eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,

25 burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise.

26 And when a man strikes the eye of his slave or the eye of his female slave and destroys it, he shall let him go free for his eye.

27 And if he knocks out the tooth of his slave or the tooth of his female slave, he shall let him go free for his tooth.

28 And when an ox gores a man or a woman and the man or the woman dies, the ox shall surely be stoned, and its flesh shall not be eaten, and the owner of the ox is clear.

29 But if the ox has been a gorer in the past, and its owner has been warned, and he does not guard it, and it kills a man or a woman, the ox shall be stoned, and its owner also shall be put to death.

30 If a ransom is imposed on him, he shall give the redemption price for his life, according to everything that is imposed on him.

31 Whether it gores a son or it gores a daughter, according to this ordinance it shall be done to him.

32 If the ox gores a slave or a female slave, he shall give thirty shekels of silver to his master, and the ox shall be stoned.

33 And when a man opens a pit, or when a man digs a pit and does not cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls in there,

34 the owner of the pit shall pay. He shall return money to the owner of the fallen animal, and the dead animal shall be his.

35 And when a man's ox strikes the ox of his neighbour and it dies, they shall sell the live ox and divide its price, and they shall also divide the dead ox.

36 Or if it was known that the ox has been a gorer in the past, and its owner does not guard it, he shall surely pay an ox for the ox, and the dead ox shall be his.

Exodus 24

1 And to Moses he said, "Come up to Yahweh, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel, and you shall bow down from far away.

2 And Moses alone shall come near to Yahweh, and they shall not come near, and the people shall not come up with him."

3 And Moses came and told the people all the words of Yahweh and all the ordinances, and all the people answered with one voice and said, "All the words that Yahweh has spoken we will do."

4 And Moses wrote all the words of Yahweh, and he rose early in the morning and built an altar under the mountain, and twelve standing stones for the twelve tribes of Israel.

5 And he sent the young men of the children of Israel, and they offered burnt offerings and sacrificed sacrifices of peace offerings to Yahweh, bulls.

6 And Moses took half of the blood and put it in the basins, and half of the blood he threw on the altar.

7 And he took the book of the covenant and read it in the ears of the people, and they said, "All that Yahweh has spoken we will do and we will listen."

8 And Moses took the blood and threw it on the people, and he said, "Behold, the blood of the covenant that Yahweh has cut with you, on all these words."

9 And Moses went up, and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel,

10 and they saw the God of Israel, and under his feet was like a work of pavement of sapphire, and like the very heaven for clearness.

11 And on the nobles of the children of Israel he did not put his hand, and they beheld God, and they ate and drank.

12 And Yahweh said to Moses, "Come up to me to the mountain and be there, and I will give you the tablets of stone, and the teaching and the commandment that I have written, to teach them."

13 And Moses rose, and Joshua his attendant, and Moses went up to the mountain of God.

14 And to the elders he said, "Sit here for us until we return to you. And behold, Aaron and Hur are with you. Whoever has a matter, let him come near to them."

15 And Moses went up to the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.

16 And the glory of Yahweh dwelt on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days, and he called to Moses on the seventh day from the midst of the cloud.

17 And the appearance of the glory of Yahweh was like a devouring fire on the top of the mountain, in the eyes of the children of Israel.

18 And Moses came into the midst of the cloud and went up to the mountain, and Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights.

Exodus 25

1 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 "Speak to the children of Israel, and they shall take for me an offering. From every man whose heart makes him willing, you shall take my offering.

3 And this is the offering that you shall take from them: gold and silver and bronze,

4 and blue and purple and scarlet, and fine linen and goats' hair,

5 and rams' skins dyed red, and tahash skins, and acacia wood,

6 oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and for the incense of spices,

7 onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastplate.

8 And they shall make for me a sanctuary, and I will dwell in their midst.

9 According to all that I am showing you, the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its vessels, so you shall make it.

10 And they shall make an ark of acacia wood: two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

11 And you shall overlay it with pure gold; inside and outside you shall overlay it, and you shall make on it a crown of gold all around.

12 And you shall cast for it four rings of gold, and put them on its four feet, and two rings on its one side, and two rings on its second side.

13 And you shall make poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with gold.

14 And you shall bring the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark with them.

15 In the rings of the ark the poles shall be; they shall not be removed from it.

16 And you shall put into the ark the testimony that I will give you.

17 And you shall make a cover of pure gold: two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its width.

18 And you shall make two cherubim of gold; of hammered work you shall make them, from the two ends of the cover.

19 And make one cherub from this end, and one cherub from that end. From the cover you shall make the cherubim, on its two ends.

20 And the cherubim shall be spreading their wings upward, covering with their wings over the cover, and their faces, a man to his brother; toward the cover shall be the faces of the cherubim.

21 And you shall put the cover on the ark, above, and into the ark you shall put the testimony that I will give you.

22 And I will meet with you there, and I will speak with you from above the cover, from between the two cherubim that are on the ark of the testimony, all that I will command you for the children of Israel.

23 And you shall make a table of acacia wood: two cubits its length, and a cubit its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

24 And you shall overlay it with pure gold, and you shall make for it a crown of gold all around.

25 And you shall make for it a rim of a handbreadth all around, and you shall make a crown of gold for its rim all around.

26 And you shall make for it four rings of gold, and you shall put the rings on the four corners that are at its four feet.

27 Beside the rim the rings shall be, as holders for poles, to carry the table.

28 And you shall make the poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with gold, and the table shall be carried with them.

29 And you shall make its dishes and its spoons and its jars and its bowls, with which a drink offering is poured; of pure gold you shall make them.

30 And you shall put on the table bread of the face, before me, always.

31 And you shall make a lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work the lampstand shall be made: its base and its shaft, its cups, its buds and its flowers shall be from it.

32 And six branches going out from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from its one side, and three branches of the lampstand from its second side,

33 three cups shaped like almond blossoms in the one branch, a bud and a flower, and three cups shaped like almond blossoms in the other branch, a bud and a flower; so for the six branches going out from the lampstand.

34 And in the lampstand, four cups shaped like almond blossoms, its buds and its flowers,

35 and a bud under two of the branches from it, and a bud under two of the branches from it, and a bud under two of the branches from it, for the six branches going out from the lampstand.

36 Their buds and their branches shall be from it; all of it one hammered work of pure gold.

37 And you shall make its lamps, seven, and they shall set up its lamps, and it shall give light over against its face.

38 And its tongs and its fire-pans shall be of pure gold.

39 Of a talent of pure gold he shall make it, with all these vessels.

40 And see, and make them according to their pattern, which you are being shown on the mountain."

Exodus 26

1 And you shall make the tabernacle of ten curtains, of fine linen twisted, and blue and purple and scarlet, with cherubim, the work of a skilled craftsman you shall make them.

2 The length of the one curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width four cubits, for the one curtain; one measure for all the curtains.

3 Five of the curtains shall be joined, a woman to her sister, and five curtains joined, a woman to her sister.

4 And you shall make loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain, at the end of the joined set, and so you shall do on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second joined set.

5 Fifty loops you shall make in the one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make at the end of the curtain that is in the second joined set; the loops shall be opposite, a woman to her sister.

6 And you shall make fifty clasps of gold, and you shall join the curtains, a woman to her sister, with the clasps, and the tabernacle shall be one.

7 And you shall make curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the tabernacle; eleven curtains you shall make them.

8 The length of the one curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the width four cubits, for the one curtain; one measure for the eleven curtains.

9 And you shall join five of the curtains by themselves, and six of the curtains by themselves, and you shall double over the sixth curtain at the front of the face of the tent.

10 And you shall make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain, the outermost in the joined set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain of the second joined set.

11 And you shall make fifty clasps of bronze, and you shall bring the clasps into the loops, and you shall join the tent, and it shall be one.

12 And the hanging excess that is left over in the curtains of the tent, the half of the curtain that is left over, shall hang over the back of the tabernacle.

13 And the cubit from this side and the cubit from that side, in the excess in the length of the curtains of the tent, shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle, from this side and from that side, to cover it.

14 And you shall make a covering for the tent, of rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of tahash skins, above.

15 And you shall make the boards for the tabernacle, of acacia wood, standing.

16 Ten cubits shall be the length of the board, and a cubit and half of the cubit the width of the one board.

17 Two tenons for the one board, set in order, a woman to her sister; so you shall do for all the boards of the tabernacle.

18 And you shall make the boards for the tabernacle, twenty boards for the south side, southward.

19 And you shall make forty sockets of silver under the twenty boards: two sockets under the one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under the one board for its two tenons.

20 And for the second side of the tabernacle, for the north side, twenty boards,

21 and their forty sockets of silver: two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the one board.

22 And for the rear of the tabernacle, westward, you shall make six boards.

23 And you shall make two boards for the corners of the tabernacle in the rear.

24 And they shall be matching below, and together they shall be complete at its head, to the one ring; so it shall be for the two of them; for the two corners they shall be.

25 And there shall be eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets: two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the one board.

26 And you shall make bars of acacia wood, five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,

27 and five bars for the boards of the second side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the side of the tabernacle at the rear, westward.

28 And the middle bar, in the midst of the boards, shall pass through from end to end.

29 And you shall overlay the boards with gold, and you shall make their rings of gold, as holders for the bars, and you shall overlay the bars with gold.

30 And you shall raise up the tabernacle according to its ordinance, which you were shown on the mountain.

31 And you shall make a veil of blue and purple and scarlet, and fine linen twisted, the work of a skilled craftsman; he shall make it with cherubim.

32 And you shall put it on four pillars of acacia, overlaid with gold, their hooks of gold, on four sockets of silver.

33 And you shall put the veil under the clasps, and you shall bring in there, inside the veil, the ark of the testimony, and the veil shall divide for you between the holy place and the holy of holies.

34 And you shall put the cover on the ark of the testimony in the holy of holies.

35 And you shall set the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table, on the side of the tabernacle southward, and the table you shall put on the north side.

36 And you shall make a screen for the opening of the tent, of blue and purple and scarlet, and fine linen twisted, the work of an embroiderer.

37 And you shall make for the screen five pillars of acacia, and you shall overlay them with gold, their hooks of gold, and you shall cast for them five sockets of bronze.

Exodus 27

1 You shall make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide; the altar shall be square, and its height shall be three cubits.

2 And you shall make its horns on its four corners; its horns shall be of one piece with it, and you shall cover it with bronze.

3 And you shall make its pots to remove its ashes, and its shovels and its basins and its forks and its fire pans; all its vessels you shall make of bronze.

4 And you shall make for it a grating, a work of bronze network, and you shall make on the network four bronze rings on its four ends.

5 And you shall put it under the ledge of the altar below, and the network shall reach to the middle of the altar.

6 And you shall make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and you shall cover them with bronze.

7 And its poles shall be brought into the rings, and the poles shall be on the two sides of the altar when it is carried.

8 Hollow with planks you shall make it; as he showed you on the mountain, so shall they make it.

9 And you shall make the court of the tabernacle: for the south side, southward, hangings for the court of fine twisted linen, one hundred cubits long for the one side,

10 and its pillars twenty and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be of silver.

11 And likewise for the north side in length, hangings one hundred cubits long, and its pillars twenty and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be of silver.

12 And the width of the court for the west side: hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars ten and their sockets ten.

13 And the width of the court for the east side, eastward, shall be fifty cubits.

14 And fifteen cubits of hangings for the one side; their pillars three and their sockets three.

15 And for the second side, fifteen cubits of hangings; their pillars three and their sockets three.

16 And for the gate of the court a screen of twenty cubits, of blue and purple and scarlet and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer; their pillars four and their sockets four.

17 All the pillars of the court all around shall be joined with silver bands; their hooks shall be of silver and their sockets of bronze.

18 The length of the court shall be one hundred cubits, and the width fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits, of fine twisted linen, and their sockets of bronze.

19 All the vessels of the tabernacle in all its service, and all its pegs and all the pegs of the court, shall be of bronze.

20 And you, you shall command the children of Israel, and they shall bring to you pure beaten olive oil for the light, to cause a lamp to burn continually.

21 In the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil that is over the testimony, Aaron and his sons shall arrange it from evening to morning before Yahweh; it shall be a statute forever throughout their generations, from the children of Israel.

Exodus 28

1 And you, bring near to you Aaron your brother and his sons with him from among the children of Israel, to serve me as priest — Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's sons.

2 And you shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother, for glory and for beauty.

3 And you, you shall speak to all the wise-hearted, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, and they shall make Aaron's garments to make him holy, to serve me as priest.

4 And these are the garments that they shall make: a breastplate and an ephod and a robe and a chequered tunic, a turban and a sash. And they shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons, to serve me as priest.

5 And they shall take the gold and the blue and the purple and the scarlet and the fine linen.

6 And they shall make the ephod of gold, blue, purple, scarlet and fine twisted linen, the work of a skilled craftsman.

7 It shall have two joining shoulder pieces at its two ends, and it shall be joined together.

8 And the woven band of its fastening that is on it shall be like its work, of one piece with it: gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twisted linen.

9 And you shall take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the children of Israel:

10 six of their names on the one stone, and the names of the six remaining on the second stone, in the order of their birth.

11 With the work of a stone engraver, the engravings of a signet, you shall engrave the two stones with the names of the children of Israel; set in gold settings you shall make them.

12 And you shall put the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, stones of memorial for the children of Israel, and Aaron shall bear their names before Yahweh on his two shoulders for a memorial.

13 And you shall make gold settings,

14 and two chains of pure gold; at their ends you shall make them, of corded work, and you shall put the corded chains on the settings.

15 And you shall make a breastplate of judgment, the work of a skilled craftsman; like the work of the ephod you shall make it: gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twisted linen you shall make it.

16 It shall be square, doubled; a span its length and a span its width.

17 And you shall set in it a setting of stone, four rows of stone: a row of carnelian, topaz and emerald shall be the first row;

18 and the second row shall be turquoise, sapphire and diamond;

19 and the third row jacinth, agate and amethyst;

20 and the fourth row chrysolite and onyx and jasper; they shall be set in gold in their settings.

21 And the stones shall be according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve, according to their names; like the engravings of a signet, each according to his name, they shall be for the twelve tribes.

22 And you shall make on the breastplate chains at the ends, of corded work, of pure gold.

23 And you shall make on the breastplate two gold rings, and you shall put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate.

24 And you shall put the two cords of gold on the two rings at the ends of the breastplate.

25 And the two ends of the two cords you shall put on the two settings, and you shall put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, at its front.

26 And you shall make two gold rings and put them on the two ends of the breastplate, on its edge that is toward the side of the ephod, inward.

27 And you shall make two gold rings and put them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod below, at its front, close to its joining, above the woven band of the ephod.

28 And they shall bind the breastplate by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue, to be on the woven band of the ephod, and the breastplate shall not come loose from the ephod.

29 And Aaron shall bear the names of the children of Israel in the breastplate of judgment on his heart when he goes in to the holy place, for a memorial before Yahweh continually.

30 And you shall put in the breastplate of judgment the Urim and the Thummim, and they shall be on Aaron's heart when he goes in before Yahweh, and Aaron shall bear the judgment of the children of Israel on his heart before Yahweh continually.

31 And you shall make the robe of the ephod all of blue.

32 And the opening for his head shall be in its middle; its opening shall have a border all around, the work of a weaver, like the opening of a coat of mail it shall be for it; it shall not be torn.

33 And you shall make on its hem pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet, all around its hem, and gold bells among them all around:

34 a gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, on the hem of the robe all around.

35 And it shall be on Aaron for service, and its sound shall be heard when he goes in to the holy place before Yahweh and when he goes out, and he shall not die.

36 And you shall make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it, like the engravings of a signet, 'Holy to Yahweh.'

37 And you shall put it on a cord of blue, and it shall be on the turban; at the front of the turban it shall be.

38 And it shall be on Aaron's forehead, and Aaron shall bear the iniquity of the holy things that the children of Israel make holy, in all their holy gifts, and it shall be on his forehead continually, for acceptance for them before Yahweh.

39 And you shall weave the tunic of fine linen in chequer work, and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and a sash you shall make, the work of an embroiderer.

40 And for Aaron's sons you shall make tunics, and you shall make sashes for them, and caps you shall make for them, for glory and for beauty.

41 And you shall put them on Aaron your brother and on his sons with him, and you shall anoint them and fill their hand and make them holy, and they shall serve me as priests.

42 And make for them linen breeches to cover the flesh of nakedness; from the waist to the thighs they shall be.

43 And they shall be on Aaron and on his sons when they go in to the Tent of Meeting or when they draw near to the altar to serve in the holy place, and they shall not bear iniquity and die; it shall be a statute forever for him and for his seed after him.

Exodus 29

1 And this is the thing that you shall do for them to make them holy, to serve as priest for me: take one young bull, a son of cattle, and two rams without defect,

2 and unleavened bread and unleavened cakes mixed with oil and unleavened wafers spread with oil; of fine wheat flour you shall make them.

3 And you shall put them in one basket and bring them near in the basket, and the bull and the two rams.

4 And Aaron and his sons you shall bring near to the door of the Tent of Meeting, and you shall wash them with water.

5 And you shall take the garments and put on Aaron the tunic and the robe of the ephod and the ephod and the breastplate, and you shall fasten the ephod on him with its woven band.

6 And you shall put the turban on his head, and you shall put the holy crown on the turban.

7 And you shall take the anointing oil and pour it on his head and anoint him.

8 And his sons you shall bring near, and you shall put tunics on them.

9 And you shall gird them with a sash — Aaron and his sons — and bind caps on them, and the priesthood shall be theirs by a statute forever, and you shall fill the hand of Aaron and the hand of his sons.

10 And you shall bring the bull before the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the bull.

11 And you shall slaughter the bull before Yahweh, at the door of the Tent of Meeting.

12 And you shall take some of the blood of the bull and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and all the blood you shall pour out at the base of the altar.

13 And you shall take all the fat that covers the innards, and the lobe on the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, and you shall burn them on the altar.

14 And the flesh of the bull and its skin and its dung you shall burn with fire outside the camp; it is a sin offering.

15 And the one ram you shall take, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

16 And you shall slaughter the ram and take its blood and throw it on the altar all around.

17 And the ram you shall cut into its pieces, and you shall wash its innards and its legs and put them on its pieces and on its head.

18 And you shall burn the whole ram on the altar; it is a burnt offering to Yahweh, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to Yahweh.

19 And you shall take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

20 And you shall slaughter the ram and take some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear and on the lobe of his sons' right ear, and on the thumb of their right hand and on the big toe of their right foot, and you shall throw the blood on the altar all around.

21 And you shall take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil and sprinkle it on Aaron and on his garments and on his sons and on his sons' garments with him, and he shall be holy, and his garments, and his sons and his sons' garments with him.

22 And you shall take from the ram the fat and the fat tail and the fat that covers the innards and the lobe of the liver and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them and the right thigh — for it is a ram of consecration —

23 and one loaf of bread and one cake of oiled bread and one wafer, from the basket of unleavened bread that is before Yahweh.

24 And you shall put the whole on Aaron's palms and on his sons' palms, and you shall wave them as a wave offering before Yahweh.

25 And you shall take them from their hand and burn them on the altar on the burnt offering, for a pleasing aroma before Yahweh; it is a fire offering to Yahweh.

26 And you shall take the breast of Aaron's ram of consecration and wave it as a wave offering before Yahweh, and it shall be your portion.

27 And you shall make holy the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution, which was waved and which was lifted up, from the ram of consecration, from what is for Aaron and from what is for his sons.

28 And it shall be for Aaron and for his sons as a due forever from the children of Israel, for it is a contribution, and a contribution it shall be from the children of Israel from their peace offerings — their contribution to Yahweh.

29 And the holy garments that are Aaron's shall be for his sons after him, to be anointed in them and to fill their hand in them.

30 Seven days shall the priest from his sons in his place wear them, who comes into the Tent of Meeting to serve in the holy place.

31 And the ram of consecration you shall take, and you shall boil its flesh in a holy place.

32 And Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram and the bread that is in the basket, at the door of the Tent of Meeting.

33 And they shall eat those things with which atonement was made, to fill their hand, to make them holy; and a stranger shall not eat, for they are holy.

34 And if any of the flesh of the consecration or of the bread remains until the morning, you shall burn what remains with fire; it shall not be eaten, for it is holy.

35 And you shall do for Aaron and for his sons so, according to all that I have commanded you; seven days you shall fill their hand.

36 And a bull of sin offering you shall offer daily for the atonement, and you shall cleanse the altar when you make atonement for it, and you shall anoint it to make it holy.

37 Seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and make it holy, and the altar shall be most holy; whatever touches the altar shall be holy.

38 And this is what you shall offer on the altar: two lambs a year old, daily, continually.

39 The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer at twilight.

40 And a tenth of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin of beaten oil, and a drink offering of a quarter of a hin of wine, for the one lamb.

41 And the second lamb you shall offer at twilight; according to the grain offering of the morning and according to its drink offering you shall do for it, for a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to Yahweh.

42 A continual burnt offering throughout your generations, at the door of the Tent of Meeting before Yahweh, where I will meet with you there, to speak to you there.

43 And I will meet there with the children of Israel, and it shall be made holy by my glory.

44 And I shall make holy the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and Aaron and his sons I shall make holy, to serve as priest for me.

45 And I shall dwell among the children of Israel, and I shall be God for them.

46 And they shall know that I am Yahweh their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, so that I might dwell among them; I am Yahweh their God.

Exodus 30

1 And you shall make an altar for burning incense; of acacia wood you shall make it.

2 A cubit its length and a cubit its width — it shall be square — and two cubits its height; its horns shall be of one piece with it.

3 And you shall cover it with pure gold — its top and its walls all around and its horns — and you shall make for it a gold border all around.

4 And two gold rings you shall make for it, under its border, on its two ribs; on its two sides you shall make them, and it shall be for holders for poles, to carry it with them.

5 And you shall make the poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.

6 And you shall put it before the veil that is over the ark of the testimony, before the cover that is over the testimony, where I will meet with you there.

7 And Aaron shall burn on it incense of spices; morning by morning, when he tends the lamps, he shall burn it.

8 And when Aaron lights the lamps at twilight he shall burn it — a continual incense before Yahweh throughout your generations.

9 You shall not offer on it strange incense, or a burnt offering or a grain offering, and you shall not pour a drink offering on it.

10 And Aaron shall make atonement on its horns once in the year; from the blood of the sin offering of atonement, once in the year, he shall make atonement for it throughout your generations; it is most holy to Yahweh.

11 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

12 When you take the sum of the children of Israel according to their counted ones, each man shall give a ransom for his life to Yahweh when you count them, and there shall be no plague among them when you count them.

13 This they shall give — everyone who passes over to the counted ones — half a shekel by the shekel of the sanctuary (twenty gerahs the shekel); half a shekel, a contribution to Yahweh.

14 Everyone who passes over to the counted ones, from twenty years old and upward, shall give Yahweh's contribution.

15 The rich shall not give more and the poor shall not give less than half a shekel, in giving Yahweh's contribution, to make atonement for your lives.

16 And you shall take the atonement silver from the children of Israel and give it for the service of the Tent of Meeting, and it shall be for the children of Israel a memorial before Yahweh, to make atonement for your lives.

17 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

18 And you shall make a basin of bronze and its stand of bronze, for washing, and you shall put it between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and you shall put water there.

19 And Aaron and his sons shall wash from it their hands and their feet.

20 When they go in to the Tent of Meeting they shall wash with water and they shall not die; or when they draw near to the altar to serve, to burn a fire offering to Yahweh.

21 And they shall wash their hands and their feet and they shall not die, and it shall be for them a statute forever — for him and for his seed throughout their generations.

22 And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

23 And you, take for yourself the chief spices: flowing myrrh, five hundred, and spice cinnamon, half of it, two hundred and fifty, and spice cane, two hundred and fifty,

24 and cassia, five hundred, by the shekel of the sanctuary, and olive oil, a hin.

25 And you shall make it a holy anointing oil, a blended perfume, the work of a perfumer; it shall be a holy anointing oil.

26 And you shall anoint with it the Tent of Meeting and the ark of the testimony,

27 and the table and all its vessels, and the lampstand and its vessels, and the altar of incense,

28 and the altar of burnt offering and all its vessels, and the basin and its stand.

29 And you shall make them holy, and they shall be most holy; whatever touches them shall be holy.

30 And Aaron and his sons you shall anoint, and you shall make them holy, to serve as priest for me.

31 And to the children of Israel you shall speak, saying: A holy anointing oil shall this be for me throughout your generations.

Exodus 39

1 And from the blue and the purple and the scarlet they made service garments, for serving in the holy place, and they made the holy garments that were for Aaron, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

2 He made the ephod of gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twisted linen.

3 They beat out the plates of gold and cut them into threads, to work them into the blue and into the purple and into the scarlet and into the fine linen, the work of a designer.

4 They made shoulder pieces for it, joined together; at its two ends it was joined together.

5 And the band of its fastening that was on it was from it, like its work: gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twisted linen, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

6 They made the onyx stones, set round in settings of gold, engraved with the engravings of a signet, according to the names of the children of Israel.

7 He set them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, stones of remembrance for the children of Israel, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

8 He made the breastplate, the work of a designer, like the work of the ephod: gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twisted linen.

9 It was square, doubled they made the breastplate: a span was its length, and a span its width, doubled.

10 They filled in it four rows of stone: a row of ruby, topaz, and beryl — the first row;

11 and the second row, a turquoise, a sapphire, and a diamond;

12 and the third row, a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;

13 and the fourth row, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a jasper; set round in settings of gold in their settings.

14 And the stones were according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve, according to their names; engravings of a signet, each according to his name, for the twelve tribes.

15 They made on the breastplate chains at the ends, a work of cords, of pure gold.

16 They made two settings of gold and two rings of gold, and put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate.

17 They put the two cords of gold on the two rings, on the ends of the breastplate.

18 And the two ends of the two cords they put on the two settings, and put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, toward its front.

19 They made two rings of gold and set them on the two ends of the breastplate, on its edge that was toward the side of the ephod, inward.

20 They made two rings of gold and put them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod from below, toward its front, close by its joining, above the band of the ephod.

21 They bound the breastplate from its rings to the rings of the ephod with a thread of blue, to be on the band of the ephod, and so that the breastplate should not be loosened from the ephod, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

22 He made the robe of the ephod, the work of a weaver, wholly of blue.

23 And the opening of the robe was within it, like the opening of a coat of mail; a binding was round its opening, so that it should not be torn.

24 They made on the skirts of the robe pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet, twisted.

25 They made bells of pure gold, and put the bells among the pomegranates on the skirts of the robe all around, among the pomegranates:

26 a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, on the skirts of the robe all around, for serving, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

27 They made the tunics of fine linen, the work of a weaver, for Aaron and for his sons,

28 and the turban of fine linen, and the fine headbands of fine linen, and the linen trousers of fine twisted linen,

29 and the sash of fine twisted linen and blue and purple and scarlet, the work of an embroiderer, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

30 They made the plate, the holy crown, of pure gold, and wrote on it a writing, the engravings of a signet: Holy to Yahweh.

31 They put on it a thread of blue, to put it on the turban from above, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

32 And all the work of the tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting was finished, and the children of Israel did according to all that Yahweh commanded Moses; so they did.

33 They brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its vessels, its clasps, its boards, its bars, and its pillars and its sockets,

34 and the covering of the rams' skins dyed red, and the covering of the tachash skins, and the veil of the screen,

35 the ark of the testimony and its poles, and the cover,

36 the table, all its vessels, and the bread of the presence,

37 the pure lampstand, its lamps — the lamps of the arrangement — and all its vessels, and the oil for the light,

38 and the golden altar, and the anointing oil, and the incense of the spices, and the screen for the entrance of the tent,

39 the bronze altar and the bronze grating that belonged to it, its poles and all its vessels, the basin and its base,

40 the hangings of the court, its pillars and its sockets, and the screen for the gate of the court, its cords and its pegs, and all the vessels of the service of the tabernacle for the Tent of Meeting,

41 the service garments for serving in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, for serving as priests.

42 According to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, so the children of Israel did all the work.

43 Moses saw all the work, and behold, they had done it: as Yahweh had commanded, so they had done; and Moses blessed them.

Exodus 40

1 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

2 "On the day of the first month, on the first of the month, you shall raise up the tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting.

3 You shall put there the ark of the testimony, and you shall screen the ark with the veil.

4 You shall bring in the table and arrange its arrangement, and you shall bring in the lampstand and raise up its lamps.

5 You shall set the golden altar for incense before the ark of the testimony, and put the screen of the entrance for the tabernacle.

6 You shall set the altar of the burnt offering before the entrance of the tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting.

7 You shall set the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and put water there.

8 You shall set up the court all around, and put the screen of the gate of the court.

9 "You shall take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and all that is in it, and make it holy, and all its vessels, and it shall be holy.

10 You shall anoint the altar of the burnt offering and all its vessels, and make the altar holy, and the altar shall be most holy.

11 You shall anoint the basin and its base, and make it holy.

12 "You shall bring Aaron and his sons near to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and wash them with water.

13 You shall clothe Aaron with the holy garments, and anoint him, and make him holy, and he shall serve me as priest.

14 You shall bring his sons near, and clothe them with tunics.

15 You shall anoint them as you anointed their father, and they shall serve me as priests; and their anointing shall be for them for a priesthood forever, throughout their generations."

16 Moses did so: according to all that Yahweh commanded him, so he did.

17 It was in the first month, in the second year, on the first of the month, that the tabernacle was raised up.

18 Moses raised up the tabernacle, and set its sockets, and set up its boards, and put in its bars, and raised up its pillars.

19 He spread the tent over the tabernacle, and put the covering of the tent over it from above, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

20 He took and put the testimony into the ark, and set the poles on the ark, and put the cover on the ark from above.

21 He brought the ark into the tabernacle, and set up the veil of the screen, and screened the ark of the testimony, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

22 He put the table in the Tent of Meeting, on the side of the tabernacle northward, outside the veil.

23 He arranged on it an arrangement of bread before Yahweh, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

24 He set the lampstand in the Tent of Meeting, opposite the table, on the side of the tabernacle southward.

25 He raised up the lamps before Yahweh, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

26 He set the golden altar in the Tent of Meeting before the veil,

27 and burned on it incense of spices, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

28 He set up the screen of the entrance for the tabernacle.

29 He set the altar of the burnt offering at the entrance of the tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting, and offered up on it the burnt offering and the grain offering, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

30 He set the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and put water there for washing.

31 And Moses and Aaron and his sons washed from it their hands and their feet:

32 when they came into the Tent of Meeting and when they drew near to the altar, they washed, as Yahweh commanded Moses.

33 He raised up the court all around the tabernacle and the altar, and put the screen of the gate of the court. So Moses finished the work.

34 Then the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting, and the glory of Yahweh filled the tabernacle.

35 Moses could not come into the Tent of Meeting, because the cloud rested on it, and the glory of Yahweh filled the tabernacle.

36 And when the cloud was raised up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel journeyed on, in all their journeys;

37 and if the cloud was not raised up, they did not journey until the day it was raised up.

38 For the cloud of Yahweh was on the tabernacle by day, and fire would be in it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, in all their journeys.
