Clear English Bible — 2 Kings (Chapters 1–25): A Clear English Translation

This completes the Masoretic book of 2 Kings in this edition. The remaining books of the Old Testament stand in this edition as a clear-English modernization of the World English Bible (WEB) Classic text named in the front matter, except the books the front matter names as fresh translations made directly from the Hebrew; the New Testament stands on the Greek text named there. Insertion record — 24 November 2026. The Masoretic book of 2 Kings (25 chapters, 719 verses) was inserted as a fresh translation made directly from the Masoretic Hebrew under Robert Tilford's Fresh Tanakh commission ruling of 23 November 2026 and its authorization-forward amendment, confirmed the same day, replacing the earlier WEB-based text of the book. The book carries one seam against the numbering it replaces, at a chapter boundary only: the Masoretic chapter 11 counts 20 verses and chapter 12 counts 22, against the English scheme's 21 and 21, because the verse the Masoretic text numbers 12:1 ("Jehoash was seven years old when he began to reign") stands in the English scheme as 11:21, and Masoretic 12:2–22 stand at the numbers the English scheme numbers 12:1–21 — so the block is reseated to the Masoretic numbering throughout, and the block header is unchanged. (The wave brief expected 854 verses in the English scheme; the ground check found 719 in both Masoretic witnesses, in JPS 1917 as printed, and in the seated block, and the build stands on the ground count.) Six independent checker passes that did not build the text read the draft against the Hebrew line and both controls: C1 (chapters 1–4) returned 114 PASS and no errors; C2 (5–8) 108 PASS and 1 error; C3 (9–12) 115 PASS and no errors; C4 (13–17) 151 PASS and 2 errors; C5 (18–21) 120 PASS and 1 error; C6 (22–25) 105 PASS and 2 errors — 713 verses passed as built, and the six errors (5:12, where the draft had printed the ketiv "Amanah" for the qere "Abanah"; 13:16, where "draw the bow" misread the Hebrew's "put your hand on the bow"; 14:2, where the queen mother's name stood as "Jehoaddan" for "Jehoaddin"; 18:27, where the draft had printed the plain "urine" against the qere's "the waters of their feet"; 23:24, where "the household gods" put a God-word in a verse whose designation target is none — the Hebrew is the teraphim; and 23:29, where the draft's pronouns made Josiah the killer where the Hebrew has Pharaoh Necoh kill Josiah) were corrected with the checkers' exact replacement texts and the whole book re-verified by count. 2 Kings 1

1 Moab rebelled against Israel after Ahab died.

2 Ahaziah fell through the lattice of his upper room in Samaria and was injured, and he sent messengers and told them, "Go and consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, whether I will recover from this injury."

3 But an angel of Yahweh spoke to Elijah the Tishbite: "Get up, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and say to them, 'Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are going to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?'

4 Therefore Yahweh says this: 'The bed you have gone up to, you will not come down from it, for you will surely die.'" And Elijah went.

5 The messengers came back to him, and he said to them, "Why have you come back?"

6 They told him, "A man came up to meet us and said to us, 'Go, return to the king who sent you, and say to him, "Yahweh says this: Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are sending to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron? Therefore the bed you have gone up to, you will not come down from it, for you will surely die."'"

7 He said to them, "What did the man look like who came up to meet you and spoke these words to you?"

8 They answered him, "A hairy man, with a leather belt fastened around his waist." He said, "It is Elijah the Tishbite."

9 Then he sent to him a commander of fifty with his fifty men. He went up to him, and there he was, sitting on the top of the hill. He said to him, "Man of God, the king has spoken: 'Come down.'"

10 Elijah answered and said to the commander of fifty, "If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty." And fire came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.

11 Again he sent to him another commander of fifty with his fifty. He spoke up and said to him, "Man of God, the king has said this: 'Come down at once.'"

12 Elijah answered and said to them, "If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty." And fire of God came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.

13 Again he sent a third commander of fifty with his fifty. The third commander of fifty went up, came and knelt on his knees before Elijah, pleaded with him, and said to him, "Man of God, please let my life and the lives of these fifty servants of yours be precious in your eyes.

14 See, fire has come down from heaven and consumed the first two commanders of fifty with their fifties; but now let my life be precious in your eyes."

15 The angel of Yahweh said to Elijah, "Go down with him; do not be afraid of him." So he got up and went down with him to the king.

16 He said to him, "Yahweh says this: 'Because you sent messengers to consult Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron — is it because there is no God in Israel to consult his word? — therefore the bed you have gone up to, you will not come down from it, for you will surely die.'"

17 So he died, according to the word of Yahweh that Elijah had spoken. Jehoram reigned in his place, in the second year of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, because he had no son.

18 The rest of the acts of Ahaziah that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

2 Kings 2

1 When Yahweh was about to take Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha were on their way from Gilgal.

2 Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, please, for Yahweh has sent me as far as Beth-el." Elisha said, "As Yahweh lives and as you yourself live, I will not leave you." So they went down to Beth-el.

3 The sons of the prophets who were at Beth-el came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today Yahweh is taking your lord away from over your head?" He said, "Yes, I know it; be silent."

4 Elijah said to him, "Elisha, stay here, please, for Yahweh has sent me to Jericho." He said, "As Yahweh lives and as you yourself live, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

5 The sons of the prophets who were at Jericho drew near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today Yahweh is taking your lord away from over your head?" He answered, "Yes, I know it; be silent."

6 Elijah said to him, "Stay here, please, for Yahweh has sent me to the Jordan." He said, "As Yahweh lives and as you yourself live, I will not leave you." So the two of them went on.

7 Fifty men of the sons of the prophets went and stood opposite them at a distance, while the two of them stood by the Jordan.

8 Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the waters, and they parted to one side and the other, and the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

9 When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I should do for you before I am taken from you." Elisha said, "Please let a double portion of your spirit come to me."

10 He said, "You have asked a hard thing. If you see me as I am taken from you, it will be so for you; but if not, it will not be so."

11 As they went on, walking and talking, suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them, and Elijah went up in a whirlwind to heaven.

12 Elisha saw it and cried out, "My father, my father, the chariot of Israel and its horsemen!" And he saw him no more. He took hold of his own clothes and tore them into two pieces.

13 He picked up Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him, went back, and stood on the bank of the Jordan.

14 He took Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him, struck the waters, and said, "Where is Yahweh, the God of Elijah?" He too struck the waters, and they parted to one side and the other, and Elisha crossed over.

15 When the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho saw him from opposite, they said, "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha." They came to meet him and bowed to the ground before him.

16 They said to him, "Look, there are fifty able men with your servants. Please let them go and look for your lord, in case the spirit of Yahweh has lifted him up and thrown him down on one of the mountains or into one of the valleys." He said, "Do not send them."

17 They pressed him until he was embarrassed, and he said, "Send them." They sent fifty men, and they searched for three days but did not find him.

18 They returned to him while he was staying at Jericho, and he said to them, "Did I not tell you, 'Do not go'?"

19 The men of the city said to Elisha, "Look, the site of this city is good, as my lord sees, but the water is bad and the land causes miscarriage."

20 He said, "Bring me a new bowl and put salt in it." They brought it to him.

21 He went out to the spring of the waters, threw salt there, and said, "Yahweh says this: 'I have healed these waters; from now on there will be no more death or miscarriage from them.'"

22 So the waters have remained healed to this day, according to the word Elisha spoke.

23 He went up from there to Beth-el. As he was going up on the road, some young boys came out of the city, jeered at him, and said to him, "Go up, baldhead! Go up, baldhead!"

24 He turned behind him, saw them, and cursed them in the name of Yahweh. Then two she-bears came out of the forest and tore apart forty-two of the children.

25 He went from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

2 Kings 3

1 Jehoram son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.

2 He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, though not like his father and his mother, for he removed the pillar of Baal that his father had made.

3 Yet he clung to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, with which he made Israel sin; he did not turn away from them.

4 Mesha, king of Moab, was a sheep breeder, and he paid the king of Israel one hundred thousand lambs and the wool of one hundred thousand rams.

5 When Ahab died, the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

6 King Jehoram went out of Samaria at that time and mustered all Israel.

7 He went and sent to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, saying, "The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me to Moab to fight?" He said, "I will go up; I am as you are, my people as your people, my horses as your horses."

8 He said, "By which road shall we go up?" He answered, "By the road of the wilderness of Edom."

9 The king of Israel, the king of Judah, and the king of Edom went, and they made a roundabout journey of seven days, and there was no water for the camp or for the animals that followed them.

10 The king of Israel said, "Alas, Yahweh has called these three kings together to give them into the hand of Moab."

11 Jehoshaphat said, "Is there no prophet of Yahweh here, through whom we may inquire of Yahweh?" One of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, "Elisha son of Shaphat is here, who poured water on Elijah's hands."

12 Jehoshaphat said, "The word of Yahweh is with him." So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to him.

13 Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What have I to do with you? Go to your father's prophets and to your mother's prophets." The king of Israel said to him, "No, for Yahweh has called these three kings together to give them into the hand of Moab."

14 Elisha said, "As Yahweh of hosts lives, before whom I stand, if it were not that I regard Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, I would not look at you or see you.

15 But now bring me a musician." When the musician played, the hand of Yahweh came upon him.

16 He said, "Yahweh says this: 'Make this valley full of ditches, ditch after ditch.'

17 For Yahweh says this: 'You will see no wind and you will see no rain, yet this valley will fill with water, and you will drink, you and your livestock and your animals.'

18 This is a light thing in the eyes of Yahweh; he will also give Moab into your hand.

19 You will strike every fortified city and every choice city, fell every good tree, stop up every spring of water, and ruin every good plot of land with stones."

20 In the morning, at the time of the offering, water suddenly came from the direction of Edom, and the land filled with water.

21 All Moab heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, and everyone old enough to gird on a belt and upward was summoned, and they stood at the border.

22 They rose early in the morning, and the sun shone on the water, and the Moabites saw the water opposite them red as blood.

23 They said, "This is blood. The kings have surely destroyed one another and struck each other down. Now, Moab, to the spoil!"

24 They came to the camp of Israel, and Israel rose and struck Moab, and they fled before them. They pressed in and struck Moab down.

25 They tore down the cities; on every good plot of land each man threw his stone and filled it; they stopped up every spring of water and felled every good tree, until only its stones were left at Kir-hareseth, and the slingers surrounded it and struck it.

26 When the king of Moab saw that the battle was too strong for him, he took with him seven hundred men who drew the sword, to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

27 He took his firstborn son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him up as a burnt offering on the wall. And great wrath came upon Israel, and they withdrew from him and returned to the land.

2 Kings 4

1 A certain woman, one of the wives of the sons of the prophets, cried out to Elisha, saying, "Your servant, my husband, is dead. You know that your servant feared Yahweh. The creditor has come to take my two children for himself as slaves."

2 Elisha said to her, "What can I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" She said, "Your servant has nothing at all in the house except a flask of oil."

3 He said, "Go, borrow vessels for yourself from outside, from all your neighbors, empty vessels; do not borrow few.

4 Then come in and shut the door behind you and behind your sons, and pour into all these vessels, and set aside each one that is full."

5 She left him, shut the door behind her and behind her sons; they kept bringing the vessels to her, and she kept pouring.

6 When the vessels were full, she said to her son, "Bring me another vessel." He said to her, "There is no vessel left." And the oil stopped.

7 She came and told the man of God. He said, "Go, sell the oil and pay your debt, and you and your sons shall live on what is left."

8 One day Elisha passed through Shunem, where there was a prominent woman, and she pressed him to eat food. Whenever he passed by, he would turn in there to eat food.

9 She said to her husband, "Look, I know that this is a holy man of God who passes by us regularly.

10 Please let us make a small walled upper room and put there for him a bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp, and when he comes to us he can turn in there."

11 One day he came there, turned into the upper room, and lay down there.

12 He said to Gehazi, his servant, "Call this Shunammite." He called her, and she stood before him.

13 He said to him, "Say to her now, 'Look, you have taken all this trouble for us. What can be done for you? Shall I speak for you to the king or to the commander of the army?'" She answered, "I live among my own people."

14 He said, "What, then, can be done for her?" Gehazi answered, "Well, she has no son, and her husband is old."

15 He said, "Call her." He called her, and she stood in the doorway.

16 He said, "At this appointed time, about this time next year, you will embrace a son." She said, "No, my lord, man of God, do not deceive your servant."

17 The woman conceived and bore a son at that appointed time, about that time the next year, as Elisha had told her.

18 The child grew. One day he went out to his father, to the reapers.

19 He said to his father, "My head, my head!" He said to the servant, "Carry him to his mother."

20 He lifted him and brought him to his mother. He sat on her knees until noon, and died.

21 She went up, laid him on the bed of the man of God, shut the door on him, and went out.

22 She called to her husband and said, "Please send me one of the servants and one of the donkeys, so that I can run to the man of God and come back."

23 He said, "Why are you going to him today? It is neither new moon nor Sabbath." She said, "It is well."

24 She saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive on and go; do not hold back my riding unless I tell you."

25 She went and came to the man of God at Mount Carmel. When the man of God saw her from a distance, he said to Gehazi, his servant, "Look, there is that Shunammite.

26 Now run, please, to meet her and say to her, 'Is it well with you? Is it well with your husband? Is it well with the child?'" She answered, "It is well."

27 She came to the man of God at the hill and took hold of his feet. Gehazi came near to push her away, but the man of God said, "Leave her alone, for her soul is bitter within her, and Yahweh has hidden it from me and has not told me."

28 She said, "Did I ask for a son from my lord? Did I not say, 'Do not deceive me'?"

29 He said to Gehazi, "Gird up your loins, take my staff in your hand, and go. If you meet a man, do not greet him, and if a man greets you, do not answer him. Lay my staff on the boy's face."

30 The boy's mother said, "As Yahweh lives and as you yourself live, I will not leave you." So he got up and followed her.

31 Gehazi passed on ahead of them and laid the staff on the boy's face, but there was no sound and no sign of attention. He went back to meet him and told him, saying, "The boy has not awakened."

32 Elisha came into the house, and there was the boy, dead, laid on his bed.

33 He went in, shut the door behind the two of them, and prayed to Yahweh.

34 He went up and lay on the child, put his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes, and his hands on his hands, and stretched himself over him, and the child's flesh became warm.

35 He got up again, walked once here and once there in the house, went up, and stretched himself over him. The boy sneezed seven times, and the boy opened his eyes.

36 He called Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite." He called her, and she came to him. He said, "Lift up your son."

37 She came, fell at his feet, and bowed to the ground. She lifted up her son and went out.

38 Elisha returned to Gilgal. There was a famine in the land, and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him. He said to his servant, "Set on the large pot and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."

39 One went out into the field to gather greens, found a wild vine, gathered wild gourds from it, a lapful, came and sliced them into the pot of stew, for they did not know what they were.

40 They poured it out for the men to eat. As they ate from the stew, they cried out and said, "Man of God, there is death in the pot!" And they could not eat it.

41 He said, "Bring flour." He threw it into the pot and said, "Pour it out for the people, and let them eat." And there was nothing harmful in the pot.

42 A man came from Baal-shalishah and brought the man of God bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley and fresh grain in his sack. He said, "Give it to the people, and let them eat."

43 His servant said, "How can I set this before a hundred men?" He said, "Give it to the people, and let them eat, for Yahweh says this: 'They shall eat and have some left.'"

44 He set it before them, and they ate and had some left, according to the word of Yahweh.

2 Kings 5

1 Naaman, commander of the army of the king of Aram, was a great man before his lord and held in high esteem, because through him Yahweh had given victory to Aram; the man was a mighty warrior, but he had leprosy.

2 Aramean raiding bands had gone out and taken captive a young girl from the land of Israel, and she served Naaman's wife.

3 She said to her mistress, "If only my lord were with the prophet who is in Samaria! Then he would cure him of his leprosy."

4 Naaman went in and told his lord, saying, "This is what the girl from the land of Israel said."

5 The king of Aram said, "Go, and I will send a letter to the king of Israel." So he set out and took with him ten talents of silver, six thousand pieces of gold, and ten changes of clothing.

6 He brought the letter to the king of Israel; it said, "When this letter reaches you, see, I have sent my servant Naaman to you, so that you may cure him of his leprosy."

7 When the king of Israel read the letter, he tore his clothes and said, "Am I God, able to kill and to make alive, that this man sends to me to cure a man of his leprosy? Just consider and see how he is picking a quarrel with me."

8 When Elisha the man of God heard that the king of Israel had torn his clothes, he sent to the king, saying, "Why have you torn your clothes? Let him come to me, and he will know that there is a prophet in Israel."

9 So Naaman came with his horses and his chariots and stood at the entrance of Elisha's house.

10 Elisha sent a messenger to him, saying, "Go and wash in the Jordan seven times, and your flesh will be restored to you, and you will be clean."

11 But Naaman grew angry and went away, saying, "See, I thought he would surely come out to me, stand, and call on the name of Yahweh his God, and wave his hand over the place, and cure the leper."

12 "Are not Abanah and Pharpar, the rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? Could I not wash in them and be clean?" So he turned and went off in a rage.

13 His servants came near and spoke to him, saying, "My father, if the prophet had told you to do some great thing, would you not have done it? How much more, then, when he tells you, 'Wash, and be clean'?"

14 So he went down and dipped himself seven times in the Jordan, as the man of God had said, and his flesh was restored like the flesh of a young boy, and he was clean.

15 He returned to the man of God, he and his whole company, and he came and stood before him and said, "See now, I know that there is no God in all the earth except in Israel; so please accept a gift from your servant."

16 But he said, "As Yahweh lives, before whom I stand, I will accept nothing." Naaman urged him to take it, but he refused.

17 Naaman said, "If not, please let your servant be given two mules' load of earth, for your servant will no longer offer burnt offering or sacrifice to other gods, but only to Yahweh."

18 "In this one thing may Yahweh pardon your servant: when my master goes into the house of Rimmon to bow down there, leaning on my hand, and I bow down in the house of Rimmon — when I bow down in the house of Rimmon, may Yahweh pardon your servant in this thing."

19 He said to him, "Go in peace." So Naaman departed from him and went some distance.

20 Gehazi, the servant of Elisha the man of God, said, "See, my master has spared this Naaman the Aramean by not accepting from his hand what he brought. As Yahweh lives, I will run after him and get something from him."

21 So Gehazi hurried after Naaman. When Naaman saw someone running after him, he got down from the chariot to meet him and said, "Is all well?"

22 He said, "All is well. My master has sent me, saying, 'See, just now two young men from the hill country of Ephraim, from the sons of the prophets, have come to me; please give them a talent of silver and two changes of clothing.'"

2 Kings 17

1 In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king in Samaria over Israel, and he reigned nine years.

2 He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, though not as the kings of Israel who were before him.

3 Shalmaneser king of Assyria came up against him, and Hoshea became his servant and brought him tribute.

4 The king of Assyria found treachery in Hoshea, for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt and had not brought tribute to the king of Assyria as he had year by year, so the king of Assyria arrested him and bound him in prison.

5 The king of Assyria came up through all the land, and he came up to Samaria and besieged it for three years.

6 In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and exiled Israel to Assyria, and he settled them in Halah and on the Habor, the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.

7 This happened because the children of Israel sinned against Yahweh their God, who brought them up from the land of Egypt, from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and they feared other gods.

8 They walked in the statutes of the nations whom Yahweh drove out before the children of Israel, and in those the kings of Israel had made.

9 The children of Israel secretly did things that were not right against Yahweh their God, and they built high places for themselves in all their cities, from a watchman's tower to a fortified city.

10 They set up pillars and Asherim for themselves on every high hill and under every green tree.

11 There they burned incense at all the high places, as the nations did whom Yahweh exiled before them, and they did evil things to provoke Yahweh.

12 They served the idols, of which Yahweh had said to them, "You shall not do this thing."

13 Yahweh warned Israel and Judah by every prophet and every seer, saying, "Turn from your evil ways and keep my commandments and my statutes, according to all the law that I commanded your fathers and that I sent to you by my servants the prophets."

14 They would not listen, but stiffened their neck like the neck of their fathers, who did not trust in Yahweh their God.

15 They rejected his statutes and his covenant that he made with their fathers and his warnings that he gave them, and they went after emptiness and became empty, and after the nations around them, concerning whom Yahweh had commanded them not to do as they did.

16 They abandoned all the commandments of Yahweh their God and made for themselves a cast image, two calves, and they made an Asherah and bowed down to all the host of heaven and served Baal.

17 They made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire, and they practiced divination and omens and sold themselves to do what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, to provoke him.

18 Yahweh was very angry with Israel, and he removed them from his presence; none was left except the tribe of Judah alone.

19 Judah also did not keep the commandments of Yahweh their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel that they had made.

20 Yahweh rejected all the offspring of Israel and afflicted them and gave them into the hand of plunderers, until he had cast them from his presence.

21 For he tore Israel away from the house of David, and they made Jeroboam son of Nebat king, and Jeroboam drove Israel away from following Yahweh and caused them to sin a great sin.

22 The children of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam that he did; they did not turn from them,

23 until Yahweh removed Israel from his presence, as he had spoken by all his servants the prophets. Israel was exiled from its own land to Assyria, to this day.

24 The king of Assyria brought people from Babylon and from Cuthah and from Avva and from Hamath and Sepharvaim, and he settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the children of Israel, and they took possession of Samaria and lived in its cities.

25 At the beginning of their dwelling there, they did not fear Yahweh, so Yahweh sent lions among them, and they killed some of them.

26 They said to the king of Assyria, saying, "The nations that you exiled and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the law of the God of the land, so he has sent lions among them, and they are killing them, because they do not know the law of the God of the land."

27 The king of Assyria commanded, saying, "Send there one of the priests whom you exiled from there, and let them go and live there, and let him teach them the law of the God of the land."

28 One of the priests whom they had exiled from Samaria came and lived in Beth-el, and he taught them how to fear Yahweh.

29 Each nation made its own gods and placed them in the houses of the high places that the Samaritans had made, each nation in the cities where they lived.

30 The men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, the men of Cuth made Nergal, and the men of Hamath made Ashima.

31 The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burned their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

32 They feared Yahweh, and they made for themselves priests of the high places from among their own ranks, and they served for them in the houses of the high places.

33 They feared Yahweh and served their own gods, according to the custom of the nations from which they had been exiled.

34 To this day they do according to their former customs; they do not fear Yahweh, and they do not do according to their statutes or their ordinances or the law or the commandment that Yahweh commanded the children of Jacob, whom he named Israel.

35 Yahweh made a covenant with them and commanded them, saying, "You shall not fear other gods, and you shall not bow down to them or serve them or sacrifice to them.

36 But Yahweh, who brought you up from the land of Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm — him you shall fear, and to him you shall bow down, and to him you shall sacrifice.

37 The statutes and the ordinances and the law and the commandment that he wrote for you you shall be careful to do all the days, and you shall not fear other gods.

38 The covenant that I made with you you shall not forget, and you shall not fear other gods.

39 But Yahweh your God you shall fear, and he will deliver you from the hand of all your enemies."

40 They would not listen, but they did according to their former custom.

41 These nations feared Yahweh and served their carved images; their children and their children's children also, as their fathers did, so they do to this day.

2 Kings 25

1 In the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon came, he and all his army, against Jerusalem, and encamped against it; and they built siege works against it all around.

2 The city was under siege until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah.

3 On the ninth day of the month, the famine grew severe in the city, and there was no bread for the people of the land.

4 The city was breached, and all the men of war fled by night by the way of the gate between the two walls, which was by the king's garden, while the Chaldeans were around the city; and he went by the way of the Arabah.

5 The army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king, and overtook him in the plains of Jericho; and all his army was scattered from him.

6 They seized the king, and brought him up to the king of Babylon at Riblah, and they pronounced judgment on him.

7 They slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, and put out Zedekiah's eyes, and bound him with bronze fetters, and brought him to Babylon.

8 In the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month — that was the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon — Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard, a servant of the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.

9 He burned the house of Yahweh, and the king's house, and all the houses of Jerusalem; every great house he burned with fire.

10 All the army of the Chaldeans who were with the captain of the guard broke down the walls of Jerusalem all around.

11 The rest of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who had deserted to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the multitude, Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard exiled.

12 But some of the poorest of the land the captain of the guard left, as vinedressers and as tillers of the soil.

13 The pillars of bronze that were in the house of Yahweh, and the stands, and the bronze sea that was in the house of Yahweh, the Chaldeans broke in pieces, and carried their bronze to Babylon.

14 The pots, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the dishes, and all the vessels of bronze with which they served, they took away.

15 The fire pans and the basins — what was of gold, in gold, and what was of silver, in silver — the captain of the guard took away.

16 The two pillars, the one sea, and the stands that Solomon had made for the house of Yahweh — the bronze of all these vessels was beyond weighing.

17 The height of the one pillar was eighteen cubits, and a capital of bronze was on it, and the height of the capital was three cubits, with network and pomegranates on the capital all around, all of bronze; and the second pillar was like these, with its network.

18 The captain of the guard took Seraiah the chief priest, and Zephaniah the second priest, and the three keepers of the threshold;

19 and from the city he took one officer who was in charge of the men of war, and five men of those who saw the king's face, who were found in the city, and the scribe of the commander of the army, who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the city.

20 Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard took them, and brought them to the king of Babylon at Riblah.

21 The king of Babylon struck them down and put them to death at Riblah in the land of Hamath. So Judah went into exile out of its land.

22 As for the people who were left in the land of Judah, whom Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon had left, he appointed over them Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan.

23 When all the captains of the forces, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah, they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah — Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah the son of the Maacathite — they and their men.

24 Gedaliah swore to them and to their men, and said to them, "Do not fear the servants of the Chaldeans; live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you."

25 In the seventh month, Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, the son of Elishama, of the royal seed, came, and ten men with him, and struck down Gedaliah, so that he died, and also the Jews and the Chaldeans who were with him at Mizpah.

26 All the people, from small to great, and the captains of the forces, arose and went to Egypt, for they were afraid of the Chaldeans.

27 In the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiachin the king of Judah, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, Evil-merodach the king of Babylon, in the year that he became king, lifted up the head of Jehoiachin the king of Judah out of prison.

28 He spoke kindly to him, and gave him a throne above the thrones of the kings who were with him in Babylon.

29 He changed his prison garments, and he ate bread regularly before him all the days of his life.

30 And his allowance — a regular allowance was given him from the king, each day's portion — all the days of his life.
