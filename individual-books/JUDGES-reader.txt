Judges 1

1 After Joshua died, the children of Israel inquired of Yahweh, saying, "Who will go up for us first against the Canaanites, to fight against them?"

2 Yahweh said, "Judah will go up. See, I have given the land into his hand."

3 Judah said to Simeon his brother, "Come up with me into my allotted portion, and let us fight against the Canaanites; then I too will go with you into your allotted portion." So Simeon went with him.

4 Judah went up, and Yahweh gave the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their hand, and they struck down ten thousand men at Bezek.

5 They found Adoni-bezek at Bezek and fought against him, and they struck down the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

6 Adoni-bezek fled, but they pursued him, caught him, and cut off his thumbs and his big toes.

7 Adoni-bezek said, "Seventy kings, with their thumbs and their big toes cut off, used to gather food under my table. As I have done, so God has repaid me." They brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

8 The children of Judah fought against Jerusalem, captured it, struck it with the edge of the sword, and set the city on fire.

9 Afterward the children of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanites who lived in the hill country, in the Negev, and in the lowland.

10 Judah went against the Canaanites who lived in Hebron — the name of Hebron was formerly Kiriath-arba — and they struck down Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

11 From there he went against the inhabitants of Debir — the name of Debir was formerly Kiriath-sepher.

12 Caleb said, "To the man who strikes Kiriath-sepher and captures it, I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife."

13 Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, captured it, and Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife.

14 When she came to him, she urged him to ask her father for a field. She dismounted from the donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What do you want?"

15 She said to him, "Give me a blessing. Since you have set me in the land of the Negev, give me springs of water as well." So Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

16 The descendants of the Kenite, Moses' father-in-law, went up from the city of palm trees with the children of Judah into the wilderness of Judah, which lies in the Negev of Arad, and they went and lived with the people.

17 Judah went with Simeon his brother, and they struck down the Canaanites who lived in Zephath and devoted it to destruction, and the city was named Hormah.

18 Judah captured Gaza and its territory, Ashkelon and its territory, and Ekron and its territory.

19 Yahweh was with Judah, and he took possession of the hill country, but he could not dispossess the inhabitants of the valley, because they had iron chariots.

20 They gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had spoken, and he dispossessed from there the three sons of Anak.

21 The children of Benjamin did not dispossess the Jebusites who lived in Jerusalem, so the Jebusites have lived with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.

22 The house of Joseph also went up against Bethel, and Yahweh was with them.

23 The house of Joseph spied out Bethel — the name of the city was formerly Luz.

24 The watchmen saw a man coming out of the city, and they said to him, "Please show us the way into the city, and we will deal kindly with you."

25 He showed them the way into the city, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword, but they let the man and all his family go.

26 The man went to the land of the Hittites, built a city, and named it Luz; that is its name to this day.

27 Manasseh did not dispossess Beth-shean and its villages, or Taanach and its villages, or the inhabitants of Dor and its villages, or the inhabitants of Ibleam and its villages, or the inhabitants of Megiddo and its villages, for the Canaanites were determined to live in that land.

28 When Israel grew strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, but they did not dispossess them completely.

29 Ephraim did not dispossess the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, so the Canaanites lived in Gezer among them.

30 Zebulun did not dispossess the inhabitants of Kitron or the inhabitants of Nahalol, so the Canaanites lived among them and were put to forced labor.

31 Asher did not dispossess the inhabitants of Acco, or the inhabitants of Sidon, or Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob.

32 So the Asherites lived among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, for they did not dispossess them.

33 Naphtali did not dispossess the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh or the inhabitants of Beth-anath, so they lived among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, but the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath were put to forced labor for them.

34 The Amorites pressed the children of Dan into the hill country, for they would not let them come down into the valley.

35 The Amorites were determined to live in Mount Heres, in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim, but the hand of the house of Joseph grew heavy on them, and they were put to forced labor.

36 The border of the Amorites ran from the ascent of Akrabbim, from Sela and upward.

Judges 2

1 The messenger of Yahweh came up from Gilgal to Bochim and said, "I brought you up out of Egypt and brought you into the land that I swore to your fathers, and I said, 'I will never break my covenant with you.

2 And you — you shall make no covenant with the inhabitants of this land; you shall tear down their altars.' But you have not listened to my voice. What is this you have done?

3 So I also said, 'I will not drive them out before you; they will be thorns in your sides, and their gods will be a snare to you.'"

4 When the messenger of Yahweh had spoken these words to all the children of Israel, the people lifted up their voices and wept.

5 They named that place Bochim, and they sacrificed there to Yahweh.

6 When Joshua had dismissed the people, the children of Israel went, each to his inheritance, to take possession of the land.

7 The people served Yahweh all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who lived long after Joshua, who had seen all the great work of Yahweh that he had done for Israel.

8 Joshua son of Nun, the servant of Yahweh, died at the age of one hundred and ten years.

9 They buried him within the border of his inheritance at Timnath-heres, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

10 All that generation was also gathered to their fathers, and another generation arose after them who did not know Yahweh or the work that he had done for Israel.

11 The children of Israel did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh and served the Baals.

12 They abandoned Yahweh, the God of their fathers, who had brought them out of the land of Egypt, and they went after other gods, from among the gods of the peoples who were around them, and bowed down to them, and they provoked Yahweh.

13 They abandoned Yahweh and served Baal and the Ashtaroth.

14 The anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and he gave them into the hand of plunderers, who plundered them, and he sold them into the hand of their enemies all around, so that they could no longer stand before their enemies.

15 Wherever they went out, the hand of Yahweh was against them for harm, just as Yahweh had spoken and just as Yahweh had sworn to them, and they were in great distress.

16 Then Yahweh raised up judges, who saved them from the hand of those who plundered them.

17 Yet they did not listen even to their judges, for they whored after other gods and bowed down to them. They turned quickly from the way in which their fathers had walked, who obeyed the commandments of Yahweh; they did not do so.

18 Whenever Yahweh raised up judges for them, Yahweh was with the judge and saved them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge, for Yahweh was moved to pity by their groaning because of those who oppressed and crushed them.

19 But when the judge died, they turned back and acted more corruptly than their fathers, going after other gods to serve them and bow down to them; they did not drop any of their practices or their stubborn way.

20 The anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and he said, "Because this nation has transgressed my covenant that I commanded their fathers and has not listened to my voice,

21 I too will no longer dispossess before them any of the nations that Joshua left when he died,

22 in order to test Israel by them — whether they will keep the way of Yahweh, walking in it as their fathers kept it, or not."

23 So Yahweh left those nations, not dispossessing them quickly, and he did not give them into the hand of Joshua.

Judges 3

1 These are the nations that Yahweh left, to test Israel by them — all who had not known any of the wars of Canaan —

2 only so that the generations of the children of Israel might know war, to teach those who had not known it before:

3 the five rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites who lived on Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal-hermon as far as Lebo-hamath.

4 They were there to test Israel by them, to know whether Israel would listen to the commandments of Yahweh, which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.

5 The children of Israel lived among the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.

6 They took their daughters as wives for themselves and gave their own daughters to their sons, and they served their gods.

7 The children of Israel did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh; they forgot Yahweh their God and served the Baals and the Asheroth.

8 The anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram-naharaim, and the children of Israel served Cushan-rishathaim eight years.

9 The children of Israel cried out to Yahweh, and Yahweh raised up a deliverer for the children of Israel, who delivered them: Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother.

10 The Spirit of Yahweh was upon him, and he judged Israel. He went out to war, and Yahweh gave Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram, into his hand, and his hand prevailed over Cushan-rishathaim.

11 The land had rest for forty years, and Othniel son of Kenaz died.

12 The children of Israel again did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh strengthened Eglon, king of Moab, against Israel, because they had done what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh.

13 He gathered to himself the children of Ammon and Amalek, and he went and struck Israel, and they took possession of the city of palm trees.

14 The children of Israel served Eglon, king of Moab, eighteen years.

15 The children of Israel cried out to Yahweh, and Yahweh raised up for them a deliverer: Ehud son of Gera, the Benjamite, a man restricted in his right hand. The children of Israel sent tribute by his hand to Eglon, king of Moab.

16 Ehud made for himself a sword with two edges, a cubit long, and he strapped it under his clothing on his right thigh.

17 He presented the tribute to Eglon, king of Moab. Now Eglon was a very fat man.

18 When he had finished presenting the tribute, he sent away the people who had carried the tribute.

19 But he himself turned back from the carved images that were by Gilgal and said, "I have a secret word for you, O king." The king said, "Silence!" and all who stood by him went out from him.

20 Ehud came to him while he was sitting alone in his cool upper chamber, and Ehud said, "I have a word from God for you." He rose from his seat.

21 Ehud reached with his left hand, took the sword from his right thigh, and thrust it into his belly.

22 The hilt also went in after the blade, and the fat closed over the blade, for he did not draw the sword out of his belly, and the excrement came out.

23 Ehud went out into the vestibule, shut the doors of the upper chamber behind him, and locked them.

24 He had gone out when Eglon's servants came, and they saw that, look, the doors of the upper chamber were locked, and they said, "Surely he is covering his feet in the cool chamber."

25 They waited until they were ashamed, and look, he was not opening the doors of the upper chamber, so they took the key and opened them, and look, their lord had fallen to the ground, dead.

26 Ehud escaped while they delayed; he passed the carved images and escaped to Seirah.

27 When he arrived, he blew the trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim, and the children of Israel went down with him from the hill country, with him at their head.

28 He said to them, "Follow after me, for Yahweh has given your enemies, the Moabites, into your hand." They went down after him and seized the fords of the Jordan against Moab, and they let no one cross.

29 They struck down about ten thousand men of Moab at that time, every stout man and every man of valor, and no man escaped.

30 Moab was subdued that day under the hand of Israel, and the land had rest for eighty years.

31 After him came Shamgar son of Anath, who struck down six hundred Philistine men with an oxgoad, and he too saved Israel.

Judges 4

1 The children of Israel again did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Ehud was dead.

2 Yahweh sold them into the hand of Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor, whose army commander was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth-haggoyim.

3 The children of Israel cried out to Yahweh, for he had nine hundred iron chariots, and he had oppressed the children of Israel harshly for twenty years.

4 Deborah, a woman, a prophet, the wife of Lappidoth — she was judging Israel at that time.

5 She used to sit under the palm of Deborah, between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the children of Israel came up to her for judgment.

6 She sent and called Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh-naphtali and said to him, "Has Yahweh, the God of Israel, not commanded: Go and march to Mount Tabor, and take with you ten thousand men from the children of Naphtali and from the children of Zebulun?

7 And I will draw out to you, at the river Kishon, Sisera, the commander of Jabin's army, with his chariots and his multitude, and I will give him into your hand."

8 Barak said to her, "If you go with me, I will go; but if you do not go with me, I will not go."

9 She said, "I will certainly go with you. Only, the glory will not be yours on the way you are going, for Yahweh will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman." Deborah arose and went with Barak to Kedesh.

10 Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh, and ten thousand men went up at his feet, and Deborah went up with him.

11 Now Heber the Kenite had separated from the Kenites, from the children of Hobab, Moses' father-in-law, and he pitched his tent as far as the oak in Zaanannim, which is by Kedesh.

12 They told Sisera that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor.

13 Sisera summoned all his chariots — nine hundred iron chariots — and all the people who were with him, from Harosheth-haggoyim to the river Kishon.

14 Deborah said to Barak, "Arise, for this is the day on which Yahweh has given Sisera into your hand. Has Yahweh not gone out before you?" So Barak went down from Mount Tabor, with ten thousand men after him.

15 Yahweh threw Sisera and all his chariots and all his camp into confusion by the edge of the sword before Barak, and Sisera got down from his chariot and fled on foot.

16 Barak pursued the chariots and the camp as far as Harosheth-haggoyim, and all Sisera's camp fell by the edge of the sword; not even one was left.

17 Sisera fled on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, for there was peace between Jabin, king of Hazor, and the house of Heber the Kenite.

18 Jael went out to meet Sisera and said to him, "Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; do not be afraid." He turned aside to her, into the tent, and she covered him with a blanket.

19 He said to her, "Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty." She opened the skin of milk, gave him a drink, and covered him.

20 He said to her, "Stand at the entrance of the tent, and if anyone comes and asks you and says, 'Is there a man here?' you shall say, 'No.'"

21 Jael, Heber's wife, took a tent peg and took a hammer in her hand and came to him quietly and drove the peg into his temple, and it went down into the ground, while he was fast asleep and exhausted, and he died.

22 And look, Barak was pursuing Sisera, and Jael went out to meet him and said to him, "Come, and I will show you the man you are seeking." He came in to her, and look, Sisera had fallen dead, with the peg in his temple.

23 God subdued Jabin, king of Canaan, on that day before the children of Israel.

24 The hand of the children of Israel pressed harder and harder on Jabin, king of Canaan, until they had cut off Jabin, king of Canaan.

Judges 5

1 Then Deborah and Barak son of Abinoam sang on that day, saying:

2 When the leaders took the lead in Israel, when the people offered themselves willingly, bless Yahweh.

3 Hear, kings; give ear, rulers. I, to Yahweh, I will sing; I will make music to Yahweh, the God of Israel.

4 Yahweh, when you went out from Seir, when you marched from the field of Edom, the earth trembled; the heavens also dripped, yes, the clouds dripped water.

5 The mountains flowed down before Yahweh — this Sinai — before Yahweh, the God of Israel.

6 In the days of Shamgar son of Anath, in the days of Jael, the highways ceased, and those who walked the paths went by crooked byways.

7 Village life ceased in Israel; it ceased until I arose, Deborah, until I arose, a mother in Israel.

8 They chose new gods; then there was war in the gates. Was a shield or a spear seen among forty thousand in Israel?

9 My heart is with the commanders of Israel, who offered themselves willingly among the people. Bless Yahweh.

10 You who ride on tawny she-donkeys, you who sit on saddlecloths, and you who walk on the way, speak of it.

11 Far from the noise of archers, among the watering places, there let them recount the righteous deeds of Yahweh, the righteous deeds of his village folk in Israel. Then the people of Yahweh went down to the gates.

12 Awake, awake, Deborah; awake, awake, utter a song. Rise, Barak, and take your captives captive, son of Abinoam.

13 Then a remnant came down to the nobles, a people; Yahweh came down for me among the mighty.

14 From Ephraim, those whose root is in Amalek; after you, Benjamin, among your peoples. From Machir, commanders came down, and from Zebulun those who bear the scribe's staff.

15 And my princes in Issachar were with Deborah; and Issachar, so was Barak; into the valley he was sent on foot. Among the divisions of Reuben there were great resolves of heart.

16 Why did you sit between the sheepfolds, to hear the pipings for the flocks? Among the divisions of Reuben there were great searchings of heart.

17 Gilead stayed across the Jordan; and Dan, why did he linger by the ships? Asher sat at the shore of the sea and stayed by its inlets.

18 Zebulun was a people who scorned their lives to the point of death, and Naphtali also, on the heights of the field.

19 Kings came, they fought; then the kings of Canaan fought at Taanach by the waters of Megiddo. They took no gain of silver.

20 From heaven they fought; the stars from their courses fought against Sisera.

21 The torrent Kishon swept them away, that ancient torrent, the torrent Kishon. March on, my soul, with strength.

22 Then the horses' hooves hammered, from the galloping, the galloping of his mighty ones.

23 Curse Meroz, said the messenger of Yahweh; curse bitterly its inhabitants, because they did not come to the help of Yahweh, to the help of Yahweh among the mighty.

24 Blessed above women be Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite; above women in the tent let her be blessed.

25 Water he asked; milk she gave. In a bowl fit for nobles she brought him curds.

26 Her hand reached for the tent peg, and her right hand for the workers' hammer; and she hammered Sisera, she crushed his head, she shattered and pierced his temple.

27 Between her feet he sank, he fell, he lay; between her feet he sank, he fell; where he sank, there he fell, destroyed.

28 Through the window she looked out and cried, the mother of Sisera, through the lattice: Why does his chariot delay in coming? Why do the hoofbeats of his chariots linger?

29 The wisest of her ladies answer her; yes, she herself returns the answer to herself:

30 Are they not finding and dividing the spoil — a womb, two wombs for every man; spoil of dyed cloth for Sisera, spoil of dyed cloth embroidered, dyed cloth, two embroidered pieces for the necks of the spoil?

31 So let all your enemies perish, Yahweh, and let those who love him be like the going forth of the sun in its strength. And the land had rest forty years.

Judges 6

1 The children of Israel did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh gave them into the hand of Midian seven years.

2 The hand of Midian was strong against Israel. Because of Midian the children of Israel made for themselves the hiding places that are in the mountains, and the caves, and the strongholds.

3 And it happened, whenever Israel sowed, that Midian and Amalek and the children of the east came up, and they came up against them.

4 They encamped against them and destroyed the produce of the land as far as Gaza, and left no sustenance in Israel, and no sheep or ox or donkey.

5 For they would come up with their livestock and their tents, and they came like locusts in multitude; they and their camels were without number, and they came into the land to destroy it.

6 Israel was brought very low because of Midian, and the children of Israel cried out to Yahweh.

7 And when the children of Israel cried out to Yahweh because of Midian,

8 Yahweh sent a man, a prophet, to the children of Israel, and he said to them: Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: I myself brought you up from Egypt and brought you out of the house of slaves.

9 I delivered you from the hand of Egypt and from the hand of all who oppressed you; I drove them out before you and gave you their land.

10 And I said to you: I am Yahweh your God; you shall not fear the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you are living. But you have not listened to my voice.

11 The messenger of Yahweh came and sat under the oak that was in Ophrah, which belonged to Joash the Abiezrite; and his son Gideon was beating out wheat in the winepress, to hide it from Midian.

12 The messenger of Yahweh appeared to him and said to him: Yahweh is with you, mighty man of valor.

13 Gideon said to him: Please, my lord, if Yahweh is with us, why has all this found us? And where are all his wonders that our fathers recounted to us, saying, Did Yahweh not bring us up from Egypt? But now Yahweh has abandoned us and given us into the palm of Midian.

14 Yahweh turned to him and said: Go in this strength of yours, and you shall save Israel from the palm of Midian. Have I not sent you?

15 He said to him: Please, Lord, with what shall I save Israel? Look, my clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the youngest in my father's house.

16 Yahweh said to him: Surely I will be with you, and you shall strike Midian as one man.

17 He said to him: If I have found favor in your eyes, then make for me a sign that it is you who are speaking with me.

18 Do not depart from here, please, until I come to you and bring out my offering and set it before you. And he said: I will stay until you return.

19 Gideon went in and prepared a young goat and unleavened cakes from an ephah of flour; the meat he put in a basket, and the broth he put in a pot, and he brought it out to him under the oak and presented it.

20 The messenger of God said to him: Take the meat and the unleavened cakes and put them on this rock, and pour out the broth. And he did so.

21 The messenger of Yahweh stretched out the end of the staff that was in his hand and touched the meat and the unleavened cakes, and fire rose from the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened cakes; and the messenger of Yahweh went from his eyes.

22 Gideon saw that he was the messenger of Yahweh. And Gideon said: Ah, Lord Yahweh! For I have seen the messenger of Yahweh face to face.

24 Gideon built an altar there to Yahweh and called it Yahweh Is Peace. To this day it is still in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.

27 Gideon took ten men from his servants and did as Yahweh had spoken to him; but because he feared his father's household and the men of the city too much to do it by day, he did it by night.

38 And it was so. He rose early the next day, squeezed the fleece, and wrung dew from the fleece, a bowl full of water.

39 Gideon said to God: Do not let your anger burn against me; let me speak just this once. Let me test just this once with the fleece, please: let there be dryness on the fleece alone, and on all the ground let there be dew.

40 God did so that night: there was dryness on the fleece alone, and on all the ground there was dew.

Judges 7

1 Jerubbaal — that is, Gideon — rose early, and all the people who were with him, and they encamped by the spring of Harod; and the camp of Midian was north of him, by the hill of Moreh, in the valley.

2 Yahweh said to Gideon: The people who are with you are too many for me to give Midian into their hand, lest Israel vaunt itself against me, saying, My own hand has saved me.

3 Now, please proclaim in the hearing of the people, saying: Whoever is afraid and trembling, let him turn back and depart from Mount Gilead. And twenty-two thousand of the people turned back, and ten thousand remained.

4 Yahweh said to Gideon: The people are still too many. Bring them down to the water, and I will refine them for you there; and it shall be that the one of whom I say to you, This one shall go with you — he shall go with you; and everyone of whom I say to you, This one shall not go with you — he shall not go.

5 He brought the people down to the water, and Yahweh said to Gideon: Everyone who laps from the water with his tongue, as a dog laps, set him apart by himself; and everyone who kneels on his knees to drink, likewise.

6 The number of those who lapped, with their hand to their mouth, was three hundred men; and all the rest of the people knelt on their knees to drink water.

7 Yahweh said to Gideon: By the three hundred men who lapped I will save you, and I will give Midian into your hand; and let all the people go, each man to his place.

8 They took the people's provisions in their hand, and their trumpets; and he sent every man of Israel away, each to his tents, but he held fast to the three hundred men. And the camp of Midian was below him in the valley.

9 That night Yahweh said to him: Rise, go down against the camp, for I have given it into your hand.

10 But if you are afraid to go down, go down, you and Purah your servant, to the camp,

11 and hear what they say; and afterward your hands will be strengthened, and you will go down against the camp. So he went down, he and Purah his servant, to the edge of the armed men who were in the camp.

12 Midian and Amalek and all the children of the east were lying in the valley like locusts in multitude, and their camels were without number, like the sand on the seashore in multitude.

13 Gideon came, and look, a man was recounting a dream to his companion, and he said: Look, I dreamed a dream, and look, a cake of barley bread was tumbling into the camp of Midian; it came to a tent, struck it, and it fell; it turned it upside down, and the tent lay flat.

14 His companion answered and said: This is nothing but the sword of Gideon son of Joash, a man of Israel. God has given Midian and all the camp into his hand.

15 When Gideon heard the recounting of the dream and its interpretation, he bowed down in worship; he returned to the camp of Israel and said: Rise, for Yahweh has given the camp of Midian into your hand.

16 He divided the three hundred men into three companies, and he put trumpets into the hand of all of them, and empty jars, and torches inside the jars.

17 He said to them: Watch me and do likewise. Look, when I come to the edge of the camp, it shall be that as I do, so you shall do.

18 When I blow the trumpet, I and all who are with me, then you also blow the trumpets all around the whole camp, and say: For Yahweh and for Gideon!

19 Gideon and the hundred men who were with him came to the edge of the camp at the beginning of the middle watch, just when they had set the watch; and they blew the trumpets and shattered the jars that were in their hands.

20 The three companies blew the trumpets and broke the jars; they held the torches in their left hands and the trumpets in their right hands for blowing, and they cried: A sword for Yahweh and for Gideon!

21 They stood, each man in his place, all around the camp; and all the camp ran, and they shouted, and they fled.

22 They blew the three hundred trumpets, and Yahweh set each man's sword against his companion and against all the camp; and the camp fled as far as Beth-shittah toward Zererah, as far as the edge of Abel-meholah by Tabbath.

23 The men of Israel were called out from Naphtali and from Asher and from all Manasseh, and they pursued after Midian.

24 Gideon sent messengers throughout all the hill country of Ephraim, saying: Come down to meet Midian, and seize the waters for yourselves as far as Beth-barah, and the Jordan. And all the men of Ephraim were called out, and they seized the waters as far as Beth-barah, and the Jordan.

25 They captured the two princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb; they killed Oreb at the Rock of Oreb, and Zeeb they killed at the Winepress of Zeeb, and they pursued Midian; and they brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon, across the Jordan.

1 The men of Ephraim said to him, "What is this thing you have done to us, not calling us when you went to fight against Midian?" And they contended with him fiercely.

2 He said to them, "What have I done now compared with you? Is not the gleaning of Ephraim better than the grape harvest of Abiezer?

4 Gideon came to the Jordan and crossed over, he and the three hundred men who were with him, exhausted yet pursuing.

5 He said to the men of Succoth, "Please give loaves of bread to the people who follow me, for they are exhausted, and I am pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian."

6 The officials of Succoth said, "Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your hand, that we should give bread to your army?"

15 He came to the men of Succoth and said, "Here are Zebah and Zalmunna, about whom you taunted me, saying, 'Is the hand of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your hand, that we should give bread to your exhausted men?'"

35 And they showed no loyal love to the house of Jerubbaal, Gideon, in return for all the good that he had done for Israel.

1 Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem, to his mother's brothers, and spoke to them and to the whole clan of his mother's father's house, saying,

1 The men of Ephraim were called together and crossed to Zaphon, and they said to Jephthah, "Why did you cross over to fight against the children of Ammon and did not call us to go with you? We will burn your house over you with fire."

2 Jephthah said to them, "I and my people had a great quarrel with the children of Ammon, and I called you, but you did not deliver me from their hand.

3 When I saw that you were no deliverer, I took my life in my hand and crossed over against the children of Ammon, and Yahweh gave them into my hand. Why have you come up against me this day to fight against me?"

4 Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead and fought against Ephraim, and the men of Gilead struck Ephraim down, because they had said, "You Gileadites are fugitives of Ephraim, in the midst of Ephraim and in the midst of Manasseh."

5 Gilead seized the fords of the Jordan against Ephraim. When a fugitive of Ephraim said, "Let me cross over," the men of Gilead said to him, "Are you an Ephraimite?" and he said, "No";

6 then they said to him, "Say 'Shibboleth,' please," and he said "Sibboleth," for he could not manage to speak it right. They seized him and slaughtered him at the fords of the Jordan. At that time forty-two thousand of Ephraim fell.

7 Jephthah judged Israel six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and was buried in the cities of Gilead.

8 After him Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel.

9 He had thirty sons, and thirty daughters he sent outside, and thirty daughters he brought in from outside for his sons. He judged Israel seven years.

10 Ibzan died and was buried at Bethlehem.

11 After him Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel, and he judged Israel ten years.

12 Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried at Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.

13 After him Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel.

14 He had forty sons and thirty grandsons, who rode on seventy young donkeys. He judged Israel eight years.

15 Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite died and was buried at Pirathon in the land of Ephraim, in the hill country of the Amalekites.

Judges 13

1 The children of Israel again did what was evil in Yahweh's eyes, and Yahweh gave them into the hand of the Philistines for forty years.

2 There was a certain man from Zorah, of the clan of the Danites, whose name was Manoah; his wife was barren and had borne no child.

3 The messenger of Yahweh appeared to the woman and said to her, "Look, you are barren and have borne no child, but you will conceive and bear a son.

4 Now be careful, please, and drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean.

5 For look, you will conceive and bear a son, and no razor shall go up on his head, for the boy will be a Nazirite to God from the womb, and he will begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines."

6 The woman came and told her husband, saying, "A man of God came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of the messenger of God, very awesome. I did not ask him where he was from, and he did not tell me his name.

7 He said to me, 'Look, you will conceive and bear a son. Now drink no wine or strong drink and eat nothing unclean, for the boy will be a Nazirite to God from the womb until the day of his death.'"

8 Manoah pleaded with Yahweh and said, "Please, Lord, let the man of God whom you sent come again to us and teach us what we are to do for the boy who is to be born."

9 God listened to Manoah's voice, and the messenger of God came again to the woman while she was sitting in the field, but Manoah her husband was not with her.

10 The woman hurried and ran and told her husband, and said to him, "Look, the man who came to me that day has appeared to me."

11 Manoah rose and went after his wife and came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" He said, "I am."

12 Manoah said, "Now let your words come to pass. What shall be the rule for the boy, and what is he to do?"

13 The messenger of Yahweh said to Manoah, "Of all that I said to the woman, let her be careful.

14 She must not eat anything that comes from the grapevine, she must not drink wine or strong drink, and she must not eat anything unclean. All that I commanded her, let her keep."

15 Manoah said to the messenger of Yahweh, "Let us detain you, please, and prepare a young goat for you."

16 The messenger of Yahweh said to Manoah, "If you detain me, I will not eat your food; but if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to Yahweh." For Manoah did not know that he was the messenger of Yahweh.

17 Manoah said to the messenger of Yahweh, "What is your name, so that when your word comes to pass we may honor you?"

18 The messenger of Yahweh said to him, "Why do you ask about my name, seeing it is wonderful?"

19 Manoah took the young goat and the grain offering and offered them on the rock to Yahweh, and he acted wonderfully, while Manoah and his wife were watching.

20 When the flame went up from the altar toward heaven, the messenger of Yahweh went up in the flame of the altar, while Manoah and his wife were watching, and they fell on their faces to the ground.

21 The messenger of Yahweh did not appear again to Manoah or his wife. Then Manoah knew that he was the messenger of Yahweh.

22 Manoah said to his wife, "We shall surely die, for we have seen God."

23 His wife said to him, "If Yahweh had wanted to kill us, he would not have taken a burnt offering and a grain offering from our hand, and he would not have shown us all these things, and he would not now have let us hear such a thing as this."

24 The woman bore a son and called his name Samson. The boy grew, and Yahweh blessed him.

25 The Spirit of Yahweh began to stir him in Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

Judges 14

1 Samson went down to Timnah, and there he saw a woman among the daughters of the Philistines.

2 He came back up and told his father and his mother, saying, "I saw a woman in Timnah, one of the daughters of the Philistines. Now get her for me as a wife."

3 His father and his mother said to him, "Is there no woman among the daughters of your brothers, or among all my people, that you go to take a wife from the uncircumcised Philistines?" But Samson said to his father, "Get her for me, for she is right in my eyes."

4 His father and his mother did not know that this was from Yahweh, for he was seeking an occasion against the Philistines. At that time the Philistines were ruling over Israel.

5 Samson went down to Timnah with his father and his mother. When they came to the vineyards of Timnah, there was a young lion roaring to meet him.

6 The Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon him, and he tore the lion apart as one tears a young goat, though he had nothing in his hand. He did not tell his father or his mother what he had done.

7 He went down and spoke with the woman, and she was right in Samson's eyes.

8 After some days he returned to take her, and he turned aside to look at the carcass of the lion. There, in the lion's body, was a swarm of bees, and honey.

9 He scraped it into his hands and went on, eating as he went. He came to his father and his mother and gave some to them, and they ate. But he did not tell them that he had scraped the honey from the lion's body.

10 His father went down to the woman, and Samson held a feast there, for that is what the young men used to do.

11 When they saw him, they took thirty companions to be with him.

12 Samson said to them, "Let me put a riddle to you. If you can solve it for me within the seven days of the feast and find it out, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes.

13 But if you cannot solve it for me, you will give me thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes." They said to him, "Put your riddle, so we can hear it."

14 He said to them, "Out of the eater came food, and out of the strong came sweetness." For three days they could not solve the riddle.

15 On the seventh day they said to Samson's wife, "Coax your husband to tell us the riddle, or we will burn you and your father's house with fire. Did you invite us here to make us poor? Is that not so?"

16 Samson's wife wept before him and said, "You only hate me; you do not love me. You put a riddle to the sons of my people, and you have not told it to me." He said to her, "Look, I have not told my father or my mother; should I tell you?"

17 She wept before him the seven days of their feast. On the seventh day he told her, because she pressed him hard, and she told the riddle to the sons of her people.

18 On the seventh day, before the sun went down, the men of the city said to him, "What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?" He said to them, "If you had not plowed with my heifer, you would not have found out my riddle."

19 The Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon and struck down thirty of their men. He took what they wore and gave the changes of clothes to those who had solved the riddle. Burning with anger, he went up to his father's house.

20 Samson's wife was given to his companion, who had been his friend.

Judges 15

1 After a while, at the time of the wheat harvest, Samson visited his wife, bringing a young goat. He said, "I will go in to my wife, into the room." But her father would not let him enter.

2 Her father said, "I truly thought you utterly hated her, so I gave her to your companion. Is her younger sister not better than she? Take her instead, please."

3 Samson said to them, "This time I am blameless toward the Philistines, for I am going to do them harm."

4 Samson went and caught three hundred foxes. He took torches, turned the foxes tail to tail, and put one torch between each pair of tails, in the middle.

5 He lit the torches and let the foxes go into the standing grain of the Philistines, and he burned up the stacked grain and the standing grain, and olive orchards as well.

6 The Philistines asked, "Who did this?" They answered, "Samson, the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he took Samson's wife and gave her to his companion." So the Philistines came up and burned her and her father with fire.

7 Samson said to them, "If this is what you do, I will not stop until I have taken my revenge on you."

8 He struck them hip and thigh, a great slaughter, then went down and lived in the cleft of the rock of Etam.

9 The Philistines came up and camped in Judah, and spread out at Lehi.

10 The men of Judah said, "Why have you come up against us?" They said, "We have come up to bind Samson, to do to him as he did to us."

11 Three thousand men of Judah went down to the cleft of the rock of Etam and said to Samson, "Do you not know that the Philistines rule over us? What is this you have done to us?" He said to them, "As they did to me, so I did to them."

12 They said to him, "We have come down to bind you and hand you over to the Philistines." Samson said to them, "Swear to me that you yourselves will not strike me down."

13 They said to him, "No; we will only bind you fast and hand you over to them; we will certainly not kill you." They bound him with two new ropes and brought him up from the rock.

14 When he came to Lehi, the Philistines shouted to meet him. The Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon him, and the ropes on his arms became like flax that has been burned in fire, and his bonds melted off his hands.

15 He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey, reached out his hand, took it, and struck down a thousand men with it.

16 Samson said, "With the jawbone of a donkey — a heap, two heaps — with the jawbone of a donkey I have struck down a thousand men."

17 When he finished speaking, he threw the jawbone from his hand, and he called that place Ramath-lehi.

18 He was very thirsty, and he called to Yahweh and said, "You gave this great deliverance by the hand of your servant. Now shall I die of thirst and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised?"

19 God split open the hollow that is in Lehi, and water came out of it. He drank, his spirit returned, and he revived. Therefore he called its name En-hakkore; it is in Lehi to this day.

20 He judged Israel in the days of the Philistines for twenty years.

Judges 16

1 Samson went to Gaza, and there he saw a prostitute, and he went in to her.

2 The Gazites were told, "Samson has come here." They surrounded him and lay in wait for him all night at the gate of the city. They kept quiet all night, saying, "Until morning light; then we will kill him."

3 Samson lay until midnight. At midnight he rose, took hold of the doors of the city gate and the two posts, and pulled them up, bar and all. He put them on his shoulders and carried them to the top of the hill that faces Hebron.

4 After this, he loved a woman in the valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah.

5 The lords of the Philistines came up to her and said to her, "Coax him, and see where his great strength lies and by what means we can overpower him, so that we can bind him and subdue him. Each of us will give you eleven hundred pieces of silver."

6 Delilah said to Samson, "Tell me, please, where your great strength lies, and with what you could be bound so that you could be subdued."

7 Samson said to her, "If they bind me with seven fresh cords that have not been dried, I will become weak and be like any other man."

8 The lords of the Philistines brought her seven fresh cords that had not been dried, and she bound him with them.

9 Men were lying in wait in her inner room. She said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" He snapped the cords as a thread of tow snaps when it touches fire. So his strength was not discovered.

10 Delilah said to Samson, "Look, you have mocked me and spoken lies to me. Now tell me, please, with what you could be bound."

11 He said to her, "If they bind me fast with new ropes that have never been used for work, I will become weak and be like any other man."

12 Delilah took new ropes and bound him with them, and she said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" The men in wait were sitting in the inner room. He snapped the ropes off his arms like a thread.

13 Delilah said to Samson, "Until now you have mocked me and spoken lies to me. Tell me with what you could be bound." He said to her, "If you weave the seven locks of my head with the web of the loom."

14 She fastened it with the pin and said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" He woke from his sleep and pulled away the pin of the loom and the web.

15 She said to him, "How can you say, 'I love you,' when your heart is not with me? You have mocked me these three times and have not told me where your great strength lies."

16 Day after day she pressed him with her words and urged him, until his soul was wearied to the point of death.

17 He told her all that was in his heart and said to her, "No razor has come on my head, for I have been a Nazirite to God from my mother's womb. If I am shaved, my strength will leave me, and I will become weak and be like every other man."

18 When Delilah saw that he had told her all that was in his heart, she sent and called the lords of the Philistines, saying, "Come up this time, for he has told me all that is in his heart." The lords of the Philistines came up to her and brought the silver in their hands.

19 She made him sleep on her knees, and she called a man, who shaved off the seven locks of his head. She began to subdue him, and his strength left him.

20 She said, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" He woke from his sleep and said, "I will go out as time after time, and shake myself free." He did not know that Yahweh had left him.

21 The Philistines seized him and gouged out his eyes. They brought him down to Gaza and bound him with bronze chains, and he ground grain in the prison house.

22 But the hair of his head began to grow again after it had been shaved.

23 The lords of the Philistines gathered to offer a great sacrifice to Dagon their god and to rejoice. They said, "Our god has given Samson our enemy into our hand."

24 When the people saw him, they praised their god, for they said, "Our god has given into our hand our enemy, the destroyer of our land, who multiplied our slain."

25 When their hearts were merry, they said, "Call Samson, so he can entertain us." They called Samson from the prison house, and he performed before them, and they stood him between the pillars.

26 Samson said to the boy who held his hand, "Let me rest, and let me feel the pillars on which the house stands, so that I can lean on them."

27 The house was full of men and women, and all the lords of the Philistines were there. On the roof were about three thousand men and women watching Samson perform.

28 Samson called to Yahweh and said, "Lord Yahweh, remember me, please, and strengthen me, please, only this once, God, so that I may take vengeance on the Philistines, one act of vengeance for my two eyes."

29 Samson grasped the two middle pillars on which the house stood and braced himself against them, one in his right hand and one in his left.

30 Samson said, "Let me die with the Philistines!" He bent with all his strength, and the house fell on the lords and on all the people in it. The dead he killed in his death were more than those he had killed in his life.

31 His brothers and all his father's house came down, lifted him, brought him up, and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol, in the tomb of Manoah his father. He had judged Israel for twenty years.

Judges 17

1 There was a man from the hill country of Ephraim whose name was Micah.

2 He said to his mother, "The eleven hundred pieces of silver that were taken from you, about which you uttered a curse and also spoke it in my hearing — look, the silver is with me; I took it." His mother said, "Blessed be my son by Yahweh."

3 He returned the eleven hundred pieces of silver to his mother. His mother said, "I solemnly dedicate the silver to Yahweh from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a cast image. Now I return it to you."

4 He returned the silver to his mother, and his mother took two hundred pieces of silver and gave them to the silversmith, who made them into a carved image and a cast image. It was in Micah's house.

5 The man Micah had a house of God. He made an ephod and teraphim, and he consecrated one of his sons, who became his priest.

6 In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

7 There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah, of the family of Judah, who was a Levite, and he lived there as a sojourner.

8 The man left the city, Bethlehem in Judah, to live wherever he could find a place. On his journey he came to the hill country of Ephraim, to Micah's house.

9 Micah said to him, "Where do you come from?" He said to him, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to live wherever I can find a place."

10 Micah said to him, "Stay with me, and be a father and a priest to me, and I will give you ten pieces of silver a year, a set of clothes, and your food." The Levite went in.

11 The Levite agreed to live with the man, and the young man became to him like one of his sons.

12 Micah consecrated the Levite, and the young man became his priest and was in Micah's house.

13 Micah said, "Now I know that Yahweh will do me good, since the Levite has become my priest."

Judges 18

1 In those days there was no king in Israel, and in those days the tribe of the Danites was seeking an inheritance for itself to live in, for up to that day no inheritance among the tribes of Israel had fallen to it.

2 So the sons of Dan sent five men from their clan, from their whole number, men of valor, from Zorah and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land and to search it out, and they said to them, "Go, search out the land." And they came to the hill country of Ephraim, as far as Micah's house, and spent the night there.

3 They were beside Micah's house, and they recognized the voice of the young Levite, and they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? And what are you doing in this place? And what do you have here?"

4 And he said to them, "This and this is what Micah has done for me: he hired me, and I have become his priest."

5 And they said to him, "Ask, please, of God, so that we may know whether our way on which we are going will prosper."

6 And the priest said to them, "Go in peace. The way you are going is before Yahweh."

7 And the five men went and came to Laish, and they saw the people who were in it living in safety, after the manner of the Sidonians, quiet and secure, and there was no one in the land, possessing authority, to put them to shame in anything, and they were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.

8 And they came to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, and their brothers said to them, "What do you say?"

9 And they said, "Arise, and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and see, it is very good. And you — are you keeping still? Do not be slow to go, to come and take possession of the land.

10 When you come, you will come to a people who feel secure, and the land is wide on both sides, for God has given it into your hand, a place where there is no lack of anything that is on the earth."

11 And six hundred men of the family of the Danites, girded with weapons of war, set out from there, from Zorah and from Eshtaol.

12 And they went up and camped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. Therefore that place is called Mahaneh-dan to this day; see, it is behind Kiriath-jearim.

13 And they passed on from there to the hill country of Ephraim and came as far as Micah's house.

14 Then the five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish spoke up and said to their brothers, "Do you know that in these houses there are an ephod and teraphim and a carved image and a molten image? Now, then, consider what you will do."

15 And they turned aside there and came to the house of the young Levite, Micah's house, and asked after his welfare.

16 And the six hundred men of the sons of Dan, girded with their weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate.

17 And the five men who had gone to spy out the land went up and came in there and took the carved image and the ephod and the teraphim and the molten image, while the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men girded with weapons of war.

18 And these went into Micah's house and took the carved image of the ephod and the teraphim and the molten image, and the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

19 And they said to him, "Be silent. Put your hand over your mouth and go with us, and be a father and a priest to us. Is it better for you to be priest to the house of one man, or to be priest to a tribe and a clan in Israel?"

20 And the priest's heart was glad, and he took the ephod and the teraphim and the carved image and went into the midst of the people.

21 And they turned and went, and they put the little children and the livestock and the goods in front of them.

22 They had gone far from Micah's house when the men who were in the houses near Micah's house were called together and overtook the sons of Dan.

23 And they called out to the sons of Dan, and they turned their faces and said to Micah, "What is the matter with you, that you have been called out?"

24 And he said, "You have taken my gods, which I made, and the priest, and you have gone, and what do I have left? And how can you say to me, 'What is the matter with you?'"

25 And the sons of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, lest men embittered in spirit fall upon you and you gather in your own life and the lives of your household."

26 And the sons of Dan went on their way, and Micah saw that they were too strong for him, and he turned and went back to his house.

27 And they took what Micah had made, and the priest who was his, and they came to Laish, to a people quiet and secure, and struck them with the edge of the sword, and they burned the city with fire.

28 And there was no deliverer, because it was far from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone, and it was in the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob. And they built the city and lived in it.

29 And they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; but Laish was the name of the city at first.

30 And the sons of Dan set up the carved image for themselves, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh — he and his sons were priests to the tribe of the Danites until the day of the exile of the land.

31 And they set up for themselves Micah's carved image, which he had made, for all the days that the house of God was at Shiloh.

Judges 19

1 In those days there was no king in Israel, and there was a Levite man living as a sojourner in the remote parts of the hill country of Ephraim, and he took for himself a woman, a concubine, from Bethlehem in Judah.

2 And his concubine played the harlot against him, and she went away from him to her father's house, to Bethlehem in Judah, and she was there for a period of four months.

3 And her husband arose and went after her, to speak to her heart, to bring her back, and his servant was with him and a pair of donkeys. And she brought him into her father's house, and the young woman's father saw him and was glad to meet him.

4 And his father-in-law, the young woman's father, held him fast, and he stayed with him three days, and they ate and drank and spent the night there.

5 And on the fourth day they rose early in the morning, and he arose to go, and the young woman's father said to his son-in-law, "Strengthen your heart with a piece of bread, and afterward you may go."

6 And the two of them sat down and ate together and drank, and the young woman's father said to the man, "Be willing, please, and spend the night, and let your heart be merry."

7 And the man arose to go, but his father-in-law pressed him, and he turned back and spent the night there.

8 And he rose early in the morning on the fifth day to go, and the young woman's father said, "Strengthen your heart, please," and they lingered until the day declined, and the two of them ate.

9 And the man arose to go, he and his concubine and his servant, and his father-in-law, the young woman's father, said to him, "See, please, the day has weakened toward evening. Spend the night, please. See, the day is drawing to its close; spend the night here and let your heart be merry, and tomorrow you shall rise early for your journey and go to your tent."

10 But the man was not willing to spend the night, and he arose and went, and he came to a point opposite Jebus — that is, Jerusalem — and with him were a pair of saddled donkeys, and his concubine was with him.

11 They were beside Jebus, and the day had gone down greatly, and the servant said to his master, "Come, please, and let us turn aside into this city of the Jebusites and spend the night in it."

12 And his master said to him, "We will not turn aside into a foreigner's city, whose people are not of the sons of Israel; we will pass on as far as Gibeah."

13 And he said to his servant, "Come, and let us draw near to one of the places, and we will spend the night in Gibeah or in Ramah."

14 And they passed on and went, and the sun went down on them beside Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin.

15 And they turned aside there, to go in and spend the night in Gibeah. And he went in and sat down in the open square of the city, and there was no man to take them into his house to spend the night.

16 And see, an old man was coming from his work, from the field, in the evening, and the man was from the hill country of Ephraim and he was living as a sojourner in Gibeah, but the men of the place were Benjamites.

17 And he lifted his eyes and saw the traveling man in the open square of the city, and the old man said, "Where are you going, and where do you come from?"

18 And he said to him, "We are passing from Bethlehem in Judah to the remote parts of the hill country of Ephraim. I am from there, and I went as far as Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to the house of Yahweh, and there is no man to take me into his house.

19 And we have both straw and fodder for our donkeys, and there is also bread and wine for me and for your maidservant and for the servant who is with your servants; there is no lack of anything."

20 And the old man said, "Peace to you. Only let all your need be upon me; only do not spend the night in the open square."

21 And he brought him into his house, and he mixed fodder for the donkeys, and they washed their feet and ate and drank.

22 They were making their hearts merry, and see, the men of the city, men who were sons of worthlessness, surrounded the house, pounding on the door, and they said to the old man, the owner of the house, saying, "Bring out the man who came into your house, that we may know him."

23 And the man, the owner of the house, went out to them and said to them, "No, my brothers, do not do evil, please. Since this man has come into my house, do not do this disgraceful thing.

24 See, here are my virgin daughter and his concubine. Let me bring them out, please, and humble them and do to them what is good in your eyes, but to this man do not do this disgraceful thing."

25 But the men would not listen to him, and the man seized his concubine and brought her out to them outside, and they knew her and abused her all night until the morning, and they sent her away at the rising of the dawn.

26 And the woman came at the turning of the morning and fell at the entrance of the house of the man where her master was, until it was light.

27 And her master arose in the morning and opened the doors of the house and went out to go on his way, and see, the woman, his concubine, had fallen at the entrance of the house, with her hands on the threshold.

28 And he said to her, "Get up, and let us go," but there was no answer. And he took her up onto the donkey, and the man arose and went to his place.

29 And he came to his house and took the knife and seized his concubine and cut her, by her bones, into twelve pieces, and he sent her throughout all the territory of Israel.

30 And everyone who saw it said, "Nothing like this has happened or been seen from the day the sons of Israel came up from the land of Egypt until this day. Set your minds on it, take counsel, and speak."

Judges 20

1 And all the sons of Israel went out, and the congregation was assembled as one man, from Dan to Beersheba, and the land of Gilead, to Yahweh at Mizpah.

2 And the chiefs of all the people, all the tribes of Israel, took their stand in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand men on foot who drew the sword.

3 And the sons of Benjamin heard that the sons of Israel had gone up to Mizpah. And the sons of Israel said, "Speak: how did this evil thing happen?"

4 And the Levite man, the husband of the murdered woman, answered and said, "I came to Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin, I and my concubine, to spend the night.

5 And the masters of Gibeah rose against me and surrounded the house against me by night; they intended to kill me, and they raped my concubine, and she died.

6 And I seized my concubine and cut her in pieces and sent her throughout all the field of Israel's inheritance, for they have done a shameful and disgraceful thing in Israel.

7 See, all of you are sons of Israel. Give here your word and counsel."

8 And all the people arose as one man, saying, "No man of us will go to his tent, and no man of us will turn aside to his house.

9 And now, this is the thing that we will do to Gibeah: against it, by lot.

10 And we will take ten men out of a hundred for all the tribes of Israel, and a hundred out of a thousand, and a thousand out of ten thousand, to take provisions for the people, so that when they come they may do to Gibeah of Benjamin according to all the disgraceful thing that it has done in Israel."

11 And all the men of Israel were gathered together against the city, united as one man.

12 And the tribes of Israel sent men through all the tribes of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil thing that has happened among you?

13 And now, give up the men, the sons of worthlessness who are in Gibeah, and we will put them to death and burn away evil from Israel." But the sons of Benjamin would not listen to the voice of their brothers, the sons of Israel.

14 And the sons of Benjamin gathered from the cities to Gibeah, to go out to battle against the sons of Israel.

15 And the sons of Benjamin were mustered on that day from the cities, twenty-six thousand men who drew the sword, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, who were mustered as seven hundred chosen men.

16 Among all this people were seven hundred chosen men who were restricted in their right hand; every one of these could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.

17 And the men of Israel were mustered, besides Benjamin, four hundred thousand men who drew the sword; all of these were men of war.

18 And they arose and went up to Bethel and asked of God, and the sons of Israel said, "Who shall go up for us first to battle against the sons of Benjamin?" And Yahweh said, "Judah first."

19 And the sons of Israel arose in the morning and camped against Gibeah.

20 And the men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and the men of Israel drew up in battle array against them at Gibeah.

21 And the sons of Benjamin came out from Gibeah and destroyed to the ground, of Israel on that day, twenty-two thousand men.

22 And the people, the men of Israel, strengthened themselves and again drew up in battle array in the place where they had drawn up on the first day.

23 And the sons of Israel went up and wept before Yahweh until the evening, and they asked of Yahweh, saying, "Shall I again draw near to battle against the sons of Benjamin my brother?" And Yahweh said, "Go up against him."

24 And the sons of Israel drew near against the sons of Benjamin on the second day.

25 And Benjamin came out from Gibeah to meet them on the second day and again destroyed to the ground, of the sons of Israel, eighteen thousand men; all of these drew the sword.

26 And all the sons of Israel and all the people went up and came to Bethel, and they wept and sat there before Yahweh and fasted on that day until the evening, and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before Yahweh.

27 And the sons of Israel asked of Yahweh — and the ark of the covenant of God was there in those days,

28 and Phinehas son of Eleazar son of Aaron was standing before it in those days — saying, "Shall I again go out to battle against the sons of Benjamin my brother, or shall I stop?" And Yahweh said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give him into your hand."

29 And Israel set men in ambush around Gibeah.

30 And the sons of Israel went up against the sons of Benjamin on the third day and drew up against Gibeah as time after time before.

31 And the sons of Benjamin came out to meet the people and were drawn away from the city, and they began to strike down some of the people, as time after time before, on the highways, one of which goes up to Bethel and one to Gibeah, in the field, about thirty men in Israel.

32 And the sons of Benjamin said, "They are struck down before us as at the first," but the sons of Israel had said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways."

33 And all the men of Israel rose from their place and drew up at Baal-tamar, and the ambush of Israel burst out from its place, from Maareh-geba.

34 And ten thousand chosen men from all Israel came opposite Gibeah, and the battle was heavy, and they did not know that the evil was touching them.

35 And Yahweh struck Benjamin before Israel, and the sons of Israel destroyed, of Benjamin on that day, twenty-five thousand one hundred men; all of these drew the sword.

36 And the sons of Benjamin saw that they were struck down, and the men of Israel gave ground to Benjamin, because they trusted in the ambush that they had set against Gibeah.

37 And the men in ambush hurried and rushed against Gibeah, and the ambush moved forward and struck the whole city with the edge of the sword.

38 And the appointed sign between the men of Israel and the ambush was this: they would raise a great rising of smoke from the city.

39 And the men of Israel turned in the battle, and Benjamin began to strike down some of the men of Israel, about thirty men, for they said, "Surely he is struck down before us, as in the first battle."

40 And the rising of smoke began to go up from the city, a column of smoke, and Benjamin turned behind him, and see, the whole city had gone up toward the heavens.

41 And the men of Israel turned back, and the men of Benjamin were terrified, for they saw that the evil had touched them.

42 And they turned before the men of Israel toward the way of the wilderness, but the battle overtook them, and those who came from the cities were destroying them in their midst.

43 They surrounded Benjamin, they pursued him, they trod him down at Menuhah, as far as opposite Gibeah, toward the rising of the sun.

44 And eighteen thousand men fell from Benjamin; all of these were men of valor.

45 And they turned and fled toward the wilderness, to the rock of Rimmon, and they gleaned five thousand men of them on the highways, and they pursued closely after them as far as Gidom and struck down two thousand men of them.

46 And all who fell from Benjamin on that day were twenty-five thousand men who drew the sword; all of these were men of valor.

47 And six hundred men turned and fled toward the wilderness, to the rock of Rimmon, and they stayed at the rock of Rimmon four months.

48 And the men of Israel turned back to the sons of Benjamin and struck them with the edge of the sword, from the city, men and cattle and all that was found, and also all the cities that were found they set on fire.

Judges 21

1 And the men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah, saying, "No man of us shall give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife."

2 And the people came to Bethel and sat there until the evening before God, and they lifted their voice and wept with great weeping.

3 And they said, "Why, Yahweh, God of Israel, has this happened in Israel, that today one tribe should be missing from Israel?"

4 And on the next day the people rose early and built an altar there and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.

5 And the sons of Israel said, "Who is there among all the tribes of Israel who did not come up in the assembly to Yahweh?" For there had been the great oath concerning whoever did not come up to Yahweh at Mizpah, saying, "He shall surely be put to death."

6 And the sons of Israel grieved for Benjamin their brother and said, "Today one tribe has been cut off from Israel.

7 What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since we have sworn by Yahweh not to give them any of our daughters as wives?"

8 And they said, "Which one of the tribes of Israel did not come up to Yahweh at Mizpah?" And see, no man had come to the camp from Jabesh-gilead, to the assembly.

9 And the people were mustered, and see, there was no man there of the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead.

10 And the congregation sent twelve thousand men of the sons of valor there, and they commanded them, saying, "Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the edge of the sword, and the women and the little children.

11 And this is the thing that you shall do: every male, and every woman who has known lying with a male, you shall devote to destruction."

12 And they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young virgins who had not known a man by lying with a male, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.

13 And the whole congregation sent and spoke to the sons of Benjamin who were at the rock of Rimmon, and they proclaimed peace to them.

14 And Benjamin returned at that time, and they gave them the women whom they had kept alive from the women of Jabesh-gilead, and they did not find enough for them in this way.

15 And the people grieved for Benjamin, because Yahweh had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.

16 And the elders of the congregation said, "What shall we do for wives for those who remain, since the women have been destroyed from Benjamin?"

17 And they said, "There must be an inheritance of the survivors for Benjamin, so that a tribe will not be blotted out from Israel.

18 But we cannot give them wives from our daughters, for the sons of Israel have sworn, saying, 'Cursed is the one who gives a wife to Benjamin.'"

19 And they said, "See, there is a feast of Yahweh at Shiloh from year to year, which is north of Bethel, east of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah."

20 And they commanded the sons of Benjamin, saying, "Go and lie in wait in the vineyards,

21 and watch, and see, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, then come out from the vineyards and seize for yourselves, each man his wife, from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.

22 And when their fathers or their brothers come to plead with us, we will say to them, 'Be gracious to them for our sake, because we did not take for each man his wife in the battle, and because you did not give them to them; otherwise, at this time you would be guilty.'

23 And the sons of Benjamin did so, and they took wives according to their number from the dancers whom they had seized, and they went and returned to their inheritance and built the cities and lived in them.

24 And the sons of Israel walked from there at that time, each man to his tribe and to his clan, and they went out from there, each man to his inheritance.

25 In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.
