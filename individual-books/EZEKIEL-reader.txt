Clear English Bible — Ezekiel (Chapters 1–48): A Clear English Translation

This completes the Masoretic book of Ezekiel in this edition. The remaining books of the Old Testament stand in this edition as a clear-English modernization of the World English Bible (WEB) Classic text named in the front matter, except the books the front matter names as fresh translations made directly from the Hebrew; the New Testament stands on the Greek text named there. The book as printed here stands as PUBLISHED.

Publication — 25 November 2026. By the editor's ruling of 25 November 2026, this book of the Clear English Bible stands published. Until that ruling it stood on this shelf complete and landed, but unpublished; the editor, Robert Tilford, is the checker of last resort, and his ruling is the final check under which the book goes out. The block is reseated to the Masoretic numbering at the book's one seam: the editions' Ezekiel 20 now carries 44 verses and Ezekiel 21 carries 37, the Masoretic 21:1–5 standing where the English 20:45–49 stood and the Masoretic 21:6–37 where the English 21:1–32 stood; everywhere else the two numberings agree, and the block header is unchanged. (The wave brief's expectation that the two numberings are identical for this book does not hold and is recorded in the packet, not smoothed over.) The translation was built in six reviser passes (Masoretic chapters 1–8, 9–16, 17–24, 25–32, 33–40, 41–48) from the Masoretic text as it stands in the Westminster Leningrad Codex and Sefaria's Miqra according to the Masorah — the two witnesses consonant-exact in 1,123 of the book's 1,273 verses, the 150 divergences classified in the packet (82 Bolls extraction drops, at which the draft follows Sefaria and both controls; 58 substitutions, the great majority spelling only; 10 mixed), none touching the divine-name count — with JPS 1917 as the open control of record. Six independent checker passes that did not build the text read the draft against the Hebrew line and both controls: three passed with zero errors, and three passed with corrections — six errors in all (5:11; 13:20; 16:8; 16:21; 20:26; 23:37), every one corrected with the checker's exact replacement text and re-verified by count before insertion. The divine name יהוה stands 434 times in the book — no banked benchmark existed, so the count was established from both witnesses before the build, the two witnesses agreeing at every verse — and prints "Yahweh" at every seat; the book's signature collocation אדני יהוה, "the Lord Yahweh", stands at 217 seats, and its recognition formula כי אני יהוה, "that I am Yahweh", at 70; no standalone יהּ seat stands in Ezekiel. Ezekiel 1

1 Now in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, as I was among the exiles by the river Chebar, the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.
2 On the fifth day of the month, which was the fifth year of King Jehoiachin's exile,
3 the word of Yahweh came to Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans by the river Chebar, and the hand of Yahweh was there on him.
4 And I looked, and see, a stormy wind was coming from the north, a great cloud, with fire flashing back and forth, and a brightness around it, and out of its midst, like the gleam of glowing metal, out of the midst of the fire.
5 And out of its midst came the likeness of four living creatures. And this was their appearance: they had the likeness of a man.
6 Each one had four faces, and each one of them had four wings.
7 Their feet were straight feet, and the sole of their feet was like the sole of a calf's foot, and they sparkled like the gleam of burnished bronze.
8 And they had the hands of a man under their wings on their four sides, and the four of them had their faces and their wings:
9 their wings were joined to one another; they did not turn when they went; each one went straight forward.
10 As for the likeness of their faces, they had the face of a man, and the four of them had the face of a lion on the right side, and the four of them had the face of an ox on the left side; the four of them also had the face of an eagle.
11 Such were their faces. And their wings were spread out above; each one had two wings joined to another, and two covered their bodies.
12 And each one went straight forward; wherever the spirit was to go, they went; they did not turn when they went.
13 As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, like the appearance of torches; the fire moved back and forth among the living creatures, and the fire was bright, and lightning went out of the fire.
14 And the living creatures ran and returned like the appearance of a flash of lightning.
15 Now as I saw the living creatures, see, there was one wheel on the earth beside the living creatures, for each of its four faces.
16 The appearance of the wheels and their work was like the gleam of a beryl, and the four of them had one likeness; and their appearance and their work was as if a wheel were within a wheel.
17 When they went, they went in their four directions; they did not turn when they went.
18 As for their rims, they were high, and they were awesome; and the rims of the four of them were full of eyes all around.
19 And when the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them; and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up.
20 Wherever the spirit was to go, they went; the spirit was to go there, and the wheels were lifted up beside them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.
21 When those went, these went, and when those stood, these stood; and when those were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up beside them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.
22 And over the heads of the living creatures there was the likeness of an expanse, like the gleam of awesome crystal, stretched out over their heads above.
23 And under the expanse their wings were straight, one toward another; each one had two that covered on this side, and each one had two that covered on that side, their bodies.
24 And when they went, I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of great waters, like the voice of the Almighty, a sound of tumult like the sound of an army camp; when they stood, they let down their wings.
25 And there was a voice above the expanse that was over their heads; when they stood, they let down their wings.
26 And above the expanse that was over their heads was the likeness of a throne, like the appearance of a sapphire stone; and on the likeness of the throne was a likeness like the appearance of a man, on it above.
27 And I saw like the gleam of glowing metal, like the appearance of fire enclosing it all around, from the appearance of his waist and upward; and from the appearance of his waist and downward I saw like the appearance of fire, and there was brightness around him.
28 Like the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud on the day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness all around. This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of Yahweh. And when I saw it, I fell on my face, and I heard the voice of one speaking.

Ezekiel 2

1 And he said to me: Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak with you.
2 And a spirit entered into me when he spoke to me, and set me on my feet, and I heard him who was speaking to me.
3 And he said to me: Son of man, I am sending you to the children of Israel, to rebellious nations that have rebelled against me; they and their fathers have transgressed against me, to this very day.
4 And the children are hard-faced and stiff-hearted; I am sending you to them, and you shall say to them: Thus says the Lord Yahweh.
5 And they, whether they hear or whether they refuse — for they are a rebellious house — shall know that a prophet has been among them.
6 And you, son of man, do not be afraid of them, and do not be afraid of their words, though briers and thorns are with you and you dwell among scorpions; do not be afraid of their words, and do not be dismayed at their faces, for they are a rebellious house.
7 And you shall speak my words to them, whether they hear or whether they refuse, for they are rebellious.
8 And you, son of man, hear what I say to you: do not be rebellious like that rebellious house; open your mouth, and eat what I give you.
9 And I looked, and see, a hand was stretched out to me, and see, in it was a scroll of a book.
10 And he spread it before me, and it was written on front and back, and written on it were lamentations, and mourning, and woe.

Ezekiel 3

1 And he said to me: Son of man, eat what you find; eat this scroll, and go, speak to the house of Israel.
2 So I opened my mouth, and he made me eat this scroll.
3 And he said to me: Son of man, feed your belly and fill your insides with this scroll that I give you. Then I ate it, and it was in my mouth like honey for sweetness.
4 And he said to me: Son of man, go to the house of Israel, and speak my words to them.
5 For you are not sent to a people of deep speech and heavy tongue, but to the house of Israel,
6 not to many peoples of deep speech and heavy tongue, whose words you cannot understand. Surely, if I sent you to them, they would listen to you.
7 But the house of Israel will not be willing to listen to you, for they are not willing to listen to me, for all the house of Israel are hard of forehead and stiff of heart.
8 See, I have made your face hard against their faces, and your forehead hard against their foreheads.
9 Like a diamond, harder than flint, I have made your forehead; do not fear them, and do not be dismayed at their faces, for they are a rebellious house.
10 And he said to me: Son of man, all my words that I speak to you, take into your heart, and hear with your ears.
11 And go to the exiles, to the children of your people, and speak to them, and say to them: Thus says the Lord Yahweh, whether they hear or whether they refuse.
12 Then a spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great rushing: Blessed be the glory of Yahweh from his place,
13 and the sound of the wings of the living creatures touching one another, and the sound of the wheels beside them, and the sound of a great rushing.
14 And a spirit lifted me up and took me, and I went bitter, in the heat of my spirit, and the hand of Yahweh was strong on me.
15 And I came to the exiles at Tel Abib, who dwelt by the river Chebar, and I sat where they were sitting, and I sat there stunned among them seven days.
16 And at the end of seven days, the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
17 Son of man, I have appointed you a watchman for the house of Israel; and you shall hear a word from my mouth, and warn them from me.
18 When I say to the wicked: You shall surely die, and you do not warn him, and do not speak to warn the wicked from his wicked way, to keep him alive, that wicked man shall die in his guilt, but his blood I will require at your hand.
19 But if you warn the wicked, and he does not turn from his wickedness and from his wicked way, he shall die in his guilt, but you have delivered your soul.
20 And when a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does wrong, and I lay a stumbling block before him, he shall die; because you did not warn him, he shall die in his sin, and his righteous deeds that he did shall not be remembered, but his blood I will require at your hand.
21 But if you warn the righteous man, so that the righteous man does not sin, and he does not sin, he shall surely live, because he was warned, and you have delivered your soul.
22 And the hand of Yahweh was there on me, and he said to me: Arise, go out to the plain, and there I will speak with you.
23 And I arose and went out to the plain, and see, the glory of Yahweh stood there, like the glory that I saw by the river Chebar, and I fell on my face.
24 And a spirit entered into me and set me on my feet, and he spoke with me and said to me: Go, shut yourself inside your house.
25 And you, son of man, see, they will put ropes on you and bind you with them, and you shall not go out among them;
26 and I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth, and you shall be mute, and you shall not be a reprover to them, for they are a rebellious house.
27 But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you shall say to them: Thus says the Lord Yahweh; the one who hears, let him hear, and the one who refuses, let him refuse, for they are a rebellious house.

Ezekiel 4

1 And you, son of man, take a brick, and lay it before you, and engrave on it a city — Jerusalem;
2 and lay siege against it, and build a siege wall against it, and pour out a mound against it, and set camps against it, and place battering rams against it all around.
3 And take an iron pan, and set it as an iron wall between you and the city, and set your face toward it, and it shall be under siege, and you shall lay siege against it. It is a sign to the house of Israel.
4 And you, lie on your left side, and lay the guilt of the house of Israel on it; for the number of the days that you lie on it, you shall bear their guilt.
5 For I have appointed the years of their guilt to you as a number of days, three hundred and ninety days, and you shall bear the guilt of the house of Israel.
6 And when you have completed these, you shall lie a second time on your right side, and bear the guilt of the house of Judah forty days; a day for a year, a day for a year I have appointed to you.
7 And you shall set your face toward the siege of Jerusalem, with your arm bared, and you shall prophesy against it.
8 And see, I put ropes on you, and you shall not turn from one side to the other until you have completed the days of your siege.
9 And take wheat and barley and beans and lentils and millet and spelt, and put them in one vessel, and make them into bread for yourself; for the number of the days that you lie on your side, three hundred and ninety days, you shall eat it.
10 And your food that you eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels a day; from time to time you shall eat it.
11 And you shall drink water by measure, a sixth of a hin; from time to time you shall drink.
12 And you shall eat it as a barley cake, and you shall bake it in their sight on dung that comes out of man.
13 And Yahweh said: So shall the children of Israel eat their bread unclean among the nations where I will drive them.
14 Then I said: Ah, Lord Yahweh! See, my soul has not been polluted, and from my youth until now I have not eaten what dies of itself or is torn by beasts, and no abhorred flesh has come into my mouth.
15 And he said to me: See, I have given you cow's dung instead of man's dung, and you shall prepare your bread on it.
16 And he said to me: Son of man, see, I am breaking the staff of bread in Jerusalem, and they shall eat bread by weight and in anxiety, and they shall drink water by measure and in dismay,
17 so that they lack bread and water, and are dismayed, a man and his brother, and waste away in their guilt.

Ezekiel 5

1 And you, son of man, take a sharp sword; take it for yourself as a barber's razor, and pass it over your head and over your beard; then take scales for weighing, and divide them.
2 A third you shall burn in the fire in the midst of the city, when the days of the siege are fulfilled; and you shall take a third and strike it with the sword all around it; and a third you shall scatter to the wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.
3 And you shall take from there a few in number, and bind them in your skirts.
4 And from them again you shall take, and throw them into the midst of the fire, and burn them in the fire; from it a fire shall come out to all the house of Israel.
5 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: This is Jerusalem; I have set her in the midst of the nations, and countries are around her.
6 And she rebelled against my judgments in wickedness more than the nations, and against my statutes more than the countries that are around her, for they have rejected my judgments, and as for my statutes, they have not walked in them.
7 Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: Because you have been more turbulent than the nations that are around you, and have not walked in my statutes and have not done my judgments, and have not even done according to the judgments of the nations that are around you;
8 therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: See, I, even I, am against you, and I will execute judgments in your midst in the sight of the nations.
9 And I will do in you what I have not done, and the like of which I will not do again, because of all your abominations.
10 Therefore fathers shall eat sons in your midst, and sons shall eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments in you, and I will scatter all your remnant to every wind.
11 Therefore, as I live, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh, surely because you have defiled my sanctuary with all your detestable things and with all your abominations, I also will diminish you, and my eye shall not spare, and I also will have no pity.
12 A third of you shall die by the pestilence and be consumed by famine in your midst, and a third shall fall by the sword around you, and a third I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw out a sword after them.
13 And my anger shall spend itself, and I will rest my wrath on them, and I will be comforted; and they shall know that I, Yahweh, have spoken in my zeal, when I spend my wrath on them.
14 And I will make you a waste and a reproach among the nations that are around you, in the sight of all who pass by.
15 And it shall be a reproach and a taunt, a warning and a horror, to the nations that are around you, when I execute judgments in you in anger and in wrath and in wrathful rebukes; I, Yahweh, have spoken.
16 When I send on them the evil arrows of famine that are for destruction, which I will send to destroy you, and I increase famine on you, and break your staff of bread;
17 and I send on you famine and evil beasts, and they shall bereave you, and pestilence and blood shall pass through you, and I will bring a sword on you; I, Yahweh, have spoken.

Ezekiel 6

1 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
2 Son of man, set your face toward the mountains of Israel, and prophesy to them,
3 and say: Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord Yahweh. Thus says the Lord Yahweh to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys: See, I, even I, am bringing a sword on you, and I will destroy your high places.
4 And your altars shall be made desolate, and your sun-images shall be broken, and I will cast down your slain before your idols.
5 And I will lay the corpses of the children of Israel before their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars.
6 In all your dwelling places the cities shall be laid waste and the high places shall be desolate, so that your altars may be laid waste and bear their guilt, and your idols may be broken and cease, and your sun-images may be cut down, and your works may be wiped out.
7 And the slain shall fall in your midst, and you shall know that I am Yahweh.
8 Yet I will leave a remnant, in that you shall have some who escape the sword among the nations, when you are scattered through the lands.
9 And your escaped ones shall remember me among the nations where they were carried captive, how I was broken by their whoring heart that turned away from me, and by their eyes that whored after their idols; and they shall loathe themselves in their own sight for the evils that they did in all their abominations.
10 And they shall know that I am Yahweh; I did not speak in vain, to do this evil to them.
11 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: Strike with your hand, and stamp with your foot, and say: Alas, for all the evil abominations of the house of Israel, for they shall fall by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence.
12 The one far off shall die by the pestilence, and the one near shall fall by the sword, and the one who remains and is besieged shall die by the famine; and I will spend my wrath on them.
13 And you shall know that I am Yahweh, when their slain are among their idols around their altars, on every high hill, on all the tops of the mountains, and under every green tree, and under every thick oak, the place where they gave a pleasing aroma to all their idols.
14 And I will stretch out my hand on them, and make the land desolate and a waste, from the wilderness to Diblah, in all their dwelling places, and they shall know that I am Yahweh.

Ezekiel 7

1 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
2 And you, son of man, thus says the Lord Yahweh to the land of Israel: An end! The end has come on the four corners of the land.
3 Now the end is on you, and I will send my anger on you, and I will judge you according to your ways, and I will put on you all your abominations.
4 And my eye shall not spare you, and I will have no pity, for I will put your ways on you, and your abominations shall be in your midst, and you shall know that I am Yahweh.
5 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: An evil, one evil — see, it comes.
6 An end has come; the end has come; it has awakened against you; see, it comes.
7 The doom has come to you, inhabitant of the land; the time has come, the day of tumult is near, and not a shout of joy on the mountains.
8 Now, soon, I will pour out my wrath on you and spend my anger on you, and I will judge you according to your ways, and I will put on you all your abominations.
9 And my eye shall not spare, and I will have no pity; I will put on you according to your ways, and your abominations shall be in your midst, and you shall know that I, Yahweh, strike.
10 See the day; see, it comes; the doom has gone out; the rod has blossomed; pride has budded.
11 Violence has risen as a rod of wickedness; nothing shall remain of them, and nothing of their multitude, and nothing of their wealth, and no splendor among them.
12 The time has come; the day has arrived; let the buyer not rejoice, and let the seller not mourn, for wrath is on all its multitude.
13 For the seller shall not return to what is sold, though his life is still among the living, for the vision concerning all its multitude shall not turn back, and no one, in the guilt of his life, shall strengthen himself.
14 They have blown the trumpet and made everything ready, but no one goes to the battle, for my wrath is on all its multitude.
15 The sword is outside, and the pestilence and the famine inside; the one in the field shall die by the sword, and the one in the city, famine and pestilence shall devour him.
16 And those of them who escape shall escape, and shall be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them moaning, each in his guilt.
17 All hands shall hang limp, and all knees shall run with water.
18 And they shall gird on sackcloth, and trembling shall cover them, and shame shall be on all faces, and baldness on all their heads.
19 They shall throw their silver in the streets, and their gold shall become an unclean thing; their silver and their gold cannot deliver them on the day of the wrath of Yahweh; they shall not satisfy their soul, and they shall not fill their insides, for it has been the stumbling block of their guilt.
20 And the beauty of his ornament — he set it for pride, but they made in it the images of their abominations, their detestable things; therefore I have made it an unclean thing to them.
21 And I will give it into the hand of strangers as plunder, and to the wicked of the earth as spoil, and they shall profane it.
22 And I will turn my face from them, and they shall profane my treasured place, and robbers shall come into it and profane it.
23 Make the chain, for the land is full of the judgment of bloodshed, and the city is full of violence.
24 And I will bring the worst of the nations, and they shall possess their houses, and I will make the pride of the strong cease, and their holy places shall be profaned.
25 Anguish comes, and they shall seek peace, and there is none.
26 Disaster upon disaster shall come, and report upon report shall be, and they shall seek a vision from a prophet, and teaching shall perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders.
27 The king shall mourn, and the prince shall be clothed with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land shall tremble; I will do to them according to their way, and I will judge them by their judgments, and they shall know that I am Yahweh.

Ezekiel 8

1 And in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day of the month, as I sat in my house and the elders of Judah sat before me, the hand of the Lord Yahweh fell there on me.
2 And I looked, and see, a likeness like the appearance of fire: from the appearance of his waist downward, fire, and from his waist upward, like the appearance of brightness, like the gleam of glowing metal.
3 And he stretched out the form of a hand, and took me by a lock of my head, and a spirit lifted me up between the earth and the heaven, and brought me in visions of God to Jerusalem, to the entrance of the inner gate that faces north, where was the seat of the image of jealousy, which provokes jealousy.
4 And see, the glory of the God of Israel was there, like the appearance that I saw in the plain.
5 And he said to me: Son of man, lift your eyes now toward the north. And I lifted my eyes toward the north, and see, north of the gate of the altar was this image of jealousy, at the entrance.
6 And he said to me: Son of man, do you see what they are doing — the great abominations that the house of Israel are doing here, to drive me far from my sanctuary? But you shall see still greater abominations.
7 And he brought me to the entrance of the court, and I looked, and see, a hole in the wall.
8 And he said to me: Son of man, dig in the wall. And I dug in the wall, and see, an entrance.
9 And he said to me: Go in, and see the evil abominations that they are doing here.
10 And I went in and saw, and see, every form of creeping thing and detestable beast, and all the idols of the house of Israel, engraved on the wall all around.
11 And seventy men of the elders of the house of Israel stood before them, and Jaazaniah the son of Shaphan stood in their midst, standing before them, each with his censer in his hand, and the fragrance of the cloud of incense went up.
12 And he said to me: Have you seen, son of man, what the elders of the house of Israel do in the dark, each in his chambers of imagery? For they say: Yahweh does not see us; Yahweh has forsaken the land.
13 And he said to me: You shall see still greater abominations that they are doing.
14 And he brought me to the entrance of the gate of the house of Yahweh that faces north, and see, there the women sat, weeping for Tammuz.
15 And he said to me: Have you seen, son of man? You shall see still greater abominations than these.
16 And he brought me to the inner court of the house of Yahweh, and see, at the entrance of the temple of Yahweh, between the porch and the altar, were about twenty-five men, their backs to the temple of Yahweh and their faces to the east, and they were bowing down eastward to the sun.
17 And he said to me: Have you seen, son of man? Is it too light a thing for the house of Judah to do the abominations that they do here, that they have filled the land with violence and have turned to provoke me to anger? And see, they send the branch to their nose.
18 And I also will act in wrath; my eye shall not spare, and I will have no pity; and though they cry in my ears with a loud voice, I will not hear them.

Ezekiel 9

1 Then he cried in my ears with a loud voice, saying: Draw near, you officers of the city, each man with his destroying weapon in his hand.
2 See, six men came from the way of the upper gate, which lies toward the north, every man with his slaughter weapon in his hand. One man in the middle of them was clothed in linen, with a writer's inkhorn by his side. They went in, and stood beside the bronze altar.
3 The glory of the God of Israel went up from the cherub, whereupon it was, to the threshold of the house; and he called to the man clothed in linen, who had the writer's inkhorn by his side.
4 Yahweh said to him: Go through the middle of the city, through the middle of Jerusalem, and set a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and groan over all the detestable things that are done within it.
5 To the others he said in my hearing: Go through the city after him, and strike. Do not let your eye spare, and do not have pity:
6 kill old man, young man and virgin, little children and women, utterly, for destruction; but do not come near any man on whom is the mark. Begin at my sanctuary. Then they began at the old men who were before the house.
7 He said to them: Defile the house, and fill the courts with the slain. Go out! They went out, and struck in the city.
8 While they were killing, and I was left, I fell on my face, and cried, and said: Ah Lord Yahweh! Will you destroy all the residue of Israel in your pouring out of your wrath on Jerusalem?

16 When the cherubim went, the wheels went beside them; and when the cherubim lifted up their wings to mount up from the earth, the wheels also didn't turn from beside them.

18 Yahweh's glory went out from over the threshold of the house and stood over the cherubim.

20 This is the living creature that I saw under the God of Israel by the river Chebar; and I knew that they were cherubim.
21 Every one had four faces, and every one four wings. The likeness of the hands of a man was under their wings.
22 As for the likeness of their faces, they were the faces which I saw by the river Chebar, their appearances and themselves. They each went straight forward.

9 Son of man, hasn't the house of Israel, the rebellious house, said to you: What are you doing?

2 Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who prophesy, and say to those who prophesy out of their own heart, Hear Yahweh's word:
3 The Lord Yahweh says: Woe to the foolish prophets, who follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing!
4 Israel, your prophets have been like foxes in the waste places.
5 You have not gone up into the gaps or built up the wall for the house of Israel, to stand in the battle in Yahweh's day.
6 They have seen falsehood and lying divination, who say: The declaration of Yahweh, but Yahweh has not sent them. They have made men hope that the word would be confirmed.
7 Have you not seen a false vision, and have you not spoken a lying divination, in that you say: The declaration of Yahweh, but I have not spoken?
8 Therefore the Lord Yahweh says: Because you have spoken falsehood and seen lies, therefore, see, I am against you, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
9 My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and who utter lying divinations. They will not be in the council of my people, neither will they be written in the writing of the house of Israel, neither will they enter into the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the Lord Yahweh.
10 Because, even because they have seduced my people, saying, Peace; and there is no peace. When one builds up a wall, see, they plaster it with whitewash.

12 See, when the wall has fallen, won't it be said to you, Where is the plaster with which you have plastered it?
13 Therefore the Lord Yahweh says: I will even tear it with a stormy wind in my wrath. There will be an overflowing shower in my anger, and great hailstones in wrath to consume it.

15 Thus I will accomplish my wrath on the wall, and on those who have plastered it with whitewash. I will tell you, The wall is no more, nor those who plastered it—
16 the prophets of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem, and who see visions of peace for her, and there is no peace, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.

18 and say: The Lord Yahweh says: Woe to the women who sew cushions on all elbows and make veils for the head of persons of every stature to hunt souls! Will you hunt the souls of my people and save souls alive for yourselves?
19 You have profaned me among my people for handfuls of barley and for pieces of bread, to kill the souls who should not die and to save the souls alive who should not live, by your lying to my people who listen to lies.
20 Therefore the Lord Yahweh says: See, I am against your cushions, with which you hunt the souls to make them fly, and I will tear them from your arms. I will let the souls fly free, even the souls whom you ensnare like birds.
21 I will also tear your veils and deliver my people out of your hand; and they will no longer be in your hand to be ensnared. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.
22 Because with lies you have grieved the heart of the righteous, whom I have not made sad; and strengthened the hands of the wicked, that he should not return from his wicked way, and be saved alive.
23 Therefore you will no more see false visions nor practice divination. I will deliver my people out of your hand. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

13 and he took one of the royal seed and cut a covenant with him and brought him under an oath, and the mighty men of the land he took away,
14 that it might be a lowly kingdom, not lifting itself up, keeping his covenant so that it might stand.
15 But he rebelled against him by sending his messengers to Egypt, to give him horses and many people. Will he prosper? Will the one who does these things escape? He broke the covenant — will he escape?

24 And all the trees of the field will know that I, Yahweh, have brought down the high tree, have raised up the low tree, have dried up the green tree and made the dry tree flourish. I, Yahweh, have spoken and have done it.

Ezekiel 18

1 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
2 What is it with you, that you use this proverb on the land of Israel, saying: The fathers eat sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge?
3 As I live — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh — you will no longer have this proverb to use in Israel.
4 See, all lives are mine; as the life of the father, so also the life of the son is mine. The life that sins — it will die.
5 But if a man is righteous and does justice and righteousness,
6 and has not eaten on the mountains, and has not lifted his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, and has not defiled his neighbor's wife, and has not come near a woman in her impurity,
7 and has wronged no one, has returned the pledge to the debtor, has taken nothing by robbery, has given his bread to the hungry and covered the naked with a garment,
8 has not lent at interest and has not taken increase, has turned his hand back from iniquity, has done true justice between man and man,
9 has walked in my statutes and kept my ordinances, to do truth — he is righteous; he will surely live — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
10 And if he fathers a son who is violent, a shedder of blood, and does any one of these things,
11 while he himself did none of these things — for he has even eaten on the mountains and defiled his neighbor's wife,
12 has wronged the poor and needy, has committed robberies, has not returned the pledge, has lifted his eyes to the idols, has done an abomination,
13 has lent at interest and taken increase — will he live? He will not live. He has done all these abominations; he will surely be put to death; his blood will be on himself.
14 And see, if he fathers a son who sees all his father's sins that he did, and takes heed and does not do the like,
15 who has not eaten on the mountains, and has not lifted his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, has not defiled his neighbor's wife,
16 has wronged no one, has not taken a pledge, has not committed robbery, has given his bread to the hungry and covered the naked with a garment,
17 has turned his hand back from the poor, has not taken interest or increase, has done my ordinances, has walked in my statutes — he will not die for his father's iniquity; he will surely live.
18 His father — because he practiced oppression, robbed his brother of what he stole, and did what is not good among his people — see, he dies for his iniquity.
19 Yet you say: Why does the son not bear the father's iniquity? When the son has done justice and righteousness, has kept all my statutes and done them, he will surely live.
20 The life that sins — it will die. A son will not bear a father's iniquity, and a father will not bear a son's iniquity. The righteousness of the righteous will be on him, and the wickedness of the wicked will be on him.
21 But if the wicked man turns from all his sins that he committed, and keeps all my statutes and does justice and righteousness, he will surely live; he will not die.
22 All his transgressions that he committed will not be remembered against him; in his righteousness that he has done, he will live.
23 Have I any pleasure in the death of the wicked — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh — and not rather in his turning from his ways and living?
24 But when a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does iniquity — does according to all the abominations that the wicked man does — will he live? All his righteous deeds that he did will not be remembered; in his treachery that he committed and in his sin that he sinned, in them he will die.
25 Yet you say: The way of the Lord is not right. Hear now, house of Israel: Is my way not right? Is it not your ways that are not right?
26 When a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does iniquity and dies in them, for his iniquity that he did he will die.
27 And when a wicked man turns from his wickedness that he committed and does justice and righteousness, he will keep his own life alive.
28 And he sees and turns back from all his transgressions that he committed; he will surely live; he will not die.
29 Yet the house of Israel says: The way of the Lord is not right. House of Israel, are my ways not right? Is it not your ways that are not right?
30 Therefore I will judge you, house of Israel, each man according to his ways — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh. Turn back, and turn yourselves from all your transgressions, and iniquity will not be a stumbling block to you.
31 Cast away from you all your transgressions in which you have transgressed, and make yourselves a new heart and a new spirit; for why should you die, house of Israel?
32 For I have no pleasure in the death of the one who dies — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh. So turn back, and live.

4 And the nations heard about him; he was caught in their pit, and they brought him with hooks to the land of Egypt.
5 And she saw that she had waited in vain, her hope was lost; and she took another of her cubs and made him a young lion.
6 And he walked about in the midst of the lions; he became a young lion, and he learned to tear prey; he devoured men.
7 And he knew their widows and laid waste their cities, and the land and its fullness were desolate at the sound of his roaring.
8 And the nations set themselves against him round about from the provinces, and spread their net over him; he was caught in their pit.
9 And they put him in a cage with hooks and brought him to the king of Babylon; they brought him into strongholds, so that his voice would no longer be heard on the mountains of Israel.
10 Your mother was like a vine in your blood, planted by the waters; she was fruitful and full of branches from the many waters.
11 And she had strong rods for scepters of rulers, and her height rose up among the thick foliage, and she was seen in her height, in the multitude of her branches.
12 But she was uprooted in fury, thrown down to the ground, and the east wind dried up her fruit; her strong rods were broken off and dried up; fire consumed her.
13 And now she is planted in the wilderness, in a land dry and thirsty.
14 And fire went out from the rod of her branches and devoured her fruit, and there was no strong rod in her, no scepter for ruling. This is a lamentation, and it has become a lamentation.

Ezekiel 20

1 And it happened in the seventh year, in the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month, that men from the elders of Israel came to inquire of Yahweh and sat before me.
2 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
3 Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel and say to them: Thus says the Lord Yahweh: Have you come to inquire of me? As I live — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh — I will not be inquired of by you.
4 Will you judge them, son of man, will you judge them? Make known to them the abominations of their fathers,
5 and say to them: Thus says the Lord Yahweh: On the day I chose Israel, I lifted up my hand to the seed of the house of Jacob and made myself known to them in the land of Egypt, when I lifted up my hand to them, saying: I am Yahweh your God.
6 On that day I lifted up my hand to them, to bring them out of the land of Egypt into a land that I had searched out for them, flowing with milk and honey, which is the glory of all lands,
7 and I said to them: Each man cast away the detestable things of his eyes, and do not defile yourselves with the idols of Egypt. I am Yahweh your God.
8 But they rebelled against me and were not willing to listen to me; they did not cast away, each man, the detestable things of their eyes, and they did not forsake the idols of Egypt. And I said I would pour out my fury on them, to spend my anger on them in the midst of the land of Egypt.
9 But I acted for my name's sake, that it should not be profaned in the eyes of the nations among whom they were, in whose eyes I made myself known to them, to bring them out of the land of Egypt.
10 And I brought them out of the land of Egypt and brought them into the wilderness.
11 And I gave them my statutes and made known to them my ordinances, which a man who does them will live by them.
12 And also my Sabbaths I gave them, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am Yahweh who sanctifies them.
13 But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness; they did not walk in my statutes, and my ordinances they rejected, which a man who does them will live by them; and my Sabbaths they greatly profaned. And I said I would pour out my fury on them in the wilderness, to make an end of them.
14 But I acted for my name's sake, that it should not be profaned in the eyes of the nations, in whose eyes I brought them out.
15 And I also lifted up my hand to them in the wilderness, that I would not bring them into the land that I had given, flowing with milk and honey, which is the glory of all lands,
16 because they rejected my ordinances and did not walk in my statutes and profaned my Sabbaths, for their heart went after their idols.
17 And my eye spared them, so as not to destroy them, and I did not make a full end of them in the wilderness.
18 And I said to their children in the wilderness: Do not walk in the statutes of your fathers, and do not keep their ordinances, and do not defile yourselves with their idols.
19 I am Yahweh your God; walk in my statutes and keep my ordinances and do them,
20 and make my Sabbaths holy, and they will be a sign between me and you, that you may know that I am Yahweh your God.
21 But the children rebelled against me; they did not walk in my statutes, and my ordinances they did not keep, to do them, which a man who does them will live by them; they profaned my Sabbaths. And I said I would pour out my fury on them, to spend my anger on them in the wilderness.
22 But I turned back my hand and acted for my name's sake, that it should not be profaned in the eyes of the nations, in whose eyes I brought them out.
23 I also lifted up my hand to them in the wilderness, to scatter them among the nations and disperse them through the lands,
24 because they did not do my ordinances and rejected my statutes and profaned my Sabbaths, and their eyes were after their fathers' idols.
25 And I myself also gave them statutes that were not good, and ordinances by which they could not live,
26 and I defiled them through their own gifts, when they made every one that opens the womb pass over, so that I might lay them waste, so that they might know that I am Yahweh.
27 Therefore, son of man, speak to the house of Israel and say to them: Thus says the Lord Yahweh: In this too your fathers blasphemed me, in that they committed treachery against me.
28 And I brought them into the land that I lifted up my hand to give them, and they saw every high hill and every thick tree, and they sacrificed there their sacrifices, and they gave there the provocation of their offering, and they set there the scent of their soothing aromas, and they poured out there their drink offerings.
29 And I said to them: What is the high place to which you go? And its name is called Bamah to this day.
30 Therefore say to the house of Israel: Thus says the Lord Yahweh: Do you defile yourselves in the way of your fathers, and do you whore after their detestable things,
31 and in lifting up your gifts, in making your sons pass through the fire, do you defile yourselves with all your idols to this day? And shall I be inquired of by you, house of Israel? As I live — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh — I will not be inquired of by you,
32 and what comes up in your mind will certainly not be, in that you say: We will be like the nations, like the families of the lands, to serve wood and stone.
33 As I live — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh — surely with a strong hand and an outstretched arm and fury poured out I will be king over you,
34 and I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands in which you are scattered, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm and fury poured out,
35 and I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there I will enter into judgment with you face to face.
36 As I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will enter into judgment with you — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
37 And I will make you pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant,
38 and I will purge out from you the rebels and those who transgress against me; from the land of their sojourning I will bring them out, but into the land of Israel they will not come; and you will know that I am Yahweh.
39 And you, house of Israel — thus says the Lord Yahweh: Go, each man serve his idols, and afterward too, if you will not listen to me; but my holy name you will no longer profane with your gifts and with your idols.
40 For on my holy mountain, on the mountain of the height of Israel — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh — there all the house of Israel, all of it, will serve me in the land; there I will accept them, and there I will seek your contributions and the firstfruits of your gifts, with all your holy things.
41 As a soothing aroma I will accept you, when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands in which you have been scattered; and I will be sanctified in you in the eyes of the nations.
42 And you will know that I am Yahweh, when I bring you into the land of Israel, into the land that I lifted up my hand to give to your fathers.
43 And there you will remember your ways and all your deeds in which you defiled yourselves, and you will loathe yourselves in your own sight for all your evils that you did.
44 And you will know that I am Yahweh, when I deal with you for my name's sake, not according to your evil ways and not according to your corrupt deeds, house of Israel — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.

Ezekiel 21

1 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
2 Son of man, set your face toward the south, and preach toward the south, and prophesy against the forest of the field, the Negev.
3 And say to the forest of the Negev: Hear the word of Yahweh. Thus says the Lord Yahweh: See, I am kindling a fire in you, and it will devour in you every green tree and every dry tree; the blazing flame will not be quenched, and all faces from the Negev to the north will be scorched by it.
4 And all flesh will see that I, Yahweh, have kindled it; it will not be quenched.
5 And I said: Ah, Lord Yahweh! They are saying of me: Is he not a maker of parables?
6 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
7 Son of man, set your face toward Jerusalem, and preach toward the sanctuaries, and prophesy against the land of Israel,
8 and say to the land of Israel: Thus says Yahweh: See, I am against you, and I will draw my sword from its sheath and cut off from you righteous and wicked.
9 Because I will cut off from you righteous and wicked, therefore my sword will go out from its sheath against all flesh from the south to the north,
10 and all flesh will know that I, Yahweh, have drawn my sword from its sheath; it will not return again.
11 And you, son of man, groan; with breaking of loins and with bitterness groan before their eyes.
12 And it will be, when they say to you: Why are you groaning? — that you will say: Because of the report, for it is coming; and every heart will melt, and all hands will go limp, and every spirit will faint, and all knees will run with water. See, it is coming, and it will happen — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
13 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
14 Son of man, prophesy and say: Thus says the Lord: Say: A sword, a sword — it is sharpened, and also polished,
15 sharpened in order to slaughter a slaughter, polished that it may have a flash of lightning. Or shall we rejoice? The rod of my son despises every tree.
16 And he gave it to be polished, to be grasped in the hand; it is sharpened — the sword — and it is polished, to give it into the hand of the slayer.
17 Cry out and wail, son of man, for it is against my people, it is against all the princes of Israel; they are thrown down to the sword with my people; therefore strike your thigh.
18 For it is a testing; and what if even the despising rod will be no more? — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
19 And you, son of man, prophesy and strike hand against hand, and let the sword be doubled a third time — the sword of the slain; it is the sword of the great slain one, which pierces into them.
20 That the heart may melt and stumblings multiply, I have set the flash of the sword against all their gates. Ah! It is made for lightning, wrapped for slaughter.
21 Gather yourself together, go to the right; set yourself, go to the left — wherever your face is appointed.
22 And I also will strike my hand against my hand, and I will lay my fury to rest. I, Yahweh, have spoken.
23 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
24 And you, son of man, set for yourself two ways for the sword of the king of Babylon to come; from one land both of them will come out; and carve a hand-sign; carve it at the head of the way to the city.
25 Set a way for the sword to come to Rabbah of the children of Ammon, and to Judah in Jerusalem the fortified.
26 For the king of Babylon stands at the mother of the way, at the head of the two ways, to divine a divination; he shakes the arrows, he asks the teraphim, he looks in the liver.
27 In his right hand was the divination for Jerusalem: to set battering rams, to open the mouth in slaughter, to lift the voice in a war shout, to set battering rams against the gates, to cast up a ramp, to build a siege wall.
28 And to them it will be like a false divination in their eyes — they have sworn oaths upon oaths — but he brings iniquity to remembrance, that they may be caught.
29 Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: Because you have brought your iniquity to remembrance, your transgressions being uncovered so that your sins appear in all your deeds; because you are remembered, you will be caught in the hand.
30 And you, slain wicked one, prince of Israel, whose day has come in the time of the iniquity of the end,
31 thus says the Lord Yahweh: Remove the turban and take off the crown; this shall not be this; what is low will be raised, and what is high will be brought low.
32 A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it; this too will not be, until he comes whose is the judgment, and I will give it to him.
33 And you, son of man, prophesy and say: Thus says the Lord Yahweh to the children of Ammon and to their taunting: And say: A sword, a sword, drawn for slaughter, polished to consume, that it may flash like lightning,
34 while they see emptiness for you, while they divine lies for you, to set you on the necks of the slain of the wicked, whose day has come in the time of the iniquity of the end.
35 Return it to its sheath! In the place where you were created, in the land of your origin, I will judge you.
36 And I will pour out my indignation on you; with the fire of my wrath I will blow on you, and I will give you into the hand of brutal men, craftsmen of destruction.
37 You will be fuel for the fire; your blood will be in the midst of the land; you will not be remembered, for I, Yahweh, have spoken.

31 And I poured out my indignation on them; with the fire of my wrath I made an end of them; their way I put on their head — the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.

Ezekiel 23

1 And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying:
2 Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother,
3 and they played the whore in Egypt; in their youth they played the whore. There their breasts were pressed, and there the nipples of their virginity were fondled.
4 And their names were Oholah the elder and Oholibah her sister; and they became mine, and they bore sons and daughters. And their names: Samaria is Oholah, and Jerusalem Oholibah.
5 And Oholah played the whore while she was under me, and she lusted after her lovers, after Assyria, her neighbors,
6 clothed in blue, governors and deputies, desirable young men all of them, horsemen riding horses.
7 And she gave her whorings to them, the choice men of Assyria, all of them; and with all those she lusted after, with all their idols, she defiled herself.
8 And her whorings from Egypt she did not leave, for with her they lay in her youth, and they fondled the nipples of her virginity, and poured out their whoring on her.
9 Therefore I gave her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the sons of Assyria, after whom she lusted.
10 They uncovered her nakedness; her sons and her daughters they took, and her they killed with the sword; and she became a name among women, and judgments they executed on her.
11 And her sister Oholibah saw, and she corrupted her lusting beyond hers, and her whorings beyond the whorings of her sister.
12 After the sons of Assyria she lusted — governors and deputies, her neighbors, clothed in splendor, horsemen riding horses, desirable young men all of them.
13 And I saw that she was defiled; one way belonged to both of them.
14 And she added to her whorings, and she saw men carved on the wall, images of the Chaldeans carved with red pigment,
15 girded with belts on their waists, with flowing turbans on their heads, all of them in the appearance of officers, the likeness of the sons of Babylon, Chaldeans, the land of their birth.
16 And she lusted after them at the sight of her eyes, and she sent messengers to them to Chaldea.
17 And the sons of Babylon came to her, to the bed of love, and defiled her with their whoring; and she was defiled by them, and her soul tore itself away from them.
18 And she uncovered her whorings and uncovered her nakedness, and my soul tore itself away from her, as my soul tore itself away from her sister.
19 And she multiplied her whorings, remembering the days of her youth, when she played the whore in the land of Egypt,
20 and she lusted after their paramours, whose flesh is the flesh of donkeys and whose discharge is the discharge of horses.
21 And you revisited the lewdness of your youth, when those from Egypt fondled your nipples for the sake of the breasts of your youth.
22 Therefore, Oholibah, thus says the Lord Yahweh: See, I am rousing your lovers against you, those from whom your soul tore itself away, and I will bring them against you from round about:
23 the sons of Babylon and all the Chaldeans, Pekod and Shoa and Koa, all the sons of Assyria with them, desirable young men, governors and deputies all of them, officers and men of renown, all of them riding horses.
24 And they will come against you with weapons, chariots and wagons, and with a company of peoples; with large shield and shield and helmet they will set themselves against you round about; and I will give judgment before them, and they will judge you by their judgments.
25 And I will set my jealousy against you, and they will deal with you in fury; your nose and your ears they will remove, and your remnant will fall by the sword; they will take your sons and your daughters, and your remnant will be devoured by fire.
26 And they will strip you of your clothes and take the articles of your splendor.
27 And I will make your lewdness cease from you, and your whoring from the land of Egypt, and you will not lift your eyes to them, and Egypt you will remember no more.
28 For thus says the Lord Yahweh: See, I am giving you into the hand of those you hate, into the hand of those from whom your soul tore itself away,
29 and they will deal with you in hatred and take all your labor and leave you naked and bare, and the nakedness of your whorings will be uncovered — your lewdness and your whorings.
30 These things will be done to you, because you whored after the nations, because you were defiled with their idols.
31 In the way of your sister you walked, and I will give her cup into your hand.
32 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: The cup of your sister you will drink, the deep and the wide one; you will be for laughter and for mockery; it holds much.
33 With drunkenness and grief you will be filled — the cup of horror and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria.

2 Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt, and to his multitude: To whom are you like in your greatness?

4 The waters made it grow; the deep made it high, with its rivers going around its planting, and it sent out its channels to all the trees of the field.
5 Therefore its height was greater than all the trees of the field, and its boughs multiplied, and its branches grew long, from the abundance of waters, in its sending forth.
6 In its boughs all the birds of the sky nested, and under its branches all the beasts of the field gave birth, and in its shade all great nations dwelt.
7 And it was beautiful in its greatness, in the length of its branches, for its root was by abundant waters.
8 Cedars in the garden of God did not equal it; fir trees were not like its boughs, and plane trees were not like its branches; no tree in the garden of God was like it in its beauty.
9 I made it beautiful with the abundance of its branches, and all the trees of Eden that were in the garden of God envied it.
10 Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: Because it grew high in stature, and set its top among the thick boughs, and its heart was lifted up in its height,
11 I will give it into the hand of the mighty one of the nations; he will surely deal with it; for its wickedness I have driven it out.
12 And strangers, the ruthless of the nations, cut it off and left it; on the mountains and in all the valleys its branches fell, and its boughs were broken in all the watercourses of the land; and all the peoples of the earth went down from its shade and left it.

Ezekiel 33

1 Yahweh's word came to me, saying,
2 Son of man, speak to the children of your people, and tell them, When I bring the sword on a land, and the people of the land take a man from among them, and set him for their watchman,
3 if, when he sees the sword come on the land, he blows the trumpet and warns the people,
4 then whoever hears the sound of the trumpet and doesn't heed the warning, if the sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be on his own head.
5 He heard the sound of the trumpet and didn't take warning. His blood will be on him; whereas if he had heeded the warning, he would have delivered his soul.
6 But if the watchman sees the sword come and doesn't blow the trumpet, and the people aren't warned, and the sword comes and takes any person from among them, he is taken away in his sin, but his blood I will require at the watchman's hand.
7 So you, son of man, I have set you a watchman to the house of Israel. Therefore hear the word from my mouth, and give them warnings from me.
8 When I tell the wicked, O wicked man, you will surely die, and you don't speak to warn the wicked from his way, that wicked man will die in his sin, but I will require his blood at your hand.
9 Nevertheless, if you warn the wicked of his way to turn from it, and he doesn't turn from his way; he will die in his sin, but you have delivered your soul.
10 You, son of man, tell the house of Israel: You say this, Our transgressions and our sins are on us, and we pine away in them. How then can we live?
11 Tell them: As I live, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh, I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but that the wicked turn from his way and live. Turn, turn from your evil ways! For why will you die, house of Israel?
12 You, son of man, tell the children of your people, The righteousness of the righteous will not deliver him in the day of his disobedience. And as for the wickedness of the wicked, he will not fall by it in the day that he turns from his wickedness; neither will he who is righteous be able to live by it in the day that he sins.
13 When I tell the righteous that he will surely live, if he trusts in his righteousness and commits sin, none of his righteous deeds will be remembered; but he will die in his sin that he has committed.
14 Again, when I say to the wicked, You will surely die, if he turns from his sin and does that which is lawful and right,
15 if the wicked restore the pledge, give again that which he had taken by robbery, walk in the statutes of life, committing no sin, he will surely live. He will not die.
16 None of his sins that he has committed will be remembered against him. He has done that which is lawful and right. He will surely live.
17 Yet the children of your people say, The way of the Lord is not fair; but as for them, their way is not fair.
18 When the righteous turns from his righteousness and commits sin, he will die in them.
19 When the wicked turns from his wickedness and does that which is lawful and right, he will live by it.
20 Yet you say, The way of the Lord is not fair. House of Israel, I will judge every one of you after his ways.
21 And it happened in the twelfth year of our captivity, in the tenth month, on the fifth day of the month, that a fugitive from Jerusalem came to me, saying: The city has been struck.
22 Now Yahweh's hand had been on me in the evening, before he who had escaped came; and he had opened my mouth until he came to me in the morning; and my mouth was opened, and I was no longer mute.
23 Yahweh's word came to me, saying,
24 Son of man, those who inhabit the waste places in the land of Israel speak, saying, Abraham was one, and he inherited the land; but we are many. The land is given us for inheritance.
25 Therefore tell them, The Lord Yahweh says: You eat with the blood, and lift up your eyes to your idols, and shed blood. And should you possess the land?
26 You stand on your sword, you work a detestable thing, and every one of you defiles his neighbor's wife. So should you possess the land?
27 Tell them this, The Lord Yahweh says: As I live, surely those who are in the waste places will fall by the sword, and whoever is in the open field I will give to the wild animals to be devoured, and those who are in the strongholds and in the caves will die of the pestilence.
28 I will make the land a desolation and an astonishment. The pride of her power will cease. The mountains of Israel will be desolate, so that no one will pass through.
29 Then they will know that I am Yahweh, when I have made the land a desolation and an astonishment because of all their detestable things which they have committed.
30 As for you, son of man, the children of your people talk about you by the walls and in the doors of the houses, and speak to one another, everyone to his brother, saying, Please come and hear what the word is that comes out from Yahweh.
31 They come to you as the people come, and they sit before you as my people, and they hear your words, but don't do them; for with their mouth they show much love, but their heart goes after their gain.
32 And see, you are to them as a love song, one who has a pleasant voice and plays well on an instrument; for they hear your words, but they do not do them.
33 When this comes to pass—see, it comes—then they will know that a prophet has been among them.

Ezekiel 34

1 Yahweh's word came to me, saying,
2 Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy, and tell them, even the shepherds, The Lord Yahweh says: Woe to the shepherds of Israel who feed themselves! Shouldn't the shepherds feed the sheep?
3 You eat the fat. You clothe yourself with the wool. You kill the fatlings, but you don't feed the sheep.
4 The weak you have not strengthened, and the sick you have not healed, and the broken you have not bound up, and the strayed you have not brought back, and the lost you have not sought; but with force and with harshness you have ruled them.
5 They were scattered, because there was no shepherd. They became food to all the animals of the field, and were scattered.
6 My sheep wandered through all the mountains and on every high hill. Yes, my sheep were scattered on all the surface of the earth. There was no one who searched or sought.
7 Therefore, you shepherds, hear Yahweh's word:
8 As I live, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh, surely because my sheep became a prey, and my sheep became food to all the animals of the field, because there was no shepherd, and my shepherds didn't search for my sheep, but the shepherds fed themselves, and didn't feed my sheep,
9 therefore, you shepherds, hear Yahweh's word!
10 The Lord Yahweh says: See, I am against the shepherds. I will require my sheep at their hand, and cause them to cease from feeding the sheep. The shepherds won't feed themselves any more. I will deliver my sheep from their mouth, that they may not be food for them.
11 For the Lord Yahweh says: See, I myself, even I, will search for my sheep, and will seek them out.
12 As a shepherd seeks out his flock in the day that he is among his sheep that are scattered abroad, so I will seek out my sheep. I will deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day.
13 I will bring them out from the peoples, and gather them from the countries, and will bring them into their own land. I will feed them on the mountains of Israel, by the watercourses, and in all the inhabited places of the country.
14 I will feed them with good pasture, and their fold will be on the mountains of the height of Israel. There they will lie down in a good fold. They will feed on rich pasture on the mountains of Israel.
15 I myself will shepherd my sheep, and I myself will make them lie down, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
16 I will seek that which was lost, and will bring back that which was driven away, and will bind up that which was broken, and will strengthen that which was sick; but I will destroy the fat and the strong. I will feed them in justice.
17 As for you, O my flock, the Lord Yahweh says: See, I judge between sheep and sheep, the rams and the male goats.
18 Does it seem a small thing to you to have fed on the good pasture, but you must tread down with your feet the residue of your pasture? And to have drunk of the clear waters, but must you foul the residue with your feet?
19 As for my sheep, they eat that which you have trodden with your feet, and they drink that which you have fouled with your feet.
20 Therefore the Lord Yahweh says to them: See, I, even I, will judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep.
21 Because you thrust with side and with shoulder, and push all the diseased with your horns, until you have scattered them abroad,
22 therefore I will save my flock, and they will no more be a prey. I will judge between sheep and sheep.
23 I will set up one shepherd over them, and he will feed them, even my servant David. He will feed them, and he will be their shepherd.
24 I, Yahweh, will be their God, and my servant David prince among them. I, Yahweh, have spoken it.
25 I will make with them a covenant of peace, and will cause evil animals to cease out of the land. They will dwell securely in the wilderness and sleep in the woods.
26 I will make them and the places around my hill a blessing. I will cause the shower to come down in its season. There will be showers of blessing.
27 The tree of the field will yield its fruit, and the earth will yield its increase, and they will be secure in their land. Then they will know that I am Yahweh, when I have broken the bars of their yoke, and have delivered them out of the hand of those who made slaves of them.
28 They will no more be a prey to the nations, neither will the animals of the earth devour them; but they will dwell securely, and no one will make them afraid.
29 And I will raise up for them a planting of renown, and they will no longer be swept away by famine in the land, and they will bear the disgrace of the nations no longer.
30 They will know that I, Yahweh their God, am with them, and that they, the house of Israel, are my people, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
31 And you are my sheep, the sheep of my pasture; you are men, and I am your God, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.

14 therefore you will devour men no more, and you will bereave your nation no more, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.

4 It is a holy portion of the land; it shall be for the priests, the ministers of the sanctuary, who draw near to minister to Yahweh; and it shall be a place for their houses, and a holy place for the sanctuary.
5 And twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide shall be for the Levites, the ministers of the house, as a possession for themselves, for twenty rooms.
6 And you shall appoint the possession of the city five thousand wide and twenty-five thousand long, alongside the holy contribution; it shall be for the whole house of Israel.
7 And for the prince, on one side and on the other of the holy contribution and of the possession of the city, in front of the holy contribution and in front of the possession of the city, on the west side westward and on the east side eastward; and its length shall correspond to one of the portions, from the west border to the east border,
8 in the land; it shall be his for a possession in Israel; and my princes shall no more oppress my people, but they shall give the land to the house of Israel according to their tribes.
9 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: Enough for you, princes of Israel! Remove violence and plunder, and do justice and righteousness; lift your expulsions off my people, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
10 You shall have just balances, and a just ephah, and a just bath.
11 The ephah and the bath shall be of one measure, the bath holding a tenth of a homer, and the ephah a tenth of a homer; its measure shall be according to the homer.
12 And the shekel shall be twenty gerahs: twenty shekels, twenty-five shekels, fifteen shekels — the mina shall be yours.
13 This is the contribution that you shall set apart: a sixth of an ephah from a homer of wheat, and you shall give a sixth of an ephah from a homer of barley;
14 and the prescribed portion of oil, the bath of oil: a tenth of a bath from a kor — ten baths, a homer, for ten baths are a homer;
15 and one sheep from the flock out of two hundred, from the well-watered pastures of Israel, for a grain offering and for a burnt offering and for peace offerings, to make atonement for them, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
16 All the people of the land shall be bound to this contribution for the prince in Israel.
17 And it shall be the prince's duty to give the burnt offerings and the grain offering and the drink offering, at the feasts and at the new moons and at the Sabbaths, at all the appointed times of the house of Israel; he shall prepare the sin offering and the grain offering and the burnt offering and the peace offerings, to make atonement for the house of Israel.
18 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: In the first month, on the first day of the month, you shall take a young bull without defect, and you shall cleanse the sanctuary.
19 And the priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering and put it on the doorposts of the house, and on the four corners of the ledge of the altar, and on the posts of the gate of the inner court.
20 And so you shall do on the seventh day of the month for anyone who errs and for the simple-minded; and you shall make atonement for the house.
21 In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, you shall have the Passover, a feast of seven days; unleavened bread shall be eaten.
22 And on that day the prince shall prepare, for himself and for all the people of the land, a bull as a sin offering.
23 And during the seven days of the feast he shall prepare a burnt offering to Yahweh: seven bulls and seven rams without defect, daily, for the seven days; and a male goat daily for a sin offering.
24 And he shall prepare as a grain offering an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram, and a hin of oil for an ephah.
25 In the seventh month, on the fifteenth day of the month, at the feast, he shall do the same for the seven days: the sin offering likewise, the burnt offering likewise, and the grain offering likewise, and the oil likewise.

Ezekiel 46

1 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: The gate of the inner court that faces east shall be shut during the six working days; but on the Sabbath day it shall be opened, and on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.
2 And the prince shall enter by way of the porch of the gate from outside, and shall stand by the post of the gate, and the priests shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he shall worship at the threshold of the gate; then he shall go out, and the gate shall not be shut until the evening.
3 And the people of the land shall worship at the entrance of that gate on the Sabbaths and at the new moons, before Yahweh.
4 And the burnt offering that the prince shall offer to Yahweh on the Sabbath day shall be six lambs without defect and a ram without defect;
5 and the grain offering shall be an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs a grain offering as his hand can give, and a hin of oil for an ephah.
6 And on the day of the new moon: a young bull without defect, and six lambs and a ram; they shall be without defect.
7 And he shall prepare a grain offering: an ephah for the bull and an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs as much as his hand can reach, and a hin of oil for an ephah.
8 When the prince enters, he shall go in by way of the porch of the gate, and by the same way he shall go out.
9 And when the people of the land come before Yahweh at the appointed times, the one who enters by way of the north gate to worship shall go out by way of the south gate, and the one who enters by way of the south gate shall go out by way of the north gate; he shall not return by way of the gate by which he came in, but shall go out straight ahead.
10 And the prince shall be among them: when they enter, he shall enter, and when they go out, they shall go out together.
11 And at the feasts and at the appointed times the grain offering shall be an ephah for a bull and an ephah for a ram, and for the lambs as his hand can give, and a hin of oil for an ephah.
12 And when the prince prepares a freewill offering — a burnt offering or peace offerings, a freewill offering to Yahweh — the gate that faces east shall be opened for him, and he shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings, as he does on the Sabbath day; then he shall go out, and the gate shall be shut after he has gone out.
13 And a lamb a year old without defect you shall prepare as a burnt offering daily to Yahweh; morning by morning you shall prepare it.
14 And you shall prepare a grain offering with it morning by morning: a sixth of an ephah, and a third of a hin of oil, to moisten the fine flour — a grain offering to Yahweh continually, by perpetual statutes.
15 And they shall prepare the lamb and the grain offering and the oil morning by morning, as a continual burnt offering.
16 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: If the prince gives a gift to one of his sons, it is his inheritance; it shall belong to his sons; it is their possession by inheritance.
17 But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it shall be his until the year of liberty, then it shall return to the prince; but his inheritance shall belong to his sons — theirs it shall be.
18 And the prince shall not take from the people's inheritance, to thrust them out of their possession; from his own possession he shall give inheritance to his sons, so that my people are not scattered, each man from his possession.
19 Then he brought me through the entrance that was at the side of the gate, into the holy rooms for the priests, which face north; and see, there was a place at the far end, westward.
20 And he said to me: This is the place where the priests shall boil the guilt offering and the sin offering, and where they shall bake the grain offering, so as not to bring them out into the outer court, to make the people holy.
21 Then he brought me out into the outer court and made me pass by the four corners of the court; and see, in every corner of the court there was a court.
22 In the four corners of the court there were enclosed courts, forty long and thirty wide; one measure for the four of them in the corners.
23 And there was a row of stone all around in them, around the four, and boiling places were made under the rows all around.
24 Then he said to me: These are the houses of the cooks, where the ministers of the house shall boil the people's sacrifice.

Ezekiel 47

1 He brought me back to the entrance of the house; and see, water was coming out from under the threshold of the house eastward, for the front of the house faced east; and the water was coming down from under, from the south side of the house, south of the altar.
2 Then he brought me out by way of the north gate and led me around by the outside way to the outer gate, by way of the gate that faces east; and see, water was trickling out from the south side.
3 When the man went out eastward with the line in his hand, he measured a thousand cubits and made me pass through the water — water to the ankles.
4 Again he measured a thousand and made me pass through the water — water to the knees. Again he measured a thousand and made me pass through — water to the waist.
5 Again he measured a thousand: a river that I could not pass through, for the water had risen — water to swim in, a river that could not be passed through.
6 And he said to me: Son of man, have you seen? Then he led me and brought me back to the bank of the river.
7 When I returned, see, on the bank of the river were very many trees on one side and on the other.
8 And he said to me: These waters go out toward the eastern region and go down into the Arabah, and they come to the sea; into the sea of the outpoured waters, and the waters are healed.
9 And it shall be that every living creature that swarms, everywhere the river comes, shall live; and there shall be very many fish, for these waters have come there and they are healed, and everything shall live wherever the river comes.
10 And it shall be that fishermen shall stand beside it, from En Gedi to En Eglaim; they shall be a place for spreading nets; their fish shall be according to their kinds, like the fish of the Great Sea, very many.
11 But its swamps and its marshes shall not be healed; they are given over to salt.
12 And by the river, on its bank, on one side and on the other, shall grow every tree for food, whose leaf does not wither and whose fruit does not fail; it bears new fruit every month, for its waters come out from the sanctuary; and its fruit shall be for food, and its leaf for healing.
13 Thus says the Lord Yahweh: This is the border by which you shall inherit the land, for the twelve tribes of Israel; Joseph shall have two portions.
14 And you shall inherit it, each man like his brother, for I lifted my hand to give it to your fathers; and this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.
15 And this is the border of the land: on the north side, from the Great Sea, by the way of Hethlon, to the entrance of Zedad;
16 Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which is between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath; Hazer Hatticon, which is at the border of Hauran.
17 And the border from the sea shall be Hazar Enon, the border of Damascus, and northward, northward, the border of Hamath. And this is the north side.
18 And the east side: from between Hauran and Damascus and Gilead and the land of Israel, the Jordan; from the border to the eastern sea you shall measure. And this is the east side.
19 And the south side, southward: from Tamar to the waters of Meriboth Kadesh, on to the brook, to the Great Sea. And this is the south side, southward.
20 And the west side: the Great Sea, from the border to opposite the entrance of Hamath. This is the west side.
21 So you shall divide this land for yourselves, for the tribes of Israel.
22 And it shall be that you shall allot it as an inheritance for yourselves and for the foreigners who sojourn among you, who have fathered children among you; and they shall be to you as the native-born among the children of Israel; with you they shall receive an inheritance among the tribes of Israel.
23 And it shall be that in whatever tribe the foreigner sojourns, there you shall give him his inheritance, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.

Ezekiel 48

1 Now these are the names of the tribes: from the north end, beside the way of Hethlon to the entrance of Hamath, Hazar Enan at the border of Damascus, northward beside Hamath — and his shall be the east side and the west side — Dan, one portion.
2 And beside the border of Dan, from the east side to the west side: Asher, one portion.
3 And beside the border of Asher, from the east side to the west side: Naphtali, one portion.
4 And beside the border of Naphtali, from the east side to the west side: Manasseh, one portion.
5 And beside the border of Manasseh, from the east side to the west side: Ephraim, one portion.
6 And beside the border of Ephraim, from the east side to the west side: Reuben, one portion.
7 And beside the border of Reuben, from the east side to the west side: Judah, one portion.
8 And beside the border of Judah, from the east side to the west side, shall be the contribution that you shall set apart: twenty-five thousand wide, and in length like one of the portions, from the east side to the west side; and the sanctuary shall be in its midst.
9 The contribution that you shall set apart to Yahweh shall be twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide.
10 And for these, for the priests, shall be the holy contribution: northward twenty-five thousand, and westward ten thousand wide, and eastward ten thousand wide, and southward twenty-five thousand long; and the sanctuary of Yahweh shall be in its midst.
11 It shall be for the priests, the sanctified ones from the sons of Zadok, who kept my charge, who did not go astray when the children of Israel went astray, as the Levites went astray.
12 And it shall be theirs as a special portion from the contribution of the land, most holy, beside the border of the Levites.
13 And the Levites, alongside the border of the priests, shall have twenty-five thousand long and ten thousand wide; the whole length twenty-five thousand, and the width ten thousand.
14 And they shall not sell any of it, nor exchange it, nor shall the first portion of the land pass away, for it is holy to Yahweh.
15 And the five thousand that remain in width, in front of the twenty-five thousand, shall be common land for the city, for dwelling places and for open land; and the city shall be in its midst.
16 And these are its measurements: the north side, four thousand five hundred; and the south side, four thousand five hundred; and from the east side, four thousand five hundred; and the west side, four thousand five hundred.
17 And the city shall have open land: northward two hundred fifty, and southward two hundred fifty, and eastward two hundred fifty, and westward two hundred fifty.
18 And what remains in length, alongside the holy contribution, shall be ten thousand eastward and ten thousand westward, and it shall be alongside the holy contribution; and its produce shall be food for the workers of the city.
19 And the workers of the city, from all the tribes of Israel, shall work it.
20 The whole contribution shall be twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand; four-square you shall set apart the holy contribution, together with the possession of the city.
21 And what remains shall be for the prince, on one side and on the other of the holy contribution and of the possession of the city: in front of the twenty-five thousand of the contribution to the east border, and westward in front of the twenty-five thousand to the west border, alongside the portions, for the prince; and the holy contribution and the sanctuary of the house shall be in its midst.
22 And from the possession of the Levites and from the possession of the city, in the midst of what belongs to the prince, between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, shall be the prince's.
23 And as for the rest of the tribes: from the east side to the west side, Benjamin, one portion.
24 And beside the border of Benjamin, from the east side to the west side: Simeon, one portion.
25 And beside the border of Simeon, from the east side to the west side: Issachar, one portion.
26 And beside the border of Issachar, from the east side to the west side: Zebulun, one portion.
27 And beside the border of Zebulun, from the east side to the west side: Gad, one portion.
28 And beside the border of Gad, at the south side southward, the border shall be from Tamar to the waters of Meribath Kadesh, on to the brook, to the Great Sea.
29 This is the land that you shall allot as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel, and these are their portions, the declaration of the Lord Yahweh.
30 And these are the exits of the city: on the north side, four thousand five hundred by measure;
31 and the gates of the city shall be after the names of the tribes of Israel: three gates northward — one gate of Reuben, one gate of Judah, one gate of Levi;
32 and at the east side, four thousand five hundred, and three gates: one gate of Joseph, one gate of Benjamin, one gate of Dan;
33 and at the south side, four thousand five hundred by measure, and three gates: one gate of Simeon, one gate of Issachar, one gate of Zebulun;
34 at the west side, four thousand five hundred, their gates three: one gate of Gad, one gate of Asher, one gate of Naphtali.
35 All around it shall be eighteen thousand; and the name of the city from that day shall be: Yahweh is there.
