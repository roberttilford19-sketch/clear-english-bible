Clear English Bible — Nahum (Chapters 1–3): A Clear English Translation

This completes the Masoretic book of Nahum in this edition. The remaining books of the Old Testament stand in this edition as a clear-English modernization of the World English Bible (WEB) Classic text named in the front matter, except the books the front matter names as fresh translations made directly from the Hebrew; the New Testament stands on the Greek text named there. The book as printed here stands as DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED.

Insertion record — 23 November 2026. The Masoretic chapters of Nahum (3 chapters, 47 verses in Masoretic numbering) were inserted as a fresh translation made directly from the Masoretic Hebrew under Robert Tilford's Fresh Tanakh commission ruling of 23 November 2026 and its authorization-forward amendment, confirmed the same day, replacing the earlier WEB-based text of those chapters. The block is reseated in the Masoretic scheme: the verse that stood as English 1:15 now stands as 2:1, opening chapter 2, and the verses that stood as English 2:1–13 now stand as 2:2–14; chapter 3 keeps its bounds, and the block header is unchanged, the chapter count being the same in both schemes. The translation was built in two reviser passes (Masoretic chapter 1; chapters 2–3) from the Masoretic text as it stands in the Westminster Leningrad Codex and Sefaria's Miqra according to the Masorah — the two witnesses consonant-exact in 37 of the book's 47 verses, the ten divergences classified in the build record (six defects of the Bolls extraction, three spelling only, one form variant) — with JPS 1917 as the open control of record. Two independent checker passes that did not build the text read the draft against the Hebrew line and both controls and returned PASS with zero errors, so no correction was needed before insertion. The divine name יהוה stands 13 times in the book — no banked benchmark existed, so the count was established from both witnesses before the build; the two witnesses agree at every verse — and prints "Yahweh" at every seat; no standalone יהּ seat stands in Nahum. The difference ledger records 46 of the book's 47 verses as differing materially from the seated text or the open control, and 23 open seats stand recorded. The directed seats stand as the commission named them: 1:2–8 (the opening theophany, the partial acrostic as it stands); 1:3 ("slow to anger and great in power"); 1:7 ("Yahweh is good, a stronghold in the day of trouble"); Masoretic 2:1 (the messenger's feet, printed as Nahum's Hebrew stands); Masoretic 2:14 and 3:5 ("Behold, I am against you — the declaration of Yahweh of hosts"); and 3:19 (the closing clapping of hands, at full force). Nahum 1

1 The burden of Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.
2 Yahweh is a jealous and avenging God. Yahweh avenges and is full of wrath. Yahweh takes vengeance on his adversaries, and he keeps wrath for his enemies.
3 Yahweh is slow to anger and great in power, and he will by no means leave the guilty unpunished. Yahweh has his way in the whirlwind and in the storm, and the clouds are the dust of his feet.
4 He rebukes the sea and makes it dry, and dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel languish, and the flower of Lebanon languishes.
5 The mountains quake before him, and the hills melt away. The earth heaves at his presence, the world, and all who dwell in it.
6 Who can stand before his indignation? Who can endure the fierceness of his anger? His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are broken apart by him.
7 Yahweh is good, a stronghold in the day of trouble; and he knows those who take refuge in him.
8 But with an overflowing flood he will make a complete end of her place, and he will pursue his enemies into darkness.
9 What do you devise against Yahweh? He will make a complete end. Affliction will not rise up a second time.
10 For, entangled like thorns and drunken with their drink, they are consumed like fully dry stubble.
11 Out of you has come one who devises evil against Yahweh, who counsels wickedness.
12 Thus says Yahweh: Though they are in full strength and likewise many, even so they will be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more.
13 Now I will break his yoke from off you, and I will tear your bonds apart.
14 Yahweh has commanded concerning you: Your name will no longer be sown. From the house of your gods I will cut off the carved image and the molten image. I will make your grave, for you are worthless.

Nahum 2

1 Behold, on the mountains, the feet of a messenger who proclaims peace! Celebrate your feasts, Judah; fulfill your vows, for the worthless one will never again pass through you. He is utterly cut off.
2 A scatterer has come up against your face. Guard the stronghold! Watch the road! Strengthen your loins! Muster your strength with all your might!
3 For Yahweh is restoring the splendor of Jacob, like the splendor of Israel, for plunderers have plundered them and ruined their vine branches.
4 The shield of his warriors is reddened; the valiant men are dressed in scarlet. The chariots flash with fire of steel on the day of his preparation, and the cypress spears are brandished.
5 The chariots rage madly in the streets; they jostle one another in the squares. Their appearance is like torches; they dart like flashes of lightning.
6 He summons his mighty ones; they stumble in their march. They hurry to her wall, and the protective shelter is set in place.
7 The gates of the rivers are opened, and the palace dissolves.
8 It is decreed: she is stripped and carried away, and her maidservants moan like the voice of doves, beating on their breasts.
9 Nineveh has been like a pool of water from her earliest days, yet they are fleeing: Stand! Stand! But no one turns back.
10 Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold! For there is no end to the store, an abundance of every precious thing.
11 Empty, void, and waste! The heart melts, the knees knock together, anguish is in all loins, and the faces of them all have turned pale.
12 Where is the den of the lions, the feeding place of the young lions, where the lion and the lioness walked, and the lion's cub, and no one made them afraid?
13 The lion tore enough for his cubs and strangled prey for his lionesses; he filled his caves with prey and his dens with torn flesh.
14 Behold, I am against you — the declaration of Yahweh of hosts. I will burn her chariots in smoke, and the sword will devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers will no longer be heard.

Nahum 3

1 Woe to the bloody city! She is wholly full of lies and plunder; the prey never departs.
2 The sound of the whip, the sound of the rattling of the wheels, galloping horses, and bounding chariots!
3 Charging horsemen, the flashing sword and the glittering spear, a multitude of slain and a mass of corpses, and there is no end to the bodies; they stumble over their bodies.
4 Because of the countless harlotries of the prostitute, lovely in grace, a mistress of sorceries, who sells nations through her harlotries and families through her sorceries.
5 Behold, I am against you — the declaration of Yahweh of hosts. I will lift your skirts over your face, and I will show the nations your nakedness and the kingdoms your shame.
6 I will throw detestable filth on you and make you vile, and I will set you up as a spectacle.
7 All who see you will flee from you and say: Nineveh is laid waste. Who will grieve for her? Where shall I seek comforters for you?
8 Are you better than No-Amon, who was situated among the rivers, who had the waters around her, whose rampart was the sea, and her wall was from the sea?
9 Cush and Egypt were her strength, without limit. Put and Libya were her helpers.
10 Yet she too went into exile; she went into captivity. Her young children also were dashed in pieces at the head of every street, and they cast lots for her honored men, and all her great men were bound in chains.
11 You also will be drunk; you will be hidden. You also will seek a stronghold because of the enemy.
12 All your fortresses are like fig trees with the first-ripe figs: if they are shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater.
13 Behold, your people within you are women. The gates of your land are set wide open to your enemies; fire has devoured your bars.
14 Draw water for the siege! Strengthen your fortresses! Go into the clay and tread the mortar; take hold of the brick mold!
15 There the fire will devour you; the sword will cut you off; it will devour you like the locust. Make yourself many like the locust; make yourself many like the locust swarm.
16 You have multiplied your merchants more than the stars of the sky. The locust strips off and flies away.
17 Your crowned ones are like the locusts, and your officials are like swarms of locusts, which settle on the walls on a cold day; but when the sun rises they flee away, and their place is not known, where they are.
18 Your shepherds slumber, king of Assyria; your nobles lie down to rest. Your people are scattered on the mountains, and there is no one to gather them.
19 There is no relief for your fracture; your wound is grievous. All who hear the report of you clap their hands over you, for on whom has your evil not passed, continually?
