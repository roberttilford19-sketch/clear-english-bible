Malachi 1

1 An oracle: the word of Yahweh to Israel by the hand of Malachi.

2 I have loved you, says Yahweh. But you say: How have you loved us? Was Esau not the brother of Jacob, declares Yahweh, yet I loved Jacob,

3 But Esau I hated, and I made his mountains a desolation and gave his heritage to the jackals of the wilderness.

4 If Edom says: We have been beaten down, but we will return and build the ruins, thus says Yahweh of hosts: They will build, but I will tear down, and they will be called a border of wickedness, and the people with whom Yahweh is angry forever.

5 Your eyes will see, and you will say: Yahweh is great beyond the border of Israel.

6 A son honors a father, and a servant his master. If I am a father, where is my honor? And if I am a master, where is my fear, says Yahweh of hosts to you, the priests who despise my name. But you say: How have we despised your name?

7 You present polluted food on my altar. But you say: How have we polluted you? By saying: The table of Yahweh is contemptible.

8 And when you present the blind as a sacrifice, is that not wrong? And when you present the lame and the sick, is that not wrong? Present it, please, to your governor. Will he be pleased with you, or will he show you favor, says Yahweh of hosts.

9 And now, please plead for the favor of God, that he may be gracious to us. This has come from your hand. Will he show favor to any of you, says Yahweh of hosts.

10 Who among you will shut the doors, so that you do not kindle my altar in vain? I have no pleasure in you, says Yahweh of hosts, and I will not accept an offering from your hand.

11 For from the rising of the sun to its going down, my name is great among the nations, and in every place incense is offered to my name, and a pure offering. For my name is great among the nations, says Yahweh of hosts.

12 But you profane it by saying: The table of the Lord is polluted, and its fruit, its food, is contemptible.

13 And you say: Look, what a weariness, and you sniff at it, says Yahweh of hosts. And you bring what was stolen, and the lame, and the sick, and so you bring the offering. Should I accept it from your hand, says Yahweh.

14 Cursed is the deceiver who has a male in his flock, and vows, and sacrifices a blemished animal to the Lord. For I am a great King, says Yahweh of hosts, and my name is feared among the nations.

Malachi 2

1 And now, this command is for you, priests.

2 If you do not listen, and if you do not take it to heart to give glory to my name, says Yahweh of hosts, I will send the curse among you, and I will curse your blessings. Indeed, I have already cursed them, because you do not take it to heart.

3 Look, I will rebuke your offspring, and I will spread dung on your faces, the dung of your feasts, and you will be taken away with it.

4 And you will know that I sent this command to you, so that my covenant would be with Levi, says Yahweh of hosts.

5 My covenant with him was life and peace, and I gave them to him in reverence. He feared me, and he was in awe before my name.

6 The teaching of truth was in his mouth, and no wrong was found on his lips. He walked with me in peace and uprightness, and he turned many back from iniquity.

7 For the lips of a priest should guard knowledge, and people should seek teaching from his mouth, for he is the messenger of Yahweh of hosts.

8 But you have turned aside from the way. You have caused many to stumble in the teaching. You have corrupted the covenant of Levi, says Yahweh of hosts.

9 Therefore I have also made you contemptible and base before all the people, inasmuch as you do not keep my ways, but show partiality in the teaching.

10 Do we not all have one father? Has not one God created us? Why then do we deal treacherously, each against his brother, profaning the covenant of our fathers?

11 Judah has dealt treacherously, and an abomination has been done in Israel and in Jerusalem. For Judah has profaned the holiness of Yahweh, which he loves, and has married the daughter of a foreign god.

12 May Yahweh cut off the man who does this, the one who wakes and the one who answers, from the tents of Jacob, and the one who presents an offering to Yahweh of hosts.

13 And this is a second thing you do: you cover the altar of Yahweh with tears, with weeping and groaning, so that he no longer turns to the offering or accepts it with favor from your hand.

14 But you say: Why? Because Yahweh has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth, against whom you have dealt treacherously, though she is your companion and the wife of your covenant.

15 Did not one make them, with a remnant of spirit belonging to him? And what does the one seek? Godly offspring. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not deal treacherously against the wife of your youth.

16 For I hate divorce, says Yahweh, the God of Israel, and the one who covers his garment with violence, says Yahweh of hosts. Therefore guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not deal treacherously.

17 You have wearied Yahweh with your words. But you say: How have we wearied him? By saying: Everyone who does evil is good in the eyes of Yahweh, and he delights in them, or: Where is the God of justice?

Malachi 3

1 See, I am sending my messenger, and he will clear the way before me; and the Lord, whom you seek, will suddenly come to his temple, and the messenger of the covenant, whom you delight in: see, he comes, says Yahweh of hosts.

2 But who can endure the day of his coming? And who will stand when he appears? For he is like a refiner's fire, and like launderers' soap;

3 and he will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver, and he will purify the sons of Levi and refine them like gold and like silver, and they will present offerings to Yahweh in righteousness.

4 Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to Yahweh, as in the days of old and as in former years.

5 I will come near to you for judgment, and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, against the adulterers, against those who swear falsely, against those who oppress the hired worker in his wages, the widow and the orphan, and who turn the sojourner aside, and do not fear me, says Yahweh of hosts.

6 For I, Yahweh, do not change, and you, sons of Jacob, are not consumed.

7 From the days of your fathers you have turned aside from my statutes and have not kept them. Return to me, and I will return to you, says Yahweh of hosts. But you say: How shall we return?

8 Will a man rob God? Yet you are robbing me. But you say: How have we robbed you? In the tithe and the offering.

9 You are cursed with the curse, yet you are robbing me, even this whole nation.

10 Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in my house, and test me now in this, says Yahweh of hosts, if I will not open for you the windows of heaven and pour out for you a blessing until there is more than enough.

11 I will rebuke the devourer for you, and it will not destroy the fruit of the ground for you, and your vine in the field will not lose its fruit, says Yahweh of hosts.

12 All nations will call you blessed, for you will be a land of delight, says Yahweh of hosts.

13 Your words have been harsh against me, says Yahweh. But you say: What have we spoken against you?

14 You have said: It is useless to serve God. What gain is there that we have kept his charge and that we have walked in mourning before Yahweh of hosts?

15 And now we call the arrogant blessed; those who do wickedness are even built up; they even test God and escape.

16 Then those who feared Yahweh spoke one with another, and Yahweh listened and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before him for those who feared Yahweh and esteemed his name.

17 They will be mine, says Yahweh of hosts, my treasured possession on the day when I act, and I will spare them as a man spares his son who serves him.

18 Then once again you will discern between the righteous and the wicked, between one who serves God and one who does not serve him.

19 For see, the day is coming, burning like a furnace, and all the arrogant and all who do wickedness will be stubble, and the coming day will set them ablaze, says Yahweh of hosts, so that it will leave them neither root nor branch.

20 But for you who fear my name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings, and you will go out and leap like calves from the stall.

21 You will tread down the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet on the day when I act, says Yahweh of hosts.

22 Remember the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel: statutes and ordinances.

23 See, I am sending you Elijah the prophet before the great and terrible day of Yahweh comes.

24 He will turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and strike the land with utter destruction.
