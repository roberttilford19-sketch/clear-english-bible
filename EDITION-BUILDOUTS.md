## Card 1 — The Queen's English Edition

**The goal, in Robert's words:** another Bible based on the Clear Translation, "the King James Version language, of the these, the thous, but done in such a way that it's perfect — what was it back then, Queen's English — but it's going to be perfect. There's going to be none of this unicorn in there and all of this kind of stuff."

**Definition of done:** every book of the Clear Translation re-rendered in flawless Early Modern English under a written period-grammar sheet — thou (singular subject), thee (singular object), thy/thine, ye (plural subject), you (plural object), the verb endings in -est and -eth — with the Clear Translation's readings preserved exactly. The 1611 text's errors are NOT imported: where the King James prints "unicorn," the Hebrew word is re'em, the wild ox, and the base already reads it truly; where 1611 prints "Easter" (Acts 12:4), the base reads Passover; "charity" (1 Corinthians 13) reads love. The corrections ride in from the base. They are never re-decided inside this lane.

**The precision note, recorded because it is the edition's honest claim:** the old pronouns restore a distinction the original languages carry and modern English lost — thou against ye, one person addressed against many. Done perfectly, the old tongue is not merely prettier here; at those verses it is more exact.

**Allowed sources:** the published Clear Translation text as the sole base; period-grammar references for the grammar sheet.

**Missing access:** none. The base is in-house and hashed.

**Stop-lines:** register only — no verse's meaning may change in the transfer; wherever the old grammar forces a choice the base left open, the choice goes to Robert as a ruling and is never decided silently; the front matter states honestly that this is a register edition OF the Clear Translation, not a new translation from the Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek.

**First step, when launched:** the grammar sheet (the lane's Step Zero), then one audition book — Jonah suggested — for Robert's ear before any fleet work.

**Status: PLANNED.**

## Card 2 — The Paraphrase Edition

**The goal, in Robert's words:** "the possibility of doing a paraphrase, like some of the other translations" — a free, idiomatic retelling in the family of the Living Bible and The Message, for readers the formal text does not reach.

**Definition of done:** the Clear Translation retold in contemporary spoken English, derived from the base alone, auditioned on one short book first (Jonah or Ruth), and only then extended book by book under his word.

**Stop-lines:** the word PARAPHRASE stands on the cover and in the front matter — never presented as a translation (the house's no-crowns doctrine); the retelling introduces no reading the base does not carry; where a paraphrase choice could bend a meaning, the formal text governs and the bend is refused.

**Status: PLANNED.**

## Card 3 — The Editions App

**The goal, in Robert's words:** "a possibility of an app for that" — one app carrying the editions.

**Definition of done (diagram level):** one owned, offline app shell in the pattern of the house's existing builds — the reader chooses Clear, Queen's English, or Paraphrase, book by book, with the editions laid side by side where a reader wants to compare. Built only after the apps discussion Robert has called for, and priced by the house doctrine (free first, or the one-time ninety-nine cents; never a subscription; no user data collected).

**Stop-lines:** no build begins before the apps discussion; the shell carries only finished, ruled editions — no draft text rides in it silently.

**Status: PLANNED — diagram only.**

## Card 4 — "Talk to the Quran" (app)

**The goal, in Robert's words:** an app design "to talk to the Quran itself, where everything is related to the Quran and that is the logic loop there, so that you can't go wrong." A companion in spirit to his red-letter apps — as those are like talking to the words of Jesus himself, this one is talking to the Quran itself.

**The design, as he specified it:** the app's knowledge and logic are bounded to the Quran alone. His founding test case, in his example: if a user says "the Zakat rate is 2.5%," the app answers at once — there is no such percentage in the Quran; anyone who told you that is misleading you; what the Quran alone says is stated in its place. The purpose he names: many people follow two different sets of guidelines without realizing it, and the second set creeps in and bends the way they experience the Quran; the app exists to wake them to the difference.

**Standing rules it inherits:** the house's Quran-alone doctrine (terms defined by the Quran itself, never by hadith or tradition); honest labeling (the app's answers are the Quran's text and the editor's framing, named as such); the house app doctrine (owned, offline-first, free or one-time ninety-nine cents, no subscriptions, no user data collected).

**Status: PLANNED — intake card only, gated behind the apps discussion Robert has called for, like Cards 1–3.**
