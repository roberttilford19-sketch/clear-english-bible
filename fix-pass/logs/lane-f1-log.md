# Lane F-1 log — Old Testament deliberative “shall” review (Rulings 1 + 2)

Date: 2026-10-18. Lane: F-1 (Old Testament) of the Clear Translation Bible fix pass. Authority: FIX-PASS-BRIEF-2026-10-18.md; RULINGS-GIVEN-2026-10-18.md, Rulings 1 and 2; CEB-RULINGS-PACKET.md, Decisions 1 and 2.

Scope note: changes were applied only in `fix-pass/staging/books/` and `fix-pass/staging/individual-books/`. Editions were not edited (the coordinator applies edition changes serially). Deuterocanonical books (Judith, Tobit, Sirach, 1 Maccabees) have no individual-books reader, so those changes touch the books surface only. Record-only places Psalm 116:12, 2 Kings 20:9, and Esther 6:6 were not touched and were not judged.

## Changes applied

### 1 Samuel 5:8 — books + individual-books
- Before: What must we do with the ark of the God of Israel?
- After: What should we do with the ark of the God of Israel?
- Ruling: Ruling 1
- Ground: The Philistine lords ask what ought to be done with the ark and no duty is prescribed, so obligation, not prescribed duty, is the force.

### 1 Samuel 6:2 — books + individual-books
- Before: What must we do with Yahweh’s ark?
- After: What should we do with Yahweh’s ark?
- Ruling: Ruling 1
- Ground: The priests and diviners are asked what ought to be done, and the verse itself continues “how we should send it,” so obligation governs over the drafted “must.”

### Judith 12:12 — books only (no individual-books reader for this book)
- Before: if we shall let such a woman go
- After: if we will let such a woman go
- Ruling: Ruling 1
- Ground: The servants’ conditional looks to their own intended course, with no solemnity or precedent weight, so open intention takes “will.”

### Jonah 1:11 — books + individual-books
- Before: What shall we do to you, that the sea may be calm to us?
- After: What should we do to you, that the sea may be calm to us?
- Ruling: Ruling 1
- Ground: The sailors ask what course is required of them to calm the sea, so the question truly asks what ought to be done.

### Song of Songs 8:8 — books + individual-books
- Before: What must we do for our sister
- After: What should we do for our sister
- Ruling: Ruling 1
- Ground: The brothers deliberate what ought to be done for their sister and no duty is prescribed, so “must” fails and obligation takes “should.”

### 1 Maccabees 3:17 — books only (no individual-books reader for this book)
- Before: Shall we be able, being a small company,
- After: Will we be able, being a small company,
- Ruling: Ruling 2
- Ground: Judged with Judas’s built-in answer as one unit, the soldiers ask about their open capability and course, not an obligation, so “will” carries the force.
