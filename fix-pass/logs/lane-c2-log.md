# LANE C-2 LOG — Old Testament lineation (Ruling 8, Decision 8)

Lane: C-2 (Old Testament lineation), Clear Translation Bible fix pass, 2026-10-18.
Ruling cited at every place: **Ruling 8** — restore the poetry lineation at every Decision 8 place, working from the recorded lineation in each chapter's apparatus; presentation only, no wording, punctuation, or verse order changes; chapters the base sets as prose are never relineated.

## Headline result, with its caveat in the same breath

**No chapter in Lane C-2's scope could be relineated, because no in-scope chapter's apparatus records the base's divisions in full and in order.** That is the whole scope, not a sample: all 425 in-scope chapters were examined one by one against their chapter apparatus and their book's completion report. The Old Testament chapter files record presentation only as a general flag — that the base sets the passage in poetry or poetic lines (its q/q2 markup), in whole or in part, sometimes with a verse-span note (for example, a Selah line, a prose lead verse, or a poetry span by verse numbers). None of them carries the form the lane instructions name as the source of record: a furniture/presentation entry stating "The divisions stand in this order: ..." with each division quoted and counted by kind (q / q2 / m). That full form exists in this project only in New Testament files outside this lane (for example 1 Peter 1, where the apparatus records 2 q and 2 q2 divisions and quotes them in order). The in-scope completion reports likewise contain no per-chapter division counts — the word "divisions" does not occur in any of the sixteen in-scope completion reports — so there was nothing to cross-check a reconstruction against either.

Under the lane's own stop rule — if a chapter's apparatus does not record its divisions in full, stop that chapter, log it, and move on; do not reconstruct lineation from any other source — every poetry or mixed chapter below is therefore logged STOPPED, and every chapter whose own apparatus records the base as prose is logged SKIPPED (never relineated under Ruling 8). The pinned base snapshots were not used as a substitute source: they join each verse's lines into one line, exactly as their own notes state, so they do not preserve the divisions either.
