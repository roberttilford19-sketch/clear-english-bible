## Isaiah 44 — the single-boundary stop, anatomy recorded for the desk

Isaiah 44 stopped at verse 19: "split point falls inside a conversion span (ambiguous
alignment)." The coordinator's own diagnosis: the verse has 7 chunks (1 q, 6 q2) with
boundaries at base tokens 3, 11, 20, 29, 36, 46, 54. Exactly one boundary — at token 46,
after "…into an abomination?" — falls strictly inside a conversion span: base tokens
44–47 "an abomination? Shall" became staged tokens 44–48 "a detestable thing? Will"
(the "abomination" → "detestable thing" conversion plus "Shall" → "Will"). Token alignment
cannot place that one boundary mechanically; every other boundary in the chapter places
cleanly. To a reader the sense-placement is plain ("…a detestable thing?" | "Will I bow
down to a tree trunk?"), but placing it requires one interpretive step the pass's
discipline does not take on its own. The chapter therefore stands STOPPED, in prose as
staged, awaiting a desk ruling on whether this single boundary may be placed by sense.
No other chapter in Waves B–D stopped on alignment.
